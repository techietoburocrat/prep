# UPSC Mains — Polity Questions: 2012

[← 2011](2011.md) · [Index](00_INDEX.md) · [2013 →](2013.md)

**Paper:** General Studies **Paper I** (old pattern — final year) · **Polity marks:** ~37 / 300

> **The thinnest polity year in the whole set.** No 250-word constitutional question at all; polity survives only as 50-word and 20-word items. This is the low point immediately before the 2013 restructuring, which gave polity an entire 250-mark paper of its own.
>
> GS Paper II in 2012 carried no polity — it was International Relations, Economy, Science & Technology and Statistics.

---

## Fundamental Rights

### Q3(d) · 5 marks · ~50 words
What are the Rights within the ambit of Article 21 of the Indian Constitution?

`Article 21` `Right to life and personal liberty`

> The Article 21 strand: **2006** Q6(a) → **2012** Q3(d) → **2015** GS-II Q7 (clean environment) → **2023** GS-II Q11 → **2024** GS-II Q12 (privacy and DNA testing).

Comment on the significance of the Preamble contained in the Right to Information Act.

`RTI Act, 2005` `Transparency`

> The RTI returns in **2020** (GS-II Q2) on amendments affecting the Information Commission's autonomy.

## Statutory Bodies & Quasi-Judicial Forums

### Q3(a) · 5 marks · ~50 words
What is the 'Parivarik Mahila Lok Adalat'?

`Lok Adalat` `NCW` `Alternative dispute resolution`

> Lok Adalats get a full 10-mark question in **2024** (GS-II Q2), contrasted with arbitration tribunals.

### Q3(f) · 5 marks · ~50 words

`National Council for Senior Citizens` `Statutory bodies`
