## Federalism & Special Provisions

### Q1 · 12.5 marks · 200 words
Discuss the essentials of the 69th Constitutional Amendment Act and anomalies, if any, that have led to the recent reported conflicts between the elected representatives and the institution of the Lieutenant Governor in the administration of Delhi. Do you think that this will give rise to a new trend in the functioning of the Indian federal politics?

`69th Amendment` `Article 239AA` `Delhi` `Lieutenant Governor`

> The Delhi tussle returns in **2018** (Q11) after the Supreme Court's July 2018 judgment.

### Q2 · 12.5 marks · 200 words
To what extent is Article 370 of the Indian Constitution, bearing marginal note "Temporary provision with respect to the State of Jammu and Kashmir", temporary? Discuss the future prospects of this provision in the context of Indian polity.

`Article 370` `Jammu and Kashmir`

> Answered by events in August 2019; the successor question is **2025** Q4 on the J&K Legislative Assembly after the Reorganisation Act.

### Q7 · 12.5 marks · 200 words

`Government of India Act, 1935` `Historical underpinnings`
