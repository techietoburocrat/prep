## Preamble & Emergency

### Q6 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*
**(a)** What is the significance of a preamble to a constitution? Bring out the philosophy of the Indian polity as reflected in core constitutional values.

**(b)** Discuss the meaning of 'breakdown of constitutional machinery'. What are its effects?

`Preamble` `Article 356` `President's Rule`

> The Preamble returns in **2016** (GS-II Q5); Article 356 in **2023** (GS-II Q13).

## Equality & Public Service Commissions

### Q7 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*
**(a)** Discuss how the Constitution of India provides equal rights.

**(b)** How does the Indian Constitution seek to maintain independence of the Public Service Commission?

`Articles 14-18` `UPSC` `Article 315-323`

> The UPSC's composition and functions return in **2008** (Q6c) and its evolving role in **2009** (Q7b). Equality is compared with the US Constitution in **2021** (GS-II Q13).

## Money Bills, Finance Commission & Panchayats

### Q8 · 2 × 15 = 30 marks · ~150 words each · *answer any two*
**(a)** Define Money-bill. Discuss how it is passed in the Parliament.

**(b)** What is a Finance Commission? Discuss the main functions of the State Finance Commission.

**(c)** Discuss how state governments can exercise control over panchayats.

`Article 110` `Article 280` `Article 243-I` `Panchayati Raj`

> The Finance Commission returns in **2013** (13th FC), **2018** (constitution and ToR) and **2021** (14th FC). State control over panchayats anticipates **2023** GS-II Q3 on states' reluctance to empower local bodies.
