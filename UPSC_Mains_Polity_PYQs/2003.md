## Executive Powers

### Q6 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*
**(a)** Discuss the question of death sentence and Presidential clemency.

**(b)** Explain the discretionary powers of the Governor of a State.

`Article 72` `Pardon power` `Governor's discretion`

> Presidential clemency returns in **2011** (Q1b), **2014** (GS-II Q5) and, comparatively with the USA, in **2025** (GS-II Q3).

## Parliament

### Q7 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*

**(b)** Identify the major obstacles in the smooth functioning of Parliamentary democracy in India.

`Parliamentary control` `Accountability`

> Q7(a) is asked again nearly word-for-word in **2021** (GS-II Q4).

## Amendments, Duties & Rajya Sabha

### Q8 · 2 × 15 = 30 marks · ~150 words each · *answer any two*
**(a)** Highlight the significance of Forty Fourth Amendment to the Constitution of India.

**(b)** Identify the major Fundamental Duties.

**(c)** Explain the relevance of Rajya Sabha as a second chamber in the federal set up of Indian Parliamentary System.

`44th Amendment` `Fundamental Duties` `Rajya Sabha`

> Fundamental Duties return in **2008** (Q6a) and **2011** (Q1a). Rajya Sabha returns in **2020** (GS-II Q14).
