## Federalism & Inter-State Disputes

### Q1(a) · 20 marks · ~250 words
"Disputes between the riparian states on sharing of river waters in post-Independence India are becoming increasingly complex." Objectively analyse the major disputes in this connection, with special reference to the Southern States.

`Article 262` `Inter-State River Water Disputes Act` `Federalism`

> Asked again in **2013** (GS-II Q6), which shifts the frame to *why the constitutional mechanism has failed*.

## Constitutional Amendment

### Q2(b) · 12 marks · ~150 words
With respect to Cooperative Societies what are the salient features of the 106th and 111th Constitutional Amendment Bills as at present?

`Cooperative societies` `Part IXB`

> These bills became the **97th Amendment Act, 2011** — a useful reminder that Mains asks about pending bills, not just enacted law.

## Parliament

### Q4(a) · 12 marks · ~150 words
What are the grounds of disqualification of a Member of Parliament from either House? Quote relevant provisions in your answer.

`Article 102` `Tenth Schedule`

> **Repeats 2006 Q6(b)**; recast under the RPA 1951 in **2019** (GS-II Q11).

### Q5(e) · 5 marks · ~50 words
Legislative powers assigned to the Rajya Sabha under Art. 249 and Art. 312 of the Constitution

`Article 249` `Article 312` `Rajya Sabha`

### Q6(b) · 12 marks · ~150 words
Bring out the powers and responsibilities attached to the office of the Speaker of the Lok Sabha.

`Speaker` `Article 93`

> The Speaker returns in **2020** (GS-II Q5, "Once a Speaker, Always a Speaker") and, for state legislatures, in **2023** (GS-II Q5).
