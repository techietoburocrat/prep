## How the chapters connect (the story of the whole book)
1. **Ch 1** asks the foundational question — what actually *is* democracy, and is it worth having?
2. **Ch 2** shows how a country's founding document (the Constitution) turns democratic ideals into a workable, lasting system.
3. **Ch 3** zooms into the mechanism that keeps democracy accountable to the people — elections.
4. **Ch 4** examines the institutions (Parliament, Executive, Judiciary) that actually use the power elections hand them, and how they check each other.
5. **Ch 5** closes the book by asking what protects citizens *from* these very institutions when they overreach — rights.

Together, these five chapters form a complete arc: **definition → foundation → participation → institutions → protection.**
