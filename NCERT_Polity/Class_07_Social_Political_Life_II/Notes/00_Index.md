## How the chapters connect (the story of the whole book)
This book is built around **five big themes (Units)**, and **equality is the thread running through all of them**:
1. **Unit 1 – Equality (Ch 1):** the foundation — everyone is equal and deserves dignity.
2. **Unit 2 – State Government (Ch 2–3):** how government works, using *health* as the example.
3. **Unit 3 – Gender (Ch 4–5):** how society treats boys/girls unequally, and how women fight back.
4. **Unit 4 – Media (Ch 6):** how media shapes what we know — vital for a democracy.
5. **Unit 5 – Markets (Ch 7–8):** how goods reach us, and how markets create *unequal* gains.

Notice how Ch 8 (the shirt) ties everything back to Ch 1: real equality means a **fair wage** and **dignity** for everyone — from the cotton farmer to the factory worker.

## Suggested revision order
Read in order (1 → 8) the first time. For quick revision before a test, do the most concept-heavy chapters first: **Ch 3 → Ch 8 → Ch 6 → Ch 2 → Ch 5 → Ch 4 → Ch 7 → Ch 1.**
