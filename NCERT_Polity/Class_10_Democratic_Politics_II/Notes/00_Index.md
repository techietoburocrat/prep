## How the chapters connect (the story of the whole book)
1. **Ch 1–2** are about **sharing power** — Chapter 1 introduces power-sharing in general; Chapter 2 zooms into its most important form, federalism (vertical sharing between centre, states, and local bodies).
2. **Ch 3** is about **social divisions** (gender, religion, caste) and how their political expression can be healthy or harmful.
3. **Ch 4** is about **political parties** — the vehicles that actually contest for and share power.
4. **Ch 5** steps back to **evaluate the whole system**: given all this, what does democracy actually achieve?

The book is a natural continuation of Class 9's *Democratic Politics I*: Class 9 asked *what democracy is and how its institutions work*; Class 10 asks *how power is shared, how social divisions play out, who competes for power, and what democracy delivers.*

## Suggested revision order
Read in order (1 → 5) the first time. For quick revision before a test: **Ch 2 → Ch 4 → Ch 5 → Ch 3 → Ch 1** (most concept- and fact-dense first).
