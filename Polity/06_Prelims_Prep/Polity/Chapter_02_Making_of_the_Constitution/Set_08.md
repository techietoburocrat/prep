### 1. 🔥
Consider the following statements:

1. M.N. Roy first proposed a Constituent Assembly for India in 1934.
2. The Constituent Assembly was constituted under the Cabinet Mission Plan of 1946.
3. The Drafting Committee was constituted on 26 November 1949.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) All three
- (d) None

<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (b)**

**Why (b):** Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

**Why not 3:** The Drafting Committee was constituted on **29 August 1947** — the Prelims 2023 trap, restated here in full-chapter form.

</details>

### 2.
The original strength of the Constituent Assembly, before the reduction caused by Partition, was

- (a) 296
- (b) 299
- (c) 389
- (d) 284

<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (c)**

**Why (c):** **389** — 296 provincial plus 93 princely-state seats. 299 is the post-Partition figure; 284 is the number of signatories on adoption day; 296 is only the provincial half.

</details>

### 11. 🔥
**Statement-I:** The Drafting Committee originally had seven members.

**Statement-II:** Both B.L. Mitter and D.P. Khaitan left the Committee during its work, one by resignation and one by death, and were replaced.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

- (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
- (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (b)**

**Why (b):** Both are true, but II does not explain I — the Committee's **original** size of seven (a starting fact) is unrelated to the **later** membership changes (a subsequent fact); the changes explain why the *effective* roster shifted, not why it started at seven.

</details>

### 23.
Which one of the following best explains why the Assembly's total elapsed time (2 years, 11 months, 18 days) is much larger than its total sitting days (165)?

- (a) The two figures are actually inconsistent and cannot both be true
- (b) Elapsed time includes gaps between the Assembly's 11 sessions, committee work outside formal sittings, and the public discussion period on the Draft
- (c) The Assembly sat continuously without any breaks
- (d) The 165 figure includes only Sundays

<details><summary>Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (b)**
