## The one sentence this chapter is built around

> *"The Constituent Assembly was formed through a process inherited from a limited colonial franchise, yet through nearly three years of open deliberation, responsible exercise of sovereign authority, and a willingness to reform its own inherited assumptions for the country's future, it produced a document — and set a precedent — whose legitimacy rests on conduct rather than on its own electoral origin."*

If a question ever leaves you unsure, ask which part of that sentence it is testing. Set 10 develops this fully.
