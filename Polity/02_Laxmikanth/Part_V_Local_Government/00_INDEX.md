## 🎯 What this Part is about

The 73rd and 74th Amendments (1992) added a **third tier of government** below the Union and the states — and then left the crucial decisions to the state legislatures.

⭐ **The result is the defining tension of this Part:** panchayats and municipalities have **constitutional existence** but **statutory powers**. Their structure, elections and reservations are **guaranteed by the Constitution**; their **functions, funds and functionaries** depend entirely on what each state chooses to devolve.

> ⭐ **That single sentence answers most of the fourteen Mains questions.** The bodies were made permanent and left powerless, and every question about their "ineffectiveness" traces back to it.

## ⭐ The structure both chapters share

Learn this skeleton once and it serves for both — then note only where the two diverge.

| Element | Panchayats *(Part IX)* | Municipalities *(Part IX-A)* |
|---|---|---|
| **Amendment** | ⭐ **73rd, 1992** — in force **24 April 1993** | ⭐ **74th, 1992** — in force **1 June 1993** |
| **Tiers / types** | ⭐ **Three tiers** — village, intermediate, district | ⭐ **Three types** — Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council, Municipal Corporation |
| **Schedule** | ⭐ **Eleventh — 29 items** | ⭐ **Twelfth — 18 items** |
| **Duration** | **5 years**; re-election within **6 months** of dissolution | **Same** |
| **Minimum age** | ⭐ **21** | ⭐ **21** |
| **Women's reservation** | ⭐ **Not less than one-third** | ⭐ **Not less than one-third** |
| **Election body** | **State Election Commission** *(Art 243K)* | **Same Commission** *(Art 243ZA)* |
| **Finance body** | ⭐ **State Finance Commission, constituted by the GOVERNOR every 5 years** *(Art 243-I)* | **The same Commission** *(Art 243Y)* |
