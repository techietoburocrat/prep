# Chapter 57 — State Information Commission

**Read time ~3 min** · **Right to Information Act, 2005** · 🟡 Prelims: as a contrast · Mains: inside RTI answers

> 📌 **The shortest chapter in Part VIII.** ⭐ **Same Act, same powers, three substitutions — and one federal oddity that is worth more than the rest of the chapter.**

---

## ⭐ The three differences

| # | Element | ⭐ **CIC** | ⭐ **SIC** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⭐ **Composition** | Chief Information Commissioner + up to **10** | ⭐ **State Chief Information Commissioner + up to TEN State Information Commissioners** *(the same ceiling)* |
| 2 | ⭐ **Appointed by** | The **PRESIDENT** on a committee of the **PM, the Leader of Opposition in the Lok Sabha, and a Union Cabinet Minister** | ⭐ **The GOVERNOR** on a committee of the ⭐ **CHIEF MINISTER *(chair)*, the LEADER OF OPPOSITION in the LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY, and a STATE CABINET MINISTER nominated by the CM** |
| 3 | ⭐ **Removed by** | The **President**, after a **Supreme Court** inquiry | ⭐ **The GOVERNOR**, after a reference by the Governor and an inquiry by the ⭐ **SUPREME COURT** |

> ⚠️ ⭐ **Note difference 3 carefully, because it breaks the pattern you learnt in [Ch 43](../Part_VII_Constitutional_Bodies/43_State_Public_Service_Commission.md) and [Ch 55](55_State_Human_Rights_Commission.md).** ⭐ **The State PSC and the State Human Rights Commission are removed by the PRESIDENT. The State Information Commission is removed by the GOVERNOR — but still only on a SUPREME COURT inquiry.** ⭐ **The Supreme Court's role is the constant; the removing authority is not.**

⭐ **Jurisdiction:** ⭐ **the SIC hears second appeals and complaints against STATE public authorities** — state departments, state PSUs, panchayats, municipalities and state-financed bodies. ⭐ **Powers are identical to the CIC's, including the binding orders and the ₹250-per-day personal penalty on the PIO up to ₹25,000.**

---

## ⚠️⚠️ The federal oddity worth a paragraph in any RTI answer

⭐ **The RTI (Amendment) Act, 2019 provided that the TERM, SALARY, ALLOWANCES and other terms of service of STATE Information Commissioners shall be "as may be PRESCRIBED BY THE CENTRAL GOVERNMENT."**

> ⚠️ ⭐⭐ **Read that again: a commissioner APPOINTED BY THE GOVERNOR, on a committee chaired by the CHIEF MINISTER, hearing appeals about STATE departments, serves on terms fixed by the UNION.**
>
> ⭐ **That is a genuine federal anomaly and the single best point available on the 2019 amendment** — most answers stop at "it hurts CIC independence" and never notice the state dimension. ⚠️ **Before 2019 the Act itself fixed those terms; the states never had the power either, but nor did the Union executive.**

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The substitutions
> ⭐ **Governor for President · Chief Minister for Prime Minister · Assembly Leader of Opposition for Lok Sabha Leader of Opposition · a STATE Cabinet Minister for a Union one.**

### The removal pattern across three state bodies
> ⭐ **State PSC → removed by the PRESIDENT.** ⭐ **State Human Rights Commission → removed by the PRESIDENT.** ⚠️ ⭐ **State Information Commission → removed by the GOVERNOR.** ⭐ **All three require a SUPREME COURT inquiry.**

### The oddity
> ⚠️ ⭐ **Appointed by the Governor. Terms prescribed by the CENTRE** *(2019 amendment)*.

---

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **State Chief Information Commissioner + up to TEN State Information Commissioners**, appointed by the ⭐ **GOVERNOR** on a committee of the ⭐ **CM (chair), the Leader of Opposition in the Legislative Assembly, and a state Cabinet Minister nominated by the CM.**
- ⭐ **Removed by the GOVERNOR** — on proved misbehaviour or incapacity, ⭐ **after a reference to and inquiry by the SUPREME COURT**; also for insolvency, moral-turpitude conviction, paid employment, infirmity or a conflicting interest. ⚠️ **No reappointment.**
- ⭐ **Jurisdiction: second appeals and complaints against STATE public authorities** — including panchayats and municipalities.
- ⭐ **Powers identical to the CIC's** — civil-court powers, access to **any** record, **binding** orders, ⭐ **₹250/day personal penalty up to ₹25,000**, compensation, and recommendation of disciplinary action.
- ⚠️ ⭐⭐ **Since the 2019 amendment, the term and salary of STATE Information Commissioners are prescribed by the CENTRAL GOVERNMENT** — a standing federal objection.
