## 🎯 What this Part is about

Parts I–V described how India governs **states**. This Part describes the **exceptions** — the places the ordinary state model was thought not to fit.

⭐ **There are two kinds of exception, and they are opposite in spirit:**

| | **Chapter 39 — Union Territories** | **Chapter 40 — Scheduled and Tribal Areas** |
|---|---|---|
| **The problem** | The territory is too small, too strategic, or too special to be a state | The **population** inside an ordinary state needs a different administration |
| ⭐ **The solution** | ⭐ **Centralise** — the President administers through an administrator | ⭐ **Insulate** — carve the area out of ordinary administration |
| **Who gains power** | ⭐ **The Union** | ⭐ **The Governor** *(Fifth Schedule)* or ⭐ **the tribal community itself** *(Sixth Schedule)* |
| **Constitutional home** | **Part VIII, Articles 239–241** | **Article 244 (Part X) + Fifth and Sixth Schedules** |

> ⭐ **The thread that runs through both chapters:** ⚠️ **neither a UT legislature nor an autonomous district council holds power in its own right.** One holds it by ordinary Act of Parliament, the other by the Governor's assent. ⭐ **This Part is where you see most clearly that Indian federalism is a delegation, not a division, wherever the Constitution says so.**

## ⭐ The four tables to carry out of this Part

**1. The eight Union Territories, and which three have legislatures**
> ⭐ **Delhi · Puducherry · Jammu and Kashmir** have assemblies. ⭐ **Ladakh · Chandigarh · Andaman and Nicobar · Lakshadweep · Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu** do not.

**2. The four articles**
> ⭐ **239** administrator · ⭐ **239A** Parliament may create a legislature **by ordinary law** · ⭐ **240** the President's **regulation** power *(can repeal an Act of Parliament)* · ⭐ **241** High Courts.

**3. What is carved out of each UT legislature**
> ⭐ **Delhi — public order, police AND land.** ⭐ **J&K — public order and police only.** ⭐ **Puducherry — nothing carved out**, but central law prevails.

**4. Fifth vs Sixth Schedule**
> ⭐⭐ **Sixth = Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram.** Fifth = the other ten states with Scheduled Areas. ⭐ **Fifth: Governor + advisory TAC. Sixth: elected, law-making Autonomous District Councils.**
