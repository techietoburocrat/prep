# Chapter 77 — National Integration

[← Chapter 76](76_Pressure_Groups.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 78 →](78_Foreign_Policy.md)

**Read time ~7 min** · **No single Part — provisions scattered across the Constitution** · 🟡 Prelims: **2015, 2023** · Mains: as an essay and GS-II theme

> 📌 ⭐ **A chapter that looks like general knowledge and is actually a collection of constitutional devices.** ⚠️ **Its exam value is that it lets you assemble provisions from six different Parts into a single argument** — and that skill is exactly what a high-scoring GS-II answer needs.

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐⭐ **National integration is not UNIFORMITY — it is a sense of common belonging that survives difference.** ⭐ **India's constitutional strategy was the opposite of the assimilationist one: RECOGNISE diversity formally — through linguistic states, the Sixth Schedule, minority rights and the 371-series — and build unity through SHARED INSTITUTIONS rather than a shared identity.**

---

## PART A — ⚠️ The eight obstacles

| # | Obstacle | The constitutional response |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **1** | ⭐ **REGIONALISM** — demands for statehood, autonomy and "sons of the soil" preference | **Articles 2, 3 and 4** for reorganisation; the **371-series**; the **Finance Commission** |
| ⭐ **2** | ⭐ **COMMUNALISM** | **Articles 15, 25–28**; **secularism** as basic structure *(*Bommai*, 1994)*; **Article 51A(e)** |
| ⭐ **3** | ⭐ **CASTEISM** | **Article 17** *(untouchability)*; **Articles 15(4), 16(4), 330–342A**; the **SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act** |
| ⭐ **4** | ⭐ **LINGUISM** | **Articles 29, 30, 343–351, 350A, 350B**; ⭐ **the 1967 continuation of English** *(see [Ch 64](64_Official_Language.md))* |
| ⭐ **5** | ⭐ **TRIBALISM and ethnic conflict** | The **Fifth and Sixth Schedules**; **Article 244A**; **PESA** and the **FRA** |
| ⭐ **6** | ⭐ **EXTREMISM, insurgency and terrorism** | **Articles 352–360**; the **UAPA**; the **NIA Act, 2008** |
| ⭐ **7** | **Migration and nativism** | ⭐ **Article 19(1)(d) and (e)** — the right to move and reside freely throughout India; **Articles 15 and 16** |
| ⭐ **8** | **Economic disparity between regions** | The **Finance Commission**; **Article 275(1)**; special-category assistance |

> ⭐ **Present the obstacles WITH their constitutional answers, as above.** ⚠️ **An answer that lists problems without the provisions reads like a newspaper column; one that pairs them reads like a polity answer.**

## PART B — ⭐⭐ The integrative devices in the Constitution

| Device | Provision |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **SINGLE CITIZENSHIP** | ⭐ **Articles 5–11** — no separate state citizenship, unlike the USA. ⭐ **The single most powerful integrative choice the framers made** |
| ⭐ **ONE CONSTITUTION for the Union and the states** | ⚠️ **Unlike the USA, where each state has its own** |
| ⭐ **A SINGLE, INTEGRATED JUDICIARY** | The Supreme Court and High Courts enforce both Union and state law — see [Ch 26](../Part_III_Central_Government/26_Supreme_Court.md) |
| ⭐ **ALL-INDIA SERVICES** | ⭐ **Article 312** — Patel's ⭐ **"steel frame"**, deliberately designed so that senior administrators owe allegiance beyond the state *(see [Ch 65](65_Public_Services.md))* |
| ⭐ **ONE GENERAL ELECTORAL ROLL** | ⭐ **Article 325** — an explicit rejection of the **separate communal electorates** of 1909–1935 |
| ⭐ **FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT AND RESIDENCE** | ⭐ **Article 19(1)(d) and (e)** — the constitutional answer to nativism |
| ⭐⭐ **FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES** | ⭐ **Article 51A(c)** — to uphold and protect the **sovereignty, unity and integrity** of India; ⭐ **51A(e)** — to promote **harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood transcending religious, linguistic and regional or sectional diversities**, and to renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women; **51A(f)** — to value and preserve the composite culture; **51A(i)** — to safeguard public property and abjure violence |
| ⭐ **The PREAMBLE** | ⭐ **"INTEGRITY" was added to "unity" by the 42nd Amendment, 1976** |
| ⭐ **Article 1** | ⭐ **"India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States"** — Ambedkar's ⭐ **"indestructible Union of destructible States"**; no right of secession |
| ⭐ **Coordination machinery** | **Article 263** *(Inter-State Council)*, **Article 262** *(water disputes)*, **Articles 301–307** *(freedom of trade and commerce throughout India)* |
| ⭐ **Emergency provisions** | **Articles 352–360**, the last resort against internal disintegration |

### ⭐⭐ The 16th Amendment Act, 1963 — the "anti-secession amendment"

⭐ **On the recommendation of the Committee on National Integration and Regionalisation set up by the National Integration Council**, the 16th Amendment:
1. ⭐ **Added "the SOVEREIGNTY AND INTEGRITY OF INDIA" as a ground for imposing reasonable restrictions on the freedoms of SPEECH, ASSEMBLY and ASSOCIATION *(Articles 19(2), 19(3) and 19(4))*;**
2. ⭐ **Amended the FORMS OF OATH in the Third Schedule** so that legislators, ministers and judges must swear to ⭐ **"uphold the sovereignty and integrity of India."**

> ⭐ **That amendment is the constitutional pivot of this chapter, and it is the source of the standing tension:** ⚠️ ⭐ **integration was made a permissible ground for restricting FREE SPEECH.** ⭐ **The **Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967** was enacted under exactly that authority — which is why every debate about the UAPA is, at bottom, a debate about where integration ends and dissent begins.**

## PART C — ⭐ The National Integration Council

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Set up** | ⭐ **1961**, after a **National Integration Conference** convened by the Prime Minister |
| ⭐ **Status** | ⚠️ ⭐ **Neither constitutional nor statutory — an EXECUTIVE body** |
| ⭐ **Chaired by** | ⭐ **The PRIME MINISTER** |
| ⭐ **Members** | Union ministers, ⭐ **all Chief Ministers**, leaders of national and regional parties, chairpersons of national commissions, educationists, journalists and eminent public figures |
| ⭐ **Function** | ⭐ **To REVIEW matters relating to national integration and make RECOMMENDATIONS** — ⚠️ **purely advisory** |
| ⚠️ ⭐ **The criticism** | ⚠️ ⭐ **It has met very rarely — a handful of times in six decades, and not at all in recent years.** ⭐ **A body designed for exactly the kind of consultation India's tensions require has been left unused** — the same charge that attaches to the **Inter-State Council** under Article 263 *(see [Ch 15](../Part_II_System_of_Government/15_Inter_State_Relations.md))* |

⭐ **Other measures worth naming:** the **three-language formula**; **cultural exchange programmes and *Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat***; **NCC and NSS**; national symbols and the ⭐ **Flag Code of India, 2002** *(asked in [Prelims 2023 Q14](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2023.md))*; ⭐ **Constitution Day, 26 November** *(asked in [Prelims 2023 Q15](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2023.md))*; and **balanced regional development** through Finance Commission transfers and aspirational-district programmes.

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The eight obstacles
> ⭐ **REGIONALISM · COMMUNALISM · CASTEISM · LINGUISM · TRIBALISM · EXTREMISM · NATIVISM · ECONOMIC DISPARITY.**

### The integrative devices
> ⭐⭐ **SINGLE CITIZENSHIP · ONE CONSTITUTION · ONE JUDICIARY · ALL-INDIA SERVICES · ONE ELECTORAL ROLL · FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT · FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES 51A(c) and (e) · "INTEGRITY" in the Preamble (42nd Amendment).**

### The amendment
> ⭐⭐ **16th Amendment, 1963 — "sovereignty and integrity of India" added to Articles 19(2), (3), (4), and to the oaths in the Third Schedule.** ⚠️ ⭐ **The basis of the UAPA.**

### The Council
> ⭐ **National Integration Council — 1961 · chaired by the PRIME MINISTER · all CMs · ADVISORY · ⚠️ barely ever meets.**

### The strategy in one line
> ⭐⭐ **India integrated by RECOGNISING difference, not by erasing it — linguistic states, the Sixth Schedule, minority rights and the 371-series are integrative devices, not concessions.**

## ❓ Expected Mains question

**1. "India achieved national integration by accommodating diversity rather than suppressing it." Critically examine. (250 words)**
> *Skeleton:* (i) ⭐ **The alternative that was rejected** — a unitary state with a single language and a single legal code, on the argument that diversity would tear a new nation apart. ⭐ **The framers chose the opposite bet.** (ii) ⭐ **How accommodation was built in** — ⭐ **linguistic reorganisation of states (1956)**; ⭐ **Articles 29 and 30** protecting minority culture and educational institutions; ⭐ **the Fifth and Sixth Schedules** for tribal self-government; ⭐ **the 371-series** for special regional arrangements; ⭐ **the language settlement of 1967**, giving each non-Hindi state an effective veto over the removal of English; and ⭐ **regional parties** as a legitimate channel for regional grievance *(see [Ch 69](69_Regional_Parties.md))*. (iii) ⭐ **How unity was simultaneously secured** — ⭐ **single citizenship**; **one Constitution and one judiciary**; ⭐ **All-India Services**; **one electoral roll**; **freedom of movement**; **Article 1's indestructible Union**; and, in the last resort, **Articles 352–360**. (iv) ⚠️ ⭐ **The critical part — where it has not worked** — insurgencies persisted where accommodation was delayed rather than denied; ⚠️ ⭐ **the 16th Amendment made "integrity" a ground for restricting FREE SPEECH, and the UAPA is its long shadow**; ⚠️ **the NIC and the Inter-State Council, the two consultative bodies designed for exactly these tensions, are barely convened**; and **economic disparity between regions has widened**. (v) ⭐ **Conclude:** ⭐ **the accommodative strategy has been vindicated by the outcome — a country of this diversity has held together for seventy-five years with universal suffrage throughout** — ⚠️ ⭐ **but it was accommodation through INSTITUTIONS, and those institutions must actually be used.** ⭐ **The reform is therefore unglamorous: convene the Inter-State Council and the NIC, and settle grievances by consultation before they require the Constitution's emergency machinery.**

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **National integration ≠ uniformity.** ⭐ **The Indian strategy: RECOGNISE diversity, build unity through SHARED INSTITUTIONS.**
- ⚠️ **Eight obstacles: regionalism · communalism · casteism · linguism · tribalism · extremism · nativism · economic disparity.**
- ⭐⭐ **Integrative devices: SINGLE CITIZENSHIP *(Articles 5–11)* · one Constitution · one integrated judiciary · ⭐ ALL-INDIA SERVICES *(Article 312)* · ⭐ ONE GENERAL ELECTORAL ROLL *(Article 325)* · freedom of movement and residence *(19(1)(d), (e))* · ⭐ FUNDAMENTAL DUTIES 51A(c), (e), (f), (i) · "INTEGRITY" added to the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment · Article 1's "Union of States" · Articles 262, 263, 301–307 · Articles 352–360.**
- ⭐⭐ **16th Amendment Act, 1963 — added "the sovereignty and integrity of India" as a ground of reasonable restriction under Articles 19(2), (3) and (4), and to the OATHS in the Third Schedule.** ⚠️ **The constitutional basis of the UAPA, 1967.**
- ⭐ **NATIONAL INTEGRATION COUNCIL — 1961, chaired by the PRIME MINISTER, all Chief Ministers as members, purely ADVISORY, and ⚠️ almost never convened.**
- ⭐ **Also: the three-language formula · Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat · NCC and NSS · the Flag Code of India, 2002 · Constitution Day, 26 November.**
