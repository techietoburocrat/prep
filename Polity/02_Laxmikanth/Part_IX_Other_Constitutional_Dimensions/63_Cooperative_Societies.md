# Chapter 63 — Co-operative Societies

[← Chapter 62](../Part_VIII_Non_Constitutional_Bodies/62_NDMA.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 64 →](64_Official_Language.md)

**Read time ~5 min** · **Part IXB, Articles 243ZH–243ZT · 97th Amendment Act, 2011** · ⚪ Prelims: occasional · Mains: **2010**

> 📌 **A ⚪ chapter with one genuinely important story.** ⭐ **The 97th Amendment is the clearest illustration in the whole Constitution of what happens when Parliament amends a STATE LIST subject without the states' ratification** — ⚠️ **the Supreme Court struck down most of it in 2021.** ⭐ **Learn the amendment for its constitutional lesson, not for its co-operative content.**

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **"Co-operative societies" is Entry 32 of the STATE LIST.** ⚠️ **In 2011 Parliament wrote a whole new Part of the Constitution regulating them — and did not send it to the states for ratification.** ⭐ **In 2021 the Supreme Court struck down that Part so far as it applies to societies inside a state, leaving it alive only for MULTI-STATE co-operatives.**

---

## ⭐⭐ What the 97th Amendment Act, 2011 did *(in force 15 February 2012)*

| # | Change | ⭐ **Status today** |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⭐ **Amended ARTICLE 19(1)(c)** — the right to form **"associations or unions"** became the right to form ⭐ **"associations or unions OR CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES"** | ⭐ **VALID — untouched by the judgment** |
| 2 | ⭐ **Inserted ARTICLE 43B in the DPSP** — the State shall endeavour to promote the ⭐ **voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control and professional management** of co-operative societies | ⭐ **VALID — untouched** |
| 3 | ⭐ **Inserted PART IXB — "The Co-operative Societies", Articles 243ZH to 243ZT** | ⚠️ ⭐⭐ **STRUCK DOWN so far as it applies to co-operatives WITHIN A STATE.** ⭐ **It survives for MULTI-STATE co-operative societies and for those in Union Territories** |

### ⭐⭐ *Union of India v Rajendra N. Shah* (2021) — the reasoning to remember

> ⭐ **Part IXB regulated a STATE LIST subject *(Entry 32)*.** ⭐ **Under the PROVISO to ARTICLE 368(2), an amendment that changes the distribution of legislative powers requires RATIFICATION BY THE LEGISLATURES OF AT LEAST HALF THE STATES.** ⚠️ ⭐ **The 97th Amendment was passed WITHOUT that ratification.**
>
> ⭐ **The Court used the DOCTRINE OF SEVERABILITY** — it did **not** strike down the whole amendment, only ⭐ **Part IXB in its application to state co-operatives**, leaving **Articles 19(1)(c) and 43B intact** and Part IXB operative for **multi-state societies**, over which Parliament *does* have competence *(Union List Entry 44)*.

> ⭐⭐ **This is the single most useful case in the chapter, and its value is far beyond co-operatives.** ⚠️ **It is the live, modern example of the Article 368(2) proviso actually biting** — ⭐ **and [Prelims 2025 Q7](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2025.md) asked precisely about which amendments need state ratification.** ⭐ **Cross-reference it whenever you revise [Ch 10](../Part_I_Constitutional_Framework/10_Amendment_of_the_Constitution.md).**

## ⭐ What Part IXB provides *(as it survives, for multi-state societies)*

| Provision | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Board size** | ⭐ **A maximum of 21 directors** |
| ⭐ **Reservation** | ⭐ **ONE seat for SCs or STs, and TWO seats for WOMEN**, on the board |
| ⭐ **Term** | ⭐ **5 years** for the board and its office-bearers; ⭐ **elections must be held BEFORE the expiry of the term** |
| ⭐⭐ **Supersession** | ⭐ **A board may not be superseded or kept under suspension for more than SIX MONTHS** — ⚠️ ⭐ **extended to ONE YEAR only where the society has government shareholding, a government loan or government financial assistance** |
| ⚠️ **A key exclusion** | ⭐ **No board of a co-operative society carrying on BANKING business may be superseded** where there is no such government stake, beyond the general rule |
| ⭐ **Audit** | ⭐ **Accounts to be audited within SIX MONTHS of the close of the financial year**, by an auditor from a panel approved by the state government |
| ⭐ **General body meeting** | ⭐ **Within SIX MONTHS of the close of the financial year** |
| ⭐ **Members' rights** | ⭐ **Access to the books, information and accounts** of the society |
| **Returns** | Filing within **six months** of the close of the financial year |

⭐ **Note also:** the ⭐ **Ministry of Co-operation** was created as a separate Union ministry in **July 2021**, and the ⭐ **Multi-State Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, 2023** strengthened governance, audit and the electoral authority for such societies.

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The three limbs, and which two survived
> ⭐ **19(1)(c) — the RIGHT to form a co-operative. ✅** ⭐ **43B — the DPSP. ✅** ⚠️ ⭐ **Part IXB — struck down for STATE co-operatives, alive for MULTI-STATE ones.**

### The reason
> ⭐⭐ **Co-operative societies = STATE LIST Entry 32 → the proviso to Article 368(2) required ratification by half the states → it was not obtained → *Rajendra N. Shah* (2021), applying SEVERABILITY.**

### The numbers
> ⭐ **21 directors · 1 SC/ST seat + 2 women's seats · 5-year term · supersession max 6 MONTHS (1 year with government stake) · audit and general body meeting within 6 months.**

## ❓ Expected Mains question

**1. "With respect to co-operative societies, what are the salient features of the constitutional amendment on the subject, and what became of it?" (150 words)**
> *A version of this was asked as [Mains 2010 Q2(b)](../../04_PYQ_Mains/2010.md), on the 106th and 111th Constitution Amendment Bills — the 111th Bill became the ⭐ **97th Amendment Act, 2011**.*
> *Skeleton:* **The three limbs** — ⭐ **Article 19(1)(c)** *(a fundamental right to form co-operatives)*, ⭐ **Article 43B** *(a directive principle of voluntary, autonomous, democratic, professionally managed co-operatives)*, and ⭐ **Part IXB** *(board size, reservation, five-year term, limits on supersession, compulsory audit and general body meetings, members' right to information)*. **The purpose** — to end political supersession of elected co-operative boards and to professionalise a sector employing crores of people. ⚠️ ⭐ **What became of it** — ***Union of India v Rajendra N. Shah* (2021)** struck down **Part IXB for co-operatives within a state**, for want of **ratification by half the state legislatures** under the **proviso to Article 368(2)**, since co-operative societies are **State List Entry 32**; ⭐ **Articles 19(1)(c) and 43B survive**, as does Part IXB for **multi-state** societies. ⭐ **Conclude:** the case is a reminder that **federal procedure is a substantive limit on the amending power, not a formality** — and that the states must now legislate the reforms themselves.

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **97th Amendment Act, 2011** *(in force **15 February 2012**)* — three limbs: ⭐ **Article 19(1)(c)** adds **co-operative societies**; ⭐ **Article 43B** *(new DPSP)*; ⭐ **Part IXB, Articles 243ZH–243ZT.**
- ⚠️ ⭐⭐ ***Union of India v Rajendra N. Shah* (2021)** — **Part IXB struck down for co-operatives WITHIN A STATE**, because **"co-operative societies" is STATE LIST Entry 32** and the amendment lacked ⭐ **ratification by half the states under the proviso to Article 368(2)**. ⭐ **Severability applied — 19(1)(c) and 43B survive; Part IXB applies to MULTI-STATE societies and UTs.**
- ⭐ **Part IXB features:** max **21 directors** · **1 SC/ST + 2 women** on the board · **5-year** term with elections **before expiry** · ⭐ **supersession max 6 MONTHS**, or **1 year** where there is government shareholding, a loan or financial assistance · **audit and general body meeting within 6 months** · members' **right to information** · returns within 6 months.
- ⭐ **Ministry of Co-operation created July 2021**; ⭐ **Multi-State Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, 2023.**
