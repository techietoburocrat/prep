# Chapter 47 — National Commission for Scheduled Tribes

[← Chapter 46](46_National_Commission_SCs.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 48 →](48_National_Commission_BCs.md)

**Read time ~5 min** · **Article 338A** · 🟠 Prelims: constitutional-vs-statutory format · Mains: through tribal-rights questions

> 📌 **Read [Ch 46](46_National_Commission_SCs.md) first.** ⭐ **Structure, composition, civil-court powers, reporting chain and non-binding status are IDENTICAL.** ⚠️ **Only two things differ — and one of them is a list you should actually memorise.**

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **Same body, different beneficiary — and a much longer job description.** ⭐⭐ **Because tribal disadvantage is territorial as well as social, the NCST was given extra functions the NCSC does not have: LAND, FOREST, MINERALS, WATER, DISPLACEMENT and PESA.**

---

## PART A — ⭐ Difference 1: origin

| | **NCSC** | ⭐ **NCST** |
|---|---|---|
| **Article** | **338** | ⭐ **338A** |
| **Created by** | The **65th Amendment (1990)** made the joint Commission constitutional | ⭐⭐ **The 89th Amendment Act, 2003 — which BIFURCATED the joint Commission** |
| ⭐ **Effective** | — | ⭐ **19 February 2004** |
| **Covers** | **SCs + the Anglo-Indian community** | ⭐ **Scheduled Tribes** |

**Everything structural is the same:** ⭐ **Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + 3 members**, appointed by the **President**; ⭐ **three-year term**, no more than two terms, at least one woman; ⭐ **powers of a CIVIL COURT**; ⭐ **annual report to the President, laid before both Houses with an action-taken memorandum stating reasons for non-acceptance**; ⭐ **the Union and every state must consult it on major policy matters affecting STs**; ⚠️ **recommendations not binding**.

---

## PART B — ⭐⭐ Difference 2: the eight extra functions

⭐ **A Presidential order of 2005 assigned the NCST functions no other commission has.** ⚠️ **These are what make Chapter 47 worth reading separately — and they map directly onto [Ch 40](../Part_VI_Union_Territories_and_Special_Areas/40_Scheduled_and_Tribal_Areas.md).**

| # | Extra function |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⭐ **Ownership rights over MINOR FOREST PRODUCE** for tribal communities |
| 2 | ⭐ **Safeguarding rights over MINERAL RESOURCES and WATER RESOURCES** in Scheduled Areas, as by law provided |
| 3 | ⭐ **Development and viable LIVELIHOOD strategies** |
| 4 | ⭐⭐ **RELIEF AND REHABILITATION of tribal people DISPLACED by development projects** |
| 5 | ⭐⭐ **Preventing ALIENATION OF TRIBAL LAND, and effective rehabilitation where alienation has occurred** |
| 6 | ⭐ **Eliciting the maximum cooperation of tribal communities in AFFORESTATION** and social forestry |
| 7 | ⭐⭐ **Ensuring full implementation of PESA, 1996** — the Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act |
| 8 | ⭐ **Reducing and ultimately eliminating SHIFTING CULTIVATION** *(jhum)*, which degrades land and productivity |

> ⭐⭐ **Read that list as a diagnosis:** ⚠️ **the state has identified LAND ALIENATION, DISPLACEMENT and NON-IMPLEMENTATION OF PESA as the three defining harms of tribal India — and then gave the monitoring body no binding power over any of them.** ⭐ **That sentence is worth using in any tribal-rights answer.**

---

## PART C — ⭐ Where the NCST sits in the tribal-rights machinery

| Institution | Role |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **NCST** *(Article 338A)* | Constitutional watchdog — investigate, monitor, report |
| ⭐ **Governor + Tribes Advisory Council** *(Fifth Schedule)* | Executive protection within a state — see [Ch 40](../Part_VI_Union_Territories_and_Special_Areas/40_Scheduled_and_Tribal_Areas.md) |
| ⭐ **Autonomous District Councils** *(Sixth Schedule)* | Self-government in **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram** |
| ⭐ **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** | The nodal ministry — ⚠️ ⭐ **including for the Forest Rights Act, 2006**, which is exactly what [Prelims 2021 Q8](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2021.md) tested |
| ⭐ **Article 275(1)** | Grants-in-aid for tribal welfare and Scheduled-Area administration |
| ⭐ **Article 339** | The President's Commission on Scheduled Areas *(Dhebar, 1960)*, and Union directions on ST welfare schemes |

> ⭐ **Article 342 — the President specifies Scheduled Tribes for a state by public notification; thereafter ONLY PARLIAMENT can include or exclude a community.** ⚠️ ⭐ *Exactly what [Prelims 2024 Q15](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2024.md) asked.* ⭐ **Note the parallel: Article 341 for SCs, 342 for STs, 342A for SEBCs.**

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The two facts that are actually different
> ⭐⭐ **Article 338A, created by the 89th Amendment (2003), operative 19 February 2004.** ⭐⭐ **Plus eight extra functions the NCSC does not have.**

### The eight, compressed
> ⭐ **"FOREST, MINERALS, WATER, LIVELIHOOD, DISPLACEMENT, LAND ALIENATION, PESA, JHUM."**

### The three notification articles
> ⭐ **341 = SCs · 342 = STs · 342A = SEBCs.** ⭐ **In every case the PRESIDENT notifies first and only PARLIAMENT may amend the list** *(with the states' own SEBC lists restored by the **105th Amendment, 2021** — see [Ch 48](48_National_Commission_BCs.md))*.

## ❓ Expected Mains questions

**1. "The National Commission for Scheduled Tribes was given the widest mandate of any constitutional commission and the least capacity to fulfil it." Examine. (250 words)**
> *Skeleton:* **The mandate** — Article 338A's core functions plus the ⭐ **eight additional subjects of 2005: minor forest produce, minerals and water, livelihoods, displacement, land alienation, afforestation, PESA and shifting cultivation.** ⭐ **Each is a subject in the hands of a different authority** — forests with the state forest department, minerals with the state mining department, PESA with the panchayat department, displacement with the project authority. ⚠️ **The capacity gap** — recommendations are **not binding**; **expenditure is not charged** on the Consolidated Fund; ⚠️ **prolonged vacancies**; a small secretariat; and reliance on **state reports** for its own information. **Illustrate** — the persistent under-recognition of **community forest rights** under the FRA, and the fact that **land alienation continues despite Fifth Schedule regulations**. ⭐ **Reforms:** binding time-limits for government replies, charged expenditure, a statutory link with the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs' grievance machinery**, and **regional benches** in Scheduled Areas so the Commission does not sit only in Delhi.

**2. "Assess the role of constitutional and statutory bodies in preventing the alienation of tribal land." (250 words)**
> *Skeleton:* Frame the harm; then the machinery — ⭐ **Fifth Schedule regulation power over land transfer and money-lending** *(Governor, with the President's assent)*; ⭐ ***Samatha* (1997)** on leases in Scheduled Areas; ⭐ **PESA's consultation on land acquisition and minor minerals**; ⭐ **the FRA, 2006's recognition of individual and community rights**; ⭐ **the NCST's specific mandate on land alienation and displacement**; the **LARR Act, 2013**'s consent and rehabilitation requirements. ⚠️ **Why it still happens** — consultation is not consent; state PESA rules were delayed; benami and lease routes evade transfer bans; ⭐ **the Governor rarely exercises the disapplication power**. **Conclude** with the reform list from [Ch 40](../Part_VI_Union_Territories_and_Special_Areas/40_Scheduled_and_Tribal_Areas.md).

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **Article 338A**, created by the ⭐ **89th Amendment Act, 2003**, operative ⭐ **19 February 2004**, by **bifurcating** the joint SC-and-ST Commission.
- ⭐ **Identical to the NCSC**: Chairperson + Vice-Chairperson + **3** members; **President** appoints; **3-year** term; **civil-court powers**; **annual report → President → both Houses with an action-taken memorandum**; state reports → **Governor → state legislature**; ⚠️ **not binding**; ⭐ **mandatory consultation on major policy affecting STs**.
- ⭐⭐ **Eight extra functions (2005 Presidential order):** ⭐ **minor forest produce · minerals and water in Scheduled Areas · livelihood strategies · relief and rehabilitation of the DISPLACED · preventing LAND ALIENATION · cooperation in afforestation · full implementation of PESA, 1996 · reducing SHIFTING CULTIVATION.**
- ⭐ **Article 342** — the **President** notifies STs; ⚠️ **only PARLIAMENT** can add or remove a community. **341 = SCs, 342 = STs, 342A = SEBCs.**
- ⭐ **Nodal ministry: Tribal Affairs** — including for the **Forest Rights Act, 2006**.
