# Chapter 42 — Union Public Service Commission

[← Chapter 41](41_Election_Commission.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 43 →](43_State_Public_Service_Commission.md)

**Read time ~8 min** · **Articles 315–323 (Part XIV)** · 🟠 Prelims: via the "which bodies are constitutional" format · Mains: **2009**

> 📌 **You are sitting its exam, so read this one with unusual attention** — and note the irony worth using in an answer: ⭐ **the UPSC recruits the entire higher civil service and has almost no say in what happens to those officers afterwards.** ⚠️ **Its advice is not even binding.**

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **The UPSC is the constitutional guarantee that entry into the civil service is by MERIT and not by patronage.** ⚠️ **It is a recruiting and advising body only** — it does not manage cadres, fix pay, decide promotions on its own, or discipline officers. ⭐ **Its power is the power of an unimpeachable process, not of a command.**

---

## PART A — Composition and appointment

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Articles** | ⭐ **315–323**, Part XIV *(Services under the Union and the States)* |
| ⭐ **Composition** | **Chairman + other members.** ⚠️ ⭐ **The Constitution fixes NO number and NO qualifications** — both are left to the **President** *(strength is usually 9–11 including the Chairman)* |
| ⭐ **The one composition rule that IS constitutional** | ⭐⭐ **About HALF the members must be persons who have held office for at least TEN YEARS under the Government of India or a state government** |
| **Appointment** | ⭐ By the **PRESIDENT** |
| ⭐ **Term** | ⭐⭐ **6 years or until the age of 65, whichever is EARLIER** |
| **Resignation** | To the **President** |
| ⭐ **Acting Chairman** | If the Chairman's office is vacant or he is unable to act, the **President** may appoint **one of the members** as acting Chairman |

> ⚠️ ⭐ **The 65-vs-62 trap:** ⭐ **UPSC = 65. State PSC and Joint PSC = 62.** This single pair of numbers is the most frequently confused fact in Part VII.

### ⭐⭐ Removal — a two-track article

The **President** removes a Chairman or member, but **how** depends on the ground:

| Track | Ground | Procedure |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Article 317(1) — misbehaviour** | **Proved misbehaviour** | ⭐⭐ **The President REFERS the matter to the SUPREME COURT**, which holds an inquiry. ⚠️ ⭐ **The Supreme Court's advice is BINDING on the President** — he must remove if the Court so advises, and cannot if it does not |
| ⭐ **Article 317(3) — the four disqualifications** | **(a)** adjudged an **insolvent**; **(b)** engages in **paid employment** outside the duties of his office during his term; **(c)** in the President's opinion **unfit to continue by reason of infirmity of mind or body**; *(and a member who is "concerned or interested" in a government contract is deemed guilty of misbehaviour)* | ⭐ The President removes **directly — no reference to the Supreme Court** |

⭐ **During a Supreme Court inquiry, the President may SUSPEND** the Chairman or member.

> ⭐⭐ **This is the strongest removal protection in Part VII.** ⚠️ **Compare it in one line: the CEC needs a parliamentary address; a UPSC member needs a BINDING Supreme Court finding.** ⭐ **A judge-like standard applied without a parliamentary vote — which arguably makes it *harder* to remove a UPSC member than a CEC, because no political majority can deliver it.**

---

## PART B — ⭐ Independence, and the ceiling on it

### What protects the UPSC

1. ⭐⭐ **Expenses are CHARGED on the Consolidated Fund of India** — ⚠️ **not voted by Parliament**, so it cannot be squeezed by a budget vote;
2. ⭐ **Conditions of service cannot be varied to a member's disadvantage** after appointment;
3. ⭐ Removal only as above.

### ⭐⭐ The post-tenure bars — the most examinable table in the chapter

| Who | May afterwards be | ⚠️ **May NOT be** |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Chairman of UPSC** | ⚠️ **Nothing** | ⭐⭐ **Any further employment under the Government of India or a state** |
| ⭐ **Member of UPSC** | ⭐ **Chairman of the UPSC**, or ⭐ **Chairman of a State PSC** | Any **other** employment under the Union or a state |
| ⭐ **Chairman of a State PSC** | ⭐ **Chairman or member of the UPSC**, or **Chairman of another State PSC** | Any **other** employment |
| ⭐ **Member of a State PSC** | ⭐ **Chairman of that or another State PSC**, or ⭐ **Chairman or member of the UPSC** | Any **other** employment |

> ⭐⭐ **The pattern to hold: you may move UPWARDS within the PSC system, and nowhere else.** ⚠️ **And nobody is eligible for REAPPOINTMENT to the same office.**
>
> ⭐ **This is the sharpest independence provision in the entire book, and it is worth citing in ANY question about regulator autonomy** — including the ECI, where **no such bar exists** *(see [Ch 41](41_Election_Commission.md))*.

## PART C — ⭐ Functions, and what is kept away from it

### ⭐ Article 320 — what the UPSC does

| Function | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Examinations** | Conducts examinations for appointment to the ⭐ **All-India Services**, the **Central Services**, and the **public services of Union Territories** |
| ⭐ **Assistance to states** | ⭐ **On the request of two or more states**, assists them in framing and operating schemes of **JOINT RECRUITMENT** for services requiring special qualifications |
| ⭐ **Serving a state** | ⭐ **On the Governor's request and with the President's approval**, the UPSC may serve **all or any of the needs of a state** *(Article 315(2))* |
| ⭐ **Advice — the five heads** | ⭐ **(1)** methods of recruitment; **(2)** principles for **appointments, promotions and transfers**, and the suitability of candidates; **(3)** all **DISCIPLINARY matters** affecting a civil servant, including memorials and petitions; **(4)** claims for **reimbursement of legal costs** incurred by a civil servant in defending proceedings for acts done in official duty; **(5)** claims for **pension** in respect of injuries |
| ⭐ **Article 321** | ⭐ **PARLIAMENT may by law extend the UPSC's functions** to any further services of the Union — and a **state legislature** may do so for its own PSC |

### ⚠️⚠️ Where the UPSC is NOT consulted — the standard Prelims item

> ⭐ **Four exclusions to remember:**
> 1. ⚠️ ⭐ **Making RESERVATIONS in appointments for backward classes**;
> 2. ⚠️ ⭐ **Taking into consideration the claims of SCs and STs** in making appointments;
> 3. ⚠️ ⭐ **Selections for the chairmanship or membership of commissions or tribunals, posts of the highest diplomatic nature, and a bulk of Group C and Group D services**;
> 4. ⚠️ **A temporary or officiating appointment** where the person is unlikely to hold the post for more than **a year**.
>
> ⭐ The President may also make **regulations** specifying further matters on which the UPSC need not be consulted — ⚠️ **but every such regulation must be LAID BEFORE PARLIAMENT for at least 14 days, and Parliament may modify or annul it.**

### ⭐⭐ Article 323 — the reporting mechanism, and the limit on the UPSC's power

- ⭐ The UPSC presents an **annual report to the PRESIDENT**;
- ⭐ The President **lays it before both Houses of Parliament**, together with ⭐⭐ **a MEMORANDUM explaining every case in which the Commission's advice was NOT accepted, and the reasons for it.**

> ⭐⭐ **That memorandum is the whole answer to "is the UPSC's advice binding?"** ⚠️ **It is NOT.** The government may reject the UPSC's advice — ⭐ **it must merely explain itself to Parliament afterwards.** ⭐ **So the UPSC's sanction is publicity, not law** — precisely the same sanction that gives the MCC its force *(Ch 41)*.

### ⚠️ The other limitations, in one list

1. ⚠️ **Advisory only** — the government may act against it *(the memorandum is the only consequence)*;
2. ⚠️ ⭐ **It is a recruiting agency, not a personnel agency** — ⚠️ **classification of services, pay and service conditions, cadre management, training and career planning are all outside it** and lie with the **Department of Personnel and Training**;
3. ⚠️ **Its role can be diluted simply by not consulting it** — expanding the regulations under the proviso to Article 320(3);
4. ⚠️ **Growth of parallel recruitment** — the **SSC**, the **Railway Recruitment Boards**, banking and insurance boards recruit outside it.

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The three numbers
> ⭐ **UPSC: 6 years or 65.** ⭐ **State PSC: 6 years or 62.** ⭐ **About HALF the members must have 10 years' government service.**

### Removal
> ⭐⭐ **Misbehaviour → the President REFERS to the SUPREME COURT → its advice is BINDING.** ⭐ **Insolvency, paid employment, infirmity → removal DIRECTLY, no reference.**

### Money and afterlife
> ⭐ **Expenses CHARGED on the Consolidated Fund of India.** ⭐⭐ **The Chairman is barred from ALL further government employment. A member may rise to Chairman of the UPSC or of a State PSC — and nothing else.**

### The advice
> ⚠️ ⭐ **NOT binding.** ⭐ **Article 323 — annual report to the President → laid before Parliament with a MEMORANDUM on every rejected recommendation.**

### The four exclusions
> ⚠️ ⭐ **Reservation for backward classes · SC/ST claims · top diplomatic posts, tribunal and commission memberships, most Group C and D · sub-one-year temporary posts.**

## ❓ Expected Mains questions

**1. "In the changing context of governance in the country, what should be the role of the UPSC?" (250 words)**
> *Actually asked — [Mains 2009 Q7(b)](../../04_PYQ_Mains/2009.md).*
> *Skeleton:* **The present role** — Articles 315–323: recruitment to the All-India and Central Services; advice on recruitment methods, promotions, transfers and **disciplinary** matters; ⭐ **advisory only, with the Article 323 memorandum as its only sanction**. ⭐ **Why the context has changed** — governance now demands **domain specialists** *(finance, cyber, health, climate)*; **lateral entry** has been introduced; **contractual and mission-mode staffing** has grown; and **service delivery** rather than regulation is the measure of an officer. ⭐ **What the role should become** — (i) from a **recruiting board** to a **central human-resource institution**, with a say in **competency frameworks, mid-career assessment and empanelment**; (ii) **the neutral vetting authority for LATERAL ENTRY**, which is the single most credible way to keep specialist hiring free of patronage; (iii) **wider disciplinary jurisdiction** so its advice is sought before, not after, action; (iv) **modernised testing** aligned to actual job roles *(the standing recommendation of the **2nd ARC** and the **Baswan Committee, 2016**)*. ⚠️ **Guard against** turning it into a personnel ministry — its authority rests on being seen as **outside** the government it recruits for.

**2. "The UPSC is constitutionally the best-protected body in India and practically one of the least powerful." Examine. (250 words)**
> *Skeleton:* **The protection** — ⭐ **binding Supreme Court finding required for removal on misbehaviour**; **expenses charged** on the Consolidated Fund; **service conditions unalterable**; ⭐ **a total bar on further employment for the Chairman**. **The weakness** — ⚠️ **advice is not binding**; **Article 320(3)'s proviso and the President's regulations** let the government narrow its jurisdiction; ⚠️ **reservation, SC/ST claims and most Group C/D recruitment are excluded**; **cadre management, pay and training lie with DoPT**; parallel agencies recruit outside it. **Conclude:** the Constitution insulated the UPSC's **members** thoroughly and its **jurisdiction** hardly at all — ⭐ **independence of person is not the same as independence of function**, which is a line usable across the whole of Part VII.

**3. "What are the aims and objects of the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024? Do University and State Education Board examinations fall under it?" (150 words)**
> *Actually asked — [Mains 2024 Q11](../../04_PYQ_Mains/2024.md).*
> *Skeleton:* **Aim** — to deter organised paper leaks and impersonation in **public examinations**, after repeated leaks in national recruitment tests. **Coverage** — examinations conducted by specified **public examination authorities**, including the ⭐ **UPSC, SSC, Railway Recruitment Boards, IBPS, NTA** and central government departments. ⚠️ **State board and university examinations are NOT covered** unless a state adopts the law — the Act expressly enables states to adopt it. **Features** — offences are **cognizable, non-bailable and non-compoundable**; imprisonment of **3–5 years** and fines up to ₹10 lakh for individuals; up to **10 years and ₹1 crore** for organised crime by a service provider; ⭐ **no penalty on the candidate under this Act** *(candidates are dealt with under the examining body's own rules)*.

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **Articles 315–323, Part XIV.** ⭐ **Chairman + members; number and qualifications fixed by the PRESIDENT.** ⭐⭐ **About HALF must have 10 years' service under the Union or a state.**
- ⭐⭐ **Term: 6 years or 65 — whichever is earlier.** *(State PSC: **62**.)* Resignation to the **President**.
- ⭐⭐ **Removal — Article 317(1): misbehaviour → reference to the SUPREME COURT, whose advice BINDS the President** *(suspension possible during the inquiry)*. ⭐ **Article 317(3): insolvency · paid employment during the term · infirmity → removal without any reference.**
- ⭐ **Expenses CHARGED on the Consolidated Fund of India.** ⭐ **Service conditions cannot be varied to disadvantage.**
- ⭐⭐ **Post-tenure: the UPSC Chairman is barred from ALL further Union or state employment; a member may become UPSC Chairman or a State PSC Chairman only. No reappointment to the same office.**
- ⭐ **Functions (320):** examinations for **All-India, Central and UT services** · **joint recruitment** for two or more states · serving a state on the **Governor's request with the President's approval** *(315(2))* · advice on **recruitment methods, appointments/promotions/transfers, DISCIPLINARY matters, legal-cost reimbursement, injury pensions**. ⭐ **Article 321 — Parliament may extend its functions.**
- ⚠️ ⭐ **Not consulted on:** reservations for backward classes · SC/ST claims · **top diplomatic posts, tribunal/commission memberships, most Group C and D** · temporary posts under a year. ⭐ **Regulations excluding matters must be laid before Parliament for 14 days.**
- ⭐⭐ **Article 323 — annual report to the President, laid before Parliament with a MEMORANDUM on every case where the advice was rejected.** ⚠️ ⭐ **So the advice is NOT binding.**
- ⚠️ **Limits:** advisory only · recruiting, not personnel management *(DoPT holds cadres, pay, training)* · jurisdiction shrinkable by regulation · parallel recruiters *(SSC, RRBs)*.
