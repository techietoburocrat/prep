# Chapter 52 — Advocate General of the State

[← Chapter 51](51_Attorney_General.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 53 →](../Part_VIII_Non_Constitutional_Bodies/53_NITI_Aayog.md)

**Read time ~4 min** · **Article 165** · ⚪ Prelims: as a contrast with the Attorney General · Mains: rare

> 📌 **The last chapter of Part VII, and the shortest.** ⭐ **Article 165 is Article 76 with the Governor substituted for the President.** ⚠️ **Learn the five substitutions and the one genuine difference, and you are done.**

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **Every state needs its own highest law officer, for exactly the reasons the Union does.** ⭐⭐ **Article 165 gives it one, on the same design and with the same weakness: appointed at pleasure, with no fixed term.**

---

## ⭐⭐ The comparison table — this IS the chapter

| Element | ⭐ **Attorney General *(Article 76)*** | ⭐ **Advocate General *(Article 165)*** |
|---|---|---|
| **Appointed by** | The **President** | ⭐ **The GOVERNOR** |
| ⭐ **Qualification** | Qualified to be a **judge of the SUPREME COURT** | ⭐⭐ **Qualified to be a judge of a HIGH COURT** — a citizen of India who has **held a judicial office for 10 years**, or been an **advocate of a High Court for 10 years** |
| **Tenure** | ⚠️ **At the President's PLEASURE; no fixed term** | ⚠️ ⭐ **At the GOVERNOR'S PLEASURE; no fixed term** |
| **Remuneration** | Determined by the **President** | ⭐ **Determined by the GOVERNOR** |
| ⭐ **Right of audience** | ⭐ **All courts in INDIA** | ⭐⭐ **All courts within the STATE** |
| ⭐ **Legislative rights** | ⭐ **Article 88** — speak and take part in **both Houses of Parliament**, joint sittings and committees; ⚠️ **no vote** | ⭐⭐ **Article 177** — speak and take part in **both Houses of the state legislature** and any committee of which he is named a member; ⚠️ **no vote** |
| ⭐ **Privileges** | Those of a **Member of Parliament** *(Article 105)* | ⭐ **Those of a member of the state legislature** *(Article 194)* |
| ⭐ **Assisted by** | Solicitor General and Additional Solicitors General *(⚠️ **not in the Constitution**)* | Additional and Deputy Advocates General *(⚠️ **also not in the Constitution**)* |

> ⭐⭐ **The one difference worth flagging beyond the substitutions:** ⚠️ **the four self-denying rules that constrain the Attorney General — no brief against the government, no defending the accused without permission, no directorships — are CONVENTIONS attached to that office.** ⭐ **For the Advocate General, the equivalent restrictions depend on each state's own terms of appointment, and practice varies.**

---

## ⭐ Duties — Article 165(2)

⭐ **The Advocate General:**
1. ⭐ **Gives ADVICE to the state government on such legal matters as are referred to him by the GOVERNOR**;
2. ⭐ **Performs such other duties of a LEGAL CHARACTER as the Governor assigns**;
3. ⭐ **Discharges the functions conferred on him by the Constitution or any other law.**

⭐ **Two functions where he appears by name in other chapters:**
- ⭐ Under the **Contempt of Courts Act, 1971**, ⭐ **his consent is required for a private person to initiate criminal contempt proceedings in a HIGH COURT** — the state mirror of the Attorney General's role;
- ⭐ He is the state's counsel in **High Court** proceedings, including in matters arising from the Governor's discretionary acts *(see [Ch 30](../Part_IV_State_Government/30_Governor.md))*.

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The substitution list
> ⭐ **Governor for President · High Court judge for Supreme Court judge · state courts for all-India courts · Article 177 for Article 88 · Article 194 for Article 105.**

### The two article pairs
> ⭐ **76 / 165 — the law officers.** ⭐ **88 / 177 — speak, take part, NO VOTE.** ⭐ **105 / 194 — privileges.**

### The qualification trap
> ⚠️ ⭐ **A High Court judge's qualification is 10 years' JUDICIAL OFFICE or 10 years as an ADVOCATE.** ⚠️ **Note there is NO "distinguished jurist" route for a High Court judge — that exists only for the Supreme Court** *(see [Ch 34](../Part_IV_State_Government/34_High_Court.md))*.

---

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **Article 165 — the highest law officer of a STATE**, appointed by the ⭐ **GOVERNOR**, ⭐ **qualified to be a HIGH COURT judge** *(10 years' judicial office or 10 years as an advocate of a High Court)*.
- ⚠️ ⭐ **No fixed term; holds office at the GOVERNOR'S PLEASURE**; ⭐ **remuneration determined by the Governor**; by convention resigns with the state government.
- ⭐ **Duties (165(2)):** advise the state government on matters referred by the **Governor** · perform other duties of a **legal character** assigned by him · discharge functions under the Constitution or any law.
- ⭐⭐ **Rights: audience in ALL COURTS WITHIN THE STATE** · ⭐ **Article 177 — speak and take part in both Houses of the state legislature and its committees, but NO VOTE** · ⭐ **Article 194 privileges** of a member of the state legislature.
- ⭐ **His consent is needed for a private criminal-contempt petition in the HIGH COURT.**
- ⚠️ **Additional and Deputy Advocates General are not mentioned in the Constitution.**
