# Chapter 43 — State Public Service Commission

[← Chapter 42](42_UPSC.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 44 →](44_Finance_Commission.md)

**Read time ~5 min** · **Articles 315–323 (Part XIV)** · 🟡 Prelims: as a contrast with the UPSC · Mains: rare

> 📌 **Do not read this chapter from scratch.** ⭐ **It is the UPSC chapter with four substitutions.** Read [Ch 42](42_UPSC.md) properly, then learn only what is below — that is all the exam has ever asked for.

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **Same articles, same functions, same protections — with the GOVERNOR substituted for the President almost everywhere.** ⚠️ **Almost. The one place the substitution does NOT happen is removal — and that is the entire examinable content of this chapter.**

---

## PART A — ⭐⭐ The five differences

| # | Element | **UPSC** | ⭐ **State PSC** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Appointed by** | The **President** | ⭐ **The GOVERNOR** |
| 2 | ⭐⭐ **REMOVED by** | The **President** | ⚠️ ⭐⭐ **The PRESIDENT — NOT the Governor** |
| 3 | ⭐ **Age of retirement** | ⭐ **65** | ⭐⭐ **62** |
| 4 | **Resignation addressed to** | The **President** | ⭐ **The Governor** |
| 5 | ⭐ **Expenses charged on** | The **Consolidated Fund of INDIA** | ⭐ **The Consolidated Fund of the STATE** |

> ⭐⭐ **Difference 2 is the whole chapter.** ⚠️ **The Governor appoints, but only the President can remove.** ⭐ **Why? Because a Commission removable by the state government it recruits for would be worthless.** The same **Article 317(1)** procedure applies — ⭐ **reference to the SUPREME COURT, whose advice binds** — and the same **Article 317(3)** grounds *(insolvency, paid employment, infirmity)* allow direct removal.
>
> ⚠️ **The Governor CAN, however, SUSPEND** a Chairman or member while the Supreme Court inquiry is pending. ⭐ **So: Governor suspends, President removes.**

**Everything else is identical:** term of **6 years**; about **half** the members must have **10 years'** government service; conditions of service unalterable to disadvantage; the same **Article 320** advisory functions; the same **exclusions** from consultation; and the same **advisory-only** status.

⭐ **Article 316(1A)** — if the Chairman's office falls vacant or he cannot act, the **Governor** may appoint **one of the members** as acting Chairman.

---

## PART B — ⭐ The reporting chain, and the two cousins

### Reporting

⭐ The State PSC submits its **annual report to the GOVERNOR**, who lays it before the **STATE LEGISLATURE** together with ⭐ **a memorandum explaining every case in which the Commission's advice was not accepted.**

> ⚠️ ⭐ **Mirror-image of Article 323.** *(UPSC → President → Parliament. SPSC → Governor → state legislature.)*

### ⭐⭐ The Joint State Public Service Commission — the body most people get wrong

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⚠️ ⭐⭐ **Nature** | ⭐ **A JSPSC is a STATUTORY body, NOT a constitutional one** — even though Article 315(2) provides for it, ⚠️ **it comes into existence only when Parliament passes a law** |
| ⭐ **Created by** | ⭐ **PARLIAMENT, by law, on the REQUEST of the legislatures of two or more states** |
| ⭐ **Chairman and members appointed by** | ⭐⭐ **The PRESIDENT** *(not the Governors)* |
| ⭐ **Term** | ⭐ **6 years or 62 — the STATE PSC numbers**, not the UPSC's |
| **Removal** | By the **President**, on the same grounds |
| **Report** | To the **Governor of EACH state**, laid before **each** state legislature |

### ⭐ The UPSC serving a state — Article 315(2)

⭐ **On the request of the GOVERNOR and with the APPROVAL of the PRESIDENT**, the **UPSC** may serve **all or any of the needs of a state**.

> ⭐ **Three ways a state's recruitment can be done, and they are a favourite matching item:**
> 1. ⭐ **Its own State PSC** — constitutional, Governor appoints;
> 2. ⭐ **A Joint State PSC** — **statutory**, created by **Parliament**, **President** appoints;
> 3. ⭐ **The UPSC itself** — on the **Governor's request with the President's approval**.

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The one line that carries the chapter
> ⭐⭐ **The GOVERNOR appoints and SUSPENDS. Only the PRESIDENT removes.**

### The number
> ⭐ **62, not 65.** *(And a JSPSC also uses **62**.)*

### The status trap
> ⚠️ ⭐ **State PSC = CONSTITUTIONAL. Joint State PSC = STATUTORY.** ⭐ **And the JSPSC's members are appointed by the PRESIDENT.**

### Where the money and the report go
> ⭐ **Expenses on the Consolidated Fund of the STATE; report to the GOVERNOR, laid before the STATE LEGISLATURE with a memorandum.**

## ❓ Expected Mains question

**1. "Why did the Constitution place the removal of a State Public Service Commission's members beyond the reach of the state government that appoints them?" (150 words)**
> *Skeleton:* **The design** — the **Governor appoints** *(so the state's stake is respected)*, but ⭐ **only the President removes, and then only on a BINDING finding of the Supreme Court** under Article 317(1). **The reason** — a Commission whose members could be dismissed by the government whose recruitment it supervises would be **structurally incapable of resisting patronage**; the **appointing power is local, the removal power is federal and judicialised**. **Illustrate** with the recurring controversies over **state PSC recruitment scandals**, where the accountability route has had to run through the **President and the Supreme Court**, not the state cabinet. ⭐ **Conclude** with the general principle for Part VII: **independence is engineered at the point of EXIT, not the point of entry.**

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **Same Articles 315–323 as the UPSC.** Chairman + members; ⭐ **about half with 10 years' government service**; term ⭐ **6 years or 62**.
- ⭐ **Appointed by the GOVERNOR; resignation to the GOVERNOR; acting Chairman appointed by the GOVERNOR (316(1A)).**
- ⭐⭐ **Removed ONLY by the PRESIDENT** — Article **317(1)** misbehaviour → **reference to the Supreme Court, advice BINDING**; Article **317(3)** insolvency / paid employment / infirmity → direct. ⚠️ ⭐ **The Governor may only SUSPEND** pending the inquiry.
- ⭐ **Expenses CHARGED on the Consolidated Fund of the STATE.** ⭐ **Annual report → GOVERNOR → STATE LEGISLATURE, with a memorandum on rejected advice.**
- ⭐ **Post-tenure:** an SPSC **Chairman** may become **UPSC Chairman or member, or Chairman of another SPSC**; an SPSC **member** may become **Chairman of that or another SPSC, or Chairman or member of the UPSC**. ⚠️ **Nothing else.**
- ⭐⭐ **JSPSC — STATUTORY, created by PARLIAMENT on the request of two or more state legislatures; members appointed and removed by the PRESIDENT; 6 years or 62; reports to each Governor.**
- ⭐ **Article 315(2)** — the **UPSC may serve a state** on the **Governor's request with the President's approval**.
