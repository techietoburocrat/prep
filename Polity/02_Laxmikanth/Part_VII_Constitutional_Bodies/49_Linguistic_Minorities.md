# Chapter 49 — Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities

[← Chapter 48](48_National_Commission_BCs.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 50 →](50_CAG.md)

**Read time ~4 min** · **Article 350B** · ⚪ Prelims: occasional, as a "which is constitutional" distractor · Mains: never directly

> 📌 **The shortest chapter in the book — one article, one officer.** ⭐ **Read it once, learn four facts, and move on.** ⚠️ **Its only real exam value is as a DISTRACTOR: it is a constitutional office that most people assume is statutory.**

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> ⭐ **When states were reorganised on linguistic lines in 1956, every new state instantly created a linguistic minority inside itself.** ⭐⭐ **Article 350B was the safety valve — a single officer whose job is to check whether the promises made to those minorities are being kept.**

---

## ⭐ The provision — Article 350B

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Inserted by** | ⭐⭐ **The 7th Amendment Act, 1956**, on the recommendation of the ⭐ **States Reorganisation Commission (1955)** — *the same amendment that abolished the four-fold classification of states* |
| ⭐ **Appointed by** | ⭐ **The PRESIDENT** |
| ⭐ **Duty** | ⭐ **To investigate ALL matters relating to the SAFEGUARDS provided for linguistic minorities under the Constitution** |
| ⭐ **Reports to** | ⭐ **The President, at such intervals as the President may direct** |
| ⭐⭐ **Where the report goes** | ⭐ **The President causes the reports to be LAID BEFORE EACH HOUSE OF PARLIAMENT and SENT TO THE STATE GOVERNMENTS concerned** |
| ⚠️ **What the Constitution does NOT say** | ⚠️ ⭐ **Nothing about qualifications, tenure, salary, service conditions or the procedure for removal** — all of it is left to the government |

⭐ **In practice the office is designated the COMMISSIONER FOR LINGUISTIC MINORITIES (CLM)**, headquartered at **Allahabad (Prayagraj)**, with regional offices, and functions under the ⭐ **Ministry of Minority Affairs**.

> ⚠️ ⭐ **The structural criticism, in one line:** ⭐ **it is an OFFICER, not a COMMISSION** — no members, no civil-court powers, no statutory backing beyond the article, and ⚠️ **no protected tenure of the kind Article 148 gives the CAG or Article 317 gives the UPSC.** ⭐ **Compare it with the NCSC/NCST/NCBC of the previous three chapters and the contrast is stark: those three got constitutional commissions; linguistic minorities got one civil servant.**

---

## ⭐ The three articles it sits with

| Article | Provision |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **350** | ⭐ **Every person may submit a REPRESENTATION for redress of a grievance to any officer or authority in ANY LANGUAGE used in the Union or in the state** |
| ⭐⭐ **350A** | ⭐ **Every state and every local authority shall endeavour to provide adequate facilities for INSTRUCTION IN THE MOTHER TONGUE at the PRIMARY STAGE** to children of linguistic minority groups — and ⭐ **the PRESIDENT may issue directions to a state** for the purpose. ⚠️ **Also inserted by the 7th Amendment, 1956** |
| ⭐ **350B** | ⭐ **The Special Officer** |

⭐ **Two more worth linking:** ⭐ **Article 29(1)** — any section of citizens having a **distinct language, script or culture** has the right to conserve it; ⭐ **Article 347** — on a demand, the President may direct that a **language spoken by a substantial proportion of a state's population** be officially recognised in that state.

> ⭐⭐ **The judicial definition worth one mark:** ⚠️ ⭐ **a "linguistic minority" is determined with reference to the STATE, not the country** *(the settled position from the Article 30 line of cases)*. ⭐ **So Hindi speakers are a linguistic minority in Tamil Nadu, and Tamil speakers are one in Uttar Pradesh** — a genuinely counter-intuitive point that has appeared in statement-based questions.

---

## 🧠 Memory hooks

### The four facts
> ⭐ **Article 350B · 7th Amendment, 1956 · appointed by the PRESIDENT · reports to the PRESIDENT, who lays them before PARLIAMENT and sends them to the STATES.**

### The trio
> ⭐ **350 = representation in any language · 350A = mother-tongue instruction at the PRIMARY stage · 350B = the Special Officer.** ⭐ **350A and 350B both came with the 7th Amendment, 1956.**

### The status trap
> ⚠️ ⭐ **It IS a constitutional office** *(so it belongs on the constitutional side of any "which of these is constitutional" question)* — ⚠️ **but it is a one-man office with no prescribed tenure, no civil-court powers and non-binding reports.**

---

## ⚡ 60-second revision

- ⭐ **Article 350B**, inserted by the ⭐ **7th Amendment Act, 1956**, on the **States Reorganisation Commission's** recommendation.
- ⭐ **Appointed by the President** to ⭐ **investigate all matters relating to the safeguards for linguistic minorities** and ⭐ **report to the President at such intervals as he directs**; ⭐ **reports laid before both Houses and sent to the state governments.**
- ⚠️ ⭐ **The Constitution prescribes no qualifications, tenure, salary or removal procedure.** Designated the ⭐ **Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities**, under the **Ministry of Minority Affairs**, headquartered at **Allahabad (Prayagraj)**.
- ⭐ **Article 350** — representation in any language. ⭐ **Article 350A** — mother-tongue instruction at the **primary stage**, with the **President empowered to direct a state**. ⭐ **Article 29(1)** — the right to conserve language, script and culture. ⭐ **Article 347** — recognition of a language spoken by a substantial proportion of a state.
- ⚠️ ⭐ **A linguistic minority is reckoned STATE-WISE, not nationally.**
