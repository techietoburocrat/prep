> ⭐⭐ **Read down the last column. ELEVEN of the twelve bodies produce recommendations that bind nobody.** ⚠️ **That is not an accident — it is the Constitution's standard technique: give a body prestige, independence and civil-court powers of inquiry, and then require the government to explain itself in writing to the legislature rather than obey.** ⭐ **Say this once in any Part VII answer and it lifts the whole thing.**

## PART B — ⭐ The five patterns worth extracting

**1. ⭐ The "6 years or 65" club, and its one exception**
> ⭐ **ECI · UPSC · CAG — all 6 years or 65.** ⚠️ ⭐ **The State PSC is 6 years or 62.** *(The Finance Commission has no such rule; the AG and Advocate General have no term at all.)*

**2. ⭐⭐ Three different removal designs**
> ⭐ **Parliamentary address, like a Supreme Court judge — CEC, CAG.**
> ⭐ **A BINDING Supreme Court finding — UPSC and State PSC members** *(and note: **a State PSC member is removed by the PRESIDENT**, not the Governor)*.
> ⚠️ ⭐ **Pleasure — Attorney General, Advocate General.**

**3. ⭐ Who is barred from future office**
> ⭐ **CAG and UPSC Chairman: barred completely.** ⚠️ ⭐ **The CEC is NOT** — the standing criticism in [Ch 41](41_Election_Commission.md).

**4. ⭐ Whose money is "charged"**
> ⭐ **UPSC and CAG: expenditure CHARGED on the Consolidated Fund** *(non-votable)*. ⚠️ ⭐ **The ECI's is VOTED** — which is why "charge the ECI's expenditure" is a standing reform demand.

**5. ⭐⭐ The three-commission mirror**
> ⭐ **338 / 338A / 338B → NCSC / NCST / NCBC**, and ⭐ **341 / 342 / 342A → the SC / ST / SEBC lists.** ⭐ **65th (1990) constitutional · 89th (2003) bifurcated · 102nd (2018) NCBC · 105th (2021) states' SEBC lists restored.**

## PART C — ⚠️ The constitutional-vs-statutory trap

⭐ **[Prelims 2023 Q5](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2023.md) and [2025 Q3](../../03_PYQ_Prelims/2025.md) both asked exactly this.** ⚠️ **Learn the two columns as a pair — you cannot answer one without the other.**

| ⭐ **CONSTITUTIONAL *(this Part)*** | ⭐ **STATUTORY / EXECUTIVE *([Part VIII](../Part_VIII_Non_Constitutional_Bodies/00_INDEX.md))*** |
|---|---|
| ECI · UPSC · State PSC · Finance Commission · **GST Council** · NCSC · NCST · **NCBC** · Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities · CAG · Attorney General · Advocate General | NHRC · State HRC · CIC · State IC · CVC · **CBI** *(under the DSPE Act, 1946)* · Lokpal · NIA · NDMA · **NITI Aayog** *(executive resolution)* · NCW · NCPCR · Law Commission |

> ⚠️ ⭐ **The three that catch people out:** ⭐ **the GST Council IS constitutional** *(Article 279A)*; ⭐ **the NCBC IS constitutional since 2018** *(Article 338B)*; and ⚠️ ⭐ **a JOINT State Public Service Commission is STATUTORY** even though the ordinary State PSC is constitutional.
>
> ⚠️ **Also: NITI Aayog is neither constitutional NOR statutory — it exists by a Cabinet resolution.** ⭐ **That is the single most-asked distinction in the whole of Parts VII and VIII.**
