## 🎯 What this Part is for

Part I asked *what the Constitution is*. **Part II asks how the government actually runs** — along two axes:

- **Between organs** — is the executive separate from the legislature, or drawn from it? *(Ch 12)*
- **Between levels** — how is power divided between the Centre and the states, and what happens when that division is suspended? *(Ch 13–16)*

Everything in Part III (President, Parliament, Supreme Court) is an institution operating inside the system this Part describes.
