## 🎯 What this Part is for

Part I answers one question: **what *is* the Indian Constitution?** Where it came from, what it says about itself, who it applies to, what it guarantees, and how far it can be changed.

It is the only Part you cannot skip or skim. Every later Part — Parliament, the judiciary, federalism, the constitutional bodies — assumes you already have this vocabulary.

## 🧵 The thread running through Part I

**Parliament vs the Court, over who has the last word.** It starts in Chapter 8 and ends in Chapter 11, and it is the single most useful story in the book:

> ***Champakam* (1951) → 1st Amendment → *Golaknath* (1967) → 24th Amendment (1971) → *Kesavananda* (1973) → 42nd Amendment (1976) → *Minerva Mills* (1980) → NJAC (2015)***

Say it out loud until it is automatic. It answers questions in Chapters 7, 8, 10 and 11, and it reappears in [Part II's Chapter 16](../Part_II_System_of_Government/16_Emergency_Provisions.md) through *ADM Jabalpur* and the 44th Amendment.
