# Chapter 25 — Indian Parliamentary Group

[← Chapter 24](24_Parliamentary_Forums.md) · [Part Index](00_INDEX.md) · [Master](../00_INDEX.md) · [Chapter 26 →](26_Supreme_Court.md)

**Read time ~2 min** · ⚪ **Lowest priority in the book** · Prelims: very rare · Mains: never asked

> 📌 **Read this once and never again.** Five facts, listed below. It exists in the syllabus for completeness, not because it is examined.

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> **The Indian Parliamentary Group is not a parliamentary committee at all — it is an autonomous association of MPs** that acts as India's link to international parliamentary bodies. Its only real examinable feature is that it is **autonomous** and that the **Speaker is its ex-officio President**.

---

## The five facts ⭐

| # | Fact |
|---|---|
| 1 | ⭐ **An autonomous body** — formed in **1949**, in pursuance of a **motion adopted by the Constituent Assembly (Legislative)** on 16 August 1948 |
| 2 | ⭐ It functions as the **National Group of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU)** and the **Main Branch of the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA)** in India |
| 3 | ⭐ The **Speaker of the Lok Sabha** is the **ex-officio President** of the Group. The **Deputy Speaker of the Lok Sabha** and the **Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** are the **ex-officio Vice-Presidents**. The **Secretary-General of the Lok Sabha** is the **ex-officio Secretary-General** of the Group |
| 4 | **Membership** is open to **all members of Parliament** *(sitting members are "members"; ex-MPs are eligible for "associate membership")* |
| 5 | ⚠️ **It is the only association in India authorised** to deal with the IPU and the CPA |

### Its objectives

- To promote **personal contacts** between members of Parliament
- To study questions of **public importance** likely to come before Parliament, and to arrange **discussions, lectures and study circles**
- To arrange for members **visits to foreign countries** and to **facilitate visits by foreign parliamentarians**
- To **maintain a library** of parliamentary literature

---

## ⚠️ The one trap

⭐ **It is NOT a parliamentary committee.** It is an **autonomous association** — it is not appointed by the House, does not work under the Speaker's *direction* in the committee sense, and does not report to the House. *(Compare the definition of a parliamentary committee in [Ch 23](23_Parliamentary_Committees.md).)*

**Three bodies that are commonly confused, all involving the Speaker:**

| Body | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Parliamentary Committee** *(Ch 23)* | ⭐ **Scrutiny** — appointed by the House, **reports to the House** |
| **Parliamentary Forum** *(Ch 24)* | ⭐ **Sensitisation** — Speaker is ex-officio President, **does not report to the House** |
| **Indian Parliamentary Group** *(here)* | ⭐ **International liaison** — an **autonomous association**, Speaker is ex-officio President, links India to the **IPU and CPA** |

---

## ⚡ 20-second revision

**Autonomous body, formed 1949.** ⭐ **National Group of the IPU and Main Branch of the CPA in India** — the **only** body authorised to deal with them. ⭐ **Speaker = ex-officio President**; Deputy Speaker and Deputy Chairman = Vice-Presidents; Secretary-General of the Lok Sabha = Secretary-General. Open to **all MPs**. ⚠️ **NOT a parliamentary committee.**
