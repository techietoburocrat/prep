# Chapter 24 — Parliamentary Forums

> 📌 **The only reason this chapter exists in your syllabus is the contrast in [Mains 2011 Q4(d)](../../04_PYQ_Mains/2011.md):** *"Distinction between Department Related Parliamentary Standing Committees and Parliamentary Forums."* Learn that distinction and move on.

---

## 🎯 The one-line point

> **A Parliamentary Forum is a discussion group, not a scrutiny body.** It exists to **sensitise members** to a critical issue and give them a place to engage with experts — it does **not** examine bills, does **not** vote on demands for grants, and does **not** report to the House. That is the whole difference from a committee.

---

## The essentials

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | To provide a **platform for members to have interaction** with ministers, experts and key officials on **critical issues**, and to **sensitise members** about those issues |
| **Origin** | The **first forum — on Water Conservation and Management — was constituted in 2005** |
| ⭐ **Chairman** | The **Speaker of the Lok Sabha** is the **ex-officio President** of all forums, ⚠️ **except the Forum on Population and Public Health**, whose ex-officio President is the **Chairman of the Rajya Sabha** |
| **Vice-Presidents** | The **Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**, the **Deputy Speaker of the Lok Sabha**, the **concerned ministers**, and the **chairmen of the related DRSCs** |
| ⭐ **Membership** | **Not more than 31 members**, excluding the President of the forum — **not more than 21 from the Lok Sabha and not more than 10 from the Rajya Sabha** |
| **Term** | **One year**, coinciding with membership of the concerned committee |
| ⚠️ **Ministers** | ⭐ **A minister is not eligible to be a member** *(as with the DRSCs)* |

### The forums constituted so far

**Water Conservation and Management** *(2005 — the first)* · **Youth** · **Children** · **Population and Public Health** · **Global Warming and Climate Change** · **Disaster Management** · **Artisans and Craftspeople**.

---

## ⭐⭐ The distinction that is actually asked

| | **DRSC** *(Ch 23)* | **Parliamentary Forum** |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | ⭐ **Scrutiny** of the executive | ⭐ **Sensitisation** of members |
| **Functions** | Examines **bills referred**, **demands for grants**, **annual reports**, **policy documents** | ⚠️ **None of these.** Provides a platform for **discussion with experts and ministers** |
| **Output** | ⭐ **Reports presented to the House** | ⚠️ **Does not report to the House** |
| **Who heads it** | A member **nominated by the Speaker/Chairman** | ⭐ The **Speaker himself** *(ex-officio President)* |
| **Basis** | Rules of Procedure, under **Article 118** | An **administrative arrangement** — created by decision of the presiding officers |
| **Strength** | **31** *(21 LS + 10 RS)* | **Up to 31** *(≤21 LS + ≤10 RS)* — deliberately mirrored |
| **Ministers** | Cannot be members | Cannot be members — but ⭐ ministers **are Vice-Presidents** |

> ⭐ **The one-line answer:** **a committee examines the government; a forum educates the members.** Same size, opposite function.

---

## ⚡ 30-second revision

**Platform for interaction and sensitisation**, not scrutiny. **First forum: Water Conservation and Management, 2005.** ⭐ **Speaker is ex-officio President of all forums — except Population and Public Health, where it is the Rajya Sabha Chairman.** **Up to 31 members (≤21 LS + ≤10 RS)**, term **one year**, ⚠️ **ministers ineligible as members but serve as Vice-Presidents**. ⚠️ **Does not examine bills, does not consider demands for grants, does not report to the House.**
