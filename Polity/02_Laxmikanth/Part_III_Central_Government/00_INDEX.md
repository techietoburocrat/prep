## 🎯 What this Part is for

[Part II](../Part_II_System_of_Government/00_INDEX.md) described the **system**. Part III describes the **institutions that operate inside it** — the three organs of the Union, in order:

- **The executive** — President, Vice-President, Prime Minister, Council of Ministers, Cabinet Committees *(Ch 17–21)*
- **The legislature** — Parliament and its committees, forums and groups *(Ch 22–25)*
- **The judiciary** — the Supreme Court, and the three doctrines that define its role *(Ch 26–29)*

This is where most of GS-II's institutional questions come from.
