### 1. Provisional President of the Constituent Assembly *(Paper Q61)*
Who was the Provisional President of the Constituent Assembly before Dr. Rajendra Prasad took over?

- (a) C. Rajagopalachari
- (b) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
- (c) T.T. Krishnamachari
- (d) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** · `Constituent Assembly`

> As the **oldest member**, he chaired the first sitting on **9 December 1946**. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected permanent President on **11 December 1946**.

</details>

### 2. Government of India Act, 1935 *(Paper Q62)*
With reference to the Government of India Act, 1935, consider the following statements :

1. It provided for the establishment of an All India Federation based on the union of the British Indian Provinces and Princely States.
2. Defence and Foreign Affairs were kept under the control of the federal legislature.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 only** · `GoI Act 1935` `Historical background`

> Statement 2 is the trap. Defence and Foreign Affairs were **reserved subjects kept with the Governor-General**, entirely outside the federal legislature's reach — that was the whole point of "dyarchy at the Centre". See [`01_Historical_Background`](../02_Laxmikanth/Part_I_Constitutional_Framework/01_Historical_Background.md).

</details>

### 3. Article 368 — modes of amendment *(Paper Q66)*
As per Article 368 of the Constitution of India, the Parliament may amend any provision of the Constitution by way of :

1. Addition
2. Variation
3. Repeal

Select the correct answer using the code given below :

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) 1, 2 and 3** · `Article 368` `Amendment`

> Article 368 uses exactly these three words — amendment "by way of **addition, variation or repeal**". A direct-from-the-bare-text question. See [`10_Amendment_of_the_Constitution`](../02_Laxmikanth/Part_I_Constitutional_Framework/10_Amendment_of_the_Constitution.md).

</details>

### 4. Money Bill *(Paper Q68)*
Which of the following statements are correct in respect of a Money Bill in the Parliament?

1. Article 109 mentions special procedure in respect of Money Bills.
2. A Money Bill shall not be introduced in the Council of States.
3. The Rajya Sabha can either approve the Bill or suggest changes but cannot reject it.
4. Amendments to a Money Bill suggested by the Rajya Sabha have to be accepted by the Lok Sabha.

Select the answer using the code given below :

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1, 2 and 3
- (d) 1, 3 and 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 1, 2 and 3** · `Money Bill` `Article 109` `Article 110`

> Statement 4 is the trap: the Lok Sabha **may accept or reject** any or all of the Rajya Sabha's recommendations. The Rajya Sabha must return a Money Bill within **14 days**; it cannot reject or amend it.

</details>

### 5. North Eastern Council *(Paper Q70)*
The North Eastern Council (NEC) was established by the North Eastern Council Act, 1971. Subsequent to the amendment of NEC Act in 2002, the Council comprises which of the following members?

1. Governor of the Constituent State
2. Chief Minister of the Constituent State
3. Three Members to be nominated by the President of India
4. The Home Minister of India

Select the correct answer using the code given below :

- (a) 1, 2 and 3 only
- (b) 1, 3 and 4 only
- (c) 2 and 4 only
- (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1, 2 and 3 only** · `North Eastern Council` `Regional councils`

> The question is anchored to the **2002 amendment**, which set the composition as Governors + Chief Ministers + three Presidential nominees. The Union Home Minister became NEC **Chairman** only by a later 2018 reconstitution — outside what the 2002 amendment provided, which is why statement 4 fails.

</details>

### 6. Delimitation Commissions *(Paper Q71)*
How many Delimitation Commissions have been constituted by the Government of India till December 2023?

- (a) One
- (b) Two
- (c) Three
- (d) Four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) Four** · `Delimitation` `Article 82`

> **1952, 1963, 1973, 2002.** The gaps run roughly a decade each, then a long freeze — the 42nd and 84th Amendments froze seat allocation until after the **first census after 2026**.

</details>

### 7. 71st Amendment — Eighth Schedule *(Paper Q72)*
The Constitution (71st Amendment) Act, 1992 amends the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution to include which of the following languages?

1. Konkani
2. Manipuri
3. Nepali
4. Maithili

Select the correct answer using the code given below :

- (a) 1, 2 and 3
- (b) 1, 2 and 4
- (c) 1, 3 and 4
- (d) 2, 3 and 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1, 2 and 3** · `Eighth Schedule` `71st Amendment`

> **Ko-Ma-Ne in '92** — Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali. **Maithili** came much later, with the **92nd Amendment (2003)**, alongside Bodo, Dogri and Santhali. That 1992-vs-2003 split is the entire question.

</details>

### 8. Political parties and their leaders *(Paper Q73)*
Consider the following pairs :

| | Party | Its Leader |
|---|---|---|
| 1. | Bharatiya Jana Sangh | Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee |
| 2. | Socialist Party | C. Rajagopalachari |
| 3. | Congress for Democracy | Jagjivan Ram |
| 4. | Swatantra Party | Acharya Narendra Dev |

How many of the above are correctly matched?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Political parties` `Post-independence politics`

> Pairs **1 and 3** are right. The other two are swapped with each other's people: **C. Rajagopalachari founded the Swatantra Party** (1959), while **Acharya Narendra Dev** was a Socialist Party leader.

</details>

### 10. Union List vs State List *(Paper Q75)*
Which one of the following statements is correct as per the Constitution of India?

- (a) Inter-State trade and commerce is a State subject under the State List.
- (b) Inter-State migration is a State subject under the State List.
- (c) Inter-State quarantine is a Union subject under the Union List.
- (d) Corporation tax is a State subject under the State List.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Inter-State quarantine is a Union subject under the Union List.** · `Seventh Schedule` `Union List`

> The rule of thumb that cracks this: anything **"Inter-State"** is Union or Concurrent, never purely State. Inter-State trade and commerce, inter-State migration and inter-State quarantine all sit in the **Union List**, as does **corporation tax**.

</details>

### 11. Right to Privacy *(Paper Q76)*
Under which of the following Articles of the Constitution of India, has the Supreme Court of India placed the Right to Privacy?

- (a) Article 15
- (b) Article 16
- (c) Article 19
- (d) Article 21

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) Article 21** · `Right to Privacy` `Article 21`

> **K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (2017)** — a nine-judge bench held privacy to be intrinsic to life and personal liberty under Article 21. See [`07_Fundamental_Rights`](../02_Laxmikanth/Part_I_Constitutional_Framework/07_Fundamental_Rights.md).

</details>

### 12. Ethics Committee of the Lok Sabha *(Paper Q80)*
Which of the following statements about the Ethics Committee in the Lok Sabha are correct?

1. Initially it was an *ad-hoc* Committee.
2. Only a Member of the Lok Sabha can make a complaint relating to unethical conduct of a member of the Lok Sabha.
3. This Committee cannot take up any matter which is sub-judice.

Select the answer using the code given below :

- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 1 and 3 only** · `Parliamentary committees` `Ethics Committee`

### 13. Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam *(Paper Q81)*
Consider the following statements regarding 'Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam' :

1. Provisions will come into effect from the 18th Lok Sabha.
2. This will be in force for 15 years after becoming an Act.
3. There are provisions for the reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes Women within the quota reserved for the Scheduled Castes.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) 1, 2 and 3
- (b) 1 and 2 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1 and 3 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 2 and 3 only** · `106th Amendment` `Women's reservation`

> Statement 1 is the trap and it is the single most examinable point about this Act: the 33% reservation takes effect only **after the first census following commencement, and the delimitation carried out on its basis** — not from the 18th Lok Sabha.

</details>

### 14. Writ of Prohibition *(Paper Q83)*
A Writ of Prohibition is an order issued by the Supreme Court or High Courts to :

- (a) a government officer prohibiting him from taking a particular action.
- (b) the Parliament/Legislative Assembly to pass a law on Prohibition.
- (c) the lower court prohibiting continuation of proceedings in a case.
- (d) the Government prohibiting it from following an unconstitutional policy.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) the lower court prohibiting continuation of proceedings in a case.** · `Writs` `Article 32`

> **Prohibition is preventive, Certiorari is curative.** Prohibition stops proceedings while they are still running; certiorari quashes an order already passed. Both go **only to judicial/quasi-judicial bodies**, never to a purely administrative authority — which is why (a) and (d) are wrong.

</details>

### 15. Declaring a Scheduled Tribe *(Paper Q84)*
Consider the following statements :

1. It is the Governor of the State who recognizes and declares any community of that State as a Scheduled Tribe.
2. A community declared as a Scheduled Tribe in a State need not be so in another State.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 only** · `Article 342` `Scheduled Tribes`

> Under **Article 342** it is the **President** who specifies tribes, by public notification, *in consultation with* the Governor — and thereafter only **Parliament** can add to or remove from that list. Statement 2 is correct: ST status is **State-specific**.

</details>

### 16. Union Budget *(Paper Q85)*
With reference to Union Budget, consider the following statements :

1. The Union Finance Minister on behalf of the Prime Minister lays the Annual Financial Statement before both the Houses of Parliament.
2. At the Union level, no demand for a grant can be made except on the recommendation of the President of India.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Both 1 and 2** — *official UPSC key* · `Article 112` `Article 113` `Budget`

> ⚠️ **This key is contested, and you should know why.** **Article 112** says "the **President** shall cause to be laid before both Houses… the Annual Financial Statement", so statement 1's "on behalf of the **Prime Minister**" looks plainly wrong, and most coaching keys marked this **(b)**. UPSC nevertheless retained **(c)** and did **not** drop the question.
>
> Learn the constitutional position — Art. 112 → President causes the AFS to be laid; Art. 113 → no demand for a grant except on the President's recommendation — rather than the key. Statement 2 is correct either way.

</details>

### 17. Removal of the Speaker *(Paper Q93)*
With reference to the Speaker of the Lok Sabha, consider the following statements :

While any resolution for the removal of the Speaker of the Lok Sabha is under consideration

1. He/She shall not preside.
2. He/She shall not have the right to speak.
3. He/She shall not be entitled to vote on the resolution in the first instance.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 1 and 2 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 only** · `Speaker` `Article 94` `Article 96`

> **Article 96** is precise and counter-intuitive: while a removal resolution is under consideration the Speaker **shall not preside**, but he/she **has the right to speak in, and take part in, the proceedings** and **is entitled to vote in the first instance** (though not to a casting vote). Statements 2 and 3 invert both.

</details>

### 18. Lapsing of Bills *(Paper Q94)*
With reference to the Indian Parliament, consider the following statements :

1. A bill pending in the Lok Sabha lapses on its dissolution.
2. A bill passed by the Lok Sabha and pending in the Rajya Sabha lapses on the dissolution of the Lok Sabha.
3. A bill in regard to which the President of India notified his/her intention to summon the Houses to a joint sitting lapses on the dissolution of the Lok Sabha.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 1 and 2
- (c) 2 and 3
- (d) 3 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 1 and 2** · `Lapsing of bills` `Article 108`

> Statement 3 is the exception you must remember: once the President has **notified the intention to summon a joint sitting**, the bill **does not lapse**. Also non-lapsing: bills pending **in the Rajya Sabha alone** (never passed by the Lok Sabha), and bills already passed by both Houses awaiting assent.

</details>

### 19. Prorogation and dissolution *(Paper Q95)*
With reference to the Parliament of India, consider the following statements :

1. Prorogation of a House by the President of India does not require the advice of the Council of Ministers.
2. Prorogation of a House is generally done after the House is adjourned *sine die* but there is no bar to the President of India prorogating the House which is in session.
3. Dissolution of the Lok Sabha is done by the President of India who, save in exceptional circumstances, does so on the advice of the Council of Ministers.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 1 and 2
- (c) 2 and 3
- (d) 3 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 2 and 3** · `Prorogation` `Dissolution` `Article 85`

> Statement 1 is wrong for the most basic reason in the subject: under **Article 74** the President acts on the **aid and advice** of the Council of Ministers. Prorogation is no exception.
>
> Keep the three apart: **adjournment** (by the presiding officer), **prorogation** (by the President, ends the session), **dissolution** (by the President, ends the House itself).

</details>
