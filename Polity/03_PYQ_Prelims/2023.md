# UPSC Prelims — Polity MCQs: 2023

[← 2022](2022.md) · [Index](00_INDEX.md) · *2024 — not yet compiled*

**Paper:** General Studies Paper I · Exam date: 28 May 2023 · **Polity questions:** 15

> **Note the new formats.** 2023 introduces two styles at scale: the **"How many of the above statements are correct?"** counting format (Only one / Only two / All three / None), which removes the elimination shortcuts that used to work, and the **Statement-I / Statement-II** assertion-reason format. Both are now standard — practise them.

### 1. Due Process of Law
In essence, what does 'Due Process of Law' mean?

- (a) The principle of natural justice
- (b) The procedure established by law
- (c) Fair application of law
- (d) Equality before law

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) The procedure established by law** · `Article 21` `Due process`

</details>

### 2. Prisons — Statement I / II
Consider the following statements:

**Statement-I:** In India, prisons are managed by State Governments with their own rules and regulations for the day-to-day administration of prisons.

**Statement-II:** In India, prisons are governed by the Prisons Act, 1894 which expressly kept the subject of prisons in the control of Provincial Governments.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

- (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
- (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a)** · `Prisons` `State List` `Seventh Schedule`

</details>

### 3. Chief purpose of a Constitution
Which one of the following statements best reflects the chief purpose of the 'Constitution' of a country?

- (a) It determines the objective for the making of necessary laws.
- (b) It enables the creation of political offices and a government.
- (c) It defines and limits the powers of government.
- (d) It secures social justice, social equality and social security.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) It defines and limits the powers of government.** · `Constitutionalism`

> The same idea as **2020 Q6** and **2021 Q4** — limited government.

</details>

### 4. Amendment to overcome judicial interpretation
In India, which one of the following Constitutional Amendments was widely believed to be enacted to overcome the judicial interpretations of the Fundamental Rights?

- (a) 1st Amendment
- (b) 42nd Amendment
- (c) 44th Amendment
- (d) 86th Amendment

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 42nd Amendment** · `42nd Amendment` `Fundamental Rights`

</details>

### 5. Which are constitutional bodies
Consider the following organizations/bodies in India:

1. The National Commission for Backward Classes
2. The National Human Rights Commission
3. The National Law Commission
4. The National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

How many of the above are constitutional bodies?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Constitutional bodies` `Article 338B`

⚠️ *UPSC's official key for this question was contested; several sources mark it (a) on the basis that only the NCBC is constitutional. The NCBC's statutory-to-constitutional transformation is **2022 Mains GS-II Q5**.*

</details>

- (b) Only two
- (c) All three
- (d) None

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

### 15. Constitution Day
Consider the following statements in respect of the Constitution Day:

**Statement-I:** The Constitution Day is celebrated on 26th November every year to promote constitutional values among citizens.

**Statement-II:** On 26th November, 1949, the Constituent Assembly of India set up a Drafting Committee under the Chairmanship of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar to prepare a Draft Constitution of India.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

- (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
- (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect** · `Constitution Day` `Constituent Assembly`

> The Drafting Committee was set up on **29 August 1947**, not 26 November 1949. The significance of 26 November was also a 20-word **2009 Mains** item.

</details>
