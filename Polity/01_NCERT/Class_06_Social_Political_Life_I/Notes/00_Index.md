# Class 6 — Social and Political Life I (Civics/Polity) — Complete Notes

Detailed, exam-friendly summaries of all 9 chapters, written so a 6th-grader can understand every concept without needing to read the full textbook. Each file has: the big idea, a full explanation with examples, a glossary, a recap, and practice questions.

Original NCERT PDF for this book is not available on NCERT's site (removed during the NEP 2020 revision — see `HOW_TO_GET.txt` in this folder for how to get an official copy via DIKSHA/ePathshala if you want to cross-check). **Note:** the current official NCERT Class 6 book has actually been replaced by a combined title, *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*, with civics restructured as "Grassroots Democracy" (Parts 1–3). These notes deliberately keep the older, more UPSC-standard structure (matching this folder's name and the reading plan in `READING_GUIDE.txt`), but have been enriched with genuinely relevant concepts from the current syllabus/law that were missing on the first pass — the **Eleventh Schedule** (Ch 5), the **74th Amendment, Twelfth Schedule, and Street Vendors Act, 2014** (Ch 7, Ch 9), and **MGNREGA** (Ch 8).

## How the chapters connect (the story of the whole book)
1. **Ch 1–2** set up the foundation: India is diverse, and that diversity must be respected, not discriminated against.
2. **Ch 3–4** introduce government itself: what it is, why we need it, and what makes it democratic.
3. **Ch 5–7** zoom into local government: how villages (Panchayati Raj, rural administration) and cities (urban administration) are actually run day to day.
4. **Ch 8–9** shift to the economic side: how ordinary people in villages and cities earn their living.

## Suggested revision order
Read in chapter order (1 → 9) the first time, since each pair builds on the last. For quick revision before a test, revise in this order (most-tested concepts first): **Ch 4 → Ch 2 → Ch 5 → Ch 1 → Ch 3 → Ch 7 → Ch 6 → Ch 8 → Ch 9.**
