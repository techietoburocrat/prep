# UPSC Mains — Polity Questions: 2013

[← 2012](2012.md) · [Index](00_INDEX.md) · [2014 →](2014.md)

**Paper:** General Studies **Paper II** — *Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International Relations*
**Format:** 25 questions × 10 marks = 250 · 200 words each · all compulsory
**Polity + governance questions:** 14 of 25

> **The pattern change.** From 2013 polity gets its own 250-mark paper. Choice is abolished — every question is compulsory. Note this year's oddity: 25 questions of equal weight, a format used only in 2013.

## Parliament & the Political Process

### Q1 · 10 marks · 200 words
The role of individual MPs (Members of Parliament) has diminished over the years and as a result healthy constructive debates on policy issues are not usually witnessed. How far can this be attributed to the anti-defection law which was legislated but with a different intention?

`Anti-defection` `Tenth Schedule` `Parliament`

> Asked again in **2019** (Q15), that time without the anti-defection framing.

### Q10 · 10 marks · 200 words
Pressure group politics is sometimes seen as the informal face of politics. With regards to the above, assess the structure and functioning of pressure groups in India.

`Pressure groups` `Political process`

> First of four: **2013** → **2017** Q4 → **2019** Q3 (farmers' organisations) → **2021** Q5 (business associations) → **2025** Q15 (environmental groups).
