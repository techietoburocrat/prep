The 2013 break. Before 2013 polity was a section inside GS Paper I (usually Q6–Q9), with internal choice and factual, institutional questions. From 2013 it became GS Paper II — its own 250-mark paper, every question compulsory, all statement-based and analytical. Polity carried about 130 of 300 marks in 2000 and roughly 37 by 2012; the restructuring was a correction to exactly that.

Fidelity. Question text is reproduced as printed and keyed to the year-wise question papers, not to topic-wise compilations. Where archived transcriptions carried obvious OCR damage (the 2014 paper's “Staring”, “hat speech”, “NIIRC”; the 2015 paper's “Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1966”) the standard published wording is used.
