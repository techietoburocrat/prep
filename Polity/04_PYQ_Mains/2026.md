### What to expect in GS Paper II

Based on the 2017–2025 structure, which has been stable for nine years:

- **Q1–Q10** · 10 marks each · 150 words
- **Q11–Q20** · 15 marks each · 250 words
- **250 marks total**, all questions compulsory
- Syllabus: *Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International Relations*
- Typical split: **9–12 questions** on polity/constitution/governance, 3–5 on social justice, 4–5 on international relations

### Themes overdue for a question

- **Women's reservation** — the 106th Amendment (2023) has not yet been asked directly
- **Comparison with other constitutions** — a certainty; appears every year since 2018
