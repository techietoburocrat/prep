## Fundamental Duties

### Q1(a) · 20 marks · ~250 words
"Essentially all this is contained in Part IV-A of the Constitution is just a codification of tasks integral of the Indian way of life." Critically examine this statement.

`Part IV-A` `Article 51A` `Fundamental Duties`

> Follows **2003** Q8(b) and **2008** Q6(a) — the third outing for Fundamental Duties in nine years, this time as a critical-examination statement rather than an enumeration.

---

## Executive Clemency

### Q1(b) · 20 marks · ~250 words
"The exercise of executive clemency is not a privilege but is based on several principles, and discretion has to be exercised in public considerations." Analyse this statement in the context of the judicial powers of the President of India.

`Article 72` `Pardon power` `Judicial review of clemency`

> Part of a long chain: **2003** Q6(a) → **2011** Q1(b) → **2014** GS-II Q5 (delay in deciding mercy petitions) → **2025** GS-II Q3 (India vs USA, preemptive pardons).
