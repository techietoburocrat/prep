## Fundamental Rights & Disqualification

### Q6 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*
**(a)** What is right to life and personal liberty? How have the courts expanded its meaning in recent years?

**(b)** On what grounds can a member be disqualified from either House of Parliament?

`Article 21` `Article 102` `Anti-defection`

> Q6(a) is the direct ancestor of **2023** GS-II Q11 ("expanding horizons of the right to life and personal liberty") and **2024** GS-II Q12 (right to privacy). Q6(b) is repeated in **2010** (Q4a) and expanded in **2019** (GS-II Q11, under the RPA 1951).

## Democracy & Development

### Q7 · 30 marks · ~250 words · *answer any one*
**(a)** What is the 'strategic partnership' between India and United States of America? What are its implications for both the partners? *(International Relations — not polity)*

**(b)** Discuss economic backwardness as a major challenge of Indian democracy. Can democracy and development go together smoothly?

`Democracy and development`

## Amendment, Inter-State Council & Writs

### Q8 · 2 × 15 = 30 marks · ~150 words each · *answer any two*
**(a)** How would you differentiate between the passage of a Constitution Amendment Bill and of an Ordinary Legislative Bill?

**(b)** How does the Inter-State Council establish co-ordination between States?

**(c)** Is the High Courts' power to issue 'writs' wider than that of the Supreme Court of India?

`Article 368` `Article 263` `Article 32 vs 226`

> Q8(a) **repeats 2001 Q7(b)** almost word for word — a clear five-year cycle.

## Short Answers

### Q9 · 5 × 2 = 10 marks · ~20 words each
**(a)** Explain the following terms:
  - (i) Dissolution of the House
  - (ii) Prorogation of the House
  - (iii) Adjournment of the business of the House

**(b)** What is Consolidated Fund of India?

**(c)** To what extent can the President withhold his assent to a Bill already passed by the Parliament?

**(d)** What is India's 'Look East' Policy? *(International Relations — not polity)*

**(e)** What is meant by 'empowerment of women' in India? *(Social justice — not polity)*

`Parliamentary procedure` `Article 266` `Article 111` `Pocket veto`

> Q9(c) is the pocket-veto question; "Pocket Veto" appears again as a 20-word item in **2009** (Q10i).
