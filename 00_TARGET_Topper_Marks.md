## ⚠️ Read this first — the exam changed shape twice

You cannot compare a 2005 score to a 2025 score. The maximum marks changed three times.
Everything in this file is therefore also given as a **percentage**, which *is* comparable.

| Era | Years | Prelims (max) | Mains counted | Interview | **Final total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Era 1** — Optional in Prelims | **2000–2010** | GS 150 + Optional 300 = **450** | Essay 200 + GS×2 600 + **two** optionals 1200 = **2000** | **300** | **2300** |
| **Era 2** — CSAT introduced | **2011–2012** | GS 200 + CSAT 200 = **400** *(both counted)* | **2000** (unchanged) | **300** | **2300** |
| **Era 3a** — Mains restructured | **2013–2014** | **400** *(both counted)* | Essay 250 + GS×4 1000 + **one** optional 500 = **1750** | **275** | **2025** |
| **Era 3b** — CSAT made qualifying | **2015 → today** | GS-I **200** counted; CSAT 200 **qualifying @ 33%** | **1750** | **275** | **2025** |

**What this means for you:** only **Era 3b (2015 onwards)** is your exam. Data before 2015 is
history, not a target. I've included it because you asked for 2000-onward, but weight it accordingly.

## 1. PRELIMS — and the honest answer about topper scores

**There is no public dataset of toppers' Prelims marks, and this is not a gap in my research.**

- UPSC releases Prelims marks **only to the individual candidate**, through a login on upsc.gov.in,
  after the final result, for a limited window. There is no public per-candidate Prelims list.
- Even if there were: **Prelims marks are discarded.** Score 190/200 or 90/200 — once you qualify,
  it contributes **zero** to your rank. Rank = Mains (1750) + Interview (275) only.
- So the only meaningful Prelims benchmark is **the cut-off**, below. Any "topper scored X in
  prelims" figure you see online is either self-reported by that candidate or invented.

## 4. What the 11 years actually tell you

| Paper | 11-yr AIR-1 avg | % of max | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Optional II | 159 | **64%** | 🟢 highest-scoring paper on the exam |
| Optional I | 152 | **61%** | 🟢 second highest |
| Essay | 131 | 52% | 🟡 huge variance: 100 → 155 |
| GS IV (Ethics) | 123 | 49% | 🟢 best GS paper, most learnable |
| GS II (Polity/Gov/IR) | 118 | 47% | 🟡 best of the three factual GS papers |
| GS I | 115 | 46% | 🔴 hard to score |
| GS III | **105** | **42%** | 🔴 **weakest paper, every single year** |

**Five things worth acting on:**

1. **Optional = 500 marks and the biggest lever in the exam.** The two optional papers alone give an
   AIR-1 **311 of their 902** written marks — at 61–64% efficiency, far above any GS paper.
   ✅ **Optional is chosen: Public Administration** (Paper I Administrative Theory, Paper II Indian
   Administration). ⚠️ **But there is still no `Public Administration/` folder in `Mission/`.** That is
   now the single biggest structural gap in this repo — 500 marks with zero infrastructure, while
   Polity and Ethics (its two heaviest overlaps) are already built. **Scaffolding it is the
   highest-value thing you can do next.** For context on the field: the 2025 top-10 spread was Medical
   Science, Commerce, Anthropology, Economics, Sociology — no single "best" optional exists, so the
   Pub Ad choice is sound; what matters now is starting it.
2. **GS III is nobody's paper.** Even AIR-1s average 105/250. Don't panic at low mocks. But 115+ in
   GS III is a genuine rank-mover *precisely because* it's rare.
3. **Interview is the highest-efficiency component of all — toppers take ~68% of it.**
   The last four AIR-1s scored 193, 200, 200, **204**. But it is volatile rather than steadily
   inflating: Gaurav Agrawal scored **206 back in 2013**, and Pradeep Singh only 158 in 2019.
   A 45-mark swing between toppers, on 275 marks, decides ranks. Do not treat it as an afterthought.
4. **Essay has gone the other way.** 155 (2017) → 100 (2024) → 108 (2025). Toppers now clear
   *despite* mediocre essays. Still 250 marks; a reliable 130 is cheap insurance.
5. **GS II is your current subject.** Polity + Governance + IR, exactly what you're building in
   `Polity/`. AIR-1s average 118 here — the best of the three factual GS papers.

## 7. Data provenance & corrections applied

**What is official (UPSC-published):**
- Cut-offs for all three stages, 2005 onwards.
- Per-candidate **written total, interview marks, grand total** for recommended candidates,
  released a few weeks after each final result.
- Per-candidate **Prelims marks** — but only to that candidate, via login, for a limited window.
  Never published as a public list.

**What is NOT official:**
- **Paper-wise splits** (the Essay/GS/Optional columns in §3). These come from toppers' own
  marksheets, shared voluntarily and republished by coaching sites.
  **Validation I ran:** for all 11 rows, the 7 paper marks sum to exactly the official written
  total, and written + interview equals the official grand total. Every row passes. That is a strong
  check that these figures are genuine and not reconstructed.
- Any "topper's Prelims score" — treat as self-reported or invented.
- Any pre-2013 topper marksheet — treat as fabricated.

**Corrections applied while building this:**
1. StudyIQ's cut-off table has **2023 and 2024 Prelims shifted by one year**. Correct pairing,
   confirmed across three sources: **75.41 = CSE 2023** (record low), **87.98 = CSE 2024**.
2. IASbaba's 2011/2013 page renders cut-offs as images; the transcription returned 2013 Prelims as
   "198/250" and duplicated the 2011 row. Both discarded — ClearIAS's full series was used instead
   (2013 Prelims = **241/400**).
3. CSE 2014 final cut-off is reported as both 889 and 899. **889** is correct.
4. Several sources describe the pre-2011 total as 2750 or the modern total as 2225 — both are
   arithmetic errors. Prelims marks are never added to any total. Correct: **2300** (Era 1–2),
   **2025** (Era 3).
5. The 2013/2014 written-vs-interview splits were verified separately rather than inferred from the
   totals: Gaurav Agrawal **769 + 206**, Ira Singhal **920 + 162**. Worth noting because both are
   outliers — Gaurav's 206 is the highest AIR-1 interview score in this entire dataset despite the
   lowest written total (769), and Ira cleared with only 162 in the interview.
