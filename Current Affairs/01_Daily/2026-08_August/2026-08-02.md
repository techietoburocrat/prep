### 3. 🏛️ **Sarnath inscribed as India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage Site** — *event 25 July, in the weekend roundup*

Inscribed at the **48th session of the World Heritage Committee, Busan, Republic of Korea** (19–29 July 2026).

| | |
|---|---|
| Site | **Ancient Buddhist Site, Sarnath** — Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh |
| India's tally | **45th** — **6th in the world**, **2nd in Asia-Pacific** |

> **Why it's asked:** this is close to a guaranteed question. Sarnath is where the **Buddha delivered his first sermon** — the *Dhammacakkappavattana*, "turning of the wheel of law". That single fact links Prelims Culture, Ancient History and the Buddhism syllabus at once. Know **Dhamek** vs **Dharmarajika** vs **Chaukhandi**, and that Chaukhandi marks where the Buddha met his first five disciples.

### 4. **India becomes a Party to the WTO Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies** — *event 20 July*

India deposited its **Instrument of Acceptance** with the WTO. Handed over by **Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal** to the WTO Director-General.

> **Why it's asked:** the **Agreement on Fisheries Subsidies** is the WTO's **first** agreement with **environmental sustainability at its core**, and only the second multilateral agreement concluded since the WTO's founding. It curbs subsidies for **IUU fishing** (illegal, unreported, unregulated) and for fishing overfished stocks. India had long resisted it, arguing for protection of small-scale artisanal fishers and **special and differential treatment** — so *India accepting it* is the newsworthy turn. Note the mechanism: acceptance is by **depositing an instrument**, and the agreement enters into force when two-thirds of members do so.

**Theme:** IR / Economy · **Static hook:** WTO agreements, fisheries, blue economy · **Source:** [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286763&reg=48&lang=2)

### 5. **India's first sovereign-backed P&I insurance**

Launched by the **Department of Financial Services** under the **Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool** framework, through **New India Assurance (NIACL)**.

- **Protection & Indemnity** = third-party liability cover for shipowners
- Cover up to **USD 1.5 billion**
- Purpose: cut dependence on foreign P&I clubs, which is a sanctions-exposure vulnerability

> **Why it's asked:** "**first sovereign-backed**" is the phrase to remember. P&I cover is a genuine strategic chokepoint — the global market is dominated by a handful of International Group clubs, and losing access can strand a fleet. Connects to **maritime security**, **Maritime India Vision**, and self-reliance.

**Theme:** Economy / Security · **Static hook:** Insurance regulation (IRDAI), maritime policy · **Source:** [AffairsCloud](https://affairscloud.com/current-affairs-2-3-august-2026/) ⚠️

Took charge **31 July**. **First woman to lead GSI in its 176-year history.** Geoscientist with 30+ years' experience; specialisms **palaeontology and palynology**. Succeeds Asit Saha.

> **Why it's asked:** GSI basics travel with this — founded **1851**, HQ **Kolkata**, under the **Ministry of Mines**. ### 8. **Census 2027 — India's 16th Census**

Being conducted in **two phases across 2026 and 2027**, after postponement from 2021 due to COVID-19.

> **Why it's asked:** this is quietly one of the most consequential items of the decade, because **the delimitation freeze expires with "the first census taken after 2026"** — see your [Issue of the Week](_Week_01_Review.md). It is also the **first digital census** and the first to include **caste enumeration**. Know: Census is a **Union List** subject (Entry 69), conducted under the **Census Act, 1948**, by the **Registrar General & Census Commissioner** under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**. Two phases = **House Listing** then **Population Enumeration**.

**Theme:** Polity / Social Issues · **Static hook:** Laxmikanth — Union List, delimitation (Art. 82) · **Source:** [search roundup](https://www.thetargetclasses.com/gk/current-affairs-2-august-2026/) ⚠️
