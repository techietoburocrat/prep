## 🔴 Tier 1

### **16th India–Japan Annual Summit** — New Delhi, 1–3 July 2026

Japanese PM **Sanae Takaichi** visited India. **16th** summit, held **in India**.

**Outcomes** *(skip the itinerary, keep these)*:
- **UNICORN** (Unified Complex Radio Antenna) — agreement in principle; **the first India–Japan defence co-development programme**
- **Joint Statement on Cooperation in AI** — research, compute infrastructure, AI governance, talent
- **Joint Declaration on Economic Security Cooperation** — semiconductors, critical minerals, ICT, clean energy, pharmaceuticals
- Semiconductor manufacturing partnership and supply-chain resilience

> **Why it's asked:** summit **number** and **venue**; and "first defence co-development with Japan" is the memorable hook.

**Source:** [MEA](https://www.mea.gov.in/press-releases?dtl%2F41361%2F16th_India_Japan_Annual_Summit_13_July_2026_New_Delhi=) · [PMO](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/16th-india-japan-annual-summit-joint-statement/)

### **India–Indonesia Summit** — 8 July 2026

Held at **Prambanan Temple, Yogyakarta**. Outcomes: **BrahMos** cooperation, air-to-air missiles, joint exercises, **Maritime Safety & Security MoU** renewed, summits institutionalised.

> **Why it's asked:** **Prambanan is a UNESCO World Heritage Site — a 9th-century Hindu (Shiva) temple compound in Java.** This is an IR item *and* an Art & Culture item. UPSC likes the Indic-heritage-abroad angle: pair it with **Angkor Wat** (Cambodia) and **Borobudur** (Buddhist, also Java).

**Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/07/08/india-indonesia-bilateral-relations/)

### **Constitution (130th Amendment) Bill** — still with a Joint Committee

Removal of PM / CMs / Ministers **detained 30 consecutive days** on charges carrying **5+ years**. Introduced **20 Aug 2025**.

**Mechanism:** must resign **by the 31st day**; if not, ceases to hold office the next day.

> **Why it's asked:** contrast the three routes to losing ministerial office — this bill's detention rule, the **anti-defection** law (10th Schedule), and **Art. 75(2)** "pleasure of the President". ⚠️

**Source:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Hundred_and_Thirtieth_Amendment_of_the_Constitution_of_India)
