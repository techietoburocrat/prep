### 1. **Samudra Manthan** — National Offshore Exploration Scheme

The Cabinet's biggest decision of the week. **₹84,084 crore**, Ministry of **Petroleum & Natural Gas**, **Central Sector** scheme, FY 2026-27 → 2030-31.

| Component | Outlay |
|---|---|
| Deepwater drilling subsidy | ₹43,200 cr *(up to 50% of drilling cost, max ~₹650–675 cr/well)* |
| Seismic data acquisition | ₹28,534 cr |
| Common evacuation hubs | ₹10,000 cr |
| Oil & Gas Manufacturing/Services Zone | ₹2,000 cr |

**Target:** add **600+ MMTOE** reserves. Traces to the PM's Independence Day 2025 Red Fort speech.

> **Why it's asked:** ministry + **Central Sector vs Centrally Sponsored** + the outlay. The name is evocative so the *scheme* will be remembered; the *ministry* is what gets missed. Also note **MMTOE** = million metric tonnes of oil equivalent.

**Theme:** Economy / Energy · **Static hook:** Energy security, hydrocarbon exploration (OALP/HELP regime) · **Source:** [PMO](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/cabinet-approves-samudra-manthan-national-offshore-exploration-scheme-with-an-outlay-of-rs-84084-crore/)

### 2. **PM Surya Sarovar Yojana (PM-SSY)** — floating solar

**₹5,070 crore** · Ministry of **New & Renewable Energy** · implementing agency **SECI** (Solar Energy Corporation of India) · FY 2026-27 → 2030-31.

- **5,000 MW** floating solar PV (FSPV) on reservoirs and inland water bodies
- **Mandatory storage: minimum 2-hour duration**, 10,000 MWh total
- Central Financial Assistance **₹1 crore/MW**, post-commissioning
- De-risking grants up to **₹50 lakh/project** for feasibility studies
- Current FSPV capacity ~**700 MW** → target **5,700 MW**
- ~**10 million tonnes** CO₂ avoided annually; 16,000–17,000 jobs

> **Why it's asked:** *why floating solar?* — no land acquisition, reduces reservoir evaporation, and water-cooling raises panel efficiency. That three-part rationale is the answer to any question here. **SECI as implementing agency** is the second-most-missed fact after the ministry.

**Theme:** Environment / Economy · **Static hook:** Renewable energy targets, NDCs · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 3. **Revamped Khelo India Scheme**

**₹36,441 crore** combined outlay — an **eight-fold increase** over the previous cycle. Ministry of **Youth Affairs & Sports**. FY 2026-27 → 2030-31. Originally launched **2016-17**, restructured 2017-18.

- Aligned to **Khelo Bharat Niti 2025** and **NEP 2020**
- New category: **Emerging Khelo India Athletes (E-KIAs)**
- Schools: **Khelo India Feeder Schools (KIFS)**, **Khelo India Utkrishta Vidyalayas (KIUV)**
- Infrastructure: **NCOEs** (National Centres of Excellence), **KISCEs** (State Centres), **STCs** (SAI Training Centres), Youth Sports Companies
- New body: **National Coach Accreditation Board (NCAB)**

> **Why it's asked:** the acronym soup *is* the question — "consider the following: which of these is a component of Khelo India?" Know **NCAB** as the new body and **E-KIA** as the new category.

**Theme:** Social Issues & Schemes · **Static hook:** Sports policy, NEP 2020 · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 4. **Western Ghats ESA — expert panel extended by one year**

MoEF&CC extended the **Sanjay Kumar expert panel** by a year to resolve the Centre–State deadlock on notifying Ecologically Sensitive Areas.

| | |
|---|---|
| States | **6** — Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu |
| Kasturirangan recommendation | **~37%** of the Ghats ≈ **56,800 sq km** |
| Legal basis | **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** |
| UNESCO World Heritage Sites in the Ghats | **39** |

**The two committees — learn this pair, it's the whole question:**

| | **Gadgil** (WGEEP) | **Kasturirangan** (HLWG) |
|---|---|---|
| Constituted / reported | 2010 / 2011 | 2012 / 2013 |
| ESA extent | **Entire** Western Ghats, zoned ESZ-1/2/3 | **~37%**, "natural landscape" only |
| Approach | Ecology-first, **gram sabha**-led governance | Development-accommodating, satellite-delineated |
| Fate | Rejected by states as too restrictive | **Basis of every draft notification since** |

> ⚠️ **Sources disagree on how many draft notifications there have been** (five, six, or seven since 2014). **Don't memorise the ordinal.** Memorise the fact that matters and is not disputed: the notification has been drafted repeatedly since 2014 and **never finalised**, because states won't accept it. That deadlock *is* the exam point.

> **Why it's asked:** Gadgil-vs-Kasturirangan is one of the most reliably repeated Environment comparisons in Prelims. The **39 UNESCO sites** and the **six states** are the factual bait.

**Theme:** Environment · **Static hook:** EPA 1986, Western Ghats geography · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/), [NewsOnAir](https://newsonair.gov.in/centre-issues-fresh-esa-draft-notification-for-western-ghats-across-six-states)

### 5. **Favara–UPI cross-border corridor** (India–Maldives)

The **Maldives Monetary Authority** launched a real-time corridor linking its **Favara** instant payment system to India's **UPI**.

- Institutions: **MMA + RBI + NPCI International Payments Ltd (NIPL)**
- Phase 1: **P2P transfers only** (MVR ↔ INR), permitted for **family maintenance and gifts**
- Phase 2: QR-based merchant payments
- Banks: Bank of Maldives, Maldives Islamic Bank
- **UPI now accepted in 9 countries:** Cambodia, Singapore, UAE, France, Mauritius, Nepal, Bhutan, Qatar, Sri Lanka

> **Why it's asked:** **NIPL** — not RBI, not NPCI itself — is the entity that does UPI internationalisation. That's the trap. Also: **France** is the surprise name on the list of nine, and surprise names are exactly what options are built from.

**Theme:** IR / Economy · **Static hook:** Digital Public Infrastructure, neighbourhood policy · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 6. **Supreme Court declines a blanket ban on pellet guns**

The Bench permitted use in **exceptional crowd-control circumstances** rather than banning outright.

- **12-bore pump-action shotguns**; less-lethal kinetic projectile weapons
- ~**500 pellets** per cartridge (metal, rubber or plastic)
- Introduced **2010**; first used by **J&K Police at Sopore, August 2010**; also **CRPF** and **Rapid Action Force**
- SOPs: rarest-of-rare use, graded escalation, aim **below the waist**, proportionality
- Constitutional anchor: **Article 21**

> **Why it's asked:** Art. 21 + proportionality doctrine. For Mains this is a clean **Liberty vs Order** question — one of the [three structural tensions](../../05_Mains_Material/02_The_Eight_Lenses.md#three-structural-tensions-that-generate-most-gs-answers).

**Theme:** Polity / Security · **Static hook:** Laxmikanth — Fundamental Rights, Art. 21 · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 7. **IN-SPACe — space object re-entry guidelines**

New rules for **planned spacecraft re-entry by private entities**:
- Prior approval required
- **Casualty risk assessment below 1 in 10,000**
- Third-party liability insurance compliance

> **Why it's asked:** **IN-SPACe** = Indian National Space Promotion and Authorisation Centre, the *regulator/authoriser* under **DoS** — distinct from **NSIL** (the commercial arm) and **ISRO** (the agency). Those three get swapped in options constantly. The **1-in-10,000** threshold is a quotable number.

**Theme:** S&T · **Static hook:** Space policy, private sector in space · **Source:** [AffairsCloud](https://affairscloud.com/current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 9. **RBI defers Basel III Pillar III disclosures to 1 April 2027**

Postponed by **six months**, aligned with the **Expected Credit Loss (ECL)** framework for capital charge requirements.

> **Why it's asked:** **Basel III's three pillars** — Pillar 1 minimum capital, Pillar 2 supervisory review, **Pillar 3 market discipline/disclosure**. Pillar 3 is the disclosure one; that mapping is the question. **ECL** = forward-looking provisioning, replacing the incurred-loss model.

### 11. **Broad Peak avalanche** — 📍 mapping

| | |
|---|---|
| Elevation | **8,051 m** — **12th-highest** in the world |
| Local name | **Falchan Kangri** (Balti) |
| Range | **Karakoram** — Gilgit-Baltistan (Pak) / Xinjiang (China) border |
| Position | **8 km SE of K2**, in the **Gasherbrum massif** |
| Named by | Sir Martin Conway, 1892 |
| First ascent | 8–9 June 1957, Austrian team, **without supplementary oxygen** |
| Glaciers | Godwin-Austen, **Baltoro** |
| Protected area | **Central Karakoram National Park** |

> **Why it's asked:** Karakoram peaks are recurring map questions. The bonus concept here is the **Karakoram Anomaly** — the fact that Karakoram glaciers are *stable or advancing* while most Himalayan glaciers retreat. That anomaly is far more likely to be asked than the mountain.

**Theme:** Environment / Geography · **Static hook:** Himalayan ranges, glaciology · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️

### 16. 📝 **The case for a humane education system** — *Mains*

After youth-led protests, experts pushed to replace high-stakes single-shot admission testing with multi-dimensional evaluation.

**The argument:** single-shot competitive exams → exploitative coaching ecosystem → severe psychological toll. Proposed: decentralised assessment, competency-based evaluation, **coaching industry regulation**, structured counselling **in local languages**. UGC oversight.

> **Deployable in:** GS-II education governance, GS-I society (aspiration and stress), GS-IV ethics. Pairs directly with **Item 20** (3,500 government schools with zero admissions, from 2 Aug) — private coaching expands as public schooling hollows out. **That link is the answer**, not either fact alone.

**Theme:** Social Issues · **Source:** [Insights](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/08/01/upsc-current-affairs-1-august-2026/) ⚠️
