**Column notes**
- **Published by** — the exact body. "UN" is not an answer; UNDP, UNEP, UNCTAD, FAO, WHO are
- **What it measures** — one line. Enough to tell apart two indices with near-identical names
- **India's rank / value** — with the **total number of countries** and the **year**. A rank without a denominator is unusable

> ⚠️ **Two traps that recur:**
> 1. Government reports attributed to the wrong ministry (Economic Survey ≠ Budget documents ≠ NITI reports)
> 2. Indices that sound governmental but are published by private/think-tank bodies — and the reverse

**Reports worth catching every year regardless of news:** Economic Survey, World Development Report, Human Development Report, State of World Population, Global Hunger Index, World Happiness Report, Emissions Gap Report, State of Forest Report, NFHS rounds, PLFS.
