**Column notes**
- **Type** — mammal · bird · reptile · amphibian · fish · insect · plant · fungus · marine
- **Habitat / range** — the state or ecosystem, and **whether it is endemic to India**. Endemism is asked directly
- Three separate classification systems, three separate columns — see the trap below

> ⚠️ **The trap that costs the most marks in this theme:**
> **IUCN Red List status** (Least Concern → Critically Endangered → Extinct) — a *global scientific assessment*
> **Wildlife Protection Act schedule** (I, II, III, IV) — *Indian legal protection*
> **CITES appendix** (I, II, III) — *international trade regulation*
>
> These three do **not** move together, and options are built precisely on the assumption that you think they do. A species can be Least Concern under IUCN and still Schedule I in India. Fill all three columns or leave the row out.

**Also capture:** newly discovered species (and *where* — India's biodiversity hotspots recur), species moved between schedules or appendices, conservation programmes and their target species, invasive species, and anything involving a migration corridor.
