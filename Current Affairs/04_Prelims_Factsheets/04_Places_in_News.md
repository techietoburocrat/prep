**Column notes**
- **Type** — city · river · lake · strait · mountain range · island · region · protected area · port
- **The locational fact** — *which river it's on, which countries it borders, which sea it opens into, what it lies between.* Never just "in Africa"

**Always capture:** straits/canals/gulfs in any conflict or shipping story (**which two water bodies, which countries either side**) · rivers in flood/dam/treaty news (**source, states crossed, where it drains**) · conflict zones (**the neighbours**) · islands (**whose, which ocean**) · volcanoes and epicentres (**which plate boundary**) · new protected areas, Ramsar and heritage sites (**which state**).

> 🔑 **Do this one thing:** keep an atlas open and *point at it* while writing the row. Ten seconds of pointing beats an hour of re-reading. Places learned by name and forgotten by location score zero.
