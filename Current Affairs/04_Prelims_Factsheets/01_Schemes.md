**Column notes**
- **Ministry** — the *implementing* ministry, not the one that announced it. Most-missed column on this sheet
- **Type** — Central Sector (100% Centre) vs Centrally Sponsored (shared with states). This distinction is asked directly
- **The distinguishing fact** — cash or kind? statutory backing? age/income cut-off? subsumed which older scheme?

> ⚠️ **The classic trap:** a scheme that *replaced or subsumed* an older one. UPSC asks the old name. Note it here — see BharatNet/NOFN above.
