## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

**Prelims** wants schemes as facts: name → ministry → beneficiary → year → cash or kind → centrally sponsored or central sector. Every scheme in the news goes straight to the [schemes factsheet](../04_Prelims_Factsheets/01_Schemes.md) as a row.

**Mains** wants this theme more than any other — health, education, poverty, gender, caste, tribals, elderly, disability, urbanisation, migration. This is where GS-I society and GS-II governance answers get their **examples and data**.

- **Schemes** → the factsheet row, always
- **Data releases** → NFHS, NSSO/PLFS, Census, SECC, UDISE, economic surveys of social sectors. *Numbers you can quote in an answer*
- **Judgments & laws** on social questions → also file under Polity if constitutional
- **Committees & commissions** → what they were asked, one headline recommendation

> ⚠️ **The trap:** ministry attribution. Schemes get asked as "consider the following: which ministry implements X?" — and the intuitive guess is wrong often enough to matter. Write the ministry every time.
