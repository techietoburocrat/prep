## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

More Prelims questions come from environment current affairs than from any other theme, and they are **almost purely factual** — which makes this the single best return on capture effort in the whole folder.

- **Species in news** → IUCN status, Wildlife Protection Act schedule, habitat, endemic-to-where. *Any* species that appears in the news is a live Prelims candidate → also add to the [species factsheet](../04_Prelims_Factsheets/05_Species_in_News.md)
- **Protected areas** → national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, tiger/elephant reserves, Ramsar sites. **Which state, which river, which range** — the map question
- **Conventions & bodies** → CBD, CITES, CMS, UNFCCC/COP, Ramsar, IPCC, UNEP, GEF. Who hosts, what it binds
- **Pollution & climate** → NCAP, CAQM, emission norms, carbon markets, NDCs, net-zero targets
- **Acts & rules** → EPA 1986, Forest Conservation Act, CRZ, EIA, Biological Diversity Act

> ⚠️ **The trap:** IUCN Red List category ≠ Wildlife Protection Act schedule ≠ CITES appendix. Three different systems, routinely mixed in options. Record all three when a species appears.
