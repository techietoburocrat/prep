## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

Almost never the political event itself. Almost always the **constitutional or statutory machinery** the event exposed:

- **Supreme Court judgments** → the article interpreted, the doctrine applied, the earlier case overruled
- **Bills and Acts** → which List, which ministry, what the Act *replaced*, key definitions
- **Constitutional & statutory bodies** → composition, appointment, tenure, removal, whether constitutional or statutory (this distinction is asked relentlessly)
- **Centre–State friction** → Governor's powers, Art. 356, GST Council, All-India Services
- **Governance** → RTI, e-governance, citizens' charters, Lokpal, transparency

> ⚠️ **The trap in this theme:** "statutory vs constitutional vs executive body" and "who appoints vs who removes." Every year. Whenever a body appears in the news, write down all four facts, not just the news.
