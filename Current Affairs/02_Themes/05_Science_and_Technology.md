## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

S&T in Prelims is **almost entirely current affairs** — there is very little static syllabus. What's asked:

- **Space** → mission name, launch vehicle, orbit, agency, what it carries, which body it targets. ISRO *and* NASA/ESA/CNSA
- **Defence** → missile name, range, type (cruise/ballistic), platform, indigenous content
- **Biotech & health** → gene editing, vaccines and platforms, diseases in news, vectors, WHO classifications
- **Digital & computing** → AI regulation, semiconductors, quantum, 5G/6G, data protection, UPI/DPI
- **Awards & institutions** → Nobel categories and *what for*, ISRO/DRDO/CSIR/ICMR labs

> ⚠️ **The trap:** you're a software engineer, so the digital/computing items will feel easy and you'll over-invest there. UPSC asks *shallow* questions about computing and *specific* ones about missiles, missions and biotech. Spend the time on the unfamiliar half.

**Depth rule:** know what it does, who made it, and one distinguishing feature. Never the engineering.
