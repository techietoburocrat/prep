## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

The thinnest theme for Prelims and a guaranteed one for Mains. Keep capture **low-volume and high-value** — this bucket should stay small.

- **Internal security** → Left-wing extremism, insurgency, terror financing (FATF, NIA, UAPA), border management, AFSPA
- **Forces & agencies** → CAPFs and which ministry, NIA/NSG/NDRF mandates, tri-service commands
- **Cyber security** → CERT-In, critical infrastructure, cyber crime frameworks
- **Disaster management** → DM Act 2005, NDMA/SDMA, Sendai Framework, actual disasters and their type/cause

**Disaster events themselves:** capture the *mechanism*, not the casualty count. A cloudburst, a GLOF, a cyclone's name and category, a landslide's geology — those are Prelims-askable. Death tolls are not.

> 🔑 A cyclone in the news is really a Geography question. A flood is really a river question. File the mechanism here **and** note the river/region — that's what gets asked.
