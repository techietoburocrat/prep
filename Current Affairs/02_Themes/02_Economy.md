## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

Not market movements. The **institutions, instruments and definitions** behind them:

- **RBI & monetary policy** → MPC composition, repo/reverse repo/SDF/MSF, CRR/SLR, OMO, inflation targeting band
- **Budget & fiscal** → FRBM, types of deficit, capital vs revenue, cess vs surcharge, Finance Commission
- **Banking** → NPAs, IBC, priority sector lending, payment banks vs small finance banks, deposit insurance
- **External sector** → CAD, BoP, FDI vs FPI, rupee convertibility, trade agreements
- **Agriculture** → MSP mechanism, PDS, APMC, crop insurance
- **Bodies & indices** → SEBI, IRDAI, NITI Aayog, IIP, WPI vs CPI, GDP base year

> ⚠️ **This theme is the strongest argument for starting the static Economy subject early.** Economy current affairs is nearly unreadable without the static base — you can capture rows now, but the "why it's asked" column will stay thin until the static pass happens. That's expected; don't let it stop the capture.
