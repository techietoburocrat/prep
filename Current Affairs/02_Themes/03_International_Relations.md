## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

Prelims wants the **grouping**, not the diplomacy:

- **Organisations & groupings** → members, HQ city, founding year, who is *not* a member (the classic option)
- **Agreements & conventions** → what it binds signatories to, whether India has ratified
- **Joint exercises** → which countries, which service (naval/air/army). Frequently asked, easily cracked
- **Neighbourhood** → treaties, river disputes, border agreements, corridors and their end points
- **Global institutions** → UN bodies, IMF/World Bank instruments, WTO agreements

Mains wants the **argument**: India's stake, the constraint, the trade-off.

> ⚠️ **The trap:** groupings that sound similar — BIMSTEC vs IORA vs BBIN vs MGC. Whenever one appears, write its full membership once, and note the country that's conspicuously absent.

**Rule for state visits:** skip the itinerary, keep the outcomes. Agreements signed, MoUs, corridors named, exercises announced.
