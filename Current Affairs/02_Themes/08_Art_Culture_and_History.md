## What UPSC actually pulls from this theme

This theme is **pure Prelims profit** and almost universally under-captured, because it looks like soft news. It is not.

- **UNESCO** → World Heritage Sites inscribed (India *and* notable global), Intangible Cultural Heritage, tentative list
- **GI tags** → product + state. Asked directly, and easy marks
- **Excavations & archaeology** → site, state, period, ASI findings
- **Dance/music/craft forms** in the news → classification, state of origin, distinguishing feature
- **Anniversaries & personalities** → a freedom fighter's or reformer's centenary is UPSC telling you in advance what it will ask. Their movement, journal, organisation, associated act
- **Festivals, monuments, restorations** → dynasty, architectural style, patron

> 🔑 **The single highest-yield habit in this theme:** when a personality or site is in the news for an anniversary, immediately open the matching chapter in [History/](../../History/). The static and the current affairs are the *same question* here — more so than anywhere else in the syllabus.
