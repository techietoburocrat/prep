# 📅 THE 40-WEEK DAILY ROTATION

**15 minutes a day. One item per day. Weighted by PYQ frequency, with revision built in.**

[Index](00_INDEX.md) · [🇮🇳 India](01_India.md) · [🌍 World](02_World.md)

---

## HOW THIS IS SEQUENCED

| Principle | Why |
|---|---|
| **India first, world second** | Indian rivers (46), Indian physical (42) and Indian agriculture (44) outweigh world physical (32) in the [Prelims data](../03_PYQ_Prelims/00_INDEX.md) |
| **Political geography early and often** | **44 Prelims questions, and it is pure recall that decays fast** |
| **Every 5th week is REVISION** | Blank-draw only, no atlas until you're stuck |
| **Day 7 of every week = CURRENT AFFAIRS map** | Whatever place was in the news that week — *the highest-sticking item of the seven* |
| **Anything you got wrong re-enters the queue** | Mark it ⚠️ in your log; it comes back in the next revision week |

⚠️ **If you miss a day, do NOT double up. Just carry on. The habit surviving matters more than the schedule being met — a doubled-up session breaks the 15-minute rule that makes this sustainable.**

## 🟥 BLOCK 5 · WEEKS 35–40 — EXAM-PROXIMITY DRILL

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| **35–36** | **Speed drills.** Blank India in 10 min; blank world in 10 min. *Accuracy over completeness* |
| **37–38** | **Your ⚠️ log ONLY.** Everything you ever flagged, until nothing is flagged |
| **39** | **The five ⭐ items:** states · neighbours · hotspots · biogeographic zones · ports. *Daily, all week* |
| **40** | **Free revision + current-affairs places from the whole year** |

**Then loop back to Week 1.** *The rotation is designed to run more than once before the exam — the second pass takes half the time and fixes what the first missed.*

## 📋 THE LOG — copy this into a file and append one line a day

```
DATE        WK    ITEM                                  RESULT
2026-08-12  W1D1  Ganga system                          ✅
2026-08-13  W1D2  Indus system                          ⚠️ Ravi/Beas order wrong
2026-08-14  W1D3  Brahmaputra                           ✅
2026-08-15  W1D7  📰 Strait of Hormuz (news)            ✅
```

**Two rules for the log: one line, no prose · every ⚠️ goes back into the next revision week.** *That is the entire spaced-repetition system, and it costs ten seconds a day.*

---

## IF YOU ONLY HAVE 5 MINUTES

**Skip the blank-draw. Just find the item and trace it. A traced item beats a skipped day, every time.**

⚠️ **But do NOT let 5-minute days become the norm — the blank-draw in step 4 is where the learning happens; steps 1–3 are only preparation for it.**
