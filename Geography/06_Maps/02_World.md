## THE THREE-OVERLAY EXERCISE

> ⭐ **If you do only one world-map exercise, do this one. It takes three sessions and it makes the whole of physical geography visible at once.**
>
> **Session 1:** draw the **ocean currents** — warm red, cold blue.
> **Session 2:** on the same sheet, mark the **hot deserts.** *Watch every one land beside a cold current on a western margin.*
> **Session 3:** on the same sheet, mark the **great fisheries** and the **coral reefs.** *Watch the fisheries land on the upwellings and the reefs avoid the cold currents entirely.*
>
> **One sheet then answers: why deserts are where they are, why fisheries are where they are, why reefs are absent from Peru, and why NW Europe is mild.** *Four Mains questions and a dozen Prelims ones, on one piece of paper.*
