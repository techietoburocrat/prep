### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐
Discuss the concept of air mass and explain its role in macro-climatic changes.
`Air masses` `Fronts` `Source regions`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.10 — Atmospheric Circulation and Weather Systems](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/10_Atmospheric_Circulation_and_Weather_Systems.md)
> ⭐ **An air mass is a large body of air with uniform temperature and humidity, acquired from its SOURCE REGION.** ⭐ **Classified cP, cT, mP, mT. ⭐ Where two meet, you get a FRONT — and fronts are where temperate cyclones and most mid-latitude weather come from.** ⭐ *This is the concept behind 2024's twisters question.*

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐
In what way micro-watershed development projects help in water conservation in drought-prone and semi-arid regions of India?
`Watershed` `Ridge-to-valley` `Drought`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.6](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/06_Water_Resources.md) · [Cl.10 Ch.3](../01_NCERT/Class_10_Contemporary_India_II/Notes/03_Water_Resources.md)
> ⭐ **The principle: treat the whole catchment from ridge to valley — check dams, contour bunds, percolation tanks, afforestation — so runoff is slowed and recharged rather than lost.** ⭐ *Ralegan Siddhi and Hiware Bazar are the standard success cases.*

### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐
Present an account of the Indus Water Treaty and examine its ecological, economic and political implications in the context of changing bilateral relations.

🔗 [Cl.11 *India* Ch.3 — Drainage System](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/03_Drainage_System.md) · 🗺️ [06_Maps](../06_Maps/)
