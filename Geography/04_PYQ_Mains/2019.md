Discuss the causes of depletion of mangroves and explain their importance in maintaining coastal ecology.

### Q · 10m · 150w
What is water stress? How and why does it differ regionally in India?
`Water stress` `Per capita availability`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.6](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/06_Water_Resources.md)
> **Definition matters: water stress is measured per capita (<1,700 m³/yr stressed, <1,000 m³/yr scarce), not in absolute volume.** **Regional variation follows rainfall, aquifer geology (hard-rock peninsular vs alluvial north), and cropping pattern.**

### Q · 15m · 250w 🔁

🔗 [Leong A11 §2–3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/11_Oceans_Currents_Tides_Salinity.md) · [Cl.11 Ch.14](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/14_Movements_of_Ocean_Water.md)

### Q · 15m · 250w 🌱
Assess the impact of global warming on the coral life system with examples.
`Coral bleaching` `Zooxanthellae` `Acidification`
🔗 [Leong A10 §5](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/10_Islands_and_Coral_Reefs.md) · [Shankar D13 §3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_D_Climate_Change/13_Ozone_Acid_Rain_and_Global_Issues.md)
> **The precision that separates a strong answer: bleaching is the EXPULSION of zooxanthellae and is REVERSIBLE if the heat passes; and ACIDIFICATION is a SEPARATE problem from warming, driven by the same CO₂ but attacking calcification rather than the symbiosis.** **India's four reef regions: Gulf of Mannar, Gulf of Kachchh, Lakshadweep, Andaman & Nicobar.**
