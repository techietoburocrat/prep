### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐ 🌱 🔁
Why is the world today confronted with a crisis of availability of and access to freshwater resources?

`Water scarcity` `Access vs availability`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.6](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/06_Water_Resources.md) · [Shankar C10](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_C_Pollution/10_Water_and_Soil_Pollution.md)

> ⭐⭐ **The question deliberately says "availability AND access" — two different problems.** ⭐ **Availability is physical scarcity; access is distribution, infrastructure, affordability and governance.** ⭐ *A country can have water and still have thirsty people. Answer both halves.* 🔁 *Asked in near-identical form in 2015.*

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐
Why is the South-West Monsoon called 'Purvaiya' (easterly) in Bhojpur Region? How has this directional seasonal wind system influenced the cultural ethos of the region?

`Monsoon` `Bay of Bengal branch` `Culture`
🔗 [Cl.11 *India* Ch.4 — Climate](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/04_Climate.md)

> ⭐⭐ **The geography: the Bay of Bengal branch is deflected WESTWARD along the Ganga plain by the Himalaya, so it arrives in Bihar/eastern UP from the EAST — hence *purva* (east).** ⭐ *The cultural half — folk songs, Purvi music, agrarian calendar — is what most candidates miss. GS-I explicitly includes society.*

## 📊 What 2023 tells you

⭐ **5 of 7 are 15-markers — the opposite of 2024.** ⭐⭐ **And note how many questions have TWO explicit parts** (availability *and* access; resources *and* preparedness; vegetation *and* sanctuaries). ⭐ **A question with two clauses expects two clearly separated halves in the answer. Ignoring the second clause is the commonest way to lose marks in GS-I.**
