## 4️⃣ WHAT KIND OF QUESTION IT IS

| Question verb | Share | What it demands |
|---|---|---|
| **"Discuss / Explain"** | ~35% | Balanced exposition |
| **"Why...?" / "Account for..."** | **~25%** | **CAUSAL DERIVATION — the mechanism, not the description.** *This is the category [Leong](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/00_INDEX.md) was written for* |
| **"Critically examine / evaluate" · "How far do you agree"** | **~15%** | **QUALIFICATION. The examiner wants you to engage with the premise, not just agree** |
| **"What is X? Explain."** | ~15% | **Definitional precision — 2024 was full of these** |
| **"Compare / Differentiate / How do X and Y differ"** | **~10%** | **Two mechanisms side by side.** *Exactly what the [Climate Matrix](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/00_Comparison_Matrix.md) is built for* |

> ⭐ **The most important pattern in this table: a QUARTER of all questions are "why".** **They cannot be answered by recall. They are answered by walking a causal chain — which is precisely the method your [START_HERE §1](../00_START_HERE.md) laid out as the way to study this subject.** *The PYQs vindicate that decision.*

## 6️⃣ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOUR REMAINING HOURS

| Priority | Action |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **1** | **Write the nine repeat-theme answers in §3 properly, once each, and keep them.** *~20 of 88 questions covered by nine prepared answers is the best return available* |
| ⭐ **2** | **Drill the "why" questions.** Take every "Why…?" and "Account for…" in the corpus and answer it from the causal chain, not from memory |
| **3** | **Do NOT over-invest in landform taxonomy for Mains.** Karst, dunes, drumlins are Prelims content. Mains has never asked them |
| **4** | **Strengthen ECONOMIC geography** — industry location, resources, trade, straits. *~19 of 88, and the area candidates most neglect* |
| **5** | **Practise to the WORD LIMIT.** 150w and 250w. A good answer over the limit scores worse than a competent one within it |
| **6** | **Keep a running current-affairs list of geophysical events** — 2021 asked for named volcanic eruptions of that year, 2022 for IMD colour codes |
