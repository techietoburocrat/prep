### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐⭐ 🌱
What are the consequences of spreading of 'Dead Zones' on marine ecosystems?
`Dead zones` `Eutrophication` `Hypoxia`
🔗 [Shankar A4 §5](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_A_Ecology/04_Aquatic_and_Wetland_Ecosystems.md) · [Shankar C10](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_C_Pollution/10_Water_and_Soil_Pollution.md)
> ⭐⭐ **The mechanism must be exact: nutrient runoff → algal bloom → ⭐ the bloom DIES and its DECOMPOSITION consumes dissolved oxygen → hypoxia → fish kill.** ⭐ **Gulf of Mexico (Mississippi fertiliser); ⭐ the Arabian Sea's expanding oxygen minimum zone is the India link.**

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐ 🌱
Why is India taking keen interest in resources of Arctic region?
`Arctic policy` `Himadri` `NSR`
🔗 [Leong Climate 11 §8](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/11_Arctic_Tundra_and_Ice_Cap.md)
> ⭐⭐ **Four Indian stakes: ⭐ the Himadri research station (Ny-Ålesund, 2008) and Arctic Council observer status (2013) · ⭐ Arctic–monsoon teleconnections · ⭐ sea-level rise on a 7,500 km coastline · ⭐ hydrocarbons and the shipping economics of the Northern Sea Route.** ⭐ *India's Arctic Policy came in 2022 — after this question.*

### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐
Define mantle plume and explain its role in plate tectonics.
`Mantle plume` `Hotspot` `Volcanism`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.3](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/03_Interior_of_the_Earth.md) · [Ch.4](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/04_Distribution_of_Oceans_and_Continents.md) · [Leong A2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/02_Earth_Movements_and_Vulcanicity.md)
> ⭐⭐ **The killer example is Indian: ⭐ the ⭐ RÉUNION HOTSPOT is thought to have produced the ⭐ DECCAN TRAPS as the Indian plate drifted north over it — and the Chagos–Laccadive Ridge is the track it left behind.** ⭐ *Also Hawaii's island chain as the textbook hotspot track.*
