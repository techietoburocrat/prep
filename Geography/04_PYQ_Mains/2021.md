### Q · 15m · 250w
Briefly mention the alignment of major mountain ranges of the world and explain their impact on local weather conditions, with examples.

`Fold mountains` `Orographic rainfall` `Rain shadow`
🔗 [Leong A12 §2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/12_Weather_Elements_and_Local_Winds.md) · [Cl.11 Ch.11](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/11_Water_in_the_Atmosphere.md)

> **The alignment is the point: ranges running ACROSS the wind (Western Ghats, Himalaya) force rain; ranges running PARALLEL to it (the Aravallis vs the Arabian Sea branch) do not.** *That contrast is in [Leong Climate 2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) and it is the difference between a good and an average answer.*

### Q · 10m · 150w 🔁
Differentiate the causes of landslides in the Himalayan region and the Western Ghats.

`Landslides` `Disaster management`
🔗 [Leong A3 §2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/03_Weathering_and_Mass_Movement.md)

> 🔁 **ASKED IN NEARLY IDENTICAL WORDS IN 2014.** **The differentiator: Himalaya = young, tectonically active, seismic, fractured rock, ongoing uplift. Western Ghats = old, stable, non-seismic — landslides there are driven by intense rainfall on steep lateritic slopes, plus quarrying and construction.** *Tectonics vs rainfall is the axis.*

### Q · 15m · 250w 🌱
How do the melting of the Arctic ice and glaciers of the Antarctic differently affect the weather patterns and human activities on the Earth? Explain.

`Arctic amplification` `Sea ice vs land ice` `Albedo`
🔗 [Leong Climate 11](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/11_Arctic_Tundra_and_Ice_Cap.md) · [Shankar D12 §3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_D_Climate_Change/12_Greenhouse_Effect_and_Global_Warming.md)

> ⭐ **"Differently" is the whole question.** **Arctic = mostly SEA ice → melting does NOT raise sea level, but collapses albedo and drives Arctic amplification, jet-stream disruption, and opens the Northern Sea Route. Antarctic = LAND ice → melting DOES raise sea level, and threatens the AMOC/ocean circulation.** *Getting the sea-ice/land-ice distinction right is the answer.*

### Q · 15m · 250w
Despite India being one of the countries of the Gondwanaland, its mining industry contributes much less to its GDP in percentage. Discuss.

`Gondwana` `Mining` `Peninsular geology`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.7 — Mineral and Energy Resources](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/07_Mineral_and_Energy_Resources.md) · [Cl.11 *India* Ch.2](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/02_Structure_and_Physiography.md)

> **An unusually clever question: it asserts a geological ADVANTAGE and asks why the economic outcome doesn't match.** **Answer both halves — the geology (Gondwana coal, Dharwar mineral belts, the Chota Nagpur plateau) AND the economics (regulatory delays, land acquisition, forest clearance, low value-addition, illegal mining, poor infrastructure).**

### Q · 10m · 150w
Why is India considered as a sub-continent? Elaborate your answer.

`Physiography` `Plate tectonics`
🔗 [Cl.11 *India* Ch.1](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/01_India_Location.md) · [Ch.2](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/02_Structure_and_Physiography.md)

> **Distinct tectonic plate; walled off by the Himalaya, the Hindu Kush and the sea; self-contained drainage, climate (its own monsoon system) and biogeography; and a distinct cultural-historical region.** *Physical + human — GS-I likes both.*

## 📊 What 2021 tells you

**A strong year for CONTRASTIVE questions** — Himalaya vs Western Ghats, Arctic vs Antarctic. **That format is the examiner's favourite because it cannot be answered by recall alone; it needs you to know the *mechanism* on both sides.** *Which is exactly what the [Leong Climate Matrix](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/00_Comparison_Matrix.md) is built for.*
