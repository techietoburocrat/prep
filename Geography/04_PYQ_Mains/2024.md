## Climatology

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐
What is the phenomenon of 'cloudbursts'? Explain.

`Cloudburst` `Orographic lift` `Himalayan disasters`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.11 — Water in the Atmosphere](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/11_Water_in_the_Atmosphere.md) · [Leong A12 §2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/12_Weather_Elements_and_Local_Winds.md)

## Population / Demography

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐
What is the concept of a 'demographic winter'? Is the world moving towards such a situation?

`Demographic transition` `Fertility decline` `Ageing`
🔗 [Cl.12 *Fundamentals* Ch.2 — World Population](../01_NCERT/Class_12_Fundamentals_of_Human_Geography/Notes/02_The_World_Population.md) · [Ch.3 — Population Composition](../01_NCERT/Class_12_Fundamentals_of_Human_Geography/Notes/03_Population_Composition.md)

> ⭐ **"Demographic winter" = sustained below-replacement fertility (< 2.1) producing population decline and ageing.** ⭐ **Japan, South Korea, Italy, China. ⭐ And the India contrast — still in the dividend phase, but Kerala and Tamil Nadu are already below replacement.** *A question that rewards nuance over alarm.*

## Water Resources / Agriculture

### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐
The groundwater potential of the Gangetic valley is on a serious decline. How may it affect the food security of India?

`Groundwater depletion` `Food security` `Green Revolution belt`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.6 — Water Resources](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/06_Water_Resources.md) · [Leong Climate 2 §8](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md)

> ⭐⭐ **The strong answer names the incentive structure, not just the aquifer: ⭐ free power + MSP for paddy + a 200 cm-water crop grown where 60 cm falls.** ⭐ *Punjab–Haryana–western UP is the world's fastest depletion zone.*

## Astronomy / Earth–Sun System

### Q · 10m · 150w
What are aurora australis and aurora borealis? How are these triggered?

`Aurora` `Solar wind` `Magnetosphere`
🔗 [Leong A1](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/01_Earth_Universe_Motions_and_Time.md) *(adjacent)* · [Cl.11 Ch.8 — Atmosphere](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/08_Composition_and_Structure_of_Atmosphere.md)

> ⭐ **Charged solar-wind particles funnelled by the Earth's magnetic field into the polar upper atmosphere, exciting oxygen and nitrogen.** ⭐ **Borealis = north, Australis = south.** *A pure-recall question — either you know it or you don't.*

## Climatology

### Q · 10m · 150w
What is a twister? Why are the majority of twisters observed in areas around the Gulf of Mexico?

`Tornado` `Air masses` `Tornado Alley`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.10 — air masses & fronts](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/10_Atmospheric_Circulation_and_Weather_Systems.md)

> ⭐⭐ **The answer is an AIR-MASS answer: warm moist maritime tropical air from the Gulf meets cold dry continental polar air from Canada over flat terrain with no mountain barrier between them.** ⭐ **That collision, plus wind shear, spawns supercells.** *This is exactly the [China-type/Gulf-type](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/07_China_Type_Warm_Temperate_Eastern_Margin.md) region.*
