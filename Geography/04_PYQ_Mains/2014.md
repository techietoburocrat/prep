### Q · 15m · 250w
Why are the world's fold mountain systems located along the margins of continents? Bring out the association between the global distribution of fold mountains and earthquakes and volcanoes.
`Fold mountains` `Plate boundaries` `Ring of Fire`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.4](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/04_Distribution_of_Oceans_and_Continents.md) · [Leong A2 §3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/02_Earth_Movements_and_Vulcanicity.md)
> **One answer covers both halves: CONVERGENT PLATE MARGINS.** **Compression folds the sediment into mountains; subduction generates the earthquakes and the magma. Same boundary, three phenomena.**

### Q · 10m · 150w 🔁
**(a)** Bring out the causes for the more frequent occurrence of landslides in the Himalayas than in the Western Ghats. **(b)** There is no formation of deltas by rivers of the Western Ghats. Why?
`Landslides` `Deltas` `West coast`
🔗 [Leong A3 §2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/03_Weathering_and_Mass_Movement.md) · [Leong A4 §4](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/04_River_Landforms.md)

### Q · 10m · 150w
Why did the Green Revolution in India virtually bypass the eastern region despite fertile soil and good availability of water?
`Green Revolution` `Regional disparity`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.5](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/05_Land_Resources_and_Agriculture.md)
> **The question hands you the paradox — fertile soil AND water, yet it was bypassed.** **Answer with the non-physical constraints: tiny fragmented holdings and tenancy · absence of assured irrigation control (flooding, not scarcity, is the eastern problem) · weak credit, marketing and procurement infrastructure · the initial HYV focus on wheat rather than rice.**

### Q · 10m · 150w
Whereas the British planters had developed tea gardens all along the Shivaliks and Lesser Himalayas from Assam to Himachal Pradesh, in effect they did not succeed beyond the Darjeeling area. Explain.
`Tea` `Crop geography`
🔗 [Leong Climate 2 §7](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) · [Cl.12 *India* Ch.5](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/05_Land_Resources_and_Agriculture.md)
> **Tea's requirements are exacting: heavy well-distributed rainfall, high humidity, warm temperature, and WELL-DRAINED slopes (waterlogging kills tea).** **East of Darjeeling those conditions hold; west of it rainfall falls off, winters get colder and drier, and the monsoon weakens.** *A pure climate-and-crop question — exactly [Leong Climate 2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) material.*

### Q · 10m · 150w
Most of the unusual climatic happenings are explained as an outcome of the El-Nino effect. Do you agree?
`ENSO` `Attribution`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.10](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/10_Atmospheric_Circulation_and_Weather_Systems.md) · [Leong A11 §3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/11_Oceans_Currents_Tides_Salinity.md)
> **"Do you agree?" → qualify.** **ENSO is real and powerful — it explains Peruvian fishery collapse, Indian monsoon deficits, Australian drought. But it is ONE mode among several (IOD, MJO, NAO), it does not explain long-term warming trends, and over-attributing to it is lazy science.**

### Q · 10m · 150w
Tropical cyclones are largely confined to the south China Sea, Bay of Bengal, and the Gulf of Mexico. Why?
`Tropical cyclones` `Formation conditions`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.10](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/10_Atmospheric_Circulation_and_Weather_Systems.md) · [Leong Climate 7](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/07_China_Type_Warm_Temperate_Eastern_Margin.md)
> **The formation conditions ARE the answer: sea-surface temperature >26.5 °C to sufficient depth · latitude ~5–20° (Coriolis needed, but not at the equator where it is zero) · low vertical wind shear · pre-existing disturbance · high mid-tropospheric humidity.** **All three named basins are warm, shallow, semi-enclosed seas on WESTERN sides of oceans / eastern margins of continents.** *And note the corollary: the cold-current western margins of continents get none.*
