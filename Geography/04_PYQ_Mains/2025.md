## Geomorphology / Plate Tectonics

### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐ 🔁
Discuss how the changes in shape and sizes of continents and ocean basins of the planet take place due to tectonic movements of the crustal masses.

`Plate tectonics` `Continental drift` `Sea-floor spreading`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.4 — Distribution of Oceans and Continents](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/04_Distribution_of_Oceans_and_Continents.md) · [Leong A2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/02_Earth_Movements_and_Vulcanicity.md)

> 🔁 **Continental drift was asked in 2013 too.** The recurring core: evidence, mechanism, and the modern plate-tectonic synthesis.

## Economic Geography / Resources

### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐
Give a geographical explanation of the distribution of off-shore oil reserves of the world. How are they different from the on-shore occurrences of oil reserves?

`Mineral oil` `Continental shelf` `Sedimentary basins`
🔗 [Leong A11 §1 — ocean floor relief](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/11_Oceans_Currents_Tides_Salinity.md) · [Cl.12 *India* Ch.7](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/07_Mineral_and_Energy_Resources.md)

> ⭐ **The key link: offshore oil is on the CONTINENTAL SHELF, in sedimentary basins.** Bombay High, the Gulf of Mexico, the North Sea, the Persian Gulf.

`GIS` `Remote sensing` `AI` `Planning`

## Disaster Management 🌱

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐ 🔁
What are Tsunamis? How and where are they formed? What are their consequences? Explain with examples.

`Tsunami` `Subduction` `Disaster management`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.3 — Interior of the Earth](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/03_Interior_of_the_Earth.md) · [Leong A2 §5](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/02_Earth_Movements_and_Vulcanicity.md)
