### Q · 15m · 250w ⭐⭐
What characteristics can be assigned to monsoon climate that succeeds in feeding more than 50 percent of the world population residing in Monsoon Asia?
`Monsoon` `Intensive agriculture` `Population`
🔗 [Leong Climate 2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) · [Cl.11 *India* Ch.4](../01_NCERT/Class_11_India_Physical_Environment/Notes/04_Climate.md)
> ⭐⭐ **This question is [Leong Climate 2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) almost exactly: ⭐ heat + heavy seasonal rain + level alluvial land + abundant labour = ⭐ wet paddy rice, two or three crops a year, intensive subsistence agriculture, and the highest rural population densities on Earth.**

### Q · 10m · 150w
Mention the advantages of the cultivation of pulses because of which the year 2016 was declared as the International Year of Pulses by the United Nations.
`Pulses` `Nitrogen fixation` `Nutrition`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.5](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/05_Land_Resources_and_Agriculture.md) · [Shankar A2 §6](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_A_Ecology/02_Ecosystem_Energy_Flow_and_Cycles.md)
> ⭐ **The ecology angle scores: ⭐ legumes host Rhizobium and FIX ATMOSPHERIC NITROGEN, enriching soil and cutting fertiliser need — a direct link to the nitrogen cycle.** ⭐ *Plus protein security, low water need, drought tolerance, crop rotation.*

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐
Account for variations in oceanic salinity and discuss its multidimensional effects.
`Salinity` `Halocline` `Stratification`
🔗 [Leong A11 §4](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/11_Oceans_Currents_Tides_Salinity.md) · [Cl.11 Ch.13](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/13_Water_Oceans.md)
> ⭐⭐ **The "multidimensional effects" half is where marks are won: ⭐ salinity → density → stratification → ⭐ thermohaline circulation; and the India-specific chain — ⭐ the Bay of Bengal's low salinity floats a warm surface layer that resists mixing, which is one reason it generates more cyclones than the Arabian Sea.**

### Q · 10m · 150w ⭐ 🌱
How does the cryosphere affect global climate?
`Cryosphere` `Albedo` `Permafrost`
🔗 [Leong Climate 11 §2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/11_Arctic_Tundra_and_Ice_Cap.md) · [Leong Climate 9 §8](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/09_Siberian_Type_Taiga.md)
> ⭐⭐ **Three mechanisms: ⭐ ICE–ALBEDO FEEDBACK · ⭐ PERMAFROST carbon/methane release · ⭐ meltwater freshening the North Atlantic and threatening the AMOC.** ⭐ *All three are in your Leong notes, and together they make a complete answer.*

### Q · 10m · 150w
Petroleum refineries are not necessarily located nearer to crude oil producing areas, particularly in many of the developing countries. Explain its implications.
`Industrial location` `Refineries`
🔗 [Cl.12 *India* Ch.8](../01_NCERT/Class_12_India_People_and_Economy/Notes/08_Manufacturing_Industries.md)
> ⭐ **Crude is cheap to ship in bulk; refined products are not — so refineries follow the MARKET or the PORT (Jamnagar, Singapore, Rotterdam).**

### Q · 10m · 150w
How does the Juno Mission of NASA help to understand the origin and evolution of the Earth?
`Jupiter` `Planetary science`
🔗 [Cl.11 Ch.2 — Origin and Evolution of the Earth](../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/02_Origin_and_Evolution_of_the_Earth.md)
> ⚠️ ⭐ **A current-affairs-plus-astronomy question.** ⭐ *Jupiter formed early and retains primordial solar-nebula composition, so studying it constrains how the solar system — and Earth — formed.*

## 📊 What 2017 tells you

⭐ **6 of 8 are 10-markers.** ⭐⭐ **And notice how many are "explain the mechanism" questions rather than "discuss the policy" ones — salinity, cryosphere, monsoon, refinery location.** ⭐ **2017 rewarded the candidate who understood processes. That is precisely what [Leong Part A and Part B](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/00_INDEX.md) are for.**
