## ⚠️ A SOURCING NOTE — read before using

> **The [GS-I questions](00_INDEX.md) in this folder are VERBATIM, triangulated across three compilations.**
>
> ⚠️ **These GS-III questions are NOT.** **The available compilation renders them in COMPRESSED form** — *e.g. "Mineral resources exploit mining. Why is mining an environmental hazard?" is clearly a shortened rendering of a longer original.*
>
> **So: use these for TOPIC and TREND, which they convey accurately. Do NOT quote the wording as exact.** ⚠️ **Check any question you intend to answer in full against the official paper on [upsc.gov.in](https://upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers).**

## THE PATTERN — what GS-III actually wants

| Cluster | Share | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **DISASTER MANAGEMENT** | **~29%** | **The biggest single cluster, and it is a NAMED syllabus line. Recurring: Sendai Framework, urban floods, cloudbursts, landslides, earthquakes, drought, vulnerability/resilience frameworks** |
| **Sustainable development & resources** | **~25%** | Groundwater, freshwater tech, solar economics, river-linking, Jal Shakti, Green Grid |
| **Pollution** | ~18% | Air (NCAP, smog, AQGs), water (Ganga, industrial, oil), waste |
| **Climate change & conventions** | ~16% | Paris/NDCs, COP outcomes, Kyoto/CDM, CCUS, sea-level rise |
| **Biodiversity & protected areas** | ~7% | Wetlands, Ramsar, Biological Diversity Act |
| **EIA** | ~5% | *Small in count but explicitly named in the syllabus — and 2020's draft-EIA question was worth full marks to anyone who'd read it* |

> ⭐ **THE HEADLINE: disaster management is 29% of GS-III environment — the largest single cluster.**
>
> ✅ **This gap is now closed.** The mechanisms were always covered — [Leong A3](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/03_Weathering_and_Mass_Movement.md) for landslides, [A6](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/06_Glacial_Landforms.md) for GLOFs, [A2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/02_Earth_Movements_and_Vulcanicity.md) for earthquakes, [Climate 2](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/02_Tropical_Monsoon_and_Marine.md) for cyclones — but the **frameworks** were not.
>
> 👉 **They are now in [Shankar Part F](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Shankar_IAS_Environment/Part_F_Disaster_Management/20_Concepts_and_Frameworks.md):** the risk equation, vulnerability types, the DM cycle, the DM Act 2005 architecture, **Sendai vs Hyogo**, India's hazards one by one, and six case studies with reusable answer frames.
