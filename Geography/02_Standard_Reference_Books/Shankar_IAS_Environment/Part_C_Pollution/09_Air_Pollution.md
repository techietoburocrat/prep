# 🌫️ C9 — Air Pollution

> **The one-line point:** **Delhi's winter emissions are not much higher than its summer emissions. What changes is the atmosphere's capacity to disperse them — which is why air pollution is a meteorological problem as much as an industrial one.**

## 1️⃣ THE POLLUTANTS

### Primary vs secondary

**PRIMARY pollutants are emitted directly** (SO₂, NOₓ, CO, particulates, VOCs, lead). **SECONDARY pollutants form in the atmosphere by reaction between primaries** — **ground-level OZONE, PAN (peroxyacetyl nitrate), sulphates and nitrates, and photochemical smog.**

| Pollutant | Main sources | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **PM₂.₅** (≤2.5 µm) | **Combustion — vehicles, industry, biomass burning, crop residue; plus secondary formation** | ⭐ **The most damaging pollutant to health. Small enough to reach the ALVEOLI and enter the BLOODSTREAM.** Cardiovascular and respiratory disease, stroke, lung cancer, low birth weight |
| **PM₁₀** | Dust, construction, road dust, mining | Upper respiratory irritation |
| **SO₂** | **Coal combustion, smelters, refineries** | Respiratory; **precursor of ACID RAIN**; forms sulphate aerosols |
| **NOₓ (NO, NO₂)** | **Vehicles, power plants — any high-temperature combustion** | Respiratory; **precursor of both ACID RAIN and ground-level OZONE** |
| **CO** | **Incomplete combustion — vehicles, stoves** | **Binds haemoglobin ~200× more strongly than oxygen → carboxyhaemoglobin → tissue oxygen starvation** |
| **Ground-level OZONE (O₃)** | **SECONDARY — formed from NOₓ + VOCs in sunlight** | **Respiratory damage AND significant CROP YIELD LOSS.** ⚠️ *"Good up high, bad nearby" — the same molecule that protects us in the stratosphere is a pollutant at ground level* |
| **VOCs / benzene** | Fuel evaporation, solvents, paints | Carcinogenic; ozone precursor |
| **Lead** | **Historically petrol** — *India eliminated leaded petrol in 2000, a genuine success* | Neurotoxic, especially in children |
| **Ammonia (NH₃)** | **Agriculture — fertiliser and livestock** | **Forms secondary particulate; an under-recognised major PM₂.₅ precursor in India** |
| **Black carbon** | Diesel, biomass, brick kilns | **A short-lived climate pollutant; deposits on Himalayan snow and accelerates melt** *(see [Leong A6](../../GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/06_Glacial_Landforms.md))* |

### The two smogs — a clean Prelims distinction

| | **CLASSICAL / LONDON / SULPHUROUS SMOG** | **PHOTOCHEMICAL / LOS ANGELES SMOG** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cause** | **Coal burning — SO₂ + particulates + fog** | **NOₓ + VOCs + STRONG SUNLIGHT** |
| **Season** | **WINTER; cool, humid, calm** | **SUMMER; hot, sunny, dry** |
| **Chemistry** | **REDUCING** | **OXIDISING** |
| **Key products** | Sulphuric acid aerosol | **OZONE, PAN, aldehydes** |
| **Colour** | Grey/black | **Brown haze** |
| **Classic event** | **The GREAT LONDON SMOG, 1952 → the UK Clean Air Act 1956** | Los Angeles, Mexico City, and increasingly Indian summers |

> **Delhi's winter smog is largely of the classical type in its meteorology (inversion, calm, humid) but with a modern emissions mix — vehicles and crop burning rather than domestic coal.**

## 2️⃣ STANDARDS & MEASUREMENT

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **NAAQS** | **National Ambient Air Quality Standards — set by CPCB. The current version dates from 2009 and covers 12 pollutants** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *(PM₁₀, PM₂.₅, SO₂, NO₂, CO, O₃, NH₃, Pb, benzene, benzo(a)pyrene, arsenic, nickel)* |
| ⭐ **AQI** | **Air Quality Index — launched 2015 under "Swachh Bharat".** **Six categories: GOOD (0–50) · SATISFACTORY (51–100) · MODERATE (101–200) · POOR (201–300) · VERY POOR (301–400) · SEVERE (401–500).** **Computed from 8 pollutants; the AQI reported is that of the WORST-performing pollutant, not an average** 🎯 *That last detail is asked* |
| **Monitoring** | **NAMP (National Air Quality Monitoring Programme) · CAAQMS (continuous automatic stations) · SAFAR** |
| ⚠️ | **WHO guideline values are considerably STRICTER than India's NAAQS** — *so "meeting Indian standards" and "safe" are not the same statement* |

## 3️⃣ INDIA'S SOURCES — and the honest apportionment

| Source | Note |
|---|---|
| **Vehicles** | **Now regulated by BHARAT STAGE VI, adopted in 2020 — India LEAPFROGGED BS-V entirely, going straight from BS-IV to BS-VI.** *A genuinely notable regulatory decision* |
| **Industry & thermal power** | **Coal plants — SO₂, NOₓ, fly ash, mercury. FGD (flue-gas desulphurisation) retrofits have been repeatedly deadline-extended** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` |
| **Crop residue burning** | **Punjab, Haryana, western UP — concentrated in Oct–Nov, coinciding exactly with the onset of inversion season.** **Root cause: the very short window between paddy harvest and wheat sowing, itself worsened by groundwater-preservation laws that delayed paddy transplanting.** **Solutions: Happy Seeder, bio-decomposer, crop diversification, ex-situ use as biomass** 🎯 *An agricultural policy producing an air-quality crisis — an excellent cross-sectoral example* |
| **Construction & road dust** | A very large PM₁₀ share in Indian cities |
| **Waste burning** | Open burning of municipal and plastic waste |
| ⭐ **INDOOR / HOUSEHOLD air pollution** | **Biomass and dung burning for cooking. Historically among the largest environmental health burdens in India, falling overwhelmingly on WOMEN AND CHILDREN.** ** PMUY (Ujjwala) LPG scheme is the main response** ⚠️ *— with the known issue that refill uptake matters more than connections issued* |

> **The point worth making about apportionment: source shares vary by city, by season and by pollutant, and single-cause narratives ("it's all stubble burning" / "it's all vehicles") are wrong everywhere.** **Stubble burning is a large but SEASONAL contributor in the NCR; vehicles, dust and industry are the year-round base load.** 🎯 *Saying this correctly is worth a mark on its own.*

## 4️⃣ POLICY & INSTITUTIONS

| Instrument | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981** | **The parent statute; CPCB and SPCBs derive their air powers from it** — *see [E15](../Part_E_Governance/15_Indian_Environmental_Acts.md)* |
| ⭐ **NCAP** | **National Clean Air Programme, launched 2019.** **A time-bound reduction target for particulate concentrations across 130+ "non-attainment" cities** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *the current target and city count — both have been revised* |
| ⭐ **GRAP** | **Graded Response Action Plan — pre-agreed emergency measures for Delhi-NCR that trigger AUTOMATICALLY at defined AQI thresholds** (construction bans, truck entry restrictions, school closures, and at the worst stage a near-shutdown). *Its virtue is that it removes discretion and delay* |
| **CAQM** | **Commission for Air Quality Management in the NCR and Adjoining Areas — a statutory body (2021) with authority ACROSS state boundaries.** *This matters: air does not respect state borders, and the earlier failure was a coordination failure between Delhi, Punjab, Haryana, UP and Rajasthan* |
| **Others** | **BS-VI · leaded petrol phase-out (2000) · CNG conversion of Delhi's public transport (a Supreme Court-driven success) · FAME / EV push · smog towers** ⚠️ *(smog towers are widely regarded as ineffective theatre — worth saying so)* |

## 5️⃣ 🌱 HEALTH & EQUITY

**Air pollution is among the largest environmental risk factors for mortality in India** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *specific figures — the Global Burden of Disease and Lancet Commission estimates are the sources.*

| Dimension | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Health effects** | Respiratory disease, COPD, asthma, **cardiovascular disease and stroke**, lung cancer, **low birth weight and impaired childhood lung development**, *and growing evidence on cognitive effects* |
| **Equity** | **The poorest are most exposed and least able to avoid it — outdoor workers, informal settlements near industry and highways, and women cooking on biomass.** **Air purifiers and sealed homes are a private adaptation available only to those who can pay** 🎯 *A sharp environmental-justice line* |
| **Agriculture** | **Ground-level ozone measurably reduces wheat and rice yields; "atmospheric brown clouds" dim the surface and may weaken the monsoon** |
| **Climate link** | **Black carbon and methane are SHORT-LIVED CLIMATE POLLUTANTS — cutting them delivers BOTH air-quality and near-term climate benefits.** *One of the few genuine win-wins, and worth naming as such* |

## 📝 `[P]` / `[M]` / 🎯

### `[P]` PRELIMS
- **PRIMARY (emitted) vs SECONDARY (formed in air — ozone, PAN, photochemical smog)**
- ⭐ **PM₂.₅ reaches the alveoli and the bloodstream — the most health-damaging pollutant**
- **CO binds haemoglobin ~200× more strongly than O₂**
- **Ground-level ozone = NOₓ + VOCs + sunlight. "Good up high, bad nearby." Damages crops**
- **LONDON smog: winter, coal, SO₂, REDUCING. LOS ANGELES smog: summer, sunlight, NOₓ+VOC, OXIDISING, produces ozone & PAN**
- **NAAQS by CPCB (2009), 12 pollutants. AQI 2015, 6 categories, reports the WORST pollutant not the average**
- **AQI bands: Good 0–50 · Satisfactory 51–100 · Moderate 101–200 · Poor 201–300 · Very Poor 301–400 · Severe 401–500**
- **BS-VI adopted 2020 — India SKIPPED BS-V**
- **Leaded petrol eliminated 2000**
- **NCAP 2019 (non-attainment cities) · GRAP (automatic AQI triggers) · CAQM = statutory, 2021, cross-state NCR**
- **Ammonia from agriculture is a major PM₂.₅ precursor; black carbon = short-lived climate pollutant**

### `[M]` MAINS
- ⭐ **Why winter, not why polluted** — the inversion/boundary-layer explanation. *Almost no answer includes it*
- ⭐ **Stubble burning as an agricultural-policy artefact** — the harvest window, groundwater law, and why the fix is agronomic not punitive
- **CAQM and the airshed principle** — pollution is regional, governance was state-wise
- **Air pollution as an equity issue** — exposure and the ability to buy avoidance
- **Short-lived climate pollutants as a genuine win-win**
- **Source apportionment is seasonal and city-specific** — beware single-cause narratives
