# Why this folder is empty — and what to use instead

**Shankar IAS *Environment* is in print and copyrighted.** No free official PDF exists; the copies online are scanned pirate uploads, so nothing was downloaded here.

**The notes are complete without it** — [21 files, 3,510 lines, plus Part F on disaster management](../00_INDEX.md). ⚠️ Unlike the Leong notes, **these are full notes rather than a delta**, because most of Shankar (Acts, conventions, protected-area categories, pollution, EIA, institutions) appears nowhere else in the Geography material.

**Where the book genuinely can't help you anyway:** every volatile figure — forest cover, tiger/Ramsar/protected-area counts, COP numbers, IUCN statuses, NDC targets — **expires**, and any printed edition is stale the year it ships. Those are tagged ⚠️`[VERIFY]` throughout, with a **primary-source register in [`00_INDEX.md`](../00_INDEX.md)** pointing at FSI, NTCA, Ramsar and UNFCCC. **For those facts the official source beats the book, in any format.**

Buy a physical copy if you want it at hand for revision. It is not blocking anything.
