## 📌 Read this first — ⭐ this folder works DIFFERENTLY from the Leong folder

⭐ **The [G.C. Leong notes](../GC_Leong/00_INDEX.md) are a DELTA.** They deliberately skip whatever your NCERT notes already cover, because Leong and NCERT teach the same syllabus at two depths.

> ⭐⭐ **Shankar IAS is NOT a delta book. Most of it does not exist anywhere else in your notes.**
>
> ⭐ **Acts · conventions · protected-area categories · species projects · pollution standards · EIA procedure · institutions — NCERT contains almost none of this, and Leong contains none of it at all.**
>
> ⭐ **So these are FULL notes, not increments.** The exception is [Part A](Part_A_Ecology/), where NCERT genuinely overlaps — and that part is cross-linked rather than duplicated.

### ⭐⭐ Why this book matters more than its page count suggests

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prelims** | ⭐⭐⭐ **Environment is ~10–22 Qs/year.** ⭐ **And they are the CHEAPEST marks in the paper** — factual, recall-based, and drawn from a finite, learnable list of Acts, conventions, species and protected areas |
| **Mains GS-III** | ⭐⭐ *"Conservation, environmental pollution and degradation, environmental impact assessment"* — **an explicit syllabus line** |
| **Mains GS-II** | ⭐ International conventions, climate diplomacy, India's negotiating positions |
| **Essay** | ⭐ Climate, sustainability and development-vs-environment are perennial |
| **Current Affairs** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The single most CA-linked subject you have.** COPs, ISFR, tiger census, new Ramsar sites, NGT rulings — all live |
| ⭐ **Effort profile** | ⭐⭐ **Low conceptual difficulty, HIGH factual density.** ⭐ *The opposite of physical geography. This book is not hard to understand — it is hard to retain* |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The strategic point, stated bluntly: this is the most efficient block in your entire Prelims preparation.** ⭐ **Physical geography needs understanding, which takes time. ⭐ Environment needs organised recall, which takes repetition.** ⭐ **And repetition is cheap if the material is organised well — which is what [00_QUICK_REVISION_TABLES.md](00_QUICK_REVISION_TABLES.md) is for.**

## ⚠️⚠️ THE MOST IMPORTANT WARNING IN THIS FOLDER — VOLATILE FIGURES

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐ **Environment is the ONLY subject in your prep where the FACTS THEMSELVES EXPIRE.**
>
> ⭐ **The number of tiger reserves, Ramsar sites, national parks; the ISFR forest-cover percentage; the latest census figures; the current COP number — all of these CHANGE, some of them every year.**
>
> ⭐⭐ **Every such figure in this folder is marked** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` **and carries the vintage I wrote it at.** ⭐ **Treat those as ORIENTATION, never as exam-ready facts.**
>
> ⭐⭐ **Before Prelims 2027 you must refresh the whole volatile set from primary sources — and the [Current Affairs folder](../../../Current%20Affairs/00_START_HERE.md) is where that refresh should be happening month by month, not in a panic in April.**

### ⚠️ The volatile register — check every one of these before quoting

| Figure | ⭐ Primary source to check |
|---|---|
| ⭐ Forest & tree cover %, mangrove cover | ⭐ **India State of Forest Report (ISFR), FSI — biennial** |
| ⭐ Tiger numbers & number of tiger reserves | ⭐ **NTCA / All-India Tiger Estimation — quadrennial** |
| ⭐ Elephant, lion, rhino, leopard, snow leopard numbers | ⭐ **MoEFCC / respective census reports** |
| ⭐ Number of Ramsar sites | ⭐ **Ramsar Secretariat / MoEFCC** — *India has been adding these rapidly* |
| ⭐ Numbers of National Parks, Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, Conservation & Community Reserves | ⭐ **ENVIS / Wildlife Institute of India — National Wildlife Database** |
| ⭐ Biosphere Reserves on the UNESCO MAB list | ⭐ **UNESCO MAB** |
| ⭐ Number of UNESCO World Heritage Sites (natural) | ⭐ **UNESCO** |
| ⭐ Current COP number & outcomes (UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD, Ramsar) | ⭐ **The convention secretariats** |
| ⭐ India's NDC targets and progress | ⭐ **UNFCCC NDC registry / MoEFCC** |
| ⭐ IUCN Red List status of individual species | ⭐ **iucnredlist.org** — *statuses are revised continuously* |
| ⭐ NCAP city list & AQI standards | ⭐ **CPCB** |
| ⭐ Installed renewable capacity | ⭐ **MNRE / CEA** |

⭐ **Where a figure is stable — a convention's year of signing, an Act's provisions, the definition of a category — it is NOT marked, and you can rely on it.**

## 🪜 How to read this folder

1. ⭐⭐ **Read [Part E](Part_E_Governance/) EARLY, not last.** ⭐ **It is counter-intuitive but correct: the Acts and conventions are the densest Prelims block, they are pure recall, and recall needs the LONGEST runway of spaced repetition.** ⭐ **Start them now and revisit monthly.** *Conceptual material can be learned later and still stick; lists cannot.*
2. ⭐ **Then Part A → B → C → D** in order. Part A is the foundation the rest assumes.
3. ⭐⭐ **Build the [Quick Revision Tables](00_QUICK_REVISION_TABLES.md) into your weekly cycle from day one.** They are the deliverable; the chapters are the scaffolding.
4. ⭐⭐ **Wire this folder to [Current Affairs](../../../Current%20Affairs/00_START_HERE.md).** ⭐ **Every week's environment news should be filed against a file here** — a new Ramsar site updates [B7](Part_B_Biodiversity/07_Protected_Area_Network.md), a COP outcome updates [E17](Part_E_Governance/17_International_Conventions.md). ⭐ *That is how the volatile figures stay fresh without a separate revision project.*
5. ⭐ **Then [Stage 8 — PYQs](../../00_START_HERE.md).** Environment PYQs are unusually repetitive — the same conventions and species recur — so solving them early tells you exactly which tables to drill.
