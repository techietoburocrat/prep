## 2️⃣ ⭐ INDIAN ACTS — year · what it did

| Year | Act | The one thing to remember |
|---|---|---|
| **1927** | Indian Forest Act | **RESERVED forest is STRICTER than PROTECTED** |
| **1972** | **Wildlife (Protection) Act** | **NPs, sanctuaries, schedules. 2002 → Conservation & Community Reserves · 2006 → NTCA · 2022 → schedules restructured** |
| **1974** | **Water Act** | **INDIA'S FIRST major environmental Act; created CPCB/SPCBs** |
| **1980** | **Forest (Conservation) Act** | **No forest diversion without PRIOR CENTRAL approval** |
| **1981** | **Air Act** | Extends CPCB/SPCB to air |
| **1986** | ⭐ **Environment (Protection) Act** | **UMBRELLA Act; response to BHOPAL 1984. EIA, CRZ, ESZ and waste Rules are all issued UNDER it** |
| **1991** | Public Liability Insurance Act | No-fault liability, hazardous substances |
| **2002** | **Biological Diversity Act** | **NBA (Chennai) → SBB → BMC; ABS; anti-biopiracy** |

| **2010** | **NGT Act** | **Green tribunal; expert + judicial members** |
| **2016** | CAMPA Act | Compensatory afforestation funds, NPV |

**Constitution: Art 48A (DPSP) & 51A(g) (Duty) — both 42nd Amendment 1976, which also moved FORESTS to the Concurrent List. Art 21 read to include a clean environment. WATER remains a State subject (Entry 17).**

## 5️⃣ HOTSPOTS, ZONES & BIODIVERSITY

** HOTSPOT CRITERIA (both required): ≥1,500 endemic vascular plant species AND ≥70% of original vegetation LOST.** *~36 globally* ⚠️`[VERIFY]`
** INDIA'S FOUR: Himalaya · Western Ghats & Sri Lanka · Indo-Burma · SUNDALAND (the NICOBARS).**
** India is one of 17 MEGADIVERSE countries; 2.4% of land, ~8% of species.**

** THE 10 BIOGEOGRAPHIC ZONES (Rodgers & Panwar, WII):** **Trans-Himalaya · Himalaya · Desert · Semi-arid · Western Ghats · Deccan Peninsula (largest) · Gangetic Plain · Coasts · North-East · Islands.**

## 7️⃣ POLLUTION QUICK FACTS

** AQI (2015), 6 bands: Good 0–50 · Satisfactory 51–100 · Moderate 101–200 · Poor 201–300 · Very Poor 301–400 · Severe 401–500. Reports the WORST pollutant, not an average.**
** NAAQS (2009) — 12 pollutants, set by CPCB. BS-VI adopted 2020 — India SKIPPED BS-V.**
** Smog: LONDON = winter, coal, SO₂, REDUCING. LOS ANGELES = summer, sunlight, NOₓ+VOC, OXIDISING → ozone & PAN.**
** Noise limits day/night (dB): Industrial 75/70 · Commercial 65/55 · Residential 55/45 · Silence 50/40. Silence zone = within 100 m of hospitals/schools/courts.**
** Biomedical colour codes: YELLOW (incinerate) · RED (autoclave) · WHITE (sharps) · BLUE (glass).**
** BOD = microbial oxygen demand for ORGANIC matter. COD ≥ BOD always. High BOD → low DO.**
** Arsenic = Ganga–Brahmaputra plains (arsenicosis). Fluoride = Rajasthan/Gujarat/peninsular hard rock (dental & skeletal fluorosis). Nitrate → blue baby syndrome.**
** Microplastics < 5 mm. Single-use plastic ban from 1 July 2022. EPR covers plastics, e-waste, batteries, tyres, used oil.**

## 9️⃣ INDIA'S CLIMATE POLICY

** NAPCC (2008) — 8 missions: Solar · Energy Efficiency (PAT) · Sustainable Habitat · Water · Himalayan Ecosystem · Green India · Sustainable Agriculture · Strategic Knowledge.**
** Net zero 2070, announced at COP-26 (Glasgow 2021) as part of PANCHAMRIT.**
** India's NDC is an EMISSIONS-INTENSITY target, not an absolute cut.** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *the numbers.*
** ISA (2015, Gurugram) · CDRI (2019) · Mission LiFE · National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023) · Global Biofuels Alliance (G20, 2023).**
** CBDR: stock not flow · per-capita not total · carbon space · finance & technology transfer. India opposes CBAM on this basis.**
