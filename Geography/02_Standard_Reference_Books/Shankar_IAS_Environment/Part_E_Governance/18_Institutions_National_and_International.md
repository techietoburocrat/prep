## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ INDIAN BODIES — and their legal status

> ⭐⭐ **The distinction UPSC tests most: ⭐ STATUTORY (created by an Act) vs ⭐ NON-STATUTORY (created by executive order or resolution).**

| ⭐ Body | ⭐ Status | ⭐ Role |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **MoEFCC** | ⭐ **Ministry** | ⭐ **The nodal ministry for environment, forests and climate change** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **CPCB** — Central Pollution Control Board | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — ⭐ Water Act 1974** | ⭐ **Sets standards (NAAQS), monitors, coordinates SPCBs, advises the Centre** |
| ⭐⭐ **SPCBs / PCCs** | ⭐ **STATUTORY** | ⭐ **Consent to establish/operate; enforcement at state level; conduct EIA public hearings** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **NGT** — National Green Tribunal | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — ⭐ NGT Act 2010** | ⭐⭐ **Specialised tribunal for environmental disputes. ⭐ Cases to be disposed within 6 months. ⭐ Has BOTH judicial and EXPERT members — the point of it is technical competence. ⭐ Appeals lie to the ⭐ SUPREME COURT.** ⭐ *India was among the first countries to create a dedicated environmental court* |
| ⭐⭐ **NBWL** — National Board for Wildlife | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — WPA 1972** | ⭐ **Chaired by the ⭐ PRIME MINISTER; clears projects in and around protected areas (via its Standing Committee)** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **NTCA** — National Tiger Conservation Authority | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — ⭐ WPA amendment 2006** | ⭐ **Governs tiger reserves; approves tiger conservation plans; conducts the quadrennial estimation** |
| ⭐⭐ **NBA** — National Biodiversity Authority | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — ⭐ Biological Diversity Act 2002** | ⭐ **Headquartered at ⭐ CHENNAI. Regulates access by foreign entities; IPR clearance** |
| ⭐⭐ **CAQM** | ⭐⭐ **STATUTORY — ⭐ 2021** | ⭐ **Air quality across Delhi-NCR AND adjoining states — ⭐ cross-boundary authority** |
| ⭐ **CAMPA / National Authority** | ⭐ **STATUTORY — CAMPA Act 2016** | Compensatory afforestation funds |
| ⭐⭐ **WCCB** — Wildlife Crime Control Bureau | ⭐ **STATUTORY — WPA** | ⭐ **Combats organised wildlife crime; intelligence and enforcement coordination** |
| ⭐⭐ **FSI** — Forest Survey of India | ⭐ **Non-statutory (organisation under MoEFCC), ⭐ Dehradun** | ⭐⭐ **Publishes the ⭐ INDIA STATE OF FOREST REPORT (ISFR), biennially** |
| ⭐⭐ **WII** — Wildlife Institute of India | ⭐ **Autonomous, ⭐ Dehradun** | ⭐ **Research, training, the ⭐ National Wildlife Database; the ⭐ Rodgers & Panwar biogeographic classification came from here** |
| ⭐⭐ **ZSI / BSI** | ⭐ **Organisations under MoEFCC** | ⭐⭐ **Zoological Survey of India (⭐ Kolkata) — faunal survey. ⭐ Botanical Survey of India (⭐ Kolkata) — floral survey** |
| ⭐ **ICFRE** | Autonomous, Dehradun | Forestry research & education |
| ⭐ **IIFM, IGNFA, GBPNIHE** | Various | Forest management education; ⭐ *G.B. Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment, Almora* |
| ⭐ **CZA** — Central Zoo Authority | ⭐ **STATUTORY — WPA** | ⭐ **Regulates zoos; ex-situ conservation breeding** |
| ⭐ **AERB** | Statutory-ish, under the Atomic Energy Act | Radiation and nuclear safety |
| ⭐ **NDMA** | ⭐ **STATUTORY — Disaster Management Act 2005** | ⭐ **Chaired by the ⭐ PRIME MINISTER; disaster policy and guidelines** |
| ⭐ **CGWA / CGWB** | Under EPA / MoJS | Groundwater regulation and assessment |
| ⭐ **BEE** — Bureau of Energy Efficiency | ⭐ **STATUTORY — Energy Conservation Act 2001** | ⭐ **Star labelling, ⭐ PAT scheme, ECBC building code** |
| ⭐ **NMCG** | Under the Environment (Protection) Act framework | ⭐ **Namami Gange implementation; under the National Ganga Council** |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐ **The high-frequency Prelims facts here: ⭐ CPCB is statutory under the WATER Act 1974 (not the EPA) · ⭐ NBWL and NDMA are both chaired by the PM · ⭐ NBA is at Chennai · ⭐ FSI, WII and ICFRE are all at Dehradun · ⭐ ZSI and BSI are both at Kolkata · ⭐ NGT has expert members alongside judicial ones and appeals go to the Supreme Court.**

### ⭐⭐ The NGT — worth knowing properly

⭐ **Principal Bench: ⭐ NEW DELHI; ⭐ zonal benches at Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata and Chennai.** ⭐ **Jurisdiction covers seven scheduled environmental Acts** ⚠️ *— notably ⭐ NOT the Wildlife Protection Act 1972 or the Indian Forest Act 1927, which is a real and testable limitation.* ⭐ **Can award compensation and restitution; applies the ⭐ polluter-pays, precautionary and sustainable-development principles.**

⚠️ ⭐ **Criticisms: ⭐ vacancies and under-staffing · limited geographical reach · orders sometimes stayed on appeal · and questions about enforcement of its own orders.**

## 2️⃣ ⭐⭐ INTERNATIONAL BODIES

| ⭐ Body | ⭐ Founded / HQ | ⭐ Role |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **UNEP** — UN Environment Programme | ⭐ **1972 (from Stockholm) · ⭐ NAIROBI, KENYA** | ⭐⭐ **The UN's environmental authority. ⭐ Publishes the ⭐ Emissions Gap Report and the Adaptation Gap Report. ⭐ Its governing body is the ⭐ UNEA.** ⭐ *Notably the first major UN body headquartered in a developing country* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **IPCC** | ⭐ **1988, by ⭐ UNEP + ⭐ WMO · Geneva** | ⭐⭐⭐ **⭐ IT DOES NOT CONDUCT ITS OWN RESEARCH. ⭐ It ASSESSES and synthesises published science.** ⭐ **Three Working Groups: ⭐ WG-I physical science · ⭐ WG-II impacts & adaptation · ⭐ WG-III mitigation. ⭐ Assessment Reports; ⭐ Summary for Policymakers approved line-by-line by governments** 🎯 *"IPCC does not do research" is the classic Prelims trap* |
| ⭐⭐ **IUCN** | ⭐ **1948 · ⭐ GLAND, SWITZERLAND** | ⭐⭐ **The ⭐ RED LIST; protected-area categories; ⭐ a hybrid membership of governments AND NGOs — unusual and worth noting** |
| ⭐⭐ **WMO** | 1950 · Geneva | ⭐ **Weather, climate and water; co-parent of the IPCC; publishes the State of the Global Climate** |
| ⭐⭐ **GEF** — Global Environment Facility | ⭐ **1991 · Washington DC** | ⭐⭐ **The ⭐ FINANCIAL MECHANISM for several conventions — ⭐ CBD, UNFCCC, UNCCD, Stockholm, Minamata** |
| ⭐ **Green Climate Fund (GCF)** | ⭐ **2010 · Songdo, South Korea** | ⭐ **The main UNFCCC climate-finance vehicle** |
| ⭐⭐ **IPBES** | ⭐ **2012 · Bonn** | ⭐⭐ **⭐ "The IPCC for biodiversity" — assesses biodiversity and ecosystem services for policymakers** |
| ⭐ **WWF** | ⭐ **1961 · Gland** | ⭐ **NGO; ⭐ the ⭐ Living Planet Report; the panda logo** |
| ⭐ **TRAFFIC** | ⭐ **1976 · a joint programme of ⭐ WWF and ⭐ IUCN** | ⭐ **Monitors wildlife TRADE** |
| ⭐ **UNESCO — MAB** | ⭐ **1971** | ⭐ **The ⭐ Man and the Biosphere programme → the World Network of Biosphere Reserves; also World Heritage** |
| ⭐ **FAO** | 1945 · Rome | Food, agriculture, forestry, fisheries; the Global Forest Resources Assessment |
| ⭐ **ISA — International Solar Alliance** | ⭐ **2015 · ⭐ GURUGRAM, INDIA** | ⭐⭐ **Founded by ⭐ INDIA and FRANCE — ⭐ the first major international organisation headquartered in India** |
| ⭐ **CDRI** | ⭐ **2019 · New Delhi** | ⭐ **Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, an India-led initiative** |
| ⭐ **ISA (the other one) — International Seabed Authority** | ⭐ **1994 · Kingston, Jamaica** | ⚠️ ⭐ **Under ⭐ UNCLOS; regulates deep-sea mining in the international seabed area.** ⭐ *Note the acronym clash with the solar alliance — a genuine trap* |
| ⭐ **Global Tiger Forum** | ⭐ **New Delhi** | The only intergovernmental body dedicated to tiger conservation |
| ⭐ **BirdLife International, WCMC, WRI, TERI** | Various | ⭐ **TERI (The Energy and Resources Institute) is Indian, Delhi-based, and runs the World Sustainable Development Summit** |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐ **The most-asked facts on this table: ⭐ UNEP → Nairobi · ⭐ IUCN → Gland (and it does the Red List) · ⭐ IPCC → assesses, does not research; founded by UNEP + WMO · ⭐ IPBES → the biodiversity equivalent · ⭐ GEF → financial mechanism for multiple conventions · ⭐ ISA (solar) → Gurugram, India + France.**

## 📝 `[P]` / `[M]` / 🎯

### `[P]` PRELIMS
- ⭐⭐⭐ **CPCB is statutory under the ⭐ WATER ACT 1974** (not the EPA)
- ⭐⭐ **NGT Act 2010; ⭐ principal bench New Delhi; ⭐ judicial AND expert members; ⭐ 6-month disposal; ⭐ appeals to the SUPREME COURT.** ⚠️ **Its jurisdiction does NOT cover the WPA 1972 or IFA 1927**
- ⭐⭐ **⭐ NBWL and ⭐ NDMA are both chaired by the PRIME MINISTER**
- ⭐⭐ **NTCA — WPA amendment 2006. ⭐ NBA — Biological Diversity Act 2002, ⭐ Chennai. ⭐ CAQM — 2021, statutory, cross-state**
- ⭐⭐ **⭐ Dehradun: FSI, WII, ICFRE, IGNFA. ⭐ Kolkata: ZSI, BSI**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **⭐ UNEP → NAIROBI (1972, from Stockholm). ⭐ IUCN → GLAND (1948, Red List). ⭐ IPCC → 1988, by UNEP + WMO; ⭐ ASSESSES, does NOT research. ⭐ IPBES → the biodiversity IPCC**
- ⭐⭐ **⭐ GEF = financial mechanism for CBD, UNFCCC, UNCCD, Stockholm, Minamata. ⭐ GCF → Songdo**
- ⭐⭐ **⭐ ISA (solar) → Gurugram, India + France, 2015 — ⭐ the first major international body HQ'd in India.** ⚠️ **Distinct from the International Seabed Authority (Kingston)**
- ⭐ **TRAFFIC = WWF + IUCN joint programme**
- ⭐⭐ **Reports: ISFR→FSI · Emissions Gap→UNEP · Living Planet→WWF · Global Assessment→IPBES · State of Global Climate→WMO**

### `[M]` MAINS
- ⭐⭐ **The NGT as an institutional innovation** — expert members give it technical competence a generalist court lacks; but jurisdictional gaps and vacancies limit it
- ⭐⭐ **Statutory vs non-statutory determines effectiveness** — the [Project Tiger vs Project Elephant](../Part_B_Biodiversity/08_Species_Conservation_Projects.md) contrast, and CAQM's cross-state authority
- ⭐⭐ **IPCC's assessment model** — why "government-approved Summary for Policymakers" is both its strength (buy-in) and its weakness (dilution)
- ⭐ **India as an institution-builder** — ISA and CDRI as a shift from norm-taker to norm-shaper
- ⭐ **Regulatory capacity is the binding constraint** — SPCBs are under-resourced relative to their statutory mandate
