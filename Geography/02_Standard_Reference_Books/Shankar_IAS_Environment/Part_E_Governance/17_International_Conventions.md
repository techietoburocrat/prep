# 🌐 E17 — International Environmental Conventions

> **The one-line point:** **A convention is a framework; a protocol sets the binding targets; an amendment revises them. Get that three-tier structure and half the confusion in this topic disappears.**

## 1️⃣ ⭐ THE STRUCTURE — understand this before memorising anything

```
   CONVENTION  →  the FRAMEWORK. States agree there is a
                       problem and agree to cooperate.
                       Usually NO binding targets.
        ↓
   PROTOCOL    →  the TEETH. Binding commitments, timetables,
                       mechanisms. Negotiated later, separately
                       ratified.
        ↓
   AMENDMENT    →  revises the protocol.

   COP = Conference of the Parties — the governing body
            that meets periodically to review and negotiate.
```

**Worked example: VIENNA CONVENTION (1985, framework) → MONTREAL PROTOCOL (1987, binding phase-outs) → KIGALI AMENDMENT (2016, adds HFCs).**
**And: UNFCCC (1992, framework) → KYOTO PROTOCOL (1997) → PARIS AGREEMENT (2015).**

| **CMS / BONN Convention** | **1979** | **Bonn** | **MIGRATORY species** | **Appendix I = endangered migratory species · Appendix II = those needing international agreements. India hosted CMS COP-13 at Gandhinagar, 2020 — mascot "Gibi" the Great Indian Bustard** |

| ⭐ **MONTREAL PROTOCOL** | **1987** | Montreal | **OZONE — the teeth** | **Phases out CFCs, halons, HCFCs. THE ONLY UNIVERSALLY RATIFIED UN TREATY. Multilateral Fund** |
| **BASEL Convention** | **1989** | **Basel** | **Transboundary movement of HAZARDOUS WASTE** | **Stops rich countries dumping hazardous waste on poor ones. 2019 Plastic Waste Amendments** |

| **UNCCD** | **1994** | Paris *(adopted)* | **DESERTIFICATION** | **The third Rio convention. India hosted COP-14, New Delhi, 2019 — New Delhi Declaration. LDN = SDG 15.3** |
| ⭐ **KYOTO PROTOCOL** | **1997** *(in force 2005)* | **Kyoto** | **CLIMATE — binding targets** | **Binding cuts for DEVELOPED (Annex I) countries ONLY. | **CARTAGENA Protocol** | **2000** | Under the **CBD** | **BIOSAFETY — living modified organisms (LMOs/GMOs)** | **Advance Informed Agreement; enshrines the PRECAUTIONARY approach in trade** |
| **STOCKHOLM Convention** | **2001** | Stockholm | **POPs — Persistent Organic Pollutants** | **The original "DIRTY DOZEN." Driven substantially by POP accumulation in the ARCTIC** *(see [Leong Climate 11](../../GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/11_Arctic_Tundra_and_Ice_Cap.md))* |
| **ROTTERDAM Convention** | **1998** | Rotterdam | **Prior Informed Consent for hazardous chemicals & pesticides in trade** | *Basel–Rotterdam–Stockholm are the "chemicals cluster" and hold joint COPs* |
| **NAGOYA Protocol** | **2010** | Under the **CBD** | **ACCESS AND BENEFIT SHARING (ABS)** | **Operationalises the CBD's third objective — the anti-biopiracy instrument.** **Adopted at CBD COP-10, Nagoya, alongside the AICHI TARGETS** |
| **Aichi Biodiversity Targets** | **2010** | Nagoya | Biodiversity, to 2020 | ⚠️ **Essentially none were fully met** |
| ⭐ **PARIS AGREEMENT** | **2015** *(in force 2016)* | **Paris, COP-21** | **CLIMATE** | ⭐ ** ALL countries commit, via NDCs — nationally determined, BOTTOM-UP, not imposed. Goal: well below 2 °C, pursuing 1.5 °C. Global Stocktake every 5 years. "Ratchet mechanism."** ⚠️ **NDCs are NOT legally binding in content — only the procedural obligations are** |
| **MINAMATA Convention** | **2013** | **Minamata, JAPAN** | **MERCURY** | **Named for the methylmercury poisoning disaster** *(see [A2](../Part_A_Ecology/02_Ecosystem_Energy_Flow_and_Cycles.md))* |
| **KIGALI Amendment** | **2016** | Kigali, Rwanda | **HFCs** | ** HFCs do NOT deplete ozone — targeted as GREENHOUSE gases. India ratified 2021** |
| **KUNMING–MONTREAL Global Biodiversity Framework** | **2022** | **CBD COP-15** | **Biodiversity, to 2030** | **Successor to Aichi. Headline " 30×30" target — protect 30% of land and sea by 2030** |
| **BBNJ / "High Seas Treaty"** | **2023** | Under **UNCLOS** | **Marine biodiversity beyond national jurisdiction** | **MPAs on the high seas; ABS for marine genetic resources** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *entry into force* |
| **Global Plastics Treaty** | **under negotiation** | UNEA process | Plastics | ⚠️`[VERIFY]` **— live and highly examinable** |

## 3️⃣ ⭐ THE DISTINCTIONS THAT GET TESTED

### Kyoto vs Paris — the structural shift

| | **KYOTO (1997)** | **PARIS (2015)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who commits** | **DEVELOPED countries only (Annex I)** | **ALL countries** |
| **How targets set** | **TOP-DOWN — negotiated and assigned** | **BOTTOM-UP — self-determined (NDCs)** |
| **Binding?** | **Targets legally binding** | **Procedures binding; the target CONTENT is not** |
| **Coverage achieved** | **A small share of global emissions** | **Near-universal** |
| **Mechanism** | CDM, JI, emissions trading | **Article 6 carbon markets; Global Stocktake; ratchet** |

> ⭐ **The trade-off to state: Kyoto had strong obligations on few countries; Paris has weak obligations on all of them.** **Paris chose universality over stringency — on the reasoning that a treaty covering 90% of emissions loosely achieves more than one covering 15% strictly, especially after the US refused Kyoto and China and India were exempt.** 🎯 **This is the answer to "was Paris a step forward or backward?" — it was a step sideways, deliberately.**

### ⭐ CBDR — the principle India rests everything on

** COMMON BUT DIFFERENTIATED RESPONSIBILITIES AND RESPECTIVE CAPABILITIES (CBDR-RC).**

**The argument, stated properly:**
- **The problem is a STOCK, not a flow** — **CO₂ persists for centuries, so today's warming reflects CUMULATIVE HISTORICAL emissions, overwhelmingly from the industrialised world.**
- ** PER CAPITA emissions matter, not national totals** — *India's total is large because India is large; its per-capita emissions remain far below the global average.*
- **Developing countries need CARBON SPACE for development**, and have the least capacity to pay for transition.
- **Hence: differentiated obligations, plus FINANCE and TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER from developed to developing countries.**

**India's negotiating positions follow directly: opposition to CBAM · insistence on climate finance delivery (the long-unmet $100 bn/yr pledge) · support for Loss and Damage · an intensity-based rather than absolute NDC · "phase-DOWN" rather than "phase-out" of coal at COP-26.** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *the current finance goal — the NCQG replaced the $100 bn target.*

### Three lists that are NOT the same thing

| System | What it governs | Binding? |
|---|---|---|
| **IUCN RED LIST** | **Extinction RISK** | **No — scientific assessment** |
| **CITES Appendices** | **International TRADE** | **Yes — treaty obligation** |
| **WPA Schedules** | **Domestic legal PROTECTION in India** | **Yes — Indian statute** |

🎯 **A species can be Least Concern globally, CITES Appendix II, and Schedule I in India. These are three independent systems.**

## 4️⃣ THE MILESTONE CONFERENCES

| Year | Event | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **1972** | **STOCKHOLM Conference** | **Created UNEP; the beginning of international environmental governance** |
| **1987** | **BRUNTLAND REPORT — *Our Common Future*** | **Defined SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT** |
| ⭐ **1992** | **RIO EARTH SUMMIT (UNCED)** | ** THE THREE RIO CONVENTIONS: UNFCCC · CBD · UNCCD. Plus Agenda 21 and the Rio Declaration (which articulates the precautionary and polluter-pays principles)** |
| **2002** | Johannesburg (Rio+10) | Implementation focus |
| **2012** | **Rio+20** | **"The Future We Want" — launched the process leading to the SDGs** |
| **2015** | **UN Sustainable Development Summit + COP-21** | ** THE 17 SDGs (Agenda 2030) and the PARIS AGREEMENT in the same year** |

> ** THE THREE RIO CONVENTIONS is a certain Prelims item: UNFCCC (climate) · CBD (biodiversity) · UNCCD (desertification). All three trace to Rio 1992** *(UNCCD was mandated at Rio and adopted in 1994).*
