# ⚡ A2 — Ecosystem: Energy Flow, Pyramids & Cycles

> **The one-line point:** **ENERGY FLOWS THROUGH an ecosystem and is lost as heat; NUTRIENTS CYCLE WITHIN it and are reused forever. That single asymmetry explains food chains, pyramids, the 10% law and why every ecosystem needs the sun every day.**

## 1️⃣ THE TWO LAWS THAT GOVERN EVERYTHING

```
   ENERGY                          NUTRIENTS
   ────────                            ──────────

   ☀️ SUN                              ┌──────────────┐
      ↓                                │              ↓
   PRODUCERS                        PRODUCERS ← ← ← DECOMPOSERS
      ↓  (90% lost as heat)            ↓              ↑
   HERBIVORES                       CONSUMERS → → → → ┘
      ↓  (90% lost as heat)
   CARNIVORES                       CYCLES — CLOSED LOOP
      ↓  (90% lost as heat)            The same nitrogen atom is
   TOP CARNIVORES                      reused indefinitely
      ↓
   🔥 HEAT — lost to space

   FLOWS — ONE WAY, NEVER RETURNS
```

> ⭐ **State it as a single contrast and you have the chapter: "Energy flows unidirectionally and is ultimately lost as heat; matter cycles and is never lost."**
>
> **The consequence: an ecosystem can recycle its nutrients forever, but it needs a FRESH ENERGY INPUT every single day.** **Cut off the sun and every ecosystem stops within days — not because the atoms ran out, but because the energy did.**
>
> **This follows directly from the SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS: every energy transfer loses usable energy as heat.**

## 2️⃣ TROPHIC LEVELS & THE 10% LAW

| Trophic level | Who |
|---|---|
| **T1 — PRODUCERS (autotrophs)** | Green plants, algae, phytoplankton; **chemosynthetic bacteria at hydrothermal vents — the one ecosystem not powered by the sun** |
| **T2 — PRIMARY consumers** | Herbivores |
| **T3 — SECONDARY consumers** | Primary carnivores |
| **T4 — TERTIARY consumers** | Secondary carnivores |
| **T5 — Quaternary / apex** | Top carnivores |
| **DECOMPOSERS (saprotrophs)** | **Bacteria and fungi — they act on EVERY level, which is why they sit outside the numbering** |

> ⭐ **LINDEMAN'S 10% LAW: only about 10% of the energy at one trophic level is transferred to the next. The other ~90% is lost — as respiration, heat, and unconsumed or undigested material.**

**Two consequences follow immediately, and both are examinable:**

1. **FOOD CHAINS ARE SHORT — usually 3 to 5 links.** **After four transfers only ~0.01% of the original energy remains. There is not enough left to support another level.** *This is why there is no animal that preys on lions as its staple.*
2. **The energetic argument about diet: the same land supports far more people eating grain directly than eating animals fed on grain**, because one trophic step throws away ~90%. 🎯 *A clean, defensible line for a food-security or sustainability answer.*

**Types of food chain:**
- **GRAZING food chain** — begins with **living producers**. Grass → deer → tiger.
- **DETRITUS food chain** — begins with **dead organic matter**. Litter → earthworm/fungi → bird.
- **In most TERRESTRIAL and especially FOREST ecosystems, the DETRITUS chain carries far MORE energy than the grazing chain** — most leaves are never eaten alive; they fall and rot. *In many aquatic systems the grazing chain dominates instead.* 🎯 **This inversion of the intuitive answer gets asked.**

**A FOOD WEB is the interlocking network of many food chains.** **A complex web is more STABLE than a simple chain, because a consumer with alternative prey can absorb the loss of one species.** *This is the core ecological argument for biodiversity: redundancy is resilience.*

## 3️⃣ ⭐ ECOLOGICAL PYRAMIDS — including the inverted ones

**Three kinds, and the exam tests which can be inverted.**

| Pyramid | Measures | Can it be INVERTED? |
|---|---|---|
| **PYRAMID OF NUMBERS** | Number of individuals per level | **YES** |
| **PYRAMID OF BIOMASS** | Dry weight of living matter per level | **YES** |
| ⭐ **PYRAMID OF ENERGY** | Energy flow per unit area per unit time | ⭐ **NEVER. ALWAYS UPRIGHT** |

### The inversions — learn the two standard examples

```
   INVERTED PYRAMID OF NUMBERS — a single TREE ecosystem

        ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔  hyperparasites   (many)
         ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔     birds/insects    (many)
            ▔▔▔        ONE TREE          (one)
   One large producer supports thousands of consumers
```

```
   INVERTED PYRAMID OF BIOMASS — an OCEAN / POND

        ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔  ZOOPLANKTON & fish  (larger standing biomass)
           ▔▔▔▔     PHYTOPLANKTON        (tiny standing biomass)

   WHY: phytoplankton have an ENORMOUS TURNOVER RATE.
      They live only days, are eaten almost as fast as they
      reproduce, and so the biomass PRESENT at any instant is
      small — even though the biomass PRODUCED over a year is huge.
   The pyramid measures a STANDING CROP, not production.
```

> ⭐ **And here is the point that makes the whole topic make sense: the PYRAMID OF ENERGY can NEVER be inverted, because energy transfer obeys the second law of thermodynamics — you cannot have more energy at a higher level than was supplied to it.**
>
> **Numbers and biomass are SNAPSHOTS and can mislead. Energy is a RATE and cannot.** 🎯 *That contrast — snapshot vs rate — is the answer to any pyramid question.*

## 4️⃣ PRODUCTIVITY

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GPP — Gross Primary Productivity** | **The TOTAL organic matter produced by photosynthesis per unit area per unit time** |
| **NPP — Net Primary Productivity** | **GPP MINUS the producers' own RESPIRATION.** **`NPP = GPP − R`** *This is what is actually AVAILABLE to herbivores — the ecosystem's usable output* |
| **Secondary productivity** | Rate of new organic matter formed by **consumers** |
| **Standing crop** | The biomass present **at a given moment** |

### Most and least productive ecosystems

| Rank | Ecosystem | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Highest NPP** | **Coral reefs · estuaries · tropical rainforest · swamps & marshes** | Warmth + light + abundant nutrients (or, on reefs, extremely efficient recycling) |
| Moderate | Temperate forest, grassland, agricultural land | |
| **Lowest NPP** | **Deserts · tundra · the OPEN OCEAN** | **Deserts lack water; tundra lacks warmth; the open ocean lacks NUTRIENTS** |

> ⭐ **The open-ocean point is the counter-intuitive one and it gets asked.**
>
> **The open ocean covers most of the planet and is often called a BIOLOGICAL DESERT — its productivity per unit area is comparable to a true desert's.**
>
> **Why: sunlight is at the TOP and nutrients sink to the BOTTOM, and in the stratified open ocean the two never meet.** **Productivity is high only where something brings them together — UPWELLING zones, continental shelves, and current-mixing regions.**
>
> 🎯 **Which is precisely why ~90% of the world's fish catch comes from a small fraction of the ocean's area** — cross-link to [Leong A11](../../GC_Leong/Part_A_Physical/11_Oceans_Currents_Tides_Salinity.md) and [the Grand Banks](../../GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/10_Laurentian_Type_Cool_Temperate_Eastern_Margin.md).

## 5️⃣ ⭐ BIOACCUMULATION & BIOMAGNIFICATION — a guaranteed Prelims topic

| | **BIOACCUMULATION** | **BIOMAGNIFICATION** |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | **Build-up of a substance WITHIN A SINGLE ORGANISM over its lifetime**, because it is absorbed faster than it is excreted | **Increase in CONCENTRATION as you move UP the food chain, from one trophic level to the next** |
| **Direction** | **Over TIME, in one body** | **Across TROPHIC LEVELS** |

```
   BIOMAGNIFICATION — why the top predator gets the worst dose

   Water ............ 0.00005 ppm  DDT
        ↓
   Phytoplankton .... 0.04 ppm
        ↓            (each predator eats MANY prey, and the
   Zooplankton ...... 0.5 ppm       toxin from ALL of them stays
        ↓                           in its fat)
   Small fish ....... 2 ppm
        ↓
   Large fish ....... 25 ppm
        ↓
   Fish-eating bird  25–75 ppm  ← ~10 MILLION× the water
```

> ⭐ **The mechanism, stated precisely — and this is what a good answer says:**
>
> **Biomagnification requires the substance to be (a) PERSISTENT — not broken down, (b) FAT-SOLUBLE (lipophilic) — so it is stored rather than excreted, and (c) mobile in the environment.**
>
> **Energy shrinks by 90% at each step; the toxin does NOT.** **So the SAME quantity of poison ends up concentrated into one-tenth the biomass at every level.** **The magnification is arithmetic, not mysterious.**

**The classic substances:** **DDT and other organochlorine pesticides · PCBs · MERCURY (as methylmercury) · dioxins · POPs generally.**

**The classic consequences:**
- **DDT → eggshell thinning in raptors** — *bald eagle, peregrine falcon; the case documented in Rachel Carson's* **Silent Spring** *(1962), which effectively launched the modern environmental movement.*
- **MINAMATA DISEASE, Japan (1950s)** — **industrial methylmercury discharged into Minamata Bay, biomagnified through fish, causing severe neurological damage.** **The reason the MINAMATA CONVENTION ON MERCURY (2013) bears that name** — *see [E17](../Part_E_Governance/17_International_Conventions.md).*
- **POPs in the ARCTIC** — *emitted in industrial mid-latitudes, transported poleward, concentrated in the short, fat-rich Arctic food chains. Inuit populations carry some of the highest body burdens on Earth despite living beside no industry.* **The environmental-justice case that produced the STOCKHOLM CONVENTION (2001).** *Cross-link: [Leong Climate 11](../../GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/11_Arctic_Tundra_and_Ice_Cap.md).*

> 🎯 **Note how neatly this connects: an ecological process (biomagnification) → a disease (Minamata) → a treaty (Minamata Convention). Being able to run that chain in one sentence is exactly what Mains rewards.**

## 6️⃣ BIOGEOCHEMICAL CYCLES — the delta over NCERT

📎 **The carbon, nitrogen and water cycles are covered in [Cl.11 Ch.15 §3](../../../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/15_Life_on_the_Earth.md). Only the additions below.**

**Two families:** **GASEOUS cycles** (reservoir = atmosphere/ocean; **carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, water** — fast and globally well-mixed) and **SEDIMENTARY cycles** (reservoir = the Earth's crust; **phosphorus, sulphur, calcium** — slow, and locally depletable).

### The PHOSPHORUS cycle — the one NCERT skips

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **NO significant atmospheric component** | **The only major nutrient cycle with no gaseous phase.** Its reservoir is **rock** |
| **Source** | Weathering of phosphate-bearing rock |
| **It is a ONE-WAY street on human timescales** | **Phosphorus washes to the sea and settles into marine sediment, where it is locked up until geological uplift returns it — millions of years** |
| **Why it matters** | **Phosphorus is very often the LIMITING NUTRIENT in freshwater systems — so phosphate from detergents and fertiliser is the usual trigger of EUTROPHICATION** *(see [A4](04_Aquatic_and_Wetland_Ecosystems.md) and [C10](../Part_C_Pollution/10_Water_and_Soil_Pollution.md))* |
| **"Peak phosphorus"** | **Rock phosphate is a finite, geographically concentrated resource with NO substitute in agriculture.** *A genuine long-run food-security concern, and an unusually sharp point to raise* |

### The SULPHUR cycle — brief

**Reservoir in rocks and sediments, with a gaseous phase (SO₂, H₂S).** **Human input from fossil-fuel combustion is now large — and it is what produces ACID RAIN.** *See [D13](../Part_D_Climate_Change/13_Ozone_Acid_Rain_and_Global_Issues.md).*

### The NITROGEN cycle — the terms to be certain of

**NITROGEN FIXATION** (N₂ → ammonia; **Rhizobium in legume nodules, Azotobacter, cyanobacteria/Anabaena, and lightning**) → **NITRIFICATION** (ammonia → nitrite → nitrate; *Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter*) → **ASSIMILATION** by plants → **AMMONIFICATION** (decay returns ammonia) → **DENITRIFICATION** (nitrate → N₂, returned to the atmosphere; *Pseudomonas*).

⚠️ 🌱 **The human disruption is enormous and under-discussed: the Haber–Bosch process now fixes roughly as much nitrogen as all natural processes combined.** **Consequences: eutrophication and dead zones · nitrate in groundwater · NITROUS OXIDE (N₂O), a greenhouse gas ~273× as potent as CO₂ and also an ozone-depleting substance.** 🎯 **"Humans have doubled the global nitrogen cycle" is a strong opening line for a fertiliser or eutrophication answer.**
