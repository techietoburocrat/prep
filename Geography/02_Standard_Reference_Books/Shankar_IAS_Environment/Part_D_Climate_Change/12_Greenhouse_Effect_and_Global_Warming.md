# 🌡️ D12 — The Greenhouse Effect, GHGs & Carbon

> **The one-line point:** **The greenhouse effect is not the problem — without it Earth would be about −18 °C. The problem is that we have strengthened it, and the gases doing the strengthening differ enormously in how hard they push.**

## 1️⃣ THE MECHANISM, in one box

```
   Incoming SOLAR radiation is SHORT-WAVE
     → passes through the atmosphere largely unimpeded
              ↓
   Earth's surface absorbs it and warms
              ↓
   Earth re-radiates as LONG-WAVE INFRARED
              ↓
   GREENHOUSE GASES ABSORB long-wave infrared
      and re-emit it in all directions, including DOWNWARD
              ↓
   The lower atmosphere and surface stay warmer
      than they otherwise would be
```

> **The number to have: without any greenhouse effect Earth's average surface temperature would be roughly −18 °C. It is about +15 °C. So the natural greenhouse effect is worth about 33 °C — and it is the reason the planet is habitable.**
>
> **The issue is the ENHANCED greenhouse effect: additional GHGs from human activity strengthening a natural mechanism.**

** RADIATIVE FORCING** = the change in the Earth's energy balance (W/m²) caused by a factor. **Positive forcing = warming (GHGs, black carbon); negative = cooling (sulphate aerosols, which reflect sunlight).**

> ⚠️ **A subtlety worth knowing: sulphate aerosols from coal burning have been MASKING part of the warming.** **So cleaning up SO₂ — which we must do for health reasons — removes a cooling influence and reveals warming that was already committed.** 🎯 *"Air-quality improvement can unmask warming" is a genuinely sophisticated point.*

## 2️⃣ ⭐ THE GREENHOUSE GASES — learn this table

| Gas | Main sources | GWP (100-yr) | Atmospheric lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CARBON DIOXIDE (CO₂)** | **Fossil fuel combustion, deforestation, cement** | **1** *(the reference)* | **Centuries to millennia** |
| **METHANE (CH₄)** | **Paddy fields, ENTERIC FERMENTATION in ruminants, landfills, coal mines, oil & gas leaks, wetlands** | **~28–30** *(100-yr); ~80+ over 20 years* | **~12 years** |
| **NITROUS OXIDE (N₂O)** | **Nitrogenous FERTILISERS, manure, industry** | **~265–273** | **~110 years** |
| **F-GASES** — HFCs, PFCs, SF₆, NF₃ | **Refrigeration, air-conditioning, foams, electrical switchgear, semiconductors** | **Hundreds to tens of thousands (SF₆ ≈ 23,500)** | **Up to thousands of years** |
| **Water vapour** | Natural | — | **The largest natural contributor — but a FEEDBACK, not a forcing** |

> ⭐ **Two distinctions this table is really testing:**
>
> **1. WATER VAPOUR is a FEEDBACK, not a driver.** **Its concentration is set by temperature (warmer air holds more), and it leaves the atmosphere in days.** **We cannot control it directly — but it AMPLIFIES whatever CO₂ does, roughly doubling the warming.** 🎯 *"Water vapour is the biggest greenhouse gas" is a common denialist talking point; knowing why it's a feedback is the answer.*
>
> **2. GWP vs LIFETIME — the trade-off that shapes policy.** **Methane is a far more powerful warmer than CO₂ but disappears in about a decade. CO₂ is weak per molecule but effectively permanent.**
> **So: cutting METHANE buys fast relief on the near-term temperature curve; cutting CO₂ is the only thing that determines the long-run outcome.** **You need both, for different reasons.** *This is exactly why the GLOBAL METHANE PLEDGE exists alongside net-zero targets.*

** GWP (Global Warming Potential)** = how much heat a gas traps relative to the same mass of CO₂, over a stated period. **Emissions are converted to CO₂-equivalent (CO₂e) using GWP.** ⚠️ *GWP values are revised between IPCC assessment reports — quote them as approximate.*

**India-specific emissions profile:** **the energy sector dominates; agriculture is an unusually large share compared with developed countries, because of paddy (methane) and livestock (enteric fermentation) — India has the world's largest cattle population.** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *sectoral shares from India's latest Biennial Update Report / National Communication.*

## 3️⃣ THE CARBON CYCLE & SINKS

** CARBON SINK = a reservoir that absorbs more carbon than it releases. SOURCE = the reverse.**

| Sink | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **THE OCEAN** | **The largest active sink — absorbs roughly a quarter to a third of human CO₂ emissions.** ⚠️ **And the cost of that service is OCEAN ACIDIFICATION** — *see [D13](13_Ozone_Acid_Rain_and_Global_Issues.md)* |
| **FORESTS & vegetation** | **Tropical, temperate and boreal.** ⚠️ *A MATURE forest is close to carbon-NEUTRAL (respiration ≈ photosynthesis); its value is as a STOCK. A GROWING forest is a net sink* |
| **SOILS** | **Soils hold more carbon than the atmosphere and all vegetation combined. Peatlands and permafrost are the biggest stores** |
| **BLUE CARBON** | **Mangroves, seagrass, salt marsh — very high sequestration per hectare, stored mainly in the SOIL** |
| **Geological** | Rock weathering (very slow); fossil fuel reservoirs |

> ⭐ **The two sinks that can FLIP into sources — this is the tipping-point content:**
>
> **1. PERMAFROST.** **Thawing releases CO₂ and methane from organic matter frozen for millennia; Arctic permafrost holds roughly twice the carbon currently in the atmosphere.** *Full mechanism in [Leong Climate 9](../../GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/09_Siberian_Type_Taiga.md).*
> **2. THE AMAZON.** **Deforestation plus drought have pushed parts of the Amazon from net sink to net SOURCE.** *See [Leong Climate 1](../../GC_Leong/Part_B_Climate_Types/01_Equatorial_Hot_Wet.md).*
>
> **Why this matters for policy: every emissions pathway assumes the sinks keep absorbing at historical rates. If they weaken, the same emissions produce more warming — and the budget shrinks without anyone emitting more.** 🎯 *A first-rate closing paragraph for a climate answer.*

### Carbon sequestration & removal

| Approach | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Afforestation / reforestation** | **Cheap and co-beneficial** ⚠️ *but reversible (fire, felling), land-hungry, and — as [A3](../Part_A_Ecology/03_Succession_and_Terrestrial_Ecosystems.md) warns — destructive if done on grasslands* |
| **Soil carbon** | **Regenerative agriculture, no-till, cover crops, agroforestry, biochar** |
| **CCS / CCUS** | **Carbon Capture, Utilisation and Storage — capture at source, then use or inject into geological formations.** ⚠️ *Expensive; deployed at small scale relative to need* |
| **BECCS** | **Bioenergy with CCS — burn biomass, capture the CO₂; theoretically net-negative.** ⚠️ *Huge land requirements* |
| **DAC** | **Direct Air Capture — chemically strip CO₂ from ambient air.** ⚠️ *Currently very energy-intensive and costly* |
| **Enhanced weathering, ocean alkalinity** | Experimental |

> ⚠️ **The honest framing: every one of these is either small, expensive, land-hungry or unproven at scale.** **Treating removal as a substitute for emission reduction is what critics call a "moral hazard" — it licenses delay against a technology that may not arrive.** 🎯 *A balanced Mains answer says removal is necessary AND insufficient.*

## 4️⃣ EVIDENCE & OBSERVED CHANGE

📎 **Ice cores, tree rings, pollen and the rest are in [Cl.11 Ch.12](../../../01_NCERT/Class_11_Fundamentals_of_Physical_Geography/Notes/12_World_Climate_and_Climate_Change.md).** **The essentials to keep:**

- **CO₂ oscillated between roughly 180 and 300 ppm across the last ~800,000 years of glacial cycles; it is now well above 420 ppm** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *the current figure — it rises every year* — **far outside that entire range, and reached in about two centuries rather than millennia.**
- ** The KEELING CURVE** — the continuous CO₂ record from Mauna Loa since 1958; *its annual sawtooth is the Northern Hemisphere's growing season breathing in and out.*
- **The correct framing: "the climate has always changed" is TRUE and BESIDE THE POINT. What is anomalous is the RATE and the CAUSE, not the fact of change.**
- **Observed: rising global mean temperature, sea-level rise (thermal expansion + land-ice melt), Arctic sea-ice decline, glacier retreat, ocean heat content, more frequent extremes.**

⚠️ **Do NOT quote a precise current warming figure or a specific year's temperature record from memory.** ⚠️`[VERIFY]` *from the latest IPCC assessment or WMO State of the Global Climate.*
