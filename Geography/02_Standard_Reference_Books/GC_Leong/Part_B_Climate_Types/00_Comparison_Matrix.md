> ⭐⭐ **The two structural rules the map encodes:**
> 1. ⭐ **WEST margins get: hot desert (20°–30°) → Mediterranean (30°–45°) → British type (45°–60°).** ⭐ **EAST margins get: monsoon/marine (0°–25°) → China type (25°–45°) → Laurentian (40°–60°).** ⭐ **The same latitudes give different climates on different coasts.**
> 2. ⭐⭐ **Three types are NORTHERN HEMISPHERE ONLY** — ⭐ **Siberian, Laurentian, and (effectively) Tundra** — ⭐ **because there is no land in the Southern Hemisphere at 50°–70°S.**

## 🎯 THE DISCRIMINATORS — how to identify a climate from one clue

**These are the tables that actually win Prelims marks. Each row is a unique fingerprint.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ 1. By rainfall regime — the single most powerful discriminator

| If the rainfall is… | ⭐ The climate is… |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **WINTER maximum, dry summer** | ⭐⭐⭐ **MEDITERRANEAN `Cs` — and NOTHING ELSE** |
| ⭐ **All year, DOUBLE maximum at the equinoxes** | ⭐ **EQUATORIAL** |
| ⭐ **All year, no dry season, slight autumn/winter max, cool** | ⭐ **BRITISH TYPE** |
| ⭐ **All year, summer max, WARM, + typhoons** | ⭐ **CHINA TYPE** |
| ⭐ **All year, summer max, COLD winters, heavy snow** | ⭐ **LAURENTIAN** |
| ⭐ **Heavy summer max + long dry season, HOT** | ⭐ **MONSOON** (>1500 mm) or ⭐ **SAVANNA** (750–1500 mm) |
| ⭐ **Summer max but LOW total (250–750 mm), grassland** | ⭐ **STEPPE** |
| ⭐ **Low total, summer max, COLD, forested** | ⭐ **SIBERIAN** |
| ⭐ **Under 250 mm, hot** | ⭐ **DESERT** |
| ⭐ **Under 250 mm, cold, waterlogged** | ⭐ **TUNDRA** |

### ⭐⭐ 2. By temperature range

| Range | ⭐ Climate |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Smallest annual range (1–3 °C)** | ⭐ **EQUATORIAL** — and ⭐ **its diurnal range EXCEEDS its annual range** |
| ⭐ Small (10–14 °C) | ⭐ **BRITISH TYPE** — maritime |

### ⭐⭐ 4. By soil

| Soil | Climate | ⭐ Why |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **Laterite** — red, leached, infertile | ⭐ **Equatorial / monsoon** | ⭐ Heat + rain leach everything; nutrients live in the **biomass** |
| ⭐⭐ **Chernozem** — black, deep, ⭐ **the most fertile on Earth** | ⭐ **Steppe** | ⭐ Grass roots add humus throughout; cold slows decay; low rain prevents leaching |
| ⭐ **Podzol** — ash-grey, acidic, hardpan | ⭐ **Siberian / taiga** | ⭐ Acidic conifer litter + leaching |
| ⭐ **Terra rossa** — red, on limestone | ⭐ **Mediterranean** | |
| ⭐ **Brown forest soil** | ⭐ **British type** | Moderately fertile |
| ⭐ **Saline / thin / stony** | ⭐ **Desert** | Evaporation draws salts upward |
| ⭐ **Gleysol / cryosol, peaty** | ⭐ **Tundra** | Waterlogged; decay too slow |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The soil-fertility principle in one line: FERTILITY = what is ADDED minus what is REMOVED.**
> ⭐ **Equatorial: added fast, removed faster → nothing. ⭐ Steppe: added steadily, removed barely → the world's best. ⭐ Taiga: added slowly AND leached → poor.**
> 🎯 *State this and then apply it, and you have answered any soil-formation question.*

### ⭐⭐ 6. By ocean current — and what the current causes

| Current | Warm/cold | ⭐ Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **North Atlantic Drift** | 🔥 Warm | ⭐⭐ **NW Europe mild; Bergen & Murmansk ice-free** |
| ⭐ **Gulf Stream** | 🔥 Warm | Feeds the NAD; meets Labrador at the **Grand Banks** |
| ⭐ **Kuroshio** | 🔥 Warm | China type; meets Oyashio off **Hokkaido** |
| ⭐ **Labrador** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ **Grand Banks fog + fishery; icebergs (Titanic)** |
| ⭐ **Oyashio** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ NW Pacific fishery |
| ⭐ **Canary** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ **Sahara** |
| ⭐ **Benguela** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ **Namib** |
| ⭐ **Peru / Humboldt** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐⭐ **Atacama; anchovy fishery; ENSO** |
| ⭐ **California** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ Mojave/Sonoran; San Francisco fog |
| ⭐ **West Australian** | ❄️ Cold | ⭐ Great Australian desert |

> ⭐⭐ **Cold currents on WEST margins → deserts + fog + upwelling fisheries + NO coral.**
> ⭐⭐ **Warm currents on EAST margins → rain all year + typhoons + coral reefs.**
> ⭐ **Where warm MEETS cold → fog + the world's great fishing grounds.**
> 🎯 *Three lines that connect four chapters. This is the connective tissue examiners reward.*

## ⚠️ THE EIGHT TRAPS

| # | The trap | ⭐ The truth |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ 1 | *"Equatorial is the hottest climate"* | ❌ ⭐ **HOT DESERTS are.** Equatorial cloud blocks insolation |
| ⭐⭐ 2 | *"Pampas is a savanna"* | ❌ ⭐ **TEMPERATE steppe.** Llanos and Campos are savanna |
| ⭐⭐ 3 | *"All deserts are on western margins"* | ❌ ⭐ **PATAGONIA is eastern** — it's a rain-shadow desert, a different mechanism |
| ⭐⭐ 4 | *"Wind is the main erosive agent in deserts"* | ❌ ⭐ **RUNNING WATER is**, even there. Wind is distinctive, not dominant |
| ⭐⭐ 5 | *"Melting Arctic sea ice raises sea level"* | ❌ ⭐ **It doesn't — it's already floating.** LAND ice (Greenland, Antarctica, glaciers) does |
| ⭐⭐ 6 | *"Desertification happens in deserts"* | ❌ ⭐ **It happens at the SEMI-ARID MARGINS** — the Sahel. You cannot desertify a desert |
| ⭐ 7 | *"All conifers are evergreen"* | ❌ ⭐ **LARCH is a deciduous conifer** — which is how it survives the coldest Siberia |
| ⭐ 8 | *"Rainforests are the lungs of the Earth"* | ❌ ⭐ **A mature forest is roughly carbon-neutral.** Its value is as a **carbon STOCK**, not an oxygen supply |
