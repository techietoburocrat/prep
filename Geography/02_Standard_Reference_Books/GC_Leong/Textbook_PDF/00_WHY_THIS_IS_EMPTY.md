# Why this folder is empty — and why that's fine

**G.C. Leong, *Certificate Physical and Human Geography* (Oxford University Press), is in print and copyrighted.** There is no free official PDF. The copies circulating online are scanned pirate uploads, so nothing was downloaded here.

**More importantly: you do not need it.** [The Leong notes are complete](../00_INDEX.md) — 25 files, 5,526 lines — and they were written as a **delta over the finished NCERTs**, not as a summary of the book. Every file opens with what NCERT already gave you and covers only the increment. [`00_INDEX.md`](../00_INDEX.md) also carries a full **skip register**: every chapter deliberately not covered, with its reason and its NCERT replacement.

**So the book's job here is already discharged.** Buy a physical copy if you like reading from paper — but there is no gap in the notes waiting on it, and a scanned copy would add nothing a script or I could use, since scans carry no text layer.
