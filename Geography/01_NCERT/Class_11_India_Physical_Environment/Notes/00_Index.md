### Geography + Environment, merged | UPSC CSE 2027

## 📌 Read This First

**This book is the application of everything you just learned.** ⭐ *Fundamentals of Physical Geography* taught you the **mechanisms** — plate tectonics, insolation, pressure and wind, weathering, ecology. ⭐ **This book applies all of it to one country.**

⭐ **That makes it easier than *Fundamentals* was, but MORE examinable.** UPSC asks far more questions about Indian geography than about world physical geography — and this is the definitive NCERT source for it.

## 🧭 The through-line — the standard sequence, applied to India

```
   WHERE IS IT?                          Ch.1   location, extent, neighbours
        ↓
   WHAT IS IT MADE OF, AND WHY?          Ch.2   geology → physiography
        ↓
   HOW DOES WATER MOVE ACROSS IT?        Ch.3   drainage
        ↓
   WHAT IS THE ATMOSPHERE DOING?         Ch.4   climate / monsoon
        ↓
   ┌── WHAT GROWS?                       Ch.5   vegetation
   └── WHAT DOES THE ROCK BECOME?        Ch.6   soils
        ↓
   ⭐ AND WHAT GOES WRONG?                Ch.7   hazards and disasters
```

> ⭐ **Notice that Ch.7 is the pay-off, not an appendix.** ⭐ **Every hazard in India is a consequence of something in Chapters 2–6:** earthquakes from the Himalayan collision (Ch.2), floods from the monsoon and river regimes (Ch.3–4), droughts from monsoon variability (Ch.4), landslides from young unconsolidated Shiwalik rock on steep slopes (Ch.2, 6), cyclones from Bay of Bengal conditions (Ch.4). ⭐ **Physical geography is the explanation; disaster management is the response.**
