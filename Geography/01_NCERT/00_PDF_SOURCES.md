## ⚠️ These are the FULL editions, not the ones on ncert.nic.in today

NCERT **rationalised** its textbooks in 2022–23 and deleted chapters. Your notes were written against the **full** pre-rationalisation books, and **every PYQ from 2011 to 2026 was set on them too.** So the PDFs here are the full editions — sourced from NCERT's own mirrored files on the Internet Archive where the live site no longer serves them.

🔴 **If you ever re-download these from ncert.nic.in you will silently lose 11 chapters.** Here is exactly what the current edition no longer contains:

| Book | Current NCERT edition | **Chapters it has dropped — you have them here** |
|---|---|---|
| Cl.6 *The Earth Our Habitat* | ❌ **withdrawn entirely** | Whole book. Replaced under NEP by *Exploring Society: India and Beyond* |
| Cl.7 *Our Environment* | 7 of 9 | **Natural Vegetation and Wildlife** · **Human Environment: Settlement, Transport and Communication** |
| Cl.8 *Resources and Development* | 5 of 6 | **Mineral and Power Resources** |
| Cl.9 *Contemporary India I* | ❌ **withdrawn from the site** | Whole book (6 chapters) |
| Cl.10 *Contemporary India II* | 7 of 7 ✅ | — nothing dropped |
| Cl.11 *Fundamentals of Physical Geography* | 14 of 16 | **Minerals and Rocks** · **Biodiversity and Conservation** |
| Cl.11 *India: Physical Environment* | 6 of 7 | **Soils** |
| Cl.12 *Fundamentals of Human Geography* | 8 of 10 | **Population Composition** · **Human Settlements** |
| Cl.12 *India: People and Economy* | 9 of 12 | **Migration** · **Human Development** · **Manufacturing Industries** |

**Look at that list as an exam list, not a bookkeeping one.** Soils, Minerals and Rocks, Mineral and Power Resources, Biodiversity and Conservation, Migration, Manufacturing — those are **exactly** the areas Prelims keeps asking about. A 2026-edition PDF, or coaching material built on one, will quietly skip them.

## ✅ How the filenames were verified

**Polity's Class 7/8/10 folders have PDFs whose names don't match their contents.** That is not going to be repeated here, so:

1. Every chapter PDF had its **first three pages converted to text** and checked for the significant words in the filename it was about to be given. **All 81 scored 100%** — every word present.
2. The check was **negative-controlled**: a Class 9 Economics chapter deliberately labelled `03_Drainage.pdf` scored **0%**, and one labelled `02_Physical_Features_of_India.pdf` scored 66%. So a passing score means something.

**Filenames match the note filenames exactly**, so `05_Minerals_and_Rocks.md` sits beside `05_Minerals_and_Rocks.pdf`.

## 🪤 The code traps I hit — recorded so nobody repeats them

**NCERT's file codes do not follow the pattern you'd guess, and three of them serve a completely different subject:**

| Guess | Reality |
|---|---|
| `hess2` = Class 8 Geography | ❌ **Class 8 History** (*Our Pasts III*). Geography is `hess4` |
| `jess2` = Class 10 Geography | ❌ **Class 10 Economics** (*Understanding Economic Development*). Geography is `jess1` |
| `iess2` = Class 9 Geography | ❌ **Class 9 Economics**. Geography is `iess1` |
| `kegy1` = Fundamentals of Physical Geography | ❌ `kegy1` is ***India: Physical Environment***. Fundamentals is **`kegy2`** |

**Each of those was caught by opening the PDF, not by reading the code.** Downloading on the guess alone would have put three wrong books into the folders with plausible-looking names.
