# Chapter 5 — Major Domains of the Earth 🌱

> **The one-line point:** The Earth has four domains — **rock (lithosphere), air (atmosphere), water (hydrosphere)** and **life (biosphere)**. The first three are physical. The fourth exists **only in the thin zone where the other three overlap**, and it is the entire subject matter of environmental science.

## 🎬 Start Here: Life Lives in a Very Thin Shell

The Earth's radius is about **6,400 km**. Nearly all life exists between roughly **8 km up** (the highest birds) and **11 km down** (the deepest ocean trench) — and the overwhelming majority within a few hundred metres of sea level.

```
   IF THE EARTH WERE AN APPLE…

        the ENTIRE BIOSPHERE — every forest,
        every ocean, every living thing —
        would be thinner than the SKIN.

              ╱▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔╲
            ╱  ░░░░░░░░░  ╲   ← the skin: where ALL life lives
           │  ░         ░  │
           │  ░  6,400   ░ │
           │  ░   km     ░ │   ← everything below is rock and
           │  ░ of rock  ░ │      metal. Sterile. No life.
            ╲  ░░░░░░░░░  ╱
              ╲_________╱
```

**That thinness is the whole point of environmental science.** The habitable layer is not a large, forgiving buffer — it is a skin. When you later read that atmospheric CO₂ has moved from 280 to 420+ parts per million, remember you are talking about a change to a shell thinner than an apple's skin wrapped around a ball of rock.

## 1️⃣ The Four Domains

| Domain | From | Is | Contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🪨 **LITHOSPHERE** | Gk *lithos* = stone | **Solid** | Crust, continents, ocean basins, landforms, soil, minerals |
| 💨 **ATMOSPHERE** | Gk *atmos* = vapour | **Gas** | The envelope of air held by gravity |
| 💧 **HYDROSPHERE** | Gk *hydor* = water | **Liquid/ice** | Oceans, seas, rivers, lakes, groundwater, glaciers |
| 🌿 **BIOSPHERE** | Gk *bios* = life | **Living** | All life — and it exists **only where the other three meet** |

```
   THE BIOSPHERE IS AN OVERLAP, NOT A SEPARATE LAYER

              💨 ATMOSPHERE
                ╱▔▔▔▔▔╲
              ╱         ╲
            ╱     ╱▔╲     ╲
          │     ╱ 🌿  ╲     │      The BIOSPHERE is the thin
          │   ╱   LIFE  ╲   │      zone where rock, air and
          │ ╱             ╲ │      water all touch.
          ╱─────────────────╲
     🪨 LITHOSPHERE    💧 HYDROSPHERE

     Life needs ALL THREE. Remove any one and it stops.
```

> 🔑 **This is the concept the whole environment syllabus rests on.** Life is not a fifth thing sitting on top of the Earth. It is what happens **in the overlap.** A tree needs soil (litho), air (atmo) and water (hydro) simultaneously. Remove any one and it dies. **That interdependence is why damage to one domain shows up in all the others** — and why "environment" cannot be studied as a separate subject from geography. It's the same system.

## 2️⃣ 🪨 The Lithosphere

The solid outer shell: the **crust** plus the rigid upper mantle. It carries two things — **continents** (land) and **ocean basins** (the floors beneath the seas).

**The split:** **~29% land, ~71% water.** Land is unevenly distributed — most of it in the Northern Hemisphere.

### The 7 continents — in order of size

| # | Continent | Share of land | Must-know facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **ASIA** | ~30% | **Largest** continent. **Largest population.** Separated from Europe by the **Ural Mountains**. Contains **Mt. Everest (8,849 m)** — the highest point on Earth — and the **Dead Sea**, the lowest land point. The **Tropic of Cancer passes through it** |
| **2** | **AFRICA** | ~20% | **Only continent the Equator, Tropic of Cancer, Tropic of Capricorn AND Prime Meridian all cross** ⭐. **Largest desert: Sahara.** **Longest river: Nile.** Largest hot desert on Earth. Site of the **Great Rift Valley** |
| **3** | **NORTH AMERICA** | ~16% | Linked to South America by the narrow **Isthmus of Panama**. Surrounded by ocean on three sides. Contains the **Great Lakes** |
| **4** | **SOUTH AMERICA** | ~12% | The **Amazon** — world's **largest river by volume** and largest rainforest. The **Andes**, the world's **longest mountain range** (~7,000 km). **Angel Falls**, the highest waterfall |
| **5** | **ANTARCTICA** | ~9% | Centred on the **South Pole**. Permanently **ice-covered**. **No permanent human population** — only research stations. India's: **Maitri, Bharati** (and the earlier Dakshin Gangotri) |
| **6** | **EUROPE** | ~7% | Much smaller than Asia; shares a landmass with it (**Eurasia**). Deeply indented coastline. Bounded east by the **Urals** |
| **7** | **AUSTRALIA** | ~5% | **Smallest continent.** The only one that is also a single country. Lies **entirely in the Southern Hemisphere** — hence "**island continent**". **Great Barrier Reef** off its NE coast |

> 🧠 **Mnemonic for size order:** **A**sia · **A**frica · **N**orth America · **S**outh America · **A**ntarctica · **E**urope · **A**ustralia
> → *"**A**ll **A**nimals **N**eed **S**ome **A**ir, **E**ven **A**nts."*

> 🎯 **Africa's four-line fact is a genuine Prelims favourite.** Equator ✅ Tropic of Cancer ✅ Tropic of Capricorn ✅ Prime Meridian ✅ — **no other continent has all four.** Learn it as a set.

### 🌊 The 5 oceans — in order of size

| # | Ocean | Must-know facts |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **PACIFIC** | **Largest** — covers about a third of the Earth. Roughly **circular**. Contains the **Mariana Trench**, the **deepest point on Earth (~11,000 m)** ⭐. Ringed by the **"Ring of Fire"** — most of the world's volcanoes and earthquakes |
| **2** | **ATLANTIC** | **S-shaped.** Busiest ocean for **trade** — it separates the two most industrialised coastlines. Most indented coastline → best natural harbours |
| **3** | **INDIAN** | ⭐ **The only ocean named after a country.** Roughly **triangular**. India's peninsula juts into it. Strategically central to India |
| **4** | **SOUTHERN** | Encircles **Antarctica**. Recognised as a distinct ocean relatively recently |
| **5** | **ARCTIC** | **Smallest and shallowest.** Around the **North Pole**, largely ice-covered. Connected to the Pacific by the **Bering Strait** |

> ⚓ **Why the Atlantic carries the most trade** — worth understanding, not memorising. Its **highly indented coastline** creates natural harbours, and it sits between Europe/Africa and the Americas, i.e. between the historically most industrialised regions. Geography produced the trade route, not the other way round.

> 🌱 **Ring of Fire — a disaster-management hook.** The Pacific's rim concentrates the world's earthquakes and volcanoes because it is where tectonic plates collide. The 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami, the 2011 Japan tsunami, Indonesian eruptions — all belong to this pattern. **Tag this for GS-III disaster management.**

## 3️⃣ 💨 The Atmosphere

The envelope of gases held to the Earth by **gravity**, extending ~1,600 km up, though ~99% of its mass sits in the lowest ~32 km.

### Composition of dry air

```
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ NITROGEN  ████████████████████████████████  78%  │
   │ OXYGEN    ████████                          21%  │
   │ OTHERS    ▌                                  1%  │  ← argon, CO₂,
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘     neon, helium…
```

| Gas | Share | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Nitrogen** | **78%** | Essential for plant growth — but plants can't use it from the air directly. **Bacteria fix it into the soil.** The basis of all fertiliser |
| **Oxygen** | **21%** | **Respiration** — all animals and plants. Also enables combustion |
| **Carbon dioxide** | ~0.04% | 🌱 **Photosynthesis** (plants need it) **and** the greenhouse effect. **Tiny share, enormous influence** |
| **Others** | rest of 1% | Argon (most of it), neon, helium, ozone, water vapour, dust |

> 🌱 **The CO₂ point is the one to hold on to.** Carbon dioxide is about **four hundredths of one percent** of the atmosphere — and it regulates the temperature of the entire planet. **Small quantity does not mean small importance.** This is exactly why a rise from 280 ppm (pre-industrial) to 420+ ppm today matters so much: proportionally it's a huge increase in the gas that controls Earth's heat balance.
>
> ⚠️ A common error to avoid: **oxygen is not a greenhouse gas.** The main ones are **CO₂, methane, water vapour, nitrous oxide and CFCs**.

### The 5 layers — in order upward

```
   ▲ ~1,600 km
   │  5. EXOSPHERE      Extremely thin. Merges into space.
   │                    Satellites orbit here.
   │  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  ~400 km
   │  4. THERMOSPHERE   Temperature RISES steeply.
   │                    Contains the IONOSPHERE, which
   │                    REFLECTS RADIO WAVES back to Earth
   │                    → long-distance radio works.
   │  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  ~80 km
   │  3. MESOSPHERE     Temperature FALLS. Coldest layer.
   │                    METEORS BURN UP HERE. 🌠
   │  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  ~50 km
   │  2. STRATOSPHERE   Calm, cloudless, stable.
   │                    🌱 CONTAINS THE OZONE LAYER.
   │                    ✈️ JETS FLY HERE (smooth air).
   │                    Temperature RISES with height.
   │  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  ~13 km
   │  1. TROPOSPHERE    🌦️ ALL WEATHER happens here.
   │                    Clouds, rain, wind, storms.
   │                    Temperature FALLS with height.
   ▼  ─────────────────────────────────────────────  Sea level
      Earth
```

| Layer | Key fact |
|---|---|
| **Troposphere** | ⭐ **All weather.** Height ~8 km at poles, ~18 km at equator. Temperature **falls** with height |
| **Stratosphere** | ⭐ **Ozone layer.** Aircraft fly here — no clouds, no turbulence. Temperature **rises** with height |
| **Mesosphere** | ⭐ **Meteors burn up here.** The **coldest** layer |
| **Thermosphere** | Contains the **ionosphere**, which **reflects radio waves**. Very hot |
| **Exosphere** | Outermost, thinnest; merges with space |

> 🧠 **Mnemonic (bottom → top):** **T**roposphere · **S**tratosphere · **M**esosphere · **T**hermosphere · **E**xosphere → **"The Sky MustТи Empty"** — or simply **T-S-M-T-E**.

> ✈️ **Why aircraft fly in the stratosphere** — a nice piece of connected reasoning. Weather (turbulence, storms, clouds) is confined to the troposphere. Climb above it and the air is stable and thin, which means a smoother ride and less drag, hence less fuel. The layers aren't trivia; they explain things.

### 🌱 The ozone layer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | In the **stratosphere** (~15–35 km up) |
| **What it does** | Absorbs the Sun's harmful **ultraviolet (UV)** radiation |
| **Why it matters** | Without it, UV would cause **skin cancer, cataracts, immune damage**, and would kill phytoplankton — the base of the entire marine food chain |
| **What damaged it** | **CFCs** (chlorofluorocarbons) from refrigerants, aerosols, foams |
| **The response** | **Montreal Protocol, 1987** — phased out CFCs |
| **Status** | ⭐ **Healing.** The ozone layer is on track to recover by roughly the middle of this century |

> 🌱🎯 **The Montreal Protocol is the most useful example in the entire environment syllabus, and you should learn it now.**
>
> It is **the only major environmental treaty that unambiguously worked** — universally ratified, targets met, the problem measurably reversing. In any Mains answer or essay about international environmental cooperation, it is the counter-example to climate-treaty pessimism.
>
> ⚠️ **And know the distinction that UPSC tests:** **ozone depletion and global warming are two different problems.** Different gases (CFCs vs CO₂), different layers (stratosphere vs troposphere), different mechanisms (UV absorption vs heat trapping), different treaties (Montreal vs Paris). They overlap only in that CFCs happen to also be greenhouse gases. **Confusing the two is one of the most common errors in environment answers.**

### 🌱 Why the atmosphere matters

1. **Supplies oxygen and CO₂** — respiration and photosynthesis.
2. **Traps heat** — the greenhouse effect keeps Earth ~33°C warmer than it would otherwise be (Ch.1).
3. **Blocks UV** — via the ozone layer.
4. **Burns up meteoroids** — in the mesosphere.
5. **Distributes heat and water** — winds and the water cycle.

## 4️⃣ 💧 The Hydrosphere

All the water on Earth — ocean, sea, river, lake, groundwater, glacier, water vapour.

### ⭐ The water distribution — a fact with real consequences

```
   ALL THE WATER ON EARTH

   ████████████████████████████████████████████  97%  SALT (oceans)
   ██                                             3%  FRESH
                                                       │
                                    ┌──────────────────┘
                                    ▼
                     ~69% locked in GLACIERS & ICE CAPS
                     ~30% GROUNDWATER
                     ~1%  rivers, lakes, atmosphere ← what we
                                                      easily use
```

**So:** of all Earth's water, **only ~3% is fresh**, and most of *that* is frozen or deep underground. **Less than 1% of the planet's water is readily accessible fresh water.**

> 🌱 **This single statistic is the foundation of the entire water-security debate**, and it's why the "Blue Planet" name is slightly misleading. Earth is drenched in water and simultaneously short of usable water. That paradox drives:
> - **Groundwater depletion** — India is the **world's largest user of groundwater**, and extraction now exceeds recharge across much of the north-west
> - **Interstate river disputes** — Cauvery, Krishna, Ravi-Beas
> - **Glacier melt** — Himalayan glaciers feed the Ganga, Indus and Brahmaputra; they are retreating
> - **Water pollution** — contaminating the small usable fraction shrinks it further
>
> **Tag all of this for GS-III.** It also links straight to your [Current Affairs](../../../../Current%20Affairs/00_START_HERE.md) work.

### The water cycle — the engine linking three domains

```
        ☀️ Sun's energy drives the whole loop

            ☁️  CONDENSATION → clouds
             ▲              │
             │              ▼
        EVAPORATION      PRECIPITATION
        (ocean, lakes)   (rain, snow)
             ▲              │
             │              ▼
        ═══════════════════════════
             ◄── RUN-OFF ──  rivers
                            & infiltration
                            to groundwater
```

> 🔗 **Notice what the water cycle actually is: the hydrosphere, atmosphere and lithosphere exchanging material continuously**, powered by the Sun, with the biosphere participating throughout (plants transpire). **It is the clearest possible demonstration that the four domains are one system.** This is why the merged subject is the right call — you cannot explain the water cycle as "geography" or "environment"; it is simply both.

## 5️⃣ 🌿 The Biosphere — Where This Becomes Environmental Science

> **Biosphere** = the narrow zone where the lithosphere, atmosphere and hydrosphere meet and interact, and where **all life** exists.

### 🌱 Ecosystem — the core concept

> **Ecosystem** = a community of living organisms **together with the non-living environment** they interact with, functioning as a unit.

The definition contains the whole idea: **living + non-living, treated as one system.** A pond is an ecosystem — fish, algae, insects, plus the water, sunlight, dissolved oxygen and mud. Remove the non-living half from the definition and it collapses.

**Examples:** forest, pond, desert, grassland, coral reef, mangrove.

### The two great divisions of life

| **PLANT KINGDOM (flora)** | **ANIMAL KINGDOM (fauna)** |
|---|---|
| Make their own food by **photosynthesis** | Cannot; must eat plants or other animals |
| **Producers** | **Consumers** |

### 🌱 The food chain — how energy moves

```
   ☀️  SUN
        │  energy captured by
        ▼
   🌿 PRODUCERS (green plants)
        │  eaten by
        ▼
   🐛 PRIMARY CONSUMERS (herbivores — deer, insects)
        │  eaten by
        ▼
   🦊 SECONDARY CONSUMERS (carnivores — fox, snake)
        │  eaten by
        ▼
   🦅 TOP CONSUMERS (tiger, eagle)
        │  all levels eventually broken down by
        ▼
   🍄 DECOMPOSERS (bacteria, fungi)
        │  return nutrients to the soil
        └──────────► back to PRODUCERS
```

**Two things to take from this:**

1. **Every arrow starts at the Sun.** All energy in every ecosystem is captured sunlight (Ch.1).
2. **Decomposers close the loop.** Without bacteria and fungi returning nutrients to the soil, the cycle would run down. They are the least glamorous and most essential part of the system.

> 🌱 **Why removing one species can collapse a system.** Food chains interlock into **food webs**. Remove the tiger and deer populations explode; the deer overgraze; the forest thins; the soil erodes; the water table drops. **This is why conservation targets predators** — the "umbrella species" logic behind Project Tiger. You'll meet this again in Ch.8 and in far more depth in Cl.12 Biology.

### 🌱 Why life is confined to a thin shell

Life needs the overlap of all three physical domains **plus** liveable conditions:

| Requirement | Supplied by |
|---|---|
| Oxygen / CO₂ | Atmosphere |
| Water | Hydrosphere |
| Nutrients, minerals, anchorage | Lithosphere (soil) |
| Energy | The Sun |
| Tolerable temperature and pressure | Only within a narrow band |

Go too high and there's not enough oxygen or warmth. Go too deep into rock and there's no light, water or air. **The habitable band is thin — and the human enterprise is now large enough to alter it.** That sentence is the whole of environmental science.

### 🌱 How damage propagates across domains

This is the payoff of the chapter — and a genuinely strong structure for a Mains answer.

| Action | Lithosphere | Atmosphere | Hydrosphere | Biosphere |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deforestation** | Soil erosion, fertility loss | More CO₂, less O₂ | Reduced rainfall, faster run-off, floods | Habitat and species loss |
| **Burning fossil fuels** | Mining scars land | CO₂ ↑, warming, acid rain | Ocean acidification, sea-level rise | Coral bleaching, range shifts |
| **Over-extracting groundwater** | Land subsidence | — | Aquifers depleted | Wetlands and their species dry out |
| **Plastic waste** | Soil contamination | Toxins if burned | Ocean plastic, microplastics | Ingestion, entanglement, food-chain entry |

> 🎯 **Use this table structure in GS-III answers.** Most candidates describe an environmental problem in one dimension. Tracing it across all four domains — *this is the cause, here is how it moves through rock, air, water and life* — is exactly the systems thinking that scores well. And it comes straight out of a Class 6 chapter.

**Trap 1:** *"The ozone layer is in the troposphere."* **No — stratosphere.** (Ozone *at ground level* is a pollutant; ozone *in the stratosphere* is protective. Same molecule, opposite roles.)
**Trap 2:** *"Ozone depletion causes global warming."* **No — different problems.** Different gases, layers, mechanisms, treaties.
**Trap 3:** *"Oxygen is the main greenhouse gas."* No — **CO₂, methane, water vapour, N₂O, CFCs**. Oxygen isn't one.
**Trap 4:** *"3% of Earth's water is available fresh water."* Only ~3% is fresh, but most is **frozen or deep underground**. Readily usable is **under 1%**.
**Trap 5:** *"Australia is the smallest continent and Europe the second smallest."* Correct — but note **Antarctica is larger than both Europe and Australia**. People routinely underestimate it.
**Trap 6:** Temperature **falls** with height in the troposphere but **rises** in the stratosphere. The reversal is deliberate and testable.

## 🔁 60-Second Recap

1. **Four domains: lithosphere (rock), atmosphere (air), hydrosphere (water), biosphere (life).**
2. ⭐ **The biosphere is not a layer — it is the thin overlap of the other three.** Thinner than an apple's skin, relatively.
3. **Land ~29%, water ~71%.**
4. **7 continents by size:** Asia > Africa > N.America > S.America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia.
5. ⭐ **Africa alone is crossed by the Equator, both Tropics and the Prime Meridian.**
6. **5 oceans by size:** Pacific > Atlantic > Indian > Southern > Arctic. **Mariana Trench (Pacific) is the deepest point on Earth.**
7. **The Indian Ocean is the only ocean named after a country.**
8. **Air: 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 1% others.** CO₂ is ~0.04% — tiny share, planetary influence.
9. **5 layers:** Troposphere (**weather**) → Stratosphere (**ozone**, aircraft) → Mesosphere (**meteors burn**) → Thermosphere (**ionosphere**, radio) → Exosphere.
10. 🌱 **Ozone sits in the stratosphere and blocks UV.** CFCs damaged it; the **Montreal Protocol (1987)** is fixing it — the one environmental treaty that clearly worked.
11. 🌱 **Ozone depletion ≠ global warming.** Different gases, layers, mechanisms and treaties.
12. **97% of water is salt; 3% fresh — and most of that is frozen or underground.** Under 1% is readily usable.
13. 🌱 **An ecosystem is living + non-living together.** Energy flows Sun → producers → consumers → decomposers → back to soil.
14. 🌱 **Damage in one domain propagates to all four.** That propagation is the core insight of the chapter — and a ready-made Mains structure.

2. Consider the following statements:
   1. The ozone layer is located in the troposphere.
   2. Meteors generally burn up in the mesosphere.
   3. Temperature increases with height in the stratosphere.
   → *2 and 3 only. Ozone is in the **stratosphere**.*

3. Arrange in decreasing order of size: Antarctica, Europe, South America, Australia.
   → *South America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia.*

4. 🌱 With reference to the Montreal Protocol:
   1. It was adopted in 1987.
   2. It targets greenhouse gases responsible for global warming.
   3. It phased out chlorofluorocarbons.
   → *1 and 3 only. It addresses **ozone depletion**, a different problem from global warming.*

5. Of all the water on Earth, the proportion that is fresh is approximately:
   → *3% — and most of that is locked in glaciers and ice caps.*

6. The Mariana Trench is located in the:
   → *Pacific Ocean.*
