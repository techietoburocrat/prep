# 🏭 Class 10 — *Contemporary India II*
### Geography + Environment, merged | UPSC CSE 2027

---

## 📌 Read This First

**The most Prelims-dense book in the junior series.** Class 9 covered India's *physical* geography; Class 10 covers its **economic** geography — soils, forests, water, crops, minerals, industry and transport.

⭐ **This book contains more directly askable facts per page than any other NCERT you will read at this level.** Soil types and their regions, crop conditions and top producers, mineral belts, industrial locations, transport networks — all of it appears in Prelims year after year.

### ⚖️ Depth — read this one properly

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | ⭐ **~8–9 hours** — the longest of the junior books |
| **Approach** | **Not a skim.** Tables here are worth memorising |
| **Map work** | ⭐ Essential — soil regions, mineral belts, industrial centres, highways |

**Ch.1 (soils), Ch.4 (crops) and Ch.5 (minerals) are the three highest-yield chapters** in the entire junior series for Prelims.

## 🧭 The through-line

```
   WHAT DO WE HAVE?
        Resources, land, SOIL                Ch.1
        Forests and wildlife                 Ch.2
        Water                                Ch.3
             ↓
   WHAT DO WE PRODUCE FROM IT?
        Crops (primary)                      Ch.4
        Minerals and energy (primary)        Ch.5
        Manufactured goods (secondary)       Ch.6
             ↓
   HOW DOES IT ALL MOVE?
        Transport, communication, trade      Ch.7
```

> 🔑 **Notice that Chapters 2 and 3 are as much about *conflict* as about resources** — the Chipko movement, Narmada Bachao Andolan, Joint Forest Management. **NCERT is deliberately showing you that resource questions are political questions.** That framing is exactly what GS-II and GS-III reward, and it's why this book is more useful for Mains than its Class 10 label suggests.
