# Chapter 1 — Environment 🌱

> **The one-line point:** The environment is everything around us — and it comes in **three layers**: the **natural** environment we were given, the **human** environment we form as societies, and the **human-made** environment we build on top. Most environmental problems are the third layer damaging the first.

## 1️⃣ The Three Environments

```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  1. NATURAL ENVIRONMENT                            │
   │     Land, water, air, plants, animals              │
   │     → given to us; not made by anyone              │
   ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │  2. HUMAN ENVIRONMENT                              │
   │     Individuals, family, community, religion,      │
   │     economy, politics                              │
   │     → the social world humans form                 │
   ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │  3. HUMAN-MADE ENVIRONMENT                         │
   │     Buildings, roads, parks, bridges, industries,  │
   │     monuments, transport                           │
   │     → what humans construct                        │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> 🔑 **The relationship between them is the whole story.** Humans take from the natural environment (resources), transform it (the human-made environment), and organise themselves to do so (the human environment). **Environmental degradation is what happens when layer 3 grows faster than layer 1 can absorb.**

## 2️⃣ Biotic and Abiotic

| | **BIOTIC** | **ABIOTIC** |
|---|---|---|
| **Meaning** | The world of **living** things | The world of **non-living** things |
| **Includes** | Plants, animals, humans, microbes | Land, water, air, rocks, sunlight, temperature |

**Both together make up the environment.** Neither alone is sufficient — which is exactly the point of the next definition.

---

## 3️⃣ 🌱 Ecosystem — The Core Definition

> **Ecosystem** = a system formed by the **interaction of all living organisms with each other and with the physical (non-living) environment** they live in.

**Note what the definition insists on:** it is not "the living things in a place." It is living things **plus** their physical surroundings **plus the interaction between them.** Remove any of the three and it isn't an ecosystem.

| Examples | |
|---|---|
| **Natural** | Forest, pond, desert, grassland, ocean, mangrove, coral reef |
| **Human-made** | Aquarium, garden, crop field, park |

> 🐟 **The comparison that fits: an aquarium.** It contains fish (biotic), water, gravel, light and temperature (abiotic), and a set of dependencies between them (fish need oxygen the plants produce; plants need the nutrients fish waste supplies). **Change one thing — turn off the light, overfeed, raise the temperature — and the whole tank responds.** That responsiveness is what makes it a system rather than a collection.
>
> A forest is the same thing at a scale where the feedbacks take decades instead of days.

## 5️⃣ 🌱 Where the Conflict Comes From

Humans have always modified the environment. What changed is **scale and speed.**

```
   EARLY HUMANS                    MODERN HUMANS
   ─────────────                   ─────────────
   Adapted TO nature       →       Adapt nature TO themselves
   Small populations       →       8 billion people
   Hand tools              →       Industrial machinery
   Local impact            →       Planetary impact
   Waste decomposed        →       Plastic, nuclear, chemical waste
                                   that does not
```

**Consequences:**

| Human action | Environmental cost |
|---|---|
| Farming, cities, roads | **Habitat loss**, deforestation |
| Industry, vehicles | Air and water **pollution** |
| Fossil fuel burning | **Global warming** |
| Mining | Land degradation, soil loss |
| Overuse of resources | Depletion — groundwater, forests, fisheries |

> 🌱 **The framing that scores in Mains:** the problem is not that humans use the environment — every organism does. It is that humans have become the **dominant force shaping it**, faster than natural systems can adjust. This is the idea behind the term **Anthropocene**. Whether it's an official geological epoch is disputed; as a *framing* for an answer it's excellent.

## 🔁 60-Second Recap

1. **Three environments: natural, human, human-made.**
2. **Biotic = living, abiotic = non-living.** Both together form the environment.
3. ⭐ **An ecosystem is living + non-living + their interaction** — all three parts of the definition matter.
4. **The four domains** (litho, atmo, hydro, bio) each get a chapter in this book.
5. 🌱 **The environmental problem is one of scale and speed**, not of use as such — humans now modify the planet faster than it can adjust.
