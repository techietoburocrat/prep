# 🌱 Class 7 — *Our Environment*
### Geography + Environment, merged | UPSC CSE 2027

---

## 📌 Read This First

This is **the environment book of the NCERT series** — the one that most justifies the merge you asked for. Class 6 introduced the four domains in a single chapter. Class 7 gives **a whole chapter to each of them** and then adds the human layer on top.

### What changes from Class 6

| Class 6 asked | Class 7 asks |
|---|---|
| *What* is the atmosphere? | *How does it work* — pressure, winds, rainfall types |
| *What* is the hydrosphere? | *How does it move* — waves, tides, currents |
| *What* is the lithosphere? | *What's inside it* — crust, mantle, core, rocks, plates |
| *What* is the biosphere? | *How is vegetation distributed* across the whole world |
| — | **How humans interact with all of it** |

**Class 6 was a vocabulary install. Class 7 is the first pass at mechanism.** Class 11 will do it a third time with real physics — but the causal chains you build here are the ones that survive.

### ⚖️ Depth for this book

Still **Stage 1 — a fast pass.** Roughly **5–6 hours** for the whole book. But three chapters carry disproportionate weight and deserve slower reading:

- **Ch.3 Our Changing Earth** — earthquakes, volcanoes and landform processes. Heavy Prelims content.
- **Ch.4 Air** — pressure and wind systems. This is the direct parent of Class 11's hardest material.
- **Ch.6 Natural Vegetation and Wildlife** — the world's biomes. Pure environment, pure Prelims.

## 🧭 The through-line

```
   WHAT IS THE ENVIRONMENT?              Ch.1
        ↓
   THE SOLID EARTH  ───────  inside it   Ch.2  (rocks, interior)
                    └──────  changing it Ch.3  (plates, quakes, erosion)
        ↓
   THE AIR                               Ch.4
        ↓
   THE WATER                             Ch.5
        ↓
   THE LIFE                              Ch.6  (world biomes)
        ↓
   THE HUMANS ON TOP OF ALL OF IT        Ch.7  (settlement, transport)
        ↓                                Ch.8  (two river basins compared)
   LIVING IN HARD PLACES                 Ch.9  (two deserts compared)
```

**Chapters 2–6 are the four domains, one at a time. Chapters 7–9 add people.** That is the whole book.

> 🔗 **Notice the design of Ch.8 and Ch.9:** both are **paired case studies** — Amazon vs Ganga-Brahmaputra, Sahara vs Ladakh. NCERT is teaching you to compare, not just describe. **That comparative habit is exactly what Mains rewards**, so don't skip these as "just examples."
