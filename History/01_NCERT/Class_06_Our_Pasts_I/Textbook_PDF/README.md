# NCERT Class 6 History — the actual textbook PDFs

Downloaded 2026-08-05. **Two different books are here, because NCERT replaced the Class 6 History textbook.** Read this before you open them.

---

## ⚠️ The situation in one paragraph

*Our Pasts I* — the standalone Class 6 History book that every UPSC aspirant has used for twenty years — **has been withdrawn by NCERT.** Under NEP 2020, History, Geography and Civics were merged into a single Class 6 book called ***Exploring Society: India and Beyond***. Every `fess1*.pdf` URL on `ncert.nic.in` now returns **404**. The old book is only obtainable from archives.

**So this folder has both:** the old book (from the Internet Archive) because it is what your notes follow and what the exam ecosystem is built on, and the new official book (from ncert.nic.in) because it is what NCERT currently publishes.

## 📁 `Exploring_Society_India_and_Beyond_current/` — the current official book

The book NCERT actually publishes today. **14 chapters + front matter, 211 MB** (image-heavy, hence the size).

Downloaded from `ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/fees1*.pdf`. Every file opened and identified; filenames reflect real content.

**Only 4 of its 14 chapters are History**, marked `HISTORY_` in the filename:

| Chapter | Covers |
|---|---|
| `04_HISTORY_Timeline_and_Sources_of_History` | ≈ the notes' Ch.1 |
| `05_HISTORY_India_That_Is_Bharat` | Names of the land, geography — partly the notes' Ch.1 |
| `06_HISTORY_The_Beginnings_of_Indian_Civilisation` | ≈ the notes' Ch.2–3 (Harappa), heavily compressed |
| `07_HISTORY_Indias_Cultural_Roots` | ≈ the notes' Ch.4 and Ch.6 (Vedas, Buddhism, Jainism) |

The rest is Geography (Ch.2–3), Society (Ch.8–9), **Civics — the "Grassroots Democracy" trio (Ch.10–12)**, and Economics (Ch.13–14).

> 🔎 **Note for the Polity folder:** chapters 10–12 here are the replacement for the old Class 6 Civics book *Social and Political Life I*. If you ever want the current text behind your Polity Class 6 notes, it is these three files.

### Why this book is *not* enough on its own for UPSC

It compresses all of ancient India into roughly four short chapters and drops most of what Prelims actually asks about — the Mahajanapadas, the Mauryan administration, the Sangam Age, the Kushanas, Gupta administration, and most of the art and architecture. **Study `Our_Pasts_I_original_edition/`; keep this one for reference and for the current NCERT framing** (it emphasises *Jambudvipa*, "India, that is Bharat", and cultural continuity more than the old book did).
