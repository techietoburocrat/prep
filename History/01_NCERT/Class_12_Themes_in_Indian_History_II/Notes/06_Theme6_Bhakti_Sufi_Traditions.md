# Theme 6 — Bhakti–Sufi Traditions
## *Changes in Religious Beliefs and Devotional Texts (c. eighth to eighteenth century)*

> **The one-line point:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This is the single highest-yield chapter in Class 12 Part II — the Art & Culture core, the GS1 Society core, and a thousand years of religious history in one place.** *And its organising insight is that **the great traditions grew by ABSORBING what was already there**: a local wooden tribal deity becomes Jagannatha; a stone smeared with ochre becomes Parvati; a Sufi *khanqah* adopts yogic exercises and head-shaving; and Islam enters the Deccan villages **through lullabies and songs sung at the grinding stone.**

## 1️⃣ A Mosaic of Religious Beliefs and Practices ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> *"Perhaps the most striking feature of this phase is **THE INCREASING VISIBILITY OF A WIDE RANGE OF GODS AND GODDESSES IN SCULPTURE AS WELL AS IN TEXTS.** At one level, this indicates the **continued and even extended worship of the major deities — VISHNU, SHIVA and THE GODDESS — each of whom was visualised in a variety of forms.**"*

### 1.1 The integration of cults — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ TWO processes, and you must give both

```
   ** PROCESS 1 -- DISSEMINATION OF BRAHMANICAL IDEAS **
   ⭐ ** "exemplified by the COMPOSITION, COMPILATION AND
      PRESERVATION OF PURANIC TEXTS IN SIMPLE SANSKRIT VERSE,
      EXPLICITLY MEANT TO BE ACCESSIBLE TO WOMEN AND SHUDRAS,
      who were generally EXCLUDED FROM VEDIC LEARNING." **

   ** PROCESS 2 -- BRAHMANAS ABSORBING LOCAL BELIEF **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "that of THE BRAHMANAS ACCEPTING AND REWORKING
      THE BELIEFS AND PRACTICES OF THESE AND OTHER SOCIAL
      CATEGORIES." **

   ** AND THE MECHANISM: "many beliefs and practices were
      shaped through A CONTINUOUS DIALOGUE between what
      sociologists have described as ** 'GREAT' SANSKRITIC
      PURANIC TRADITIONS ** and ** 'LITTLE' TRADITIONS **
      throughout the land." **
```

**📦 THE "GREAT" AND "LITTLE" TRADITIONS BOX — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ a named concept, and NCERT's discomfort with it is examinable**

> *The terms were **coined by a sociologist named ROBERT REDFIELD in the twentieth century** to describe **the cultural practices of PEASANT SOCIETIES.** ⭐ *He found that peasants **observed rituals and customs that EMANATED FROM DOMINANT SOCIAL CATEGORIES, including PRIESTS AND RULERS** — these he classified as the **GREAT TRADITION**. At the same time peasants **also followed LOCAL PRACTICES that did not necessarily correspond** — the **LITTLE TRADITION.** ⭐ **He also noticed that BOTH CHANGED OVER TIME, THROUGH A PROCESS OF INTERACTION.**"*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And NCERT's caveat:** *"While scholars accept the significance of these categories and processes, **THEY ARE OFTEN UNCOMFORTABLE WITH THE HIERARCHY SUGGESTED BY THE TERMS GREAT AND LITTLE. THE USE OF QUOTATION MARKS FOR 'GREAT' AND 'LITTLE' IS ONE WAY OF INDICATING THIS.**"*

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NOTICE WHAT NCERT IS DOING — IT IS CRITICISING ITS OWN ANALYTICAL VOCABULARY, AND SAYING SO IN PUNCTUATION.** ⭐ *The **quotation marks are an argument**: a tradition is not *"little"* because it belongs to peasants rather than priests. **The terms describe WHO holds a practice, and smuggle in a judgement about its VALUE.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And Redfield's own finding cuts against the hierarchy: BOTH changed, THROUGH INTERACTION.** **The traffic runs in both directions — which is Process 2.**)*

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ JAGANNATHA AT PURI — the worked example, and the best in the chapter**

> *"One of **the most striking examples of this process** is evident at **PURI, ORISSA**, where **the principal deity was identified, BY THE TWELFTH CENTURY, as JAGANNATHA (literally, THE LORD OF THE WORLD), A FORM OF VISHNU.**"*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"In this instance, **A LOCAL DEITY, WHOSE IMAGE WAS AND CONTINUES TO BE MADE OF WOOD BY LOCAL TRIBAL SPECIALISTS, WAS RECOGNISED AS A FORM OF VISHNU. At the same time, VISHNU WAS VISUALISED IN A WAY THAT WAS VERY DIFFERENT FROM THAT IN OTHER PARTS OF THE COUNTRY.**"*

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **READ THE SECOND SENTENCE CAREFULLY, BECAUSE IT IS WHERE THE ARGUMENT LIVES — THE EXCHANGE WENT BOTH WAYS.** ⭐ **The local deity gained an identity as Vishnu; and VISHNU GAINED A FORM HE HAS NOWHERE ELSE IN INDIA.** *NCERT's own instruction is to compare **Fig. 6.2 (Jagannatha, Subhadra and Balarama)** with **[Part I Fig. 4.26 (Vishnu reclining on Sheshnag at Deogarh)](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md)** — *"you will notice that **the deity is represented in a VERY DIFFERENT WAY.**"* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Absorption is not conquest. The absorbing tradition is changed by what it absorbs.** *(⭐ **And the tribal specialists STILL MAKE THE IMAGE — *"was AND CONTINUES TO BE".*** **The integration did not displace the people who began it.**)*

**🪔 THE GODDESS CULTS — the same process, a different mechanism:**

> *"Worship of the goddess, **often simply in the form of A STONE SMEARED WITH OCHRE, was evidently WIDESPREAD.** ⭐ **These LOCAL DEITIES were often INCORPORATED WITHIN THE PURANIC FRAMEWORK BY PROVIDING THEM WITH AN IDENTITY AS A WIFE OF THE PRINCIPAL MALE DEITIES — sometimes they were EQUATED WITH LAKSHMI, THE WIFE OF VISHNU, in other instances, with PARVATI, THE WIFE OF SHIVA.**"*

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NOTE THE DIFFERENCE IN MECHANISM, AND IT IS A GENDERED ONE WORTH POINTING OUT.** ⭐ **A local male deity is absorbed as a FORM of Vishnu — an identity of his own, at Puri, in his own image.** *A local **goddess** is absorbed **as somebody's WIFE.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The integrating framework had a slot ready for a male deity as a manifestation, and for a female deity as a consort.** *(⭐ Compare **[Part I Theme 4 §10.2's avatars](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md)** — *"recognising each of these local deities as a form of Vishnu was one way of creating a more unified religious tradition."* **Same project, and this chapter shows the female half of it.**)*

### 1.2 Difference and conflict ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ** TANTRIC PRACTICES -- and note the two features NCERT
      highlights **
   ================================================================
   ** "Often associated with the goddess were forms of worship
      classified as TANTRIC. Tantric practices were WIDESPREAD
      in several parts of the subcontinent: " **
   * ⭐ ** "they were OPEN TO WOMEN AND MEN" **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "practitioners often IGNORED DIFFERENCES OF
     CASTE AND CLASS WITHIN THE RITUAL CONTEXT" **
   ** "Many of these ideas INFLUENCED SHAIVISM AS WELL AS
      BUDDHISM, especially in the EASTERN, NORTHERN and
      SOUTHERN parts of the subcontinent." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** VEDIC vs PURANIC -- the divergence, stated
      bluntly **
   ================================================================
   ** "All of these somewhat divergent and even disparate
      beliefs and practices WOULD COME TO BE CLASSIFIED AS
      HINDU OVER THE COURSE OF THE NEXT MILLENNIUM." **

   ⭐ ** "THE PRINCIPAL DEITIES OF THE VEDIC PANTHEON -- AGNI,
      INDRA AND SOMA -- BECOME MARGINAL FIGURES, RARELY VISIBLE
      IN TEXTUAL OR VISUAL REPRESENTATIONS." **
   ⭐ ** "And while we can catch a glimpse of VISHNU, SHIVA and
      THE GODDESS in Vedic mantras, THESE HAVE LITTLE IN COMMON
      WITH THE ELABORATE PURANIC MYTHOLOGIES." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "HOWEVER, IN SPITE OF THESE OBVIOUS
      DISCREPANCIES, THE VEDAS CONTINUED TO BE REVERED AS
      AUTHORITATIVE." **

   ================================================================
   ** THE CONFLICTS -- four of them, and NCERT names all four **
   ================================================================
   1. ** "those who valued the Vedic tradition often CONDEMNED
      PRACTICES THAT WENT BEYOND THE CLOSELY REGULATED CONTACT
      WITH THE DIVINE through the performance of SACRIFICES or
      PRECISELY CHANTED MANTRAS." **
   2. ** "those engaged in TANTRIC PRACTICES FREQUENTLY IGNORED
      THE AUTHORITY OF THE VEDAS." **
   3. ** "devotees often tended to PROJECT THEIR CHOSEN DEITY,
      EITHER VISHNU OR SHIVA, AS SUPREME." **
   4. ** "Relations with other traditions, such as BUDDHISM or
      JAINISM, were also often FRAUGHT WITH TENSION IF NOT OPEN
      CONFLICT." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE VEDIC/PURANIC POINT IS ONE OF THE MOST QUOTABLE IN THE CHAPTER AND IT SURPRISES MOST STUDENTS: AGNI, INDRA AND SOMA — the great gods of the Rigveda — BECOME MARGINAL FIGURES.** ⭐ *The deities of the **oldest** stratum largely **disappear from worship**, while **Vishnu, Shiva and the goddess**, barely glimpsed in the Vedas, become central. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the Vedas remain AUTHORITATIVE throughout.** *That combination — **a scripture revered as supreme whose principal gods are no longer worshipped** — is the single best illustration of how a living tradition actually works. *(⭐ **[Part I Theme 4 §2.1](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md)** gave you the Rigvedic hymns *"in praise of a variety of deities, ESPECIALLY AGNI, INDRA and SOMA."* **This chapter tells you what became of them.**)*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And keep the Tantric point for gender and caste answers:** ⭐ *practices **open to women and men**, in which practitioners *"**ignored differences of caste and class WITHIN THE RITUAL CONTEXT**."* **Note the careful limitation — "within the ritual context."** *NCERT is not claiming Tantra abolished caste; it is saying **the ritual space suspended it**, which is a precise and defensible claim.*

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ And NCERT then places bhakti in this setting:**

> *"The traditions of **devotion or BHAKTI** need to be located within this context. ⭐ **Devotional worship had A LONG HISTORY OF ALMOST A THOUSAND YEARS BEFORE THE PERIOD WE ARE CONSIDERING.** During this time, expressions of devotion ranged from **the routine worship of deities within temples** to **ECSTATIC ADORATION WHERE DEVOTEES ATTAINED A TRANCE-LIKE STATE. The SINGING AND CHANTING of devotional compositions was often a part of such modes of worship** — particularly true of the **VAISHNAVA and SHAIVA** sects."*

## 2️⃣ Poems of Prayer — Early Traditions of Bhakti ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ** THE PATTERN NCERT IDENTIFIES **
   ================================================================
   ** "poet-saints emerged as LEADERS AROUND WHOM THERE
      DEVELOPED A COMMUNITY OF DEVOTEES." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE BALANCED FORMULATION -- reproduce it exactly,
      because it holds two things at once: **
   ** "while BRAHMANAS REMAINED IMPORTANT INTERMEDIARIES
      BETWEEN GODS AND DEVOTEES in several forms of bhakti,
      these traditions ALSO ACCOMMODATED AND ACKNOWLEDGED WOMEN
      AND THE 'LOWER CASTES', CATEGORIES CONSIDERED INELIGIBLE
      FOR LIBERATION WITHIN THE ORTHODOX BRAHMANICAL
      FRAMEWORK." **
   ** "What also characterised traditions of bhakti was
      A REMARKABLE DIVERSITY." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** SAGUNA vs NIRGUNA -- a certain Prelims item **
   ================================================================
   ** SAGUNA ** = "WITH ATTRIBUTES"
      ** "traditions that focused on the worship of SPECIFIC
      DEITIES such as SHIVA, VISHNU AND HIS AVATARS, and FORMS
      OF THE GODDESS OR DEVI, all often conceptualised in
      ANTHROPOMORPHIC FORMS." **

   ** NIRGUNA ** = "WITHOUT ATTRIBUTES"
      ⭐ ** "worship of AN ABSTRACT FORM OF GOD." **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SAGUNA*/*NIRGUNA* DISTINCTION IS THE SPINE OF §8, SO FIX IT NOW.** ⭐ ***Saguna*: a god with a form, a name, a story, an image — the Alvars' Vishnu, the Nayanars' Shiva, Mirabai's Krishna.** ⭐ ***Nirguna*: an abstract God without attributes — Kabir and Baba Guru Nanak.*** *(⭐ **And note that NCERT calls it a classification made by *"historians of religion"* — an analytical category, not one the saints used of themselves.**)*

### 2.1 The Alvars and Nayanars of Tamil Nadu ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "Some of the EARLIEST bhakti movements (c. SIXTH CENTURY)
      were led by: " **
   ⭐ ** THE ALVARS ** -- "literally, those who are IMMERSED in
      devotion to ** VISHNU ** "
   ⭐ ** THE NAYANARS ** -- "literally, LEADERS who were devotees
      of ** SHIVA ** "
   ** "They TRAVELLED FROM PLACE TO PLACE SINGING HYMNS IN
      TAMIL in praise of their gods." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE CHAIN FROM SONG TO TEMPLE -- four steps, and
      it explains the whole sacred geography of Tamil Nadu: **
   1. ** "During their travels the Alvars and Nayanars
      IDENTIFIED CERTAIN SHRINES AS ABODES OF THEIR CHOSEN
      DEITIES." **
   2. ** "VERY OFTEN LARGE TEMPLES WERE LATER BUILT AT THESE
      SACRED PLACES." **
   3. ** "These developed as CENTRES OF PILGRIMAGE." **
   4. ⭐ ** "SINGING COMPOSITIONS OF THESE POET-SAINTS BECAME
      PART OF TEMPLE RITUALS in these shrines, AS DID WORSHIP
      OF THE SAINTS' IMAGES." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **STEP 4 IS THE REMARKABLE ONE AND ALMOST NOBODY USES IT: THE SAINTS THEMSELVES BECAME OBJECTS OF WORSHIP.** ⭐ *Poets who wandered singing about a god ended up **with their own images installed in his temples, and their songs built into the liturgy.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ And §2.4 gives the hard evidence: **inscriptional evidence from around 945 that the Chola ruler PARANTAKA I had CONSECRATED METAL IMAGES OF APPAR, SAMBANDAR AND SUNDARAR in a Shiva temple**, and that *"these were CARRIED IN PROCESSIONS during the festivals of these saints."* **A dated inscription for the deification of poets.**)*

**📦 THE COMPILATIONS — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ two names, and both are Prelims-certain**

| Compilation | Whose | Details |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***NALAYIRA DIVYAPRABANDHAM*** | **the ALVARS** | *"By the **TENTH CENTURY** the compositions of the **12 ALVARS** were compiled"* — **"Four Thousand Sacred Compositions"**; ⭐ *frequently described as **THE TAMIL VEDA*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***TEVARAM*** | **the NAYANARS** | *"The poems of **APPAR, SAMBANDAR and SUNDARAR**"*, ⭐ *"compiled and classified in the **TENTH CENTURY** ON THE BASIS OF **THE MUSIC OF THE SONGS**"* |

### 2.2 Attitudes towards caste ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> *"Some historians suggest that the Alvars and Nayanars **INITIATED A MOVEMENT OF PROTEST AGAINST THE CASTE SYSTEM AND THE DOMINANCE OF BRAHMANAS, OR AT LEAST ATTEMPTED TO REFORM THE SYSTEM.** ⭐ **To some extent this is CORROBORATED by the fact that BHAKTAS HAILED FROM DIVERSE SOCIAL BACKGROUNDS ranging from BRAHMANAS to ARTISANS AND CULTIVATORS and even from CASTES CONSIDERED 'UNTOUCHABLE'.**"*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the claim about the texts:** *"the importance of the traditions… was sometimes indicated by **the claim that THEIR COMPOSITIONS WERE AS IMPORTANT AS THE VEDAS**"* — hence the ***Nalayira Divyaprabandham* being called **the TAMIL VEDA**, *"thus claiming that the text was AS SIGNIFICANT AS THE FOUR VEDAS IN SANSKRIT that were cherished by the Brahmanas."*

**📖 SOURCE 1 — *The chaturvedin and the "outcaste"* (TONDARADIPPODI, an Alvar, himself a BRAHMANA) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**You (Vishnu) MANIFESTLY LIKE THOSE 'SERVANTS' WHO EXPRESS THEIR LOVE FOR YOUR FEET, THOUGH THEY MAY BE BORN OUTCASTES, MORE THAN THE CHATURVEDINS** *(Brahmanas versed in the four Vedas)* **WHO ARE STRANGERS AND WITHOUT ALLEGIANCE TO YOUR SERVICE.**"*

**📖 SOURCE 2 — *Shastras or devotion?* (APPAR, a Nayanar) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**O ROGUES WHO QUOTE THE LAW BOOKS, OF WHAT USE ARE YOUR GOTRA AND KULA? JUST BOW TO MARPERU'S LORD** *(Shiva, who resides in Marperu, in Thanjavur)* **AS YOUR SOLE REFUGE.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT asks: *"Do you think Tondaradippodi was OPPOSED TO THE CASTE SYSTEM?"* and *"Are there any SIMILARITIES OR DIFFERENCES in the attitudes of Tondaradippodi and Appar towards Brahmanas?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE COMPARISON IS THE BEST SMALL EXERCISE IN §2, AND THE ANSWER IS THAT THEY DIFFER IN DEGREE, NOT DIRECTION.**
> - ⭐ **TONDARADIPPODI** *(himself a Brahmana)* **inverts the hierarchy WITHOUT abolishing it.** *He keeps the categories — *"born outcastes"*, *"chaturvedins"* — and says **God prefers the devoted outcaste to the undevoted Brahmana.** ⭐ **The criterion changes from BIRTH to DEVOTION; the vocabulary of birth survives.** **He is reforming, not rejecting.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **APPAR is far more aggressive.** *He calls the reciters of the law books ***"ROGUES"***, dismisses ***GOTRA and KULA*** — the two pillars of [Part I Theme 3's](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/03_Theme3_Kinship_Caste_and_Class.md) kinship order — as **useless**, and offers **one refuge only.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SHARED MOVE, and it is the answer to the second question:** *both **relocate worth from BIRTH AND LEARNING to DEVOTION.** **Neither denies that Brahmanas exist or that Vedas matter — they deny that either is what SAVES you.**
>
> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Be careful with the verdict, because NCERT is careful.** *It says *"**SOME historians suggest**"* protest, *"**or at least attempted to REFORM**"*, and *"**TO SOME EXTENT this is corroborated.**" **Write it as a debated claim with supporting evidence, not as settled fact** — and note the strongest evidence is **the social range of the bhaktas themselves**, from Brahmanas to those considered untouchable.

> *"Perhaps one of the **MOST STRIKING FEATURES of these traditions was THE PRESENCE OF WOMEN.**"*

| | ⭐ **ANDAL** | ⭐ **KARAIKKAL AMMAIYAR** |
|---|---|---|
| **Tradition** | **a woman ALVAR** *(Vishnu)* | **a devotee of SHIVA** *(Nayanar tradition)* |
| **Her path** | ⭐ *"**saw herself as THE BELOVED OF VISHNU; her verses EXPRESS HER LOVE FOR THE DEITY**"* | ⭐ *"**adopted the path of EXTREME ASCETICISM in order to attain her goal**"* |
| **Reception** | *"widely sung **(and continue to be sung to date)**"* | *"Her compositions were **preserved within the Nayanar tradition**"* |

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND NCERT'S VERDICT ON BOTH — the sentence to memorise for any answer on women and religion:** *"**These women RENOUNCED THEIR SOCIAL OBLIGATIONS, BUT DID NOT JOIN AN ALTERNATIVE ORDER OR BECOME NUNS. ⭐ THEIR VERY EXISTENCE AND THEIR COMPOSITIONS POSED A CHALLENGE TO PATRIARCHAL NORMS.**"*

**📖 SOURCE 3 — *A demon?*: Karaikkal Ammaiyar describing herself ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**The female PEY (DEMONESS) with… BULGING VEINS, PROTRUDING EYES, WHITE TEETH and SHRUNKEN STOMACH, RED HAIRED and JUTTING TEETH, LENGTHY SHINS extending till the ankles, SHOUTS AND WAILS while WANDERING IN THE FOREST.** ⭐ This is the forest of **ALANKATU**, which is **THE HOME OF OUR FATHER (SHIVA) WHO DANCES… WITH HIS MATTED HAIR THROWN IN ALL EIGHT DIRECTIONS, AND WITH COOL LIMBS.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's question: *"List the ways in which Karaikkal Ammaiyar depicts herself as PRESENTING A CONTRAST TO TRADITIONAL NOTIONS OF FEMININE BEAUTY."***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **GO THROUGH THE LIST — every single feature is the opposite of the conventional ideal:** ⭐ **bulging veins** *(not smooth limbs)* · **protruding eyes** · **white, jutting teeth** *(not hidden)* · **a shrunken stomach** *(not the rounded form of classical sculpture)* · **red hair** *(not black)* · **long thin shins** · and she **shouts and wails** *(not soft-spoken)* — **while wandering in a forest**, not in a household.
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ANALYTICAL POINT: SHE CALLS HERSELF A *PEY* — A DEMONESS — AND MEANS IT AS AN ACHIEVEMENT.** ⭐ *Her asceticism has **destroyed the body that made her legible as a woman**, and she describes the destruction with **relish**. **Renouncing the female form is her claim to spiritual standing** — which is precisely §2.3's *"posed a challenge to patriarchal norms."* *(⭐ **And note where she places herself: in the same forest as Shiva, who also has WILD MATTED HAIR "thrown in all eight directions."** **She has made herself resemble her god rather than the ideal woman.** *That reading is worth stating, and it is available entirely from the text. (⭐ **Fig. 6.4 is a TWELFTH-CENTURY BRONZE IMAGE of her** — she too was made into an icon.)*

### 2.4 Relations with the state ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** THE CONTEXT: "From the SECOND HALF OF THE FIRST
      MILLENNIUM there is evidence for STATES, including those
      of the PALLAVAS and PANDYAS (c. SIXTH TO NINTH
      CENTURIES)." **
   ** "While BUDDHISM AND JAINISM had been prevalent in this
      region for several centuries, DRAWING SUPPORT FROM
      MERCHANT AND ARTISAN COMMUNITIES, these religious
      traditions received OCCASIONAL ROYAL PATRONAGE." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE HOSTILITY, AND ITS EXPLANATION: **
   ** "one of THE MAJOR THEMES IN TAMIL BHAKTI HYMNS IS THE
      POETS' OPPOSITION TO BUDDHISM AND JAINISM. This is
      PARTICULARLY MARKED IN THE COMPOSITIONS OF THE
      NAYANARS." **
   ⭐ ** "Historians have attempted to explain this hostility by
      suggesting that IT WAS DUE TO COMPETITION BETWEEN MEMBERS
      OF OTHER RELIGIOUS TRADITIONS FOR ROYAL PATRONAGE." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** WHAT THE CHOLAS DID (ninth to thirteenth
      centuries) -- five things, and this is the answer to
      "why were kings interested in bhaktas?" **
   ================================================================
   1. ** "supported BRAHMANICAL AND BHAKTI TRADITIONS, making
      LAND GRANTS and CONSTRUCTING TEMPLES for Vishnu and
      Shiva." **
   2. ⭐ ** THE GREAT SHIVA TEMPLES: "some of the most
      magnificent… including those at CHIDAMBARAM, THANJAVUR
      and GANGAIKONDACHOLAPURAM, were constructed under the
      patronage of CHOLA rulers." **
   3. ⭐ ** THE BRONZES: "This was also the period when some of
      the most SPECTACULAR REPRESENTATIONS OF SHIVA IN BRONZE
      SCULPTURE were produced. CLEARLY, THE VISIONS OF THE
      NAYANARS INSPIRED ARTISTS." **
   4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE HYMNS BROUGHT INTO THE TEMPLES: "These
      kings also INTRODUCED THE SINGING OF TAMIL SHAIVA HYMNS
      IN THE TEMPLES UNDER ROYAL PATRONAGE, TAKING THE
      INITIATIVE TO COLLECT AND ORGANISE THEM INTO A TEXT
      (TEVARAM)." **
   5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE SAINTS MADE INTO IMAGES: "inscriptional
      evidence from AROUND 945 suggests that the Chola ruler
      PARANTAKA I HAD CONSECRATED METAL IMAGES OF APPAR,
      SAMBANDAR AND SUNDARAR IN A SHIVA TEMPLE. These were
      CARRIED IN PROCESSIONS during the festivals of these
      saints." **

   ** AND WHY IT MATTERED POLITICALLY: **
   ⭐ ** "Both Nayanars and Alvars WERE REVERED BY THE VELLALA
      PEASANTS. NOT SURPRISINGLY, RULERS TRIED TO WIN THEIR
      SUPPORT AS WELL." **
   ** "The Chola kings often attempted to CLAIM DIVINE SUPPORT
      and PROCLAIM THEIR OWN POWER AND STATUS by building
      SPLENDID TEMPLES adorned with stone and metal sculpture
      TO RECREATE THE VISIONS OF THESE POPULAR SAINTS WHO SANG
      IN THE LANGUAGE OF THE PEOPLE." **
```

> 🔴 **NCERT's Discuss box: *"Why do you think KINGS WERE INTERESTED IN PROCLAIMING THEIR CONNECTIONS WITH BHAKTAS?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ANSWER IS IN ONE CLAUSE: *"Both Nayanars and Alvars were REVERED BY THE VELLALA PEASANTS."*** ⭐ *The **[*vellalar* are Part I Theme 2's LARGE LANDOWNERS](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/02_Theme2_Kings_Farmers_and_Towns.md)** — **the dominant agrarian class, i.e. the people whose surplus and whose loyalty a Chola king most needed.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Patronising the saints the *vellalas* revered was a way of reaching the *vellalas*.**
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE FIVE ACTS ABOVE FORM A LADDER OF INCORPORATION WORTH SPELLING OUT:** *first **build the temples**, then **fill them with sculpture inspired by the saints' visions**, then **bring the saints' HYMNS INTO THE LITURGY**, then **COMPILE those hymns into an official text (*Tevaram*)**, and finally **INSTALL THE SAINTS THEMSELVES AS METAL IMAGES and carry them in procession.** ⭐ **A popular devotional movement was absorbed into the state's religious apparatus in five steps — and NCERT dates the last one to around 945.** *(⭐ **This is §1's integration process again, one level up: not local deities into Puranic Hinduism, but a POPULAR MOVEMENT into ROYAL religion.** **And note the phrase NCERT uses of the saints — *"who sang in THE LANGUAGE OF THE PEOPLE."* **That is exactly why they were worth co-opting.**)*

## 3️⃣ The Virashaiva Tradition in Karnataka ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "The TWELFTH CENTURY witnessed the emergence of a new
      movement in KARNATAKA, led by A BRAHMANA NAMED BASAVANNA
      (1106-68) WHO WAS A MINISTER IN THE COURT OF A KALACHURI
      RULER." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE TWO NAMES -- both examinable: **
   ** VIRASHAIVAS ** = "HEROES OF SHIVA"
   ** LINGAYATS ** = "WEARERS OF THE LINGA"

   ================================================================
   ** BELIEFS AND PRACTICES **
   ================================================================
   * ⭐ ** "They worship SHIVA IN HIS MANIFESTATION AS A LINGA,
     and MEN USUALLY WEAR A SMALL LINGA IN A SILVER CASE ON A
     LOOP STRUNG OVER THE LEFT SHOULDER." **
   * ** "Those who are revered include the JANGAMA OR WANDERING
     MONKS." **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE BURIAL POINT -- and note the reasoning: **
     ** "Lingayats believe that ON DEATH THE DEVOTEE WILL BE
        UNITED WITH SHIVA AND WILL NOT RETURN TO THIS WORLD.
        THEREFORE THEY DO NOT PRACTISE FUNERARY RITES SUCH AS
        CREMATION, PRESCRIBED IN THE DHARMASHASTRAS. INSTEAD,
        THEY CEREMONIALLY BURY THEIR DEAD." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** WHAT THEY CHALLENGED, AND WHAT THEY ENCOURAGED **
   ================================================================
   ** CHALLENGED: **
   * ** "THE IDEA OF CASTE and the 'POLLUTION' attributed to
     certain groups by Brahmanas" **
   * ⭐ ** "They also QUESTIONED THE THEORY OF REBIRTH." **
   ** "These WON THEM FOLLOWERS AMONGST THOSE WHO WERE
      MARGINALISED WITHIN THE BRAHMANICAL SOCIAL ORDER." **

   ** ENCOURAGED -- "certain practices DISAPPROVED IN THE
      DHARMASHASTRAS": **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** POST-PUBERTY MARRIAGE **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE REMARRIAGE OF WIDOWS **

   ================================================================
   ** THE SOURCE **
   ================================================================
   ⭐ ** "Our understanding of the Virashaiva tradition is
      derived from VACHANAS (literally, SAYINGS) COMPOSED IN
      KANNADA BY WOMEN AND MEN WHO JOINED THE MOVEMENT." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE BURIAL POINT IS THE MOST ELEGANT THING IN §3 AND IT REPAYS EXPLANATION RATHER THAN MEMORISATION.** ⭐ **Cremation belongs to a system in which the soul returns.** *If the devotee is **united with Shiva and will not come back**, then **the funerary machinery designed to manage rebirth is pointless** — so they **bury**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The rejection of the theory of rebirth and the rejection of cremation are the SAME position, expressed once in doctrine and once in practice.** *(⭐ And note it is a **direct, visible break with the *Dharmashastras*** — a difference anyone in the village could see at every death.)*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE TWO ENCOURAGED PRACTICES ARE THE HIGHEST-VALUE ITEMS FOR GS1 SOCIETY: POST-PUBERTY MARRIAGE and WIDOW REMARRIAGE — in the TWELFTH CENTURY.** ⭐ *These are precisely the two reforms **nineteenth-century social reformers had to fight for all over again.*** **A twelfth-century movement in Karnataka had already established both**, and it is a superb fact for any answer on the indigenous roots of social reform. *(⭐ **Note also that the movement was led by a BRAHMANA who was a MINISTER at court** — **Basavanna, 1106–68.** **Radical critique came from inside the establishment, not only from outside it.** *And **the *vachanas* were composed by WOMEN AND MEN**, which matters for the gender answer.)*

**📖 SOURCE 4 — *Rituals and the real world* (a *vachana* by BASAVANNA) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**When they see A SERPENT CARVED IN STONE they POUR MILK ON IT. IF A REAL SERPENT COMES they say: 'KILL. KILL.'***
>
> *⭐ **To THE SERVANT OF THE GOD WHO COULD EAT IF SERVED they say: 'GO AWAY! GO AWAY!' But to THE IMAGE OF THE GOD WHICH CANNOT EAT they offer DISHES OF FOOD.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's questions: *"Describe Basavanna's ATTITUDE TOWARDS RITUALS. How does he attempt to CONVINCE THE LISTENER?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS ONE OF THE FINEST SHORT POEMS IN THE ENTIRE SYLLABUS, AND THE ARGUMENT IS BUILT ON TWO PERFECTLY BALANCED INVERSIONS.**
> - ⭐ **INVERSION 1 — the serpent:** *the **carved** serpent gets **milk**; the **living** serpent gets *"Kill. Kill."*
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **INVERSION 2 — the food:** *the **hungry human being who CAN eat** is driven away; **the stone image which CANNOT eat** is offered dishes of food.
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **HOW HE CONVINCES: HE DOES NOT ARGUE. HE JUST PUTS THE TWO HALVES SIDE BY SIDE AND LETS THE ABSURDITY DO THE WORK.** *There is no doctrine, no scriptural citation, no abuse — **only a description so arranged that the reader draws the conclusion.** ⭐ **The attitude is not that ritual is wrong but that IT HAS REPLACED WHAT IT WAS SUPPOSED TO EXPRESS: reverence for life, and care for the hungry.**
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Compare with [Punna's frogs and turtles](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md) and [al-Biruni on pollution](05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md).** ⭐ **All three test a ritual claim against an obvious everyday consequence, and all three win by observation rather than by authority.** *Three traditions, three centuries apart, one method of criticism.*

**📦 New religious developments — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ two consequences to note**

> *"This period also witnessed two major developments. ⭐ **On the one hand, MANY IDEAS OF THE TAMIL BHAKTAS (especially the Vaishnavas) WERE INCORPORATED WITHIN THE SANSKRITIC TRADITION, CULMINATING IN THE COMPOSITION OF ONE OF THE BEST-KNOWN PURANAS, THE BHAGAVATA PURANA.** ⭐ Second, we find **the development of TRADITIONS OF BHAKTI IN MAHARASHTRA IN THE THIRTEENTH CENTURY.**"*

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE FIRST IS §1's PROCESS 2 HAPPENING AGAIN, AND IT IS WORTH NAMING: Tamil devotional ideas flowed UPWARD into Sanskrit and produced the *BHAGAVATA PURANA*.** ⭐ **A regional vernacular movement reshaped the pan-Indian Sanskrit canon.** *The traffic between "great" and "little" traditions ran **both ways**, exactly as Redfield's own model said it did.

## 4️⃣ Religious Ferment in North India ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NCERT poses a genuine historical puzzle here, and the answer is a good Mains paragraph:** *"During the same period, in north India deities such as Vishnu and Shiva were worshipped in temples, often built with the support of rulers. **HOWEVER, HISTORIANS HAVE NOT FOUND EVIDENCE OF ANYTHING RESEMBLING THE COMPOSITIONS OF THE ALVARS AND NAYANARS TILL THE FOURTEENTH CENTURY. ⭐ HOW DO WE ACCOUNT FOR THIS DIFFERENCE?**"*

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE EXPLANATION, IN THREE MOVES **
   ================================================================

   ** MOVE 1 -- THE RAJPUT STATES AND SECURE BRAHMANAS **
   ** "in north India this was the period when SEVERAL RAJPUT
      STATES EMERGED. In most of these states BRAHMANAS
      OCCUPIED POSITIONS OF IMPORTANCE, PERFORMING A RANGE OF
      SECULAR AND RITUAL FUNCTIONS. ⭐ THERE SEEMS TO HAVE BEEN
      LITTLE OR NO ATTEMPT TO CHALLENGE THEIR POSITION
      DIRECTLY." **

   ** MOVE 2 -- BUT ALTERNATIVE LEADERS WERE GAINING GROUND **
   ⭐ ** "These included THE NATHS, JOGIS and SIDDHAS. Many of
      them CAME FROM ARTISANAL GROUPS, INCLUDING WEAVERS, who
      were becoming increasingly important with THE DEVELOPMENT
      OF ORGANISED CRAFT PRODUCTION." **
   ** "Demand for such production grew with THE EMERGENCE OF
      NEW URBAN CENTRES, and LONG-DISTANCE TRADE WITH CENTRAL
      ASIA AND WEST ASIA." **
   ⭐ ** "Many of these new religious leaders QUESTIONED THE
      AUTHORITY OF THE VEDAS, and EXPRESSED THEMSELVES IN
      LANGUAGES SPOKEN BY ORDINARY PEOPLE, WHICH DEVELOPED OVER
      CENTURIES INTO THE ONES USED TODAY." **
   ⚠️ ** "HOWEVER, IN SPITE OF THEIR POPULARITY THESE RELIGIOUS
      LEADERS WERE NOT IN A POSITION TO WIN THE SUPPORT OF THE
      RULING ELITES." **

   ** MOVE 3 -- THEN THE TURKS CHANGED THE EQUATION **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "A NEW ELEMENT in this situation was THE COMING
      OF THE TURKS which culminated in THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE
      DELHI SULTANATE (THIRTEENTH CENTURY). THIS UNDERMINED THE
      POWER OF MANY OF THE RAJPUT STATES AND THE BRAHMANAS WHO
      WERE ASSOCIATED WITH THESE KINGDOMS." **
   ** "This was accompanied by MARKED CHANGES IN THE REALM OF
      CULTURE AND RELIGION. THE COMING OF THE SUFIS was a
      significant part of these developments." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS A GENUINELY SOPHISTICATED PIECE OF HISTORICAL EXPLANATION AND IT IS WORTH LEARNING AS A MODEL.** ⭐ **NCERT explains an ABSENCE — why north India produced no Alvars — by pointing to a difference in the POLITICAL STRUCTURE, not in the religiosity of the people.** *Where Brahmanas held **both secular and ritual office** in the Rajput states, **there was no space to challenge them**; the challengers who did exist — **Naths, Jogis, Siddhas, many of them weavers and artisans** — were **popular but politically unbacked.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND MOVE 3 IS THE HINGE OF THE WHOLE CHAPTER: THE DELHI SULTANATE DID NOT CREATE NORTH INDIAN BHAKTI, BUT IT REMOVED WHAT WAS BLOCKING IT.** ⭐ *By undermining **the Rajput states and the Brahmanas attached to them**, the Turkish conquest **loosened the grip that had kept alternative religious leadership out of the elite.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And this explains the chapter's own structure**: §4 ends by pointing at the Sufis, §5–§7 deal with them, and **§8's Kabir — a weaver, of exactly the artisanal group named in Move 2 — is the result.** **NCERT has built the answer to "why Kabir in the fifteenth century and not the ninth?" into its section order.**)*

## 5️⃣ New Strands in the Fabric — Islamic Traditions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⭐ **NCERT's framing insists on long-standing contact, not sudden arrival:** *"**Arab merchants frequented ports along the western coast IN THE FIRST MILLENNIUM CE**, while **Central Asian people settled in the north-western parts of the subcontinent during the same period.** From the **seventh century, with the advent of Islam, these regions became part of what is often termed THE ISLAMIC WORLD.**"*

### 5.1 Faiths of rulers and subjects ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** THE POLITICAL SEQUENCE: **
   ⭐ ** "In 711 an Arab general named MUHAMMAD QASIM CONQUERED
      SIND, which became part of THE CALIPH'S DOMAIN." **
   ** "Later (c. thirteenth century) THE TURKS AND AFGHANS
      established THE DELHI SULTANATE. This was followed by
      SULTANATES IN THE DECCAN and other parts…; the MUGHAL
      EMPIRE in the sixteenth century; and MANY REGIONAL STATES
      in the eighteenth." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE KEY TERMS -- all Prelims-certain **
   ================================================================
   ** ULAMA ** (plural of ALIM, "one who knows") = ⭐ "SCHOLARS
      OF ISLAMIC STUDIES. As PRESERVERS OF THIS TRADITION they
      perform various RELIGIOUS, JURIDICAL AND TEACHING
      FUNCTIONS."

   ** SHARI`A ** = "THE LAW GOVERNING THE MUSLIM COMMUNITY.
      It is based on THE QUR'AN and THE HADIS (traditions of
      the Prophet, including a record of HIS REMEMBERED WORDS
      AND DEEDS)."
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND HOW IT GREW -- a superb, examinable point: **
   ** "With THE EXPANSION OF ISLAMIC RULE OUTSIDE ARABIA, IN
      AREAS WHERE CUSTOMS AND TRADITIONS WERE DIFFERENT,
      ** QIYAS (REASONING BY ANALOGY) ** and ** IJMA (CONSENSUS
      OF THE COMMUNITY) ** WERE RECOGNISED AS TWO OTHER SOURCES
      OF LEGISLATION." **
   ** "Thus, the shari`a evolved from THE QUR'AN, HADIS, QIYAS
      and IJMA." **

   ================================================================
   ** ZIMMI -- and the Indian extension **
   ================================================================
   ⭐ ** "the category of the ZIMMI, meaning PROTECTED (derived
      from the Arabic ZIMMA, protection), developed for PEOPLE
      WHO FOLLOWED REVEALED SCRIPTURES, SUCH AS THE JEWS AND
      CHRISTIANS, AND LIVED UNDER MUSLIM RULERSHIP. They paid a
      tax called JIZYA and gained THE RIGHT TO BE PROTECTED BY
      MUSLIMS." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "IN INDIA THIS STATUS WAS EXTENDED TO HINDUS AS
      WELL. rulers such as the Mughals CAME TO REGARD THEMSELVES
      AS EMPERORS OF NOT JUST MUSLIMS BUT OF ALL PEOPLES." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *QIYAS*/*IJMA* POINT IS THE MOST ANALYTICALLY USEFUL THING IN §5.1 AND IT IS EASY TO SKIM PAST.** ⭐ **The *shari`a* acquired two additional sources — reasoning by analogy, and community consensus — PRECISELY BECAUSE Islamic rule expanded "in areas where customs and traditions were different."** *A legal system built for one society had to develop **mechanisms for handling societies it had not anticipated.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is the chapter's Idea 1 — adaptation through contact — operating inside jurisprudence.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE ***ZIMMI*** EXTENSION IS THE SAME MOVE.** *The category was designed for **"people of the book"** — Jews and Christians. ⭐ **In India it was extended to Hindus**, and NCERT's next sentence completes the arc: **the Mughals came to see themselves as *"emperors of not just Muslims but of ALL PEOPLES."*** *(⭐ **The theoretical position and the practical one diverge, and NCERT says so:** *"Theoretically, Muslim rulers were to be guided by **the ulama**, who were expected to ensure that they ruled according to the shari`a. ⭐ **Clearly, the situation was COMPLICATED in the subcontinent, where there were POPULATIONS THAT DID NOT SUBSCRIBE TO ISLAM.**" **And §7.4 shows what rulers actually did about it — they went to the Sufis instead.**)*

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT RULERS ACTUALLY DID — and the two sources are excellent evidence**

> *"In effect, rulers often adopted **A FAIRLY FLEXIBLE POLICY** towards their subjects. For instance, several rulers **GAVE LAND ENDOWMENTS AND GRANTED TAX EXEMPTIONS TO HINDU, JAINA, ZOROASTRIAN, CHRISTIAN AND JEWISH RELIGIOUS INSTITUTIONS and also EXPRESSED RESPECT AND DEVOTION TOWARDS NON-MUSLIM RELIGIOUS LEADERS.** ⭐ **These grants were made by several Mughal rulers, INCLUDING AKBAR AND AURANGZEB.**"*

**📖 SOURCE 5 — *A church in Khambat*: a *farman* of AKBAR, 1598 ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"Whereas it reached our eminent and holy notice that **the PADRIS (FATHERS) OF THE HOLY SOCIETY OF JESUS WISH TO BUILD A HOUSE OF PRAYER (CHURCH) IN THE CITY OF KAMBAYAT (Khambat, in Gujarat)**; therefore an exalted mandate… is being issued, ⭐ **that THE DIGNITARIES OF THE CITY OF KAMBAYAT SHOULD IN NO CASE STAND IN THEIR WAY BUT SHOULD ALLOW THEM TO BUILD A CHURCH so that they may engage themselves in their own worship. IT IS NECESSARY THAT THE ORDER OF THE EMPEROR SHOULD BE OBEYED IN EVERY WAY.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's question: *"WHO WERE THE PEOPLE FROM WHOM AKBAR ANTICIPATED OPPOSITION TO HIS ORDER?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ANSWER IS IN THE PHRASE *"THE DIGNITARIES OF THE CITY OF KAMBAYAT"* — LOCAL NOTABLES, NOT THE EMPEROR'S OWN COURT.** ⭐ *And the tell is the emphatic closing line — *"**it is NECESSARY that the order of the Emperor should be obeyed IN EVERY WAY**"* — **which you only write if you expect resistance.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The *farman* is evidence of imperial tolerance AND of local intolerance in the same document.** *(⭐ **A useful corrective for answers that treat "Mughal policy" as if it operated uniformly on the ground.**)*

**📖 SOURCE 6 — *Reverence for the Jogi*: a letter from AURANGZEB, 1661–62 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**The possessor of the sublime station, SHIV MURAT, GURU ANAND NATH JIO! MAY YOUR REVERENCE REMAIN IN PEACE AND HAPPINESS EVER UNDER THE PROTECTION OF SRI SHIV JIO!*** … *⭐ **A PIECE OF CLOTH FOR THE CLOAK AND A SUM OF TWENTY FIVE RUPEES which have been SENT AS AN OFFERING will reach (Your Reverence)… YOUR REVERENCE MAY WRITE TO US WHENEVER THERE IS ANY SERVICE WHICH CAN BE RENDERED BY US.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's questions: *"Identify the DEITY worshipped by the Jogi. Describe the ATTITUDE of the emperor towards the Jogi."***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE DEITY IS SHIVA — named twice, as *"SHIV MURAT"* and *"SRI SHIV JIO"* — and the letter is from AURANGZEB, which is why it is in the textbook.** ⭐ **The attitude is one of DEFERENCE, not condescension:** *he addresses the Jogi as *"the possessor of **the sublime station**"* and *"**Your Reverence**"*, **invokes SHIVA'S protection over him**, sends **an offering** *(cloth and twenty-five rupees)*, and closes by **offering his own services.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **An emperor writing to a Hindu ascetic in the vocabulary of the ascetic's own faith.**
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **How to use this responsibly in an answer:** *NCERT's point is not that Aurangzeb was uniformly liberal, and you should not claim that. ⭐ **The point is the one NCERT actually makes: that grants and expressions of respect toward non-Muslim religious leaders were made "BY SEVERAL MUGHAL RULERS, INCLUDING AKBAR AND AURANGZEB"** — i.e. **the practice was structural, not the personal quirk of one emperor.** *A single document is evidence of a practice, not of a whole reign.*

### 5.2 The popular practice of Islam ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```

   4. ** SAWM ** -- "FASTING DURING THE MONTH OF RAMZAN"

   ** "HOWEVER, THESE UNIVERSAL FEATURES WERE OFTEN OVERLAID

   ================================================================

      DERIVED FROM THE QUR'AN THROUGH INDIGENOUS LITERARY
      GENRES. These included the ** GINAN ** (derived from the

   ** (b) THE MALABAR COAST ** -- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

   ** (c) MOSQUE ARCHITECTURE ** -- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Exercise 2
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **EXERCISE 2 — *"To what extent does the architecture of mosques reflect A COMBINATION OF UNIVERSAL IDEALS AND LOCAL TRADITIONS?"* — is answered by the three figures NCERT prints, and you should name all three:**
> - ⭐ **KERALA, c. thirteenth century** — *NCERT's caption: **"Note the SHIKHARA-LIKE ROOF."*** **A mosque with a temple-form roof.**
> - ⭐ **ATIYA MOSQUE, Mymensingh district, BANGLADESH, 1609** — *"**built with BRICK**"* — the material of the Bengal delta, where stone is scarce.
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SHAH HAMADAN MOSQUE, SRINAGAR**, on the banks of the Jhelum, **built 1395** — *"often regarded as **the 'JEWEL IN THE CROWN' of all the existing mosques of Kashmir**… **one of the best examples of KASHMIRI WOODEN ARCHITECTURE. Notice THE SPIRE and THE BEAUTIFULLY CARVED EAVES. It is decorated with PAPIER MACHE.**"*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE STRUCTURE OF THE ANSWER: WHAT IS UNIVERSAL IS THE ORIENTATION — the *mihrab* and *minbar* fix the building's relationship to MECCA, and that never varies. EVERYTHING ELSE IS LOCAL.** ⭐ **Roof form follows regional architecture** *(a *shikhara*-like roof in Kerala, a spire in Kashmir)*; **material follows regional availability** *(brick in Bengal, wood in Kashmir)*; **decoration follows regional craft** *(papier mâché)*. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A universal faith with a fixed axis and a locally-built body.**
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE MALABAR CASE IS THE STRONGEST SINGLE EXAMPLE OF ADAPTATION IN THE CHAPTER — worth a sentence of its own.** ⭐ **Arab traders adopted MATRILINY and MATRILOCAL RESIDENCE** — *i.e. a kinship system in which **descent runs through the mother** and **a wife stays in her natal home**.* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is not a decorative adaptation like a roof; it is a fundamental restructuring of family and inheritance, adopted from the host society.** *(⭐ **[Part I Theme 3](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/03_Theme3_Kinship_Caste_and_Class.md) defined matriliny for you, and noted that south India had its own kinship rules that the Brahmanical texts did not describe.** **Here is a third tradition adopting them.**)*

### 5.3 Names for communities — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ one of the most important passages in the book

```
   ** "We often TAKE THE TERMS HINDU AND MUSLIM FOR GRANTED, as
      labels for religious communities. ⭐ YET, THESE TERMS DID
      NOT GAIN CURRENCY FOR A VERY LONG TIME." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE EVIDENCE: "Historians who have studied
      SANSKRIT TEXTS AND INSCRIPTIONS DATING BETWEEN THE EIGHTH
      AND FOURTEENTH CENTURIES point out that THE TERM MUSALMAN
      OR MUSLIM WAS VIRTUALLY NEVER USED." **

   ** WHAT WAS USED INSTEAD -- REGION OF ORIGIN: **
   * ⭐ ** TURUSHKA ** -- the Turkish rulers
   * ⭐ ** TAJIKA ** -- "people from TAJIKISTAN"
   * ⭐ ** PARASHIKA ** -- "people from PERSIA"

   ** OR OLD TERMS RECYCLED FOR NEW ARRIVALS: **
   * ⭐ ** "the Turks and Afghans were referred to as SHAKAS and
     YAVANAS (a term used for GREEKS)" **

   ** OR THE GENERAL TERM: **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** MLECHCHHA ** -- "indicating that they DID NOT
     OBSERVE THE NORMS OF CASTE SOCIETY and SPOKE LANGUAGES
     THAT WERE NOT DERIVED FROM SANSKRIT."

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND THE CONCLUSION -- learn this sentence: **
   ** "Such terms SOMETIMES HAD A DEROGATORY CONNOTATION, BUT
      THEY RARELY DENOTED A DISTINCT RELIGIOUS COMMUNITY OF
      MUSLIMS IN OPPOSITION TO HINDUS. And… THE TERM 'HINDU'
      WAS USED IN A VARIETY OF WAYS, NOT NECESSARILY RESTRICTED
      TO A RELIGIOUS CONNOTATION." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS ONE OF THE MOST IMPORTANT PASSAGES IN THE WHOLE OF CLASS 12, AND IT IS A PRECISE, SOURCED, CHECKABLE CLAIM — NOT AN OPINION.** ⭐ **Across SEVEN CENTURIES of Sanskrit texts and inscriptions (eighth to fourteenth), the word *musalman* is VIRTUALLY NEVER USED.** *People were identified by **where they came from** — Turushka, Tajika, Parashika — or by **an old label recycled** — Shaka, Yavana — or by ***mlechchha***, which marks **caste practice and LANGUAGE**, not creed.
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ANALYTICAL POINT: THE CATEGORIES PEOPLE USED WERE ETHNIC, GEOGRAPHICAL AND LINGUISTIC — NOT RELIGIOUS.** ⭐ *NCERT is careful and you should be too: the terms **were sometimes derogatory** — this is not a claim that medieval India was free of prejudice. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The claim is that the prejudice was not organised along the religious axis we now assume.** *(⭐ **And it dovetails exactly with [Theme 5's "Hindu" box](05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md)**: *"none of these expressions indicated the religious identity of the people. **It was much later that the term developed religious connotations.**" **Two chapters, one finding, from two different bodies of evidence.** *(And note the third element: ***mlechchha* is exactly the word [Part I Theme 3](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/03_Theme3_Kinship_Caste_and_Class.md) recorded for the SHAKAS a thousand years earlier — **the same term, reused for each new group of arrivals.**)*

## 6️⃣ The Growth of Sufism ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** THE ORIGIN: "In THE EARLY CENTURIES OF ISLAM a group of
      RELIGIOUS-MINDED PEOPLE called SUFIS turned to ASCETICISM
      AND MYSTICISM IN PROTEST AGAINST THE GROWING MATERIALISM
      OF THE CALIPHATE as a religious and political
      institution." **

   ⭐ ** THE CRITIQUE: "They were CRITICAL OF THE DOGMATIC
      DEFINITIONS AND SCHOLASTIC METHODS OF INTERPRETING THE
      QUR'AN AND SUNNA adopted by THEOLOGIANS." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE ALTERNATIVE: "Instead, they laid emphasis on
      SEEKING SALVATION THROUGH INTENSE DEVOTION AND LOVE FOR
      GOD by FOLLOWING HIS COMMANDS, and by FOLLOWING THE
      EXAMPLE OF THE PROPHET MUHAMMAD whom they regarded as A
      PERFECT HUMAN BEING. The sufis thus sought AN
      INTERPRETATION OF THE QUR'AN ON THE BASIS OF THEIR
      PERSONAL EXPERIENCE." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE WORD ITSELF -- three possible derivations **
   ================================================================
   ** "SUFISM is AN ENGLISH WORD COINED IN THE NINETEENTH
      CENTURY. The word used for Sufism IN ISLAMIC TEXTS IS
      ** TASAWWUF ** ." Possible origins: **
   * ⭐ ** SUF ** -- "meaning WOOL, referring to THE COARSE
     WOOLLEN CLOTHES WORN BY SUFIS"
   * ⭐ ** SAFA ** -- "meaning PURITY"
   * ⭐ ** SUFFA ** -- "THE PLATFORM OUTSIDE THE PROPHET'S
     MOSQUE, WHERE A GROUP OF CLOSE FOLLOWERS ASSEMBLED TO
     LEARN ABOUT THE FAITH"
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE PHRASE TO CARRY IS *"AN INTERPRETATION OF THE QUR'AN ON THE BASIS OF THEIR PERSONAL EXPERIENCE."*** ⭐ **That is the whole of Sufism in one clause, and it is what put it in tension with the *ulama*.** *The scholars interpreted **by method and precedent**; the Sufis claimed **direct experiential access** — which is exactly why §7.4 says rulers turned to them: they *"**DERIVED THEIR AUTHORITY DIRECTLY FROM GOD — AND DID NOT DEPEND ON JURISTS TO INTERPRET THE SHARI`A.**"*
>
> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And note the origin of the word "Sufism" itself — an ENGLISH coinage of the NINETEENTH CENTURY.** *The tradition's own word is ***tasawwuf***. ⭐ **Using the insiders' term where you can is the same discipline as writing "Theravada" rather than "Hinayana" in [Part I Theme 4](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md).**

### 6.1 *Khanqahs* and *silsilas* — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ the institutional vocabulary

```
   ** "By THE ELEVENTH CENTURY Sufism evolved into A
      WELL-DEVELOPED MOVEMENT with a body of literature on
      Quranic studies and sufi practices." **

   ================================================================
   ** THE KHANQAH AND ITS HIERARCHY **
   ================================================================
   ** KHANQAH ** (Persian) = ⭐ ** the HOSPICE ** -- "the sufis
      began to ORGANISE COMMUNITIES AROUND" it
   ** SHAIKH ** (Arabic) / ** PIR ** or ** MURSHID ** (Persian)
      = ⭐ ** the TEACHING MASTER ** who controlled it
   ** MURIDS ** = ⭐ ** the DISCIPLES he enrolled **
   ** KHALIFA ** = ⭐ ** the SUCCESSOR he appointed **
   ** "He established RULES FOR SPIRITUAL CONDUCT AND
      INTERACTION between inmates as well as between laypersons
      and the master." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE SILSILA -- and the metaphor is the point **
   ================================================================
   ** "Sufi silsilas began to CRYSTALLISE in different parts of
      the Islamic world AROUND THE TWELFTH CENTURY." **
   ⭐ ** "The word SILSILA LITERALLY MEANS A CHAIN, signifying A
      CONTINUOUS LINK BETWEEN MASTER AND DISCIPLE, STRETCHING
      AS AN UNBROKEN SPIRITUAL GENEALOGY TO THE PROPHET
      MUHAMMAD. IT WAS THROUGH THIS CHANNEL THAT SPIRITUAL
      POWER AND BLESSINGS WERE TRANSMITTED TO DEVOTEES." **
   ⭐ ** INITIATION: "initiates TOOK AN OATH OF ALLEGIANCE, WORE
      A PATCHED GARMENT, and SHAVED THEIR HAIR." **

   ** NAMING: "Most sufi lineages were NAMED AFTER A FOUNDING
      FIGURE" -- e.g. the ** QADIRI ** order after
      ** SHAIKH ABD'UL QADIR JILANI ** . ⭐ "However, some like
      ** THE CHISHTI ORDER, were named after THEIR PLACE OF
      ORIGIN, in this case THE TOWN OF CHISHT IN CENTRAL
      AFGHANISTAN. ** " **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** WHAT HAPPENED WHEN THE SHAIKH DIED -- the chain
      from tomb to cult, and every term is examinable **
   ================================================================
   ** "When the shaikh died, HIS TOMB-SHRINE ( ** DARGAH ** , a
      Persian term meaning COURT) BECAME THE CENTRE OF DEVOTION
      for his followers." **
        |
   ** "This encouraged the practice of PILGRIMAGE or
      ** ZIYARAT ** to his grave, particularly on HIS DEATH
      ANNIVERSARY or ** URS ** (or MARRIAGE, SIGNIFYING THE
      UNION OF HIS SOUL WITH GOD)." **
        |
   ⭐ ** THE REASONING: "This was because people believed that
      IN DEATH SAINTS WERE UNITED WITH GOD, AND WERE THUS
      CLOSER TO HIM THAN WHEN LIVING. People sought their
      blessings to attain MATERIAL AND SPIRITUAL BENEFITS." **
        |
   ** "Thus evolved THE CULT OF THE SHAIKH REVERED AS
      ** WALI ** ." **
   ⭐ ** WALI ** (plural AULIYA) = "FRIEND OF GOD… a sufi who
      claimed PROXIMITY TO ALLAH, acquiring HIS GRACE
      ( ** BARAKAT ** ) to perform MIRACLES ( ** KARAMAT ** )."
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *URS* IS THE MOST BEAUTIFUL PIECE OF VOCABULARY IN THE CHAPTER AND IT EXPLAINS THE ENTIRE PRACTICE OF SHRINE PILGRIMAGE.** ⭐ ***Urs* means MARRIAGE.** *The saint's **death anniversary** is called his **wedding** — *"signifying **the union of his soul with God**."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And that single metaphor produces the whole logic: if death is a marriage to God, then the dead saint is CLOSER to God than the living one, so his tomb is a better place to seek intercession than his hospice ever was.** *The cult of the *dargah* follows necessarily from the meaning of one word.*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And note that the *silsila* solves a practical problem: HOW DOES SPIRITUAL AUTHORITY TRANSMIT?** ⭐ *A **chain** back to the Prophet, through named masters, each appointing a ***khalifa***. **That gives every Sufi master a verifiable pedigree** — which is why the *tazkiras* of §9 were written to *"establish the PRECEDENCE OF THEIR OWN ORDERS AND GLORIFY THEIR SPIRITUAL GENEALOGIES."* **The chain is also a claim, and claims get contested.**

**📦 The story of DATA GANJ BAKHSH — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ a complete Sufi biography in one box**

> *In **1039 ABU'L HASAN AL HUJWIRI**, *"a native of **HUJWIR near GHAZNI in Afghanistan**"*, was ⭐ *"**FORCED TO CROSS THE INDUS AS A CAPTIVE OF THE INVADING TURKISH ARMY.**"* He **settled in LAHORE** and wrote *"a book in **PERSIAN** called the ***KASHF-UL-MAHJUB* (UNVEILING OF THE VEILED)** **to explain the meaning of TASAWWUF, and those who practised it.**"*
>
> ⭐ *He **died in 1073 and was buried in Lahore. THE GRANDSON OF SULTAN MAHMUD OF GHAZNI CONSTRUCTED A TOMB OVER HIS GRAVE**, and this tomb-shrine **became a site of pilgrimage… especially on his DEATH ANNIVERSARY.***
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**Even today Hujwiri is revered as DATA GANJ BAKHSH or 'GIVER WHO BESTOWS TREASURES' and his mausoleum is called DATA DARBAR or 'COURT OF THE GIVER'.**"*

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NOTICE THE PARALLEL WITH AL-BIRUNI, AND IT IS EXACT.** ⭐ **Both were carried into the subcontinent AS CAPTIVES OF THE SAME TURKISH INVASIONS; both settled; both wrote in the region's learned languages; and both became foundational.** *(⭐ **[Theme 5](05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md): al-Biruni *"arrived in Ghazni AS A HOSTAGE"* in 1017. **Here: Hujwiri *"forced to cross the Indus AS A CAPTIVE"* in 1039.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two of medieval India's most important intellectual traditions were begun by men brought here against their will.**)*

### 6.2 Outside the *khanqah* — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Exercise 3

> *"Some mystics initiated movements based on **A RADICAL INTERPRETATION OF SUFI IDEALS. MANY SCORNED THE KHANQAH AND TOOK TO MENDICANCY AND OBSERVED CELIBACY. THEY IGNORED RITUALS AND OBSERVED EXTREME FORMS OF ASCETICISM.** ⭐ They were known by different names — **QALANDARS, MADARIS, MALANGS, HAIDARIS**, etc. **Because of their DELIBERATE DEFIANCE OF THE SHARI`A they were often referred to as BE-SHARI`A, IN CONTRAST TO THE BA-SHARI`A SUFIS WHO COMPLIED WITH IT.**"*

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **EXERCISE 3 ASKS FOR *"the SIMILARITIES AND DIFFERENCES between the BE-SHARI`A and BA-SHARI`A sufi traditions"* — and the answer needs BOTH halves:**

| | ⭐ **BA-SHARI`A** | ⭐ **BE-SHARI`A** |
|---|---|---|
| **Relation to the *shari`a*** | **complied with it** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"DELIBERATE DEFIANCE"** of it |
| **Institution** | organised around the ***KHANQAH***, with *silsila*, *pir*, *murids*, *khalifa* | ⭐ **"SCORNED THE KHANQAH"** — **mendicancy** |
| **Ritual** | rules of conduct; *sama*, *zikr*, *ziyarat* | ⭐ **"IGNORED RITUALS"** |
| **Discipline** | asceticism within a community | ⭐ **"EXTREME forms of asceticism"**, and **celibacy** |
| **Names** | **Chishti, Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi** | ⭐ **Qalandars, Madaris, Malangs, Haidaris** |

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SIMILARITIES, WHICH THE QUESTION ALSO ASKS FOR AND WHICH MOST ANSWERS OMIT:** *both are **mystical** rather than legalistic; both seek **salvation through devotion and personal experience** rather than through scholastic interpretation; both practise **asceticism**; and both arose from **the same protest against materialism and dogmatic theology** described in §6. ⭐ **The difference is not about the goal but about whether the LAW and the INSTITUTION are means to it or obstacles in the way.**

### 7.1 Life in the Chishti *khanqah* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** THE PHYSICAL SPACE -- Shaikh Nizamuddin's hospice
      (c. FOURTEENTH CENTURY) "on THE BANKS OF THE RIVER YAMUNA
      in GHIYASPUR, on the outskirts of what was then the city
      of Delhi": **
   * ** "several SMALL ROOMS and A BIG HALL ( ** JAMA'AT KHANA ** )
     where the inmates and visitors LIVED AND PRAYED" **
   * ** "The inmates included FAMILY MEMBERS OF THE SHAIKH, HIS
     ATTENDANTS AND DISCIPLES." **
   * ⭐ ** "The Shaikh lived in A SMALL ROOM ON THE ROOF OF THE
     HALL where he met visitors IN THE MORNING AND EVENING." **
   * ** "A VERANDA surrounded the courtyard, and A BOUNDARY WALL
     ran around the complex." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE OPEN KITCHEN -- and the word matters **
   ================================================================

   ⭐ ** WHO CAME -- and the list is the point: **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE ASSIMILATED PRACTICES -- the key evidence **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *LANGAR* RUN ON *FUTUH* IS THE HEART OF THE CHISHTI MODEL, AND THE TWO WORDS TOGETHER ARE THE ARGUMENT.** ⭐ ***Futuh* = UNASKED-FOR charity.** *The kitchen fed everyone who came, **and the Shaikh did not solicit the funds** — which is exactly why he could refuse a ruler's gift of gardens and villages in **Source 9**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A community that does not ask cannot be bought.** *(⭐ **And note the Sikh *langar* uses the same word.** **[§8.2's Baba Guru Nanak organised congregational worship (*sangat*)](#) in the same devotional world.**)*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE LIST OF VISITORS IS THE BEST SINGLE PIECE OF EVIDENCE FOR SUFI INCLUSIVENESS — quote it in full.** *"**Soldiers, slaves, singers, merchants, poets, travellers, rich and poor, HINDU JOGIS and QALANDARS.**" ⭐ **Note the last two: HINDU YOGIS, and the BE-SHARI`A qalandars of §6.2 — i.e. the *khanqah* received both people of another religion and Sufis who defied the law the Chishtis kept.**
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The four assimilated practices are exam-gold, so learn them as a set: BOWING before the Shaikh · OFFERING WATER to visitors · SHAVING initiates' HEADS · and YOGIC EXERCISES.** ⭐ *NCERT's own verdict is that these *"represented **ATTEMPTS TO ASSIMILATE LOCAL TRADITIONS**."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A Persianate mystical order in Delhi practising yoga is the single most concrete illustration of Idea 1 in the chapter.**

**📖 SOURCE 7 — *The pilgrimage of the Mughal princess JAHANARA, 1643* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> **From her own biography of Shaikh Muinuddin Chishti, the *MUNIS AL ARWAH* (*The Confidant of Spirits*):**
>
> *"After praising the one God… **this lowly FAQIRA (humble soul) JAHANARA**… went from the capital **AGRA** in the company of **my great father (Emperor SHAH JAHAN)** towards the pure region of **incomparable AJMER**… I was committed to this idea, that **every day in every station I would perform TWO CYCLES OF OPTIONAL PRAYER**…*
>
> *⭐ For several days… **I DID NOT SLEEP ON A LEOPARD SKIN AT NIGHT, I DID NOT EXTEND MY FEET IN THE DIRECTION OF THE BLESSED SANCTUARY of the revered saving master, and I DID NOT TURN MY BACK TOWARDS HIM. I PASSED THE DAYS BENEATH THE TREES.***
>
> *⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ On **Thursday, the fourth of the blessed month of RAMZAN**, I attained the happiness of pilgrimage… **With an hour of daylight remaining, I went to the holy sanctuary and RUBBED MY PALE FACE WITH THE DUST OF THAT THRESHOLD. From the doorway to the blessed tomb I WENT BAREFOOT, KISSING THE GROUND. Having entered the dome, I WENT AROUND THE LIGHT-FILLED TOMB OF MY MASTER SEVEN TIMES**… Finally, with my own hand **I PUT THE FINEST QUALITY OF ITAR ON THE PERFUMED TOMB**, and **having taken off THE ROSE SCARF that I had on my head, I PLACED IT ON THE TOP OF THE BLESSED TOMB**…"*

>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE GESTURES, AND NOTICE THAT EVERY ONE IS AN ACT OF SELF-LOWERING BY A PRINCESS OF THE EMPIRE:**
> - ⭐ **She calls herself *"this LOWLY FAQIRA"*** — a **humble soul**, a beggar, though she is Shah Jahan's daughter.
> - ⭐ **Extra prayers at every stage**; **no leopard skin to sleep on**; **days passed BENEATH THE TREES.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **She did not EXTEND HER FEET toward the sanctuary and did not TURN HER BACK to it** — *bodily orientation maintained over days.*
> - ⭐ **She rubbed her face with THE DUST OF THE THRESHOLD**, went **BAREFOOT**, **KISSED THE GROUND**, and **CIRCUMAMBULATED THE TOMB SEVEN TIMES.**
> - ⭐ **Offerings by her own hand: *ITAR* on the tomb, and HER OWN ROSE SCARF** taken from her head and laid on it.
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **HOW SHE MARKS IT AS SPECIAL: by making her own body the instrument.** *Not gifts sent, not buildings funded — **face in the dust, feet bare, back never turned.** ⭐ **The rank of the devotee is what gives the humility its meaning: the greater the person lowering herself, the greater the shrine.**
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This source is also valuable for a different reason: JAHANARA WROTE A BOOK.** *A Mughal princess **composed a biography of a Sufi saint**, in Persian, with a title of her own — the ***Munis al Arwah***. ⭐ **[Theme 5 opened by lamenting that "we have practically no accounts of travel left by women."](05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md)** **Here is a woman's first-person account of a journey, and it is in this chapter rather than that one.**

**🎵 *SAMA*, *QAWWALI* and the *QAUL* — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Art & Culture core**

> *"Also part of *ziyarat* is **THE USE OF MUSIC AND DANCE INCLUDING MYSTICAL CHANTS PERFORMED BY SPECIALLY TRAINED MUSICIANS or QAWWALS TO EVOKE DIVINE ECSTASY.** ⭐ **The sufis remember God either by RECITING THE ZIKR (THE DIVINE NAMES) or EVOKING HIS PRESENCE THROUGH SAMA` (literally, 'AUDITION') or PERFORMANCE OF MYSTICAL MUSIC.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***SAMA` WAS INTEGRAL TO THE CHISHTIS, AND EXEMPLIFIED INTERACTION WITH INDIGENOUS DEVOTIONAL TRADITIONS.***"*

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AMIR KHUSRAU AND THE *QAUL* — a certain Prelims item:** *"**AMIR KHUSRAU (1253–1325), the great POET, MUSICIAN and DISCIPLE OF SHAIKH NIZAMUDDIN AULIYA, GAVE A UNIQUE FORM TO THE CHISHTI SAMA` BY INTRODUCING THE QAUL** *(Arabic word meaning **"saying"**)*, **A HYMN SUNG AT THE OPENING OR CLOSING OF QAWWALI. This was followed by SUFI POETRY IN PERSIAN, HINDAVI OR URDU, and sometimes USING WORDS FROM ALL OF THESE LANGUAGES.** ⭐ **QAWWALS at the shrine of Shaikh Nizamuddin Auliya ALWAYS START THEIR RECITAL WITH THE QAUL. Today qawwali is performed in shrines ALL OVER THE SUBCONTINENT.**"*
>

**📖 SOURCE 8 — *Charkhanama*, a song set to the rhythm of the spinning wheel ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**As you TAKE THE COTTON, you do ZIKR-I JALI / As you SEPARATE THE COTTON you should do ZIKR-I QALBI / And as you SPOOL THE THREAD you should do ZIKR-I AINI / ⭐ ZIKR SHOULD BE UTTERED FROM THE STOMACH THROUGH THE CHEST, AND THREADED THROUGH THE THROAT. / THE THREADS OF BREATH SHOULD BE COUNTED ONE BY ONE, OH SISTER. UP TO TWENTY FOUR THOUSAND. / DO THIS DAY AND NIGHT, AND OFFER THIS TO YOUR PIR AS A GIFT.**"*

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE FINEST SOURCE IN THE CHAPTER AND IT ANSWERS EXERCISE 8 BY ITSELF. LOOK AT WHAT IT DOES.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **EACH STAGE OF SPINNING IS MATCHED TO A DIFFERENT KIND OF *ZIKR*.** *Taking the cotton, separating it, spooling the thread — **three motions, three forms of remembrance.** **The devotional practice is fitted to the WORK, not the work suspended for the devotion.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE METAPHOR IS DRAWN FROM THE WORK ITSELF: *"THE THREADS OF BREATH SHOULD BE COUNTED ONE BY ONE."*** *Breath becomes **thread**; the count becomes **spinning**. **A woman at a wheel is doing two things at once, and the poem makes them one thing.**
> - ⭐ **Note the address — *"OH SISTER"*** — and that the whole is *"offered to your PIR AS A GIFT."*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND NOW THE HISTORICAL POINT, WHICH IS THE ANSWER TO EXERCISE 8: *"IT IS THROUGH THIS MEDIUM THAT ISLAM GRADUALLY GAINED A PLACE IN THE VILLAGES OF THE DECCAN."*** ⭐ **Not through conquest, not through the *ulama*, not through royal decree — through LULLABIES, WEDDING SONGS AND SPINNING SONGS, in DAKHANI, sung by WOMEN doing housework.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And the traffic was two-way even here: the Bijapur Sufis were *"INSPIRED BY THE PRE-EXISTING BHAKTI TRADITION"* — the Lingayat *vachanas* of §3 and the Marathi *abhangs*.** **A Sufi genre shaped by bhakti, carrying Islam into Deccan villages, in women's voices. That is the whole chapter in one paragraph.**)*
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **EXERCISE 8 — *"why bhakti and sufi thinkers adopted A VARIETY OF LANGUAGES"* — should be built as a list with examples:** *⭐ **TAMIL** (Alvars, Nayanars) · **KANNADA** *vachanas* (Lingayats) · **MARATHI** *abhangs* · **HINDAVI** (Chishtis in Delhi) · **PUNJABI** (Baba Farid, Baba Guru Nanak's *shabad*) · **DAKHANI** (Bijapur Sufis) · **PERSIAN/URDU** (Sufi poetry, *masnavis*) · ***GINAN* in Punjabi, Multani, Sindhi, Kachchi, Hindi and Gujarati** (Khojahs) · ***SANT BHASHA*** (Kabir). ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The reason in one sentence: *these traditions addressed people directly rather than through a learned elite, so they used the languages people actually spoke — and NCERT's phrase for the Nayanars, "who sang in THE LANGUAGE OF THE PEOPLE", covers all of them.***

### 7.4 Sufis and the state ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```

   ⚠️ ** "However, THIS WAS BY NO MEANS A SITUATION OF ABSOLUTE

   ================================================================

   ================================================================

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE CRUCIAL PRACTICE: "The Chishtis accepted
      donations in cash and kind. RATHER THAN ACCUMULATE

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** WHY THE SULTANS NEEDED THEM -- the sharpest

   ================================================================
   ** "Kings did not simply need to DEMONSTRATE their

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "When the Turks set up the Delhi Sultanate, THEY
      RESISTED THE INSISTENCE OF THE ULAMA ON IMPOSING SHARI`A
      AS STATE LAW BECAUSE THEY ANTICIPATED OPPOSITION FROM

      ON JURISTS TO INTERPRET THE SHARI`A." **

   ⭐ ** "Besides, it was believed that THE AULIYA COULD
      INTERCEDE WITH GOD in order to improve the material and

      EXPLAINS WHY KINGS OFTEN WANTED THEIR TOMBS TO BE IN THE
      VICINITY OF SUFI SHRINES AND HOSPICES." **

   ================================================================
   ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND THE CONFLICTS -- do not omit these **
   ================================================================
   ** "However, THERE WERE INSTANCES OF CONFLICT BETWEEN THE
      SULTANS AND THE SUFIS. TO ASSERT THEIR AUTHORITY, BOTH
      EXPECTED THAT CERTAIN RITUALS BE PERFORMED SUCH AS
      PROSTRATION AND KISSING OF THE FEET." **
   ⭐ ** "Occasionally the sufi shaikh was addressed with
      HIGH-SOUNDING TITLES. For example, the disciples of
      Nizamuddin Auliya addressed him as ** SULTAN-UL-MASHAIKH
      (literally, SULTAN AMONGST SHAIKHS) ** ." **

   ** OTHER ORDERS: "the SUHRAWARDI under the Delhi Sultans and
      the NAQSHBANDI under the Mughals were ALSO ASSOCIATED

```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE LEGITIMATION ARGUMENT IS THE BEST POLITICAL ANALYSIS IN THE CHAPTER, AND IT IS THE CORE OF EXERCISE 7. STATE IT AS A PROBLEM AND A SOLUTION.**
> - ⭐ **THE PROBLEM:** *the Turks ruled a population **"the majority of whom were NON-MUSLIMS."*** **Imposing the *shari`a* as state law, as the *ulama* demanded, would have provoked opposition.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SOLUTION:** *go to the Sufis instead — because they *"**DERIVED THEIR AUTHORITY DIRECTLY FROM GOD — AND DID NOT DEPEND ON JURISTS TO INTERPRET THE SHARI`A.**"*
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **WHY IT WORKED:** *Sufi authority was **personal and charismatic**, not **legal and textual** — so it could **legitimise a ruler without committing him to a legal code his subjects would reject.** **And the Sufis were popular with the masses, whose support kings wanted anyway.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE CONFLICT PASSAGE IS THE SUBTLE PART — DO NOT LEAVE IT OUT.** ⭐ **Both Sultan and Shaikh expected *"PROSTRATION AND KISSING OF THE FEET"*** — *i.e. **they were competing for the same gestures of submission from the same people.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the disciples' title for Nizamuddin — *SULTAN-UL-MASHAIKH*, "Sultan amongst Shaikhs" — borrows the KING'S OWN WORD.** *A spiritual authority describing itself in royal vocabulary is making a claim, and the Sultans knew it.*
>
> 🔴 **NCERT's Discuss box: *"What are the POTENTIAL SOURCES OF CONFLICT in the relationship between religious and political leaders?"*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *The answer from this section: **competition for the same public deference** *(prostration, kissing of feet)*; **rival claims to legitimacy** — one derived from God directly, one needing that derivation; **the shaikh's independent moral authority**, which cannot be commanded; and **the danger of co-option** — which is why the Chishtis **spent donations rather than accumulating them.**

**📖 SOURCE 9 — *Declining a royal gift* (from the *Fawa'id-al-Fu'ad*, Nizamuddin Auliya's hospice, 1313) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**I (the author, AMIR HASAN SIJZI) had the good fortune of KISSING HIS FEET**… At this time **a local ruler had SENT HIM THE DEED OF OWNERSHIP TO TWO GARDENS AND MUCH LAND, along with THE PROVISIONS AND TOOLS FOR THEIR MAINTENANCE. The ruler had also made it clear that HE WAS RELINQUISHING ALL HIS RIGHTS.***
>
> *⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The master… HAD NOT ACCEPTED THAT GIFT. Instead, he had LAMENTED: 'WHAT HAVE I TO DO WITH GARDENS AND FIELDS AND LANDS?… NONE OF… OUR SPIRITUAL MASTERS HAD ENGAGED IN SUCH ACTIVITY.'***
>
> *Then he told an appropriate story: "…**Sultan GHIYASUDDIN, who at that time was still known as ULUGH KHAN, came to visit SHAIKH FARIDUDDIN (and) offered SOME MONEY AND OWNERSHIP DEEDS FOR FOUR VILLAGES** to the Shaikh, **the money being FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE DERVISHES, and the land FOR HIS USE.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Smiling, Shaikh al Islam (Fariduddin) said: 'GIVE ME THE MONEY. I WILL DISPENSE IT TO THE DERVISHES. BUT AS FOR THOSE LAND DEEDS, KEEP THEM. THERE ARE MANY WHO LONG FOR THEM. GIVE THEM AWAY TO SUCH PERSONS.'**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's questions: *"What aspects of THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE SUFIS AND THE STATE are best illustrated in this account? What does the account tell us about THE MODES OF COMMUNICATION between the Shaikh and his disciples?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE DISTINCTION BABA FARID DRAWS IS EXQUISITE AND IT IS THE WHOLE ANSWER: HE TAKES THE MONEY AND REFUSES THE LAND.**
> - ⭐ ***"Give me THE MONEY. I will DISPENSE IT to the dervishes."*** — **Cash is CONSUMED. It passes through him to those who need it, and leaves nothing behind.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***"But as for those LAND DEEDS, KEEP THEM."*** — **Land is PERMANENT. It generates income, requires management, creates dependants, and would turn a hospice into an estate.** *That is §7.4's principle exactly: *"**rather than ACCUMULATE donations, they preferred to USE THESE FULLY on immediate requirements.**"*
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the closing line is beautifully judged: *"THERE ARE MANY WHO LONG FOR THEM. GIVE THEM AWAY TO SUCH PERSONS."*** *No condemnation of the giver, no sermon — **he simply redirects the gift to people for whom it would be a gift.** *(⭐ **Note also that the offer itself was carefully constructed: money FOR THE DERVISHES, land FOR HIS OWN USE.** **Ulugh Khan was trying to make the Shaikh a landholder personally, and the Shaikh saw it.**)*
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **On MODES OF COMMUNICATION — the second question, and it is a source-criticism question.** ⭐ *The Shaikh **teaches by TELLING A STORY about his own master**, not by issuing a rule; the disciple **records the conversation**; and the record is a ***MALFUZAT*** *(see §9)*, compiled **with the shaikh's permission** and having *"obvious **DIDACTIC purposes**."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So we are reading an anecdote chosen for instruction, preserved by a devoted disciple, in a genre designed to edify. It is excellent evidence of Chishti VALUES, and weaker evidence of what happened on a particular day in 1313.**

**📖 SOURCE 10 — *The One Lord* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**Tell me, brother, HOW CAN THERE BE NO ONE LORD OF THE WORLD BUT TWO? WHO LED YOU SO ASTRAY? ⭐ GOD IS CALLED BY MANY NAMES: NAMES LIKE ALLAH, RAM, KARIM, KESHAV, HARI, AND HAZRAT. GOLD MAY BE SHAPED INTO RINGS AND BANGLES. ISN'T IT GOLD ALL THE SAME? DISTINCTIONS ARE ONLY WORDS WE INVENT…***
>
> *⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Kabir says THEY ARE BOTH MISTAKEN. NEITHER CAN FIND THE ONLY RAM. ONE KILLS THE GOAT, THE OTHER COWS. THEY WASTE THEIR LIVES IN DISPUTATION.**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's question: *"What is Kabir's ARGUMENT AGAINST THE DISTINCTION MADE BETWEEN GODS OF DIFFERENT COMMUNITIES?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ARGUMENT HAS THREE MOVES, AND THE GOLD IMAGE IS THE HINGE.**
> 1. ⭐ **THE NAMES ARE MANY; THE REFERENT IS ONE.** *Allah, Ram, Karim, Keshav, Hari, Hazrat — **three from each tradition, deliberately interleaved.**
> 2. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE GOLD ANALOGY:** *"**gold may be shaped into RINGS AND BANGLES. ISN'T IT GOLD ALL THE SAME?**" — **the FORM differs, the SUBSTANCE does not.** *And then the conclusion: *"**DISTINCTIONS ARE ONLY WORDS WE INVENT.**"*
> 3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE STING — HE CONDEMNS BOTH SIDES EQUALLY.** *"**Kabir says THEY ARE BOTH MISTAKEN**… **ONE KILLS THE GOAT, THE OTHER COWS. THEY WASTE THEIR LIVES IN DISPUTATION.**" ⭐ **He is not defending one community against the other; he is refusing the quarrel.** *The parallel construction — goat / cows — makes the two ritual practices **mirror images**, and equally beside the point.
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Note the word he keeps: *"the only RAM."*** *Having said all names are the same, **he still needs a name to use** — which is precisely the difficulty the *ulatbansi* poems address: **the Ultimate Reality cannot be captured in words, and yet a poet has only words.**

**⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE PROBLEM OF RECONSTRUCTING KABIR — the source-criticism that makes this section valuable**

> *"**WERE ALL THESE COMPOSED BY KABIR? WE MAY NEVER BE ABLE TO TELL WITH CERTAINTY**, although scholars have tried to analyse **the LANGUAGE, STYLE AND CONTENT** to establish which verses could be Kabir's."*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And NCERT's positive conclusion:** *"What this rich corpus of verses also signifies is that **KABIR WAS AND IS TO THE PRESENT A SOURCE OF INSPIRATION FOR THOSE WHO QUESTIONED ENTRENCHED RELIGIOUS AND SOCIAL INSTITUTIONS, IDEAS AND PRACTICES IN THEIR SEARCH FOR THE DIVINE.**"*

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE HINDU-OR-MUSLIM DEBATE, AND HOW TO HANDLE IT **
   ================================================================
   ** "his legacy was CLAIMED BY SEVERAL GROUPS… This is most
      evident in LATER DEBATES ABOUT WHETHER HE WAS A HINDU OR
      A MUSLIM BY BIRTH, debates that are REFLECTED IN
      HAGIOGRAPHIES." **
   ⭐ ** "Many of these were composed FROM THE SEVENTEENTH
      CENTURY ONWARDS, ABOUT 200 YEARS AFTER KABIR'S
      LIFETIME." **

   ** THE VAISHNAVA VERSION: "Hagiographies within the Vaishnava
      tradition attempted to suggest that HE WAS BORN A HINDU,
      KABIRDAS (Kabir itself is AN ARABIC WORD MEANING
      'GREAT'), BUT WAS RAISED BY A POOR MUSLIM FAMILY
      BELONGING TO THE COMMUNITY OF WEAVERS OR JULAHAS, WHO
      WERE RELATIVELY RECENT CONVERTS TO ISLAM. They also
      suggested that HE WAS INITIATED INTO BHAKTI BY A GURU,
      PERHAPS RAMANANDA." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND NCERT'S TWO-PART REFUTATION: **
   1. ** "the verses attributed to Kabir USE THE WORDS GURU AND
      SATGURU, BUT DO NOT MENTION THE NAME OF ANY SPECIFIC
      PRECEPTOR." **
   2. ⭐ ** "Historians have pointed out that IT IS VERY
      DIFFICULT TO ESTABLISH THAT RAMANANDA AND KABIR WERE
      CONTEMPORARIES, WITHOUT ASSIGNING IMPROBABLY LONG LIVES
      TO EITHER OR BOTH." **

   ** THE VERDICT: "while traditions linking the two CANNOT BE
      ACCEPTED AT FACE VALUE, THEY SHOW HOW IMPORTANT THE
      LEGACY OF KABIR WAS FOR LATER GENERATIONS." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CHRONOLOGICAL REFUTATION IS A LOVELY PIECE OF METHOD AND WORTH REPRODUCING: THE RAMANANDA CONNECTION FAILS ON ARITHMETIC.** ⭐ *To have Ramananda as Kabir's guru, **one or both would need an improbably long life.** **A dating check disposes of a devotional tradition.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND NCERT'S CLOSING MOVE IS THE ONE TO IMITATE — it does not simply discard the tradition, it REINTERPRETS it.** *The stories *"**cannot be accepted at face value**"* — **but the fact that groups competed to claim him "SHOWS HOW IMPORTANT THE LEGACY OF KABIR WAS FOR LATER GENERATIONS."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A false biography is still true evidence — of who wanted to own the man.** *(⭐ **Exactly [Part I Theme 4's hagiography rule](../../Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/04_Theme4_Thinkers_Beliefs_and_Buildings.md): poor evidence for what happened, excellent evidence for what followers believed.** *And note the delicious irony: **a poet who wrote *"distinctions are only words we invent"* became the subject of a two-century argument about which community he belonged to.**)*

### 8.2 Baba Guru Nanak and the Sacred Word ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "BABA GURU NANAK (1469-1539) was born in A HINDU MERCHANT
      FAMILY in a village called NANKANA SAHIB NEAR THE RIVER
      RAVI IN THE PREDOMINANTLY MUSLIM PUNJAB." **
   ⭐ ** "He TRAINED TO BE AN ACCOUNTANT AND STUDIED PERSIAN. He
      was married at a young age but HE SPENT MOST OF HIS TIME
      AMONG SUFIS AND BHAKTAS. He also TRAVELLED WIDELY." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE MESSAGE -- Exercise 5 **
   ================================================================
   ** "he advocated a form of ** NIRGUNA BHAKTI ** ." **
   ⭐ ** "He FIRMLY REPUDIATED THE EXTERNAL PRACTICES OF THE
      RELIGIONS HE SAW AROUND HIM. HE REJECTED: **
        ** SACRIFICES · RITUAL BATHS · IMAGE WORSHIP ·
           AUSTERITIES · AND THE SCRIPTURES OF BOTH HINDUS AND
           MUSLIMS. ** "
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "For Baba Guru Nanak, THE ABSOLUTE OR 'RAB' HAD
      NO GENDER OR FORM." **
   ** "He proposed A SIMPLE WAY TO CONNECT TO THE DIVINE BY
      REMEMBERING AND REPEATING THE DIVINE NAME, expressing his
      ideas through hymns called ** 'SHABAD' IN PUNJABI ** ,

   ⭐ ** "Baba Guru Nanak would SING THESE COMPOSITIONS IN
      VARIOUS RAGAS WHILE HIS ATTENDANT MARDANA PLAYED THE

   ================================================================
   ** THE ORGANISATION -- and this is what made it last **
   ================================================================
   * ** "He ORGANISED HIS FOLLOWERS INTO A COMMUNITY." **
   * ⭐ ** "He SET UP RULES FOR CONGREGATIONAL WORSHIP
     ( ** SANGAT ** ) INVOLVING COLLECTIVE RECITATION." **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "He APPOINTED ONE OF HIS DISCIPLES, ANGAD, TO

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE SCRIPTURE -- the sequence is a Prelims
      favourite **
   ================================================================
   ⭐ ** "It appears that Baba Guru Nanak DID NOT WISH TO
      ESTABLISH A NEW RELIGION, BUT AFTER HIS DEATH HIS
      FOLLOWERS CONSOLIDATED THEIR OWN PRACTICES AND
      DISTINGUISHED THEMSELVES FROM BOTH HINDUS AND MUSLIMS." **

   ** THE FIFTH preceptor, ** GURU ARJAN ** , "COMPILED Baba
      Guru Nanak's hymns ALONG WITH THOSE OF HIS FOUR SUCCESSORS
      AND OTHER RELIGIOUS POETS LIKE ** BABA FARID, RAVIDAS
      (also known as RAIDAS) AND KABIR ** in the
      ** ADI GRANTH SAHIB ** . These hymns, called
      ** 'GURBANI' ** , are COMPOSED IN VARIOUS LANGUAGES." **
        |
   ** "In the LATE SEVENTEENTH CENTURY the TENTH preceptor,
      ** GURU GOBIND SINGH ** , INCLUDED THE COMPOSITIONS OF
      THE NINTH GURU, ** GURU TEGH BAHADUR ** , AND THIS
      SCRIPTURE WAS CALLED THE ** GURU GRANTH SAHIB ** ." **
        |
   ⭐ ** "Guru Gobind Singh also LAID THE FOUNDATION OF THE
      ** KHALSA PANTH (ARMY OF THE PURE) ** AND DEFINED ITS
      FIVE SYMBOLS: ** UNCUT HAIR, A DAGGER, A PAIR OF SHORTS,

```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ***ADI GRANTH SAHIB*** IS THE SINGLE BEST PIECE OF EVIDENCE IN THIS CHAPTER FOR THE INTERPENETRATION OF THESE TRADITIONS — AND IT IS A FACT, NOT AN INTERPRETATION.** ⭐ **Guru Arjan compiled into one scripture: Baba Guru Nanak, his four successors, BABA FARID (a CHISHTI SUFI), RAVIDAS (a LEATHER WORKER), and KABIR.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A Sufi, an "untouchable" and a weaver, in the same book, in various languages.** *(⭐ **And Baba Farid is one of the five great Chishti masters in §7's table** — *the *dargah* at Ajodhan.* **His verses are in the Sikh scripture. That single fact answers half the questions this chapter can generate.**)*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND EXERCISE 5 — *"describe the major teachings of EITHER KABIR OR BABA GURU NANAK, AND THE WAYS IN WHICH THESE HAVE BEEN TRANSMITTED"* — has TWO halves, and the transmission half is where marks are lost. For Nanak:**
> - ⭐ **TEACHINGS:** ***nirguna* bhakti** · **rejection of sacrifices, ritual baths, image worship, austerities and the scriptures of both traditions** · **an Absolute (*rab*) with NO GENDER OR FORM** · **connection through remembering and repeating the Divine Name.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TRANSMISSION:** ***shabad* in PUNJABI**, **sung in various ragas with MARDANA on the RABAB** · ***SANGAT* — congregational collective recitation** · **succession of gurus for nearly 200 years, beginning with ANGAD** · **compilation by GURU ARJAN into the *ADI GRANTH SAHIB*** · **expansion by GURU GOBIND SINGH into the *GURU GRANTH SAHIB***.
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE POINT TO MAKE: Nanak's message survived intact because he BUILT AN INSTITUTION — a community, rules of worship, and a named successor.** *Compare **Mirabai (§8.3), who *"did not attract a sect or group of followers"*, and whose songs were *"transmitted ORALLY FOR CENTURIES."*** **Same devotional world; completely different transmission histories, and therefore completely different source problems.**

### 8.3 Mirabai, the devotee princess ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "MIRABAI (c. FIFTEENTH-SIXTEENTH CENTURIES) is perhaps
      THE BEST-KNOWN WOMAN POET WITHIN THE BHAKTI TRADITION." **

   ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE SOURCE PROBLEM, stated first: **
   ** "Biographies have been RECONSTRUCTED PRIMARILY FROM THE
      BHAJANS ATTRIBUTED TO HER, WHICH WERE TRANSMITTED ORALLY
      FOR CENTURIES." **

   ================================================================
   ** THE LIFE, "according to these" **
   ================================================================
   * ** "a RAJPUT PRINCESS FROM MERTA IN MARWAR who was MARRIED
     AGAINST HER WISHES to A PRINCE OF THE SISODIA CLAN OF
     MEWAR, RAJASTHAN." **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "She DEFIED HER HUSBAND AND DID NOT SUBMIT TO
     THE TRADITIONAL ROLE OF WIFE AND MOTHER, instead
     RECOGNISING KRISHNA, THE AVATAR OF VISHNU, AS HER
     LOVER." **
   * ** "Her in-laws TRIED TO POISON HER, but she ESCAPED FROM
     THE PALACE TO LIVE AS A WANDERING SAINT composing songs
     that are characterised by INTENSE EXPRESSIONS OF
     EMOTION." **
   * ⭐ ** "According to some traditions, HER PRECEPTOR WAS
     RAIDAS, A LEATHER WORKER. THIS WOULD INDICATE HER DEFIANCE
     OF THE NORMS OF CASTE SOCIETY." **
   * ** "After rejecting the comforts of her husband's palace,
     she is supposed to have DONNED THE WHITE ROBES OF A WIDOW
     OR THE SAFFRON ROBE OF THE RENOUNCER." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND HER AFTERLIFE, which is the important part: **
   ** "ALTHOUGH MIRABAI DID NOT ATTRACT A SECT OR GROUP OF
      FOLLOWERS, SHE HAS BEEN RECOGNISED AS A SOURCE OF
      INSPIRATION FOR CENTURIES. HER SONGS CONTINUE TO BE SUNG
      BY WOMEN AND MEN, ESPECIALLY THOSE WHO ARE POOR AND
      CONSIDERED 'LOW CASTE' IN GUJARAT AND RAJASTHAN." **
```

**📖 SOURCE 11 — *Love for the Lord* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

> *"**I will build A FUNERAL PYRE OF SANDALWOOD AND ALOE; LIGHT IT BY YOUR OWN HAND. When I am burned away to cinders; SMEAR THIS ASH UPON YOUR LIMBS**… **let FLAME BE LOST IN FLAME.**"*
>
> **And in another verse:** *"⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **WHAT CAN MEWAR'S RULER DO TO ME? IF GOD IS ANGRY, ALL IS LOST, BUT WHAT CAN THE RANA DO?**"*

> 🔴 **NCERT's question: *"What does this indicate about MIRABAI'S ATTITUDE TOWARDS THE KING?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TOTAL AND EXPLICIT DEFIANCE — and the structure of the couplet is the argument.** ⭐ *She sets up **a hierarchy of powers**: *"**IF GOD IS ANGRY, ALL IS LOST**"* — **there is one authority that matters** — *"**BUT WHAT CAN THE RANA DO?**"* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The rhetorical question answers itself: NOTHING.** *A princess of Mewar, addressing the ruler of Mewar, tells him he is **irrelevant.** *(⭐ **And note the political sting: the Rana of Mewar was among the most powerful Rajput rulers of the age.** **She names him and dismisses him.**)*
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The first verse is worth reading carefully too, because it inverts something.** ⭐ *A woman describing **her own funeral pyre, built of sandalwood and aloe, lit BY HER LORD'S OWN HAND**, and her ash **smeared on his limbs** — *"let flame be lost in flame."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The imagery of a widow's pyre is taken over and transformed into a union she CHOOSES and CONTROLS.** *(⭐ **Set this beside [Theme 5 §7's sati material](05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md), where the defining feature was the ABSENCE OF CONSENT, and the contrast is complete.** **Handle it carefully and precisely: the point is about POETIC IMAGERY and AGENCY, not an endorsement of the practice.**)*

> 🔴 **NCERT's Discuss box: *"Why do you think the traditions of KABIR, BABA GURU NANAK and MIRABAI REMAIN SIGNIFICANT IN THE TWENTY-FIRST CENTURY?"***
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A strong answer picks a different reason for each:** ⭐ **KABIR** — *"a SOURCE OF INSPIRATION FOR THOSE WHO QUESTIONED ENTRENCHED RELIGIOUS AND SOCIAL INSTITUTIONS"*, and his refusal of the communal quarrel is **directly relevant to a plural society.** ⭐ **BABA GURU NANAK** — because **he built an institution**: a living community, a scripture, a continuing tradition of *sangat* and *langar*. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MIRABAI** — because *"her songs CONTINUE TO BE SUNG BY WOMEN AND MEN, ESPECIALLY THOSE WHO ARE POOR AND CONSIDERED 'LOW CASTE'"* — **a woman who refused marriage, caste and royal authority remains usable by exactly the people those structures still constrain.**

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SHANKARADEVA — the box, and a named regional tradition worth having:** *"In the **LATE FIFTEENTH CENTURY, SHANKARADEVA emerged as one of the leading proponents of VAISHNAVISM IN ASSAM.** His teachings, often known as the ***BHAGAVATI DHARMA*** because they were based on the ***BHAGAVAD GITA* and the *BHAGAVATA PURANA***, focused on **ABSOLUTE SURRENDER TO THE SUPREME DEITY**, in this case Vishnu. ⭐ **He emphasised the need for *NAAM KIRTAN*, RECITATION OF THE NAMES OF THE LORD in *SAT SANGA* or CONGREGATIONS OF PIOUS DEVOTEES.** He also encouraged the establishment of ***SATRA* or MONASTERIES** for the transmission of spiritual knowledge, and ***NAAM GHAR* or PRAYER HALLS**. ⭐ **Many of these institutions and practices CONTINUE TO FLOURISH in the region.** His major compositions include the ***KIRTANA-GHOSHA***."*

## 9️⃣ Reconstructing Histories of Religious Traditions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⛔⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CHAPTER'S PAYLOAD SECTION, and the four Sufi source-types are near-certain Prelims material.**

```
   ** THE RANGE OF SOURCES: "SCULPTURE, ARCHITECTURE, STORIES
      ABOUT RELIGIOUS PRECEPTORS, COMPOSITIONS ATTRIBUTED TO
      WOMEN AND MEN engaged in the quest of understanding the

   ⭐ ** THE DIFFICULTY: "they include A WIDE VARIETY, WRITTEN IN
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NCERT'S WARNING ABOUT THE *TAZKIRAS* IS THE MOST IMPORTANT SENTENCE IN §9, AND IT IS A MODEL OF BALANCED SOURCE CRITICISM:** *"**The authors of the tazkiras OFTEN SOUGHT TO ESTABLISH THE PRECEDENCE OF THEIR OWN ORDERS AND GLORIFY THEIR SPIRITUAL GENEALOGIES. ⭐ MANY DETAILS ARE OFTEN IMPLAUSIBLE, FULL OF ELEMENTS OF THE FANTASTIC. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ STILL THEY ARE OF GREAT VALUE FOR HISTORIANS AND HELP THEM TO UNDERSTAND MORE FULLY THE NATURE OF THE TRADITION.**"*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Note the three-step structure — say what it IS, say what is WRONG with it, say why it is STILL WORTH USING.** ⭐ *A *tazkira* is written **to prove one's own order is the senior one** *(recall §6.1's *silsila* — a chain back to the Prophet, and therefore a contested claim)*; **many details are fantastic**; **and it remains valuable** because it tells you **what the tradition believed about itself.** *(⭐ **Copy that three-step move for ANY source in ANY chapter and your answers improve immediately.**)*

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE CHAPTER'S CLOSING PROBLEM — a genuinely delicate one, stated honestly:**

> *"Remember that **each of the traditions… GENERATED A WIDE RANGE OF TEXTUAL AND ORAL MODES OF COMMUNICATION, SOME OF WHICH HAVE BEEN PRESERVED, MANY OF WHICH HAVE BEEN MODIFIED IN THE PROCESS OF TRANSMISSION, AND OTHERS ARE PROBABLY LOST FOREVER.**"*
>
> *"**VIRTUALLY ALL THESE RELIGIOUS TRADITIONS CONTINUE TO FLOURISH TO DATE.** ⭐ This continuity has **CERTAIN ADVANTAGES for historians as it allows them to COMPARE CONTEMPORARY PRACTICES WITH THOSE DESCRIBED IN TEXTUAL TRADITIONS or shown in old paintings and TO TRACE CHANGES.***
>
> *⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **At the same time, BECAUSE THESE TRADITIONS ARE PART OF PEOPLES' LIVED BELIEFS AND PRACTICES, THERE IS OFTEN A LACK OF ACCEPTANCE OF THE POSSIBILITY THAT THESE MAY HAVE CHANGED OVER TIME. THE CHALLENGE FOR HISTORIANS IS TO UNDERTAKE SUCH INVESTIGATIONS WITH SENSITIVITY, WHILE AT THE SAME TIME RECOGNISING THAT RELIGIOUS TRADITIONS, LIKE OTHER TRADITIONS, ARE DYNAMIC AND CHANGE OVER TIME.**"*

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE MOST DIFFICULT PROBLEM IN THE WHOLE OF PART II, AND NCERT NAMES IT PLAINLY.** ⭐ **A living tradition is BOTH an advantage and an obstacle: you can watch it, but its adherents may not accept that it has a history at all.** *And NCERT's resolution is the right one and worth quoting: **investigate WITH SENSITIVITY, while recognising that religious traditions, LIKE OTHER TRADITIONS, ARE DYNAMIC AND CHANGE OVER TIME.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And the chapter has PROVED it changes: Agni, Indra and Soma became marginal (§1.2); a tribal wooden image became Vishnu (§1.1); Chishtis in Delhi practised yogic exercises (§7.1); Amir Khusrau invented the *qaul* in the fourteenth century and it is still sung today (§7.2).** **Change is not an accusation against a tradition; it is the record of its life.**)*

### 🔴 Prelims traps — the claim vs the truth

| ❌ The plausible-sounding claim | ✅ What the chapter actually says |
|---|---|
| Integration meant Brahmanas imposing their ideas | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TWO processes** — dissemination of Puranic texts **AND "the Brahmanas ACCEPTING AND REWORKING the beliefs… of other social categories"** |
| "Great" and "little" traditions is NCERT's own framework | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Coined by the sociologist **ROBERT REDFIELD** in the twentieth century; NCERT keeps the **quotation marks** because scholars are *"uncomfortable with the HIERARCHY suggested"* |
| Jagannatha was always a form of Vishnu | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A LOCAL DEITY**, image *"made of WOOD BY LOCAL TRIBAL SPECIALISTS"*, **identified as a form of Vishnu BY THE TWELFTH CENTURY** — and Vishnu was thereby *"visualised in a way VERY DIFFERENT from that in other parts of the country"* |
| Local goddesses were absorbed as forms of Devi | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ They were incorporated **"by providing them with AN IDENTITY AS A WIFE of the principal male deities"** — equated with **Lakshmi or Parvati** |
| Vedic gods remained central | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AGNI, INDRA and SOMA "BECOME MARGINAL FIGURES, RARELY VISIBLE"** — yet *"the Vedas CONTINUED TO BE REVERED AS AUTHORITATIVE"* |
| Tantric practices excluded women | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**OPEN TO WOMEN AND MEN**"*, and practitioners *"often IGNORED DIFFERENCES OF CASTE AND CLASS **WITHIN THE RITUAL CONTEXT**"* |
| *Saguna* means "without attributes" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***SAGUNA* = WITH attributes** *(specific deities, anthropomorphic)*; ***NIRGUNA* = WITHOUT** *(an abstract god)* |
| Alvars worshipped Shiva | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALVARS = VISHNU** *("those IMMERSED in devotion")*; **NAYANARS = SHIVA** *("LEADERS")* |
| The *Tevaram* is an Alvar compilation | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***TEVARAM* = the NAYANARS*** (Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar), compiled in the tenth century **on the basis of THE MUSIC of the songs**. The **ALVARS'** anthology is the ***NALAYIRA DIVYAPRABANDHAM*** — **12 Alvars, "Four Thousand Sacred Compositions", called THE TAMIL VEDA** |
| Andal and Karaikkal Ammaiyar followed the same path | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ANDAL (Vishnu) saw herself as THE BELOVED of the deity; KARAIKKAL AMMAIYAR (Shiva) adopted EXTREME ASCETICISM** and describes herself as a ***PEY* (demoness)** |
| Bhakti women became nuns | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"These women **renounced their social obligations, BUT DID NOT JOIN AN ALTERNATIVE ORDER OR BECOME NUNS**"* |
| The Cholas opposed the bhakti saints | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ They **built temples, introduced Tamil Shaiva hymns into temple worship, compiled the *Tevaram*, and — c.945, under PARANTAKA I — CONSECRATED METAL IMAGES of Appar, Sambandar and Sundarar** |
| Basavanna was a wandering ascetic | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A BRAHMANA (1106–68) who was A MINISTER in the court of a KALACHURI ruler** |
| Lingayats cremate their dead | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **They BURY them ceremonially** — because they believe the devotee **unites with Shiva and WILL NOT RETURN**, so the *Dharmashastra* funerary rites are pointless |
| Lingayats accepted the theory of rebirth | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **They QUESTIONED it** — and challenged caste and pollution, while **encouraging POST-PUBERTY MARRIAGE and WIDOW REMARRIAGE** |
| *Vachanas* were composed in Sanskrit | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **In KANNADA**, *"by WOMEN AND MEN who joined the movement"* |
| North India had bhakti movements from the sixth century | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"historians have **NOT FOUND EVIDENCE of anything resembling the compositions of the Alvars and Nayanars TILL THE FOURTEENTH CENTURY**"* |
| The *shari`a* derives only from the Qur'an | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **From the QUR'AN, HADIS, QIYAS (reasoning by analogy) and IJMA (consensus)** — the last two recognised as Islamic rule expanded into areas with different customs |
| *Zimmi* status applied only to Jews and Christians | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"IN INDIA THIS STATUS WAS EXTENDED TO HINDUS AS WELL"**; *zimmis* paid **JIZYA** and gained **the right to protection** |
| Only Akbar made grants to non-Muslim institutions | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"These grants were made by **several Mughal rulers, INCLUDING AKBAR AND AURANGZEB**"* — see **Aurangzeb's 1661–62 letter to a Jogi** |
| *Ginan* is an Arabic term | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Derived from the Sanskrit *JNANA*, "knowledge"** — devotional poems of the **KHOJAHS** *(a branch of the ISMAILIS, a Shi`a sect)*, in **Punjabi, Multani, Sindhi, Kachchi, Hindi and Gujarati**, sung **in special ragas** |
| Arab settlers in Kerala kept their own customs | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ They **adopted MALAYALAM**, and **local customs such as MATRILINY and MATRILOCAL RESIDENCE** |
| Mosque architecture is uniform | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Universal in ORIENTATION** *(mihrab, minbar)*; **variable in ROOFS and MATERIALS** — a **shikhara-like roof** in Kerala, **brick** at the Atiya mosque (Bangladesh, 1609), **carved wood and papier mâché** at the Shah Hamadan mosque (Srinagar, 1395) |
| Sanskrit texts of the 8th–14th centuries called Muslims "Muslims" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"the term MUSALMAN or Muslim was **VIRTUALLY NEVER USED**"* — instead **TURUSHKA, TAJIKA, PARASHIKA**, or the recycled **SHAKA / YAVANA**, or the general **MLECHCHHA** |
| "Sufism" is the tradition's own word | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **An ENGLISH word coined in the NINETEENTH CENTURY**; the Islamic term is ***TASAWWUF*** — possibly from ***suf*** (wool), ***safa*** (purity) or ***suffa*** (the platform outside the Prophet's mosque) |
| *Silsila* means a lineage of teachings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Literally A CHAIN** — an unbroken spiritual genealogy **to the Prophet Muhammad**, through which *"spiritual power and blessings were transmitted"* |
| *Urs* means a death anniversary | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ It **means MARRIAGE** — *"signifying **THE UNION OF HIS SOUL WITH GOD**"* — observed on the death anniversary |
| The Chishti order is named after its founder | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Named after A PLACE — the town of CHISHT in central Afghanistan.** *(The **QADIRI** order is the one named after a founder, **Shaikh Abd'ul Qadir Jilani**)* |
| Muinuddin Chishti's *dargah* is in Delhi | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AJMER.** *Of the five, **three are in Delhi** (Bakhtiyar Kaki, Nizamuddin Auliya, Chiragh-i Dehli); **Fariduddin Ganj-i Shakar's is at AJODHAN, in present-day Pakistan** |
| The Chishti *langar* was funded by royal endowment | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Run on ***FUTUH* — "UNASKED-FOR CHARITY."** And the Chishtis **spent donations rather than accumulating them** |
| The Chishtis kept local practices out | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ They adopted **bowing before the Shaikh, offering water to visitors, shaving initiates' heads, and YOGIC EXERCISES** — *"attempts to ASSIMILATE LOCAL TRADITIONS"* |
| Akbar visited Ajmer once | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **FOURTEEN TIMES**, sometimes two or three times a year, **until 1580**; in **1568 he gave a huge cauldron (*DEGH*)** and built a mosque in the compound |
| *Qawwali* has no known origin | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AMIR KHUSRAU (1253–1325)**, disciple of Nizamuddin Auliya, **introduced the *QAUL*** — a hymn sung at the opening or closing of *qawwali*, still used first at Nizamuddin's shrine |
| Baba Farid's verses are only in Sufi collections | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **They were INCORPORATED IN THE GURU GRANTH SAHIB** |
| Islam spread in the Deccan villages by conquest | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Through **DAKHANI poems** *"probably sung by WOMEN while performing household chores like GRINDING GRAIN AND SPINNING"*, plus ***LURINAMA* (lullabies)** and ***SHADINAMA* (wedding songs)** — *"it is through this medium that Islam gradually gained a place"* |
| The Sultans imposed the *shari`a* as state law | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **They RESISTED the *ulama*'s insistence**, anticipating opposition from a majority non-Muslim population, and **turned to the SUFIS, "who derived their authority DIRECTLY FROM GOD"** |
| Nizamuddin accepted the land grant offered in 1313 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **He REFUSED it** — and told the story of **Baba Farid taking the MONEY (to dispense to dervishes) but returning the LAND DEEDS** |
| Kabir's verses survive in one collection | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THREE — the *KABIR BIJAK* (Kabirpanth, Varanasi), the *KABIR GRANTHAVALI* (Dadupanth, Rajasthan), and the *ADI GRANTH SAHIB*** — all compiled **long after his death** |
| Kabir named Ramananda as his guru | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The verses *"use the words GURU and SATGURU, but **DO NOT MENTION THE NAME OF ANY SPECIFIC PRECEPTOR**"*, and it is *"very difficult to establish that Ramananda and Kabir were CONTEMPORARIES"* |
| Baba Guru Nanak set out to found a new religion | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"It appears that he **DID NOT WISH TO ESTABLISH A NEW RELIGION**, but AFTER HIS DEATH his followers consolidated their own practices"* |
| The *Adi Granth Sahib* contains only Sikh gurus' hymns | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Guru Arjan compiled Nanak and his four successors ALONG WITH BABA FARID, RAVIDAS (RAIDAS) AND KABIR** |
| The *Guru Granth Sahib* and *Adi Granth* are the same | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Guru Gobind Singh added the compositions of the ninth guru, GURU TEGH BAHADUR**, and the scripture was then called the ***GURU GRANTH SAHIB*** |
| Mirabai founded a sect | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**Mirabai DID NOT ATTRACT A SECT OR GROUP OF FOLLOWERS**"* — her *bhajans* were **transmitted ORALLY for centuries** |
| *Malfuzat* means letters | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***MALFUZAT* = "UTTERED", CONVERSATIONS. *MAKTUBAT* = "WRITTEN", LETTERS. *TAZKIRA* = biographical account.** The first Indian *tazkira* is the ***SIYAR-UL-AULIYA* of MIR KHWURD KIRMANI** |

| ***Murid* / *khalifa*** | ⭐ **The disciple / the appointed successor** |
| ***Silsila*** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Literally **A CHAIN** — an unbroken spiritual genealogy **to the Prophet**, transmitting power and blessings |
| ***Dargah*** | ⭐ Persian, **"COURT"** — the shaikh's tomb-shrine |
| ***Ziyarat* / *urs*** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Pilgrimage to the tomb / the death anniversary — literally "MARRIAGE", the union of the soul with God** |
| ***Wali* (pl. *auliya*)** | ⭐ **"Friend of God"** — claiming **proximity to Allah**, His grace (***barakat***), and miracles (***karamat***) |
| ***Be-shari`a* / *ba-shari`a*** | ⭐ Sufis who **defied** the *shari`a* *(Qalandars, Madaris, Malangs, Haidaris)* / those who **complied** |
| ***Jama'at khana*** | The **big hall** of a *khanqah*, where inmates and visitors lived and prayed |
| ***Langar* / *futuh*** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The open kitchen / UNASKED-FOR charity**, on which it ran |
| ***Zikr* / *sama*** | ⭐ **Reciting the Divine Names / "audition", the performance of mystical music** |
| ***Qawwali* / *qaul*** | ⭐ The performance / **the hymn Amir Khusrau introduced** to open or close it |
| ***Auqaf* / *inam*** | **Charitable trusts / tax-free land** granted by Sultans |
| ***Masnavi*** | **A long poem** using **human love as an allegory** of divine love — e.g. **Jayasi's *PADMAVAT*** |
| ***Lurinama* / *shadinama*** | ⭐ **Lullabies / wedding songs** — the Dakhani genres through which *"Islam gradually gained a place in the villages of the Deccan"* |
| ***Sant bhasha* / *ulatbansi*** | ⭐ **The special language of nirguna poets / "UPSIDE-DOWN SAYINGS"** in which everyday meanings are inverted |
| ***Shabad* / *gurbani* / *sangat*** | ⭐ **Baba Guru Nanak's hymns / the compiled hymns / congregational worship** |
| ***Malfuzat* / *maktubat* / *tazkira*** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Uttered" (conversations) / "written" (letters) / "to mention and memorialise" (biographies of saints)** |

## 🔁 Recap — the chapter in ten lines

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Religions grew by ABSORPTION** — a tribal wooden image became **Jagannatha**; local goddesses became **wives of the great gods**; and **Vishnu himself was changed** by being worshipped at Puri.
2. **Agni, Indra and Soma became marginal figures** while Vishnu, Shiva and the goddess rose — **and the Vedas remained authoritative throughout.**
3. **The ALVARS (Vishnu) and NAYANARS (Shiva)** sang in **Tamil**, identified shrines that became **great pilgrimage temples**, and **ended up as metal images inside them** — consecrated by **Parantaka I around 945**.
4. ⭐ **They and the VIRASHAIVAS criticised caste** — Appar dismissing *gotra* and *kula*, the Lingayats rejecting **pollution and rebirth**, burying their dead, and encouraging **post-puberty marriage and widow remarriage in the twelfth century.**
5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **North India produced no equivalent until the fourteenth century** because **Brahmanas held both secular and ritual office in the Rajput states** — until **the Delhi Sultanate undermined both.**
6. **The *shari`a* itself grew by adaptation** — adding ***qiyas*** and ***ijma*** as Islamic rule met unfamiliar customs — and ***zimmi*** status was **extended to Hindus.**
7. ⭐ **Islam was practised locally**: **Khojah *ginan* in six languages**, **Arab settlers in Malabar adopting Malayalam and MATRILINY**, and **mosques with shikhara-like roofs, brick walls and papier mâché.**
8. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **For seven centuries of Sanskrit sources, the word *musalman* is virtually never used** — people were identified by **region and language**, not creed.
9. **The CHISHTIS dominated because they adapted** — a *langar* run on **unasked-for charity**, visitors including **Hindu jogis**, **yogic exercises**, **Hindavi and Dakhani verse** — and **the Sultans needed them** because Sufi authority came **directly from God** and did not require imposing the *shari`a* on a non-Muslim majority.
10. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the two streams met in §8** — **Kabir** naming God as **Allah, Khuda, Brahman and Atman alike**, **Baba Guru Nanak** building a community whose scripture contains **a Chishti Sufi, a leather worker and a weaver**, and **Mirabai**, who founded no sect and is still sung by the poor.
