### 1. Kingdoms Associated with the Buddha's Life
Which of the following Kingdoms were associated with the life of the Buddha?

1. Avanti
2. Gandhara
3. Kosala
4. Magadha

- (a) 1, 2 and 3
- (b) 2 and 4
- (c) 1, 3 and 4 only
- (d) 3 and 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) 3 and 4** *(corrected)* · `Ancient India` `Buddha's biography` — Kosala (Shravasti, Jetavana monastery) and Magadha (Bodh Gaya, Rajgir) were the Buddha's real spheres of activity; Gandhara lay far to the north-west, outside his teaching region, and Avanti had no direct personal association. ⚠️ **This file's primary source (superkalam.com) initially reported (c) as correct; cross-checked against multiple independent sources, the official key is (d).** Note also: this exact question, with slightly different option wording, was **repeated in 2015** — see [2015.md](2015.md) Q13.

</details>

### 3. Queen Victoria's Proclamation, 1858
What was/were the object/objects of Queen Victoria's Proclamation (1858)?

1. To disclaim any intention to annex Indian States.
2. To place the Indian administration under the British Crown.
3. To regulate the East India Company's trade with India.

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 and 2 only** · `Modern India` `1858 Proclamation` — cross-link: [Spectrum file 12](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/12_Topup_Tables.md), Part E, on the post-1858 administrative reorganisation. By 1858, the Company's **commercial functions had already ended**, so trade regulation was not a Proclamation object.

</details>

### 8. Sattriya Dance
With reference to the famous Sattriya dance, consider the following statements:

1. Sattriya is a combination of music, dance and drama.
2. It is a centuries-old living tradition of Vaishnavites of Assam.
3. It is based on classical Ragas and Talas of devotional songs composed by Tulsidas, Kabir and Mirabai.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 1 and 2 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 1 and 2 only** · `Art & Culture` `Classical dance` — Sattriya's devotional compositions are drawn from **Srimanta Sankardeva's own Assamese Vaishnavite tradition**, not Tulsidas/Kabir/Mirabai (who belong to other regional Bhakti streams).

</details>

### 10. The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy
Which one of the following pairs does not form part of the six systems of Indian Philosophy?

- (a) Mimamsa and Vedanta
- (b) Nyaya and Vaisheshika
- (c) Lokayata and Kapalika
- (d) Sankhya and Yoga

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Lokayata and Kapalika** *(not part of the six orthodox Vedic systems)* · `Ancient India` `Philosophy` — the six *astika* systems are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimamsa and Vedanta; Lokayata (Charvaka materialism) and Kapalika (a Tantric Shaiva sect) are both outside this canonical six.

</details>

### 17. "Bijak" and PushtiMarg
Consider the following statements:

1. 'Bijak' is a composition of the teachings of Saint Dadu Dayal.
2. The philosophy of PushtiMarg was propounded by Madhvacharya.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) Neither 1 nor 2** · `Medieval India` `Bhakti philosophy` — *Bijak* records the teachings of **Kabir**, not Dadu Dayal; PushtiMarg was propounded by **Vallabhacharya**, not Madhvacharya.

</details>

### 21. Sculptures and Sites
With reference to the Indian history of art and culture, consider the following pairs:

| Famous work of sculpture | Site |
|---|---|
| 1. A grand image of Buddha's Mahaparinirvana, with numerous celestial musicians above and sorrowful followers below | Ajanta |
| 2. A huge image of Varaha Avatar of Vishnu rescuing Goddess Earth, sculpted on rock | Mount Abu |
| 3. 'Arjuna's Penance'/'Descent of Ganga' sculpted on huge boulders | Mamallapuram |

Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 1 and 3 only** · `Art & Culture` `Rock-cut sculpture` — the great Varaha rock-relief is at **Udayagiri (Madhya Pradesh)**, not Mount Abu (which is famous instead for its Dilwara Jain marble temples).

</details>

### 22. Languages with Classical Status

1. Gujarati
2. Kannada
3. Telugu

Which of the above has/have been declared a 'Classical Language' by the Government?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 3 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 2 and 3 only** · `Art & Culture` `Classical languages` — Gujarati has **not** been given classical-language status; Kannada (2008) and Telugu (2008) both have.

</details>
