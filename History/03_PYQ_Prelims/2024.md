### 1. Archaeological Sites, States and Descriptions
Consider the following information:

| Archaeological Site | State | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Chandraketugarh | Odisha | Trading port town |
| 2. Inamgaon | Maharashtra | Chalcolithic site |
| 3. Mangadu | Kerala | Megalithic site |
| 4. Salihundam | Andhra Pradesh | Rock-cut cave shrines |

In which of the above rows is the given information correctly matched?

- (a) 1 and 2
- (b) 2 and 3
- (c) 3 and 4
- (d) 1 and 4

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 and 3** · `Ancient India` `Archaeological sites` — Chandraketugarh is actually in **West Bengal**, not Odisha; Salihundam is famous for **Buddhist stupas and monastery remains**, not rock-cut cave shrines.

</details>

### 2. Upanishads and Puranas
Consider the following statements:

1. There are no parables in the Upanishads.
2. The Upanishads were composed earlier than the Puranas.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 only** · `Ancient India` `Vedic literature` — the Upanishads **do** contain well-known parables (e.g. Nachiketa in the Katha Upanishad), making statement 1 false.

</details>

### 3. Epithets of the Buddha
With reference to ancient India, Gautama Buddha was generally known by which of the following epithets?

1. Nayaputta
2. Shakyamuni
3. Tathagata

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1, 2 and 3
- (d) None of the above are epithets of Gautama Buddha

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 and 3 only** · `Ancient India` `Buddhist terminology` — "Nayaputta" (Nataputta) is actually an epithet of **Mahavira** (Jainism), not the Buddha — a classic cross-tradition trap.

</details>

### 4. Portuguese Fort at Bhatkal
Who among the following rulers of medieval India gave permission to the Portuguese to build a fort at Bhatkal?

- (a) Krishnadevaraya
- (b) Narasimha Saluva
- (c) Muhammad Shah III
- (d) Yusuf Adil Shah

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) Krishnadevaraya** · `Medieval India` `Vijayanagara` `Advent of the Europeans` — permitted Albuquerque to build the fort at Bhatkal (Uttara Kannada, Karnataka) in 1510. Cross-link: [Spectrum file 02](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/02_Advent_of_the_Europeans.md).

</details>

### 5. The Government of India Act, 1935
With reference to the Government of India Act, 1935, consider the following statements:

1. It provided for the establishment of an All India Federation based on the union of British Indian Provinces and Princely States.
2. Defence and Foreign Affairs were kept under the control of the federal legislature.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 only** · `Modern India` `GoI Act 1935` — Defence and Foreign Affairs were actually kept as the **Governor-General's personal reserved subjects**, outside the federal legislature's control, making statement 2 false. Cross-link: [Spectrum file 08](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/08_From_Simon_to_Cabinet_Mission.md) covers the 1935 Act's full architecture.

</details>

### 6. Post-Independence Party Leaders
Consider the following pairs:

| Party | Its Leader |
|---|---|
| 1. Bharatiya Jana Sangh | Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee |
| 2. Socialist Party | C. Rajagopalachari |
| 3. Congress for Democracy | Jagjivan Ram |
| 4. Swatantra Party | Acharya Narendra Dev |

How many of the above are correctly matched?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Modern India` `Post-independence party system` — pairs 1 and 3 are correct; the **Socialist Party's** central figures were Jayaprakash Narayan/Ram Manohar Lohia, not Rajagopalachari (who instead founded the **Swatantra Party**); Acharya Narendra Dev is instead linked to the **Congress Socialist Party/Praja Socialist Party**, not Swatantra — a clean cross-swap distractor.

</details>

### 7. Ryotwari and Permanent Settlement
With reference to revenue collection by Cornwallis, consider the following statements:

1. Under the Ryotwari Settlement of revenue collection, the peasants were exempted from revenue payment in case of bad harvests or natural calamities.
2. Under the Permanent Settlement in Bengal, if the Zamindar failed to pay his revenues to the state on or before the fixed date, he would be removed from his Zamindari.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

⚠️ **UPSC dropped this question from the official final answer key** — a source defect flagged here rather than silently omitted. The question itself is also internally confused, since **Cornwallis is associated with the Permanent Settlement, not the Ryotwari Settlement** (introduced later by Alexander Read and Thomas Munro — see [2017.md](2017.md) Q7), which may be why it was withdrawn. Reported answer prior to withdrawal: **(b) 2 only**.

</details>

### 9. Sanghabhuti's Commentary
Sanghabhuti, an Indian Buddhist monk who travelled to China at the end of the fourth century AD, was the author of a commentary on —

- (a) Prajnaparamita Sutra
- (b) Visuddhimagga
- (c) Sarvastivada Vinaya
- (d) Lalitavistara

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Sarvastivada Vinaya** · `Ancient India` `Buddhist monastic transmission to China`

</details>

### 10. UNESCO World Heritage Inscriptions of 2023
Consider the following properties included in the World Heritage List released by UNESCO:

1. Shantiniketan
2. Rani-ki-Vav
3. Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas
4. Mahabodhi Temple Complex at Bodhgaya

How many of the above properties were included in 2023?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Art & Culture` `UNESCO World Heritage` — **Shantiniketan and the Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas** were the 2023 inscriptions; Rani-ki-Vav (2014) and the Mahabodhi Temple Complex (2002) were inscribed much earlier.

</details>

### 11. Bhasa's Work
Which one of the following is a work attributed to the playwright Bhasa?

- (a) Kavyalankara
- (b) Natyashastra
- (c) Madhyama-vyayoga
- (d) Mahabhashya

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Madhyama-vyayoga** · `Ancient India` `Sanskrit drama` — a pre-Kalidasa playwright.

</details>
