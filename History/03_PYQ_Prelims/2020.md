### 1. Gupta-Era Ports
With reference to the period of the Gupta dynasty in ancient India, the towns Ghantasala, Kaduara and Chaul were well known as —

- (a) Ports handling foreign trade
- (b) Capitals of powerful kingdoms
- (c) Places of exquisite stone art and architecture
- (d) Important Buddhist pilgrimage centres

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) Ports handling foreign trade** · `Ancient India` `Gupta period` `Trade`

</details>

### 3. Ancient Place Names and Modern Locations
With reference to the history of India, consider the following pairs:

1. Bhilsa — Madhya Pradesh
2. Dwarasamudra — Maharashtra
3. Girinagar — Gujarat
4. Sthanesvara — Uttar Pradesh

Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

- (a) 1 and 3 only
- (b) 1 and 4 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 2 and 4 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 and 3 only** *(corrected — see below)* · `Ancient India` `Historical geography` — Bhilsa (ancient Vidisha) is correctly in Madhya Pradesh, and Girinagar is correctly in Gujarat; Dwarasamudra was the Hoysala capital, in **Karnataka**, not Maharashtra, and Sthanesvara (Thanesar) is in **Haryana**, not Uttar Pradesh. ⚠️ **This file's first-pass source (superkalam.com) reported (b) as correct; cross-checking against multiple independent sources confirms the official UPSC key is (a) — corrected here rather than left standing.**

</details>

### 4. Chronological Order of Regional Powers
Consider the following events in the history of India:

1. Rise of the Pratiharas under King Bhoja
2. Establishment of Pallava power under Mahendravarman-I
3. Establishment of Chola power by Parantaka-I
4. The Pala dynasty founded by Gopala

What is the correct chronological order of the above events, starting from the earliest time?

- (a) 2-1-4-3
- (b) 3-1-4-2
- (c) 2-4-1-3
- (d) 3-4-1-2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 2-4-1-3** · `Medieval India` `Regional dynasties` `Chronology` — Pallava (7th c.) → Pala (8th c.) → Pratihara/Bhoja (9th c.) → Chola/Parantaka I (10th c.).

</details>

### 6. "Desher Katha"
With reference to the book 'Desher Katha' written by Sakharam Ganesh Deuskar during the freedom struggle, consider the following statements:

1. It warned against the Colonial State's hypnotic conquest of the mind.
2. It inspired the performance of swadeshi street plays and folk songs.
3. The use of 'desh' by Deuskar was in the specific context of the region of Bengal.

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) 1 and 2 only** · `Modern India` `Swadeshi Movement` `Nationalist literature` — cross-link: [Spectrum file 10](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/10_Early_Nationalism_Topups.md), Part C, on the Swadeshi cultural sphere. Deuskar's *desh* meant **India as a whole**, not only Bengal, making statement 3 false.

</details>

### 7. The Gandhi–Irwin Pact
The Gandhi–Irwin Pact included which of the following?

1. Invitation to Congress to participate in the Round Table Conference
2. Withdrawal of Ordinances promulgated in connection with the Civil Disobedience Movement
3. Acceptance of Gandhi's suggestion for enquiry into police excesses
4. Release of only those prisoners who were not charged with violence

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 1, 2 and 4 only
- (c) 3 only
- (d) 2, 3 and 4 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 1, 2 and 4 only** · `Modern India` `Civil Disobedience Movement` `Round Table Conferences` — cross-link: [Spectrum file 12](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/12_Topup_Tables.md), Part C, on the RTCs. Gandhi's demand for a police-excesses inquiry was one of the points the Pact explicitly **rejected**, making statement 3 false.

</details>

### 8. Decline of Indigo Cultivation
Indigo cultivation in India declined by the beginning of the 20th century because of —

- (a) Peasant resistance to the oppressive conduct of planters
- (b) Its unprofitability in the world market because of new inventions
- (c) National leaders' opposition to the cultivation of indigo
- (d) Government control over the planters

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Its unprofitability in the world market because of new inventions** · `Modern India` `Agrarian history` — the invention of **synthetic indigo dye** (Germany, 1890s-1900s) is the standard explanation. Cross-link: [Spectrum file 07](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/07_Peasant_and_Labour_Movements.md), on the Indigo Revolt's earlier phase.

</details>

### 9. The Rakhmabai Case
In the context of Indian history, the Rakhmabai case of 1884 revolved around —

1. Women's right to gain education
2. Age of consent
3. Restitution of conjugal rights

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 and 3 only** · `Modern India` `Social reform` `Women's legal history` — the case, over Rakhmabai's refusal to live with a husband married to her in childhood, fed directly into the **Age of Consent Act, 1891**.

</details>

### 17. Scholars and Their Royal Associations
With reference to the scholars/litterateurs of ancient India, consider the following statements:

1. Panini is associated with Pushyamitra Shunga.
2. Amarasimha is associated with Harshavardhana.
3. Kalidasa is associated with Chandragupta-II.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 3 only** · `Ancient India` `Classical scholars` — Panini predates Pushyamitra Shunga by centuries (pre-Mauryan grammarian); Amarasimha is conventionally placed under the **Guptas** broadly, not specifically Harshavardhana. Only the Kalidasa–Chandragupta II association is secure.

</details>

### 18. Buddhist Sects
With reference to the religious history of India, consider the following statements:

1. Sthaviravadins belong to Mahayana Buddhism.
2. The Lokottaravadin sect was an offshoot of the Mahasanghika sect of Buddhism.
3. The deification of Buddha by the Mahasanghikas fostered Mahayana Buddhism.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 and 3 only** · `Ancient India` `Buddhist schools` — the Sthaviravadins belong to **Hinayana/Theravada** Buddhism, not Mahayana, making statement 1 false.

</details>
