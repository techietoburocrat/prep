## 📌 Sourcing & fidelity

- **2023** and later years came from sources that render the raw paper inline, with original serial numbers preserved where confirmed (2023, and 14 of 21 questions in 2018).
- **Most years (2013–2022, 2025–2026)** came from a topic-tagged compilation (`superkalam.com`, sorted into Ancient/Medieval/Modern/Art & Culture per year) that reproduces full question text and options faithfully but does **not** preserve original paper serial numbers — those files are numbered in compiled order instead, and say so up top.
- ⚠️ **Two answer-key errors were caught and corrected during compilation** — the source's own reported "correct answer" was cross-checked against independent sources and found wrong on two questions:
  - **2015 Q13 / 2014 Q1** (the repeated "kingdoms associated with the Buddha's life" question) — source said Avanti+Kosala+Magadha; the official UPSC key is **Kosala+Magadha only**.
  - **2020 Q3** (Bhilsa/Dwarasamudra/Girinagar/Sthanesvara place-name pairs) — source said "1 and 4 only"; the official key is **"1 and 3 only."**
  
  Both are corrected in place with a note explaining the fix, rather than either silently trusting the first source or silently overriding it without explanation. **This is a real caution about the underlying source** — treat any single-source answer key as provisional, especially on older or more obscure questions, the same discipline Polity's PYQ folder already established for its own source.
- **One question was withdrawn by UPSC itself** (2024, the Ryotwari/Permanent Settlement question) — flagged in place, not silently dropped from the file.

## 🏷️ What comes up most (across the 239 compiled questions)

| Theme | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Cross-swapped pair distractors** | The single most common trap format — two genuinely correct facts (a king and his real dynasty, a river and its real ancient name) swapped between each other in the options, rather than one side being invented. Seen in nearly every year: 2018's Sanskrit College founder, 2020/2026's Vitasta/Asikni rivers, 2022's Chandela/Paramara kings. |
| **"Recently in the news" pegs into old facts** | Somnath Temple's Circuit House (2022), the Ramanuja statue (2022), the film *Boong*'s BAFTA win (2026). A current-affairs hook is bait into a static fact — read past the peg. |
| **Repeated questions across years** | The "kingdoms associated with the Buddha's life" question ran in both 2014 and 2015 with slightly different option sets. Worth knowing UPSC does recycle question *content*, if not exact wording. |
| **GoI Act 1919/1935 mechanics** | Reserved vs Transferred subjects (2017, 2022), residuary powers (2018), federal structure (2024) — tested from several angles across years. Cross-link: [Spectrum file 08](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/08_From_Simon_to_Cabinet_Mission.md). |
| **Peasant/labour movement specifics** | Tebhaga (2013), Eka Movement vs Bardoli (2026), Factories Act/Lokhande (2017), Trade Disputes Act (2017) — cross-link: [Spectrum file 07](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/07_Peasant_and_Labour_Movements.md). |
| **Revolutionary-era organisation identification** | Ghadar vs Bengal revolutionaries (2022), Ghadar's own founding (2014) — cross-link: [Spectrum file 06](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/06_Revolutionaries_and_Swarajists.md). |
| **UNESCO recognitions as a standing sub-theme** | World Heritage inscriptions (2024, 2026's Moidams), Intangible Cultural Heritage (2024's Garba) — a reliably tested current-affairs/history crossover every year now. |

## 📖 How to use this set

1. **Attempt before revealing.** Answers are inside `<details>` blocks — they stay collapsed until you click.
2. **Practise the 2023-onward formats separately.** The "How many of the above statements are correct?" counting format and the Statement-I/Statement-II format both defeat the old elimination tricks — drill these on 2023–2026 specifically.
3. **Pair with the Spectrum notes.** Every question here that has a direct Spectrum cross-link is tagged inline — use it to jump straight to the fuller explanation.
4. **Don't over-weight "recently in the news" framing** when it appears — it's a hook into an old fact, not a signal that the fact itself is new.
5. **Treat any single answer key with mild suspicion on obscure questions.** Two were wrong in this very set before correction — if an answer surprises you and the topic is obscure, it's worth a second check rather than assuming the file is infallible.
