### 1. Fa-hien's Visit
Fa-hien (Faxian), the Chinese pilgrim, travelled to India during the reign of —

- (a) Samudragupta
- (b) Chandragupta II
- (c) Kumaragupta I
- (d) Skandagupta

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Chandragupta II** · `Ancient India` `Gupta period` `Foreign travellers`

</details>

### 2. Mahajanapadas and Their Rivers
With reference to ancient India (600–322 BC), consider the following pairs — territorial region and the river flowing in the region:

1. Asmaka — Godavari
2. Kamboja — Vipas
3. Avanti — Mahanadi
4. Kosala — Sarayu

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All the four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Ancient India` `Mahajanapadas` — Asmaka/Godavari and Kosala/Sarayu are correct; Kamboja is associated with the **Kabul/Kunar river region**, not the Vipas (Beas); Avanti (around Ujjain) is associated with the **Kshipra river**, not the Mahanadi.

</details>

### 3. Ashokan Administrative Officers

- (a) Village-level administration
- (b) District-level administration
- (c) Provincial administration
- (d) Level of the central administration

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) District-level administration** · `Ancient India` `Mauryan administration`

</details>

### 4. Campaign Against Srivijaya
Who among the following led a successful military campaign against the kingdom of Srivijaya?

- (a) Amoghavarsha (Rashtrakuta)
- (b) Prataparudra (Kakatiya)
- (c) Rajendra I (Chola)
- (d) Vishnuvardhana (Hoysala)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Rajendra I (Chola)** · `Medieval India` `Chola naval expeditions` — the famous overseas campaign against the Srivijaya (Sumatra/Malay) empire, marking Indian naval power's high point.

</details>

### 5. "Araghatta" — the Mechanism Itself
The irrigation device called 'Araghatta' was —

- (a) A water bag made of leather, pulled over a pulley
- (b) A large wheel with earthen pots tied to the outer ends of its spokes
- (c) A large earthen pot driven by bullocks
- (d) A large water bucket pulled up by rope directly by hand

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) A large wheel with earthen pots tied to the outer ends of its spokes** · `Medieval India` `Irrigation technology` — the *persian-wheel*-type mechanism. Compare with [2016.md](2016.md) Q4, which tested only the term's identification, not its physical description.

</details>

### 6. A Pallava King's Titles
Who among the following rulers in ancient/early medieval India had assumed the titles 'Mattavilasa', 'Vichitrachitta' and 'Gunabharata'?

- (a) Mahendravarman I
- (b) Simhavishnu
- (c) Narasimhavarman I
- (d) Simhavarman

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) Mahendravarman I** · `Early Medieval India` `Pallava dynasty` — a 7th-century Pallava king, also the author of the Sanskrit satirical play *Mattavilasa Prahasana*.

</details>

### 7. Congress's Stated Non-Cooperation Objective
Consider the following statements in respect of the Non-Cooperation Movement:

1. The Congress declared the attainment of 'Swaraj' by all legitimate and peaceful means to be its objective.
2. It was to be implemented in stages, with civil disobedience and non-payment of taxes as the next stage only if 'Swaraj' did not come within a year and the government resorted to repression.

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Both 1 and 2** · `Modern India` `Non-Cooperation Movement`

</details>

### 8. The Non-Cooperation Programme's Components
Consider the following subjects with regard to the Non-Cooperation Programme:

1. Boycott of law-courts and foreign cloth
2. Observance of strict non-violence
3. Retention of titles and honours without using them in public
4. Establishment of Panchayats for settling disputes

How many of the above were parts of the Non-Cooperation Programme?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All the four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) Only three** · `Modern India` `Non-Cooperation Movement` — the actual call was to **surrender/renounce titles**, not merely to retain them unused, making item 3 false as stated.

</details>
