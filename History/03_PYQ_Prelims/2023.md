### 1. Dhanyakataka
In which one of the following regions was Dhanyakataka, which flourished as a prominent Buddhist centre under the Mahasanghikas, located?

- (a) Andhra
- (b) Gandhara
- (c) Kalinga
- (d) Magadha

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) Andhra** · `Ancient India` `Buddhism` `Regional centres`

</details>

### 2. The Stupa
With reference to ancient India, consider the following statements:

1. The concept of Stupa is Buddhist in origin.
2. Stupa was generally a repository of relics.
3. Stupa was a votive and commemorative structure in Buddhist tradition.

How many of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) All three
- (d) None

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Ancient India` `Buddhist architecture` — the Stupa tradition predates Buddhism (it has pre-Buddhist funerary roots), so statement 1 is the false one; 2 and 3 are correct.

</details>

### 4. Vattakirutal

### 5. Dynasties of the Eighth Century
Consider the following dynasties:

1. Hoysala
2. Gahadavala
3. Kakatiya
4. Yadava

How many of the above dynasties established their kingdoms in the early eighth century AD?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) None

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) None** · `Medieval India` `Regional kingdoms` — all four (Hoysala, Gahadavala, Kakatiya, Yadava) rose between the 10th and 12th centuries, not the 8th.

</details>

### 6. Literary Work and Author
With reference to ancient Indian History, consider the following pairs:

| Literary work | Author |
|---|---|
| 1. Devi Chandragupta | Bilhana |
| 2. Hammira-Mahakavya | Nayachandra Suri |
| 3. Melinda-panha | Nagarjuna |
| 4. Nitivakyamrita | Somadeva Suri |

How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) Only three
- (d) All four

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Ancient India` `Medieval India` `Literature` — *Devi Chandragupta* is by **Vishakhadatta**, not Bilhana; *Melinda-panha* records a dialogue between King Menander and monk **Nagasena**, not authored by Nagarjuna. **Hammira-Mahakavya/Nayachandra Suri** and **Nitivakyamrita/Somadeva Suri** are the two correct pairs.

</details>

### 7. Animism
'Souls are not only the property of animal and plant life, but also of rocks, running water and many other natural objects not looked on as living by other religious sects.' The above statement reflects one of the core beliefs of which one of the following religious sects of ancient India?

- (a) Buddhism
- (b) Jainism
- (c) Shaivism
- (d) Vaishnavism

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Jainism** · `Ancient India` `Philosophy` `Animism`

</details>

### 62. Cave Shrines
Consider the following pairs:

| Site | Well known for |
|---|---|
| 1. Besnagar | Shaivite cave shrine |
| 2. Bhaja | Buddhist cave shrine |
| 3. Sittanavasal | Jain cave shrine |

How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

- (a) Only one
- (b) Only two
- (c) All three
- (d) None

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) Only two** · `Art & Culture` `Cave architecture` — Besnagar is famous for the **Heliodorus pillar** (a Vaishnavite dedication), not a Shaivite cave shrine; Bhaja (Buddhist) and Sittanavasal (Jain) are correctly matched.

</details>

### 63. National Handloom Day
Consider the following statements:

**Statement-I:** 7th August is declared as the National Handloom Day.

**Statement-II:** It was in 1905 that the Swadeshi Movement was launched on the same day.

Which one of the following is correct in respect of the above statements?

- (a) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (b) Both Statement-I and Statement-II are correct and Statement-II is not the correct explanation for Statement-I
- (c) Statement-I is correct but Statement-II is incorrect
- (d) Statement-I is incorrect but Statement-II is correct

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(a) Both correct, II explains I** · `Modern India` `Swadeshi Movement` — cross-link: [Spectrum file 10](../02_Standard_Reference_Books/Modern_India_Spectrum/Notes/10_Early_Nationalism_Topups.md) covers the 1905 Partition/Swadeshi launch in full.

</details>
