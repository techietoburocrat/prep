### 1. Land-Revenue and Institutional Terms
In the context of the history of India, consider the following pairs:

| Term | Description |
|---|---|
| 1. Eripatti | Land revenue from which was set apart for the maintenance of the village tank |
| 2. Taniyurs | Villages donated to a single Brahmin or a group of Brahmins |
| 3. Ghatikas | Colleges generally attached to temples |

Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 3 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1 and 3 only

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) 1 and 3 only** · `Ancient India` `Chola-era institutions` — Taniyurs were actually **large, independent revenue villages**, not Brahmin-donation villages (that description fits *brahmadeya/agrahara*).

</details>

### 11. The Bodhisattva Concept
With reference to the religious history of India, consider the following statements:

1. The concept of Bodhisattva is central to the Hinayana sect of Buddhism.
2. A Bodhisattva is a compassionate one on his way to enlightenment.
3. A Bodhisattva delays achieving his own salvation to help all sentient beings on their path to it.

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 2 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 2 and 3 only** · `Art & Culture` `Buddhism` — the Bodhisattva concept is central to **Mahayana**, not Hinayana, Buddhism.

</details>

### 15. Ajanta and Mahabalipuram
What is/are common to the two historical places known as Ajanta and Mahabalipuram?

1. Both were built in the same period.
2. Both belong to the same religious denomination.
3. Both have rock-cut monuments.

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) None of the statements given above is correct

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(b) 3 only** · `Art & Culture` `Rock-cut architecture` — Ajanta (Buddhist, largely Gupta-era) and Mahabalipuram (Hindu, Pallava-era) differ in **both period and religious denomination**; they share only the rock-cut technique.

</details>

### 16. Famous Places and Their Regions
Consider the following pairs — famous place and region:

| Place | Region |
|---|---|
| 1. Bodhgaya | Baghelkhand |
| 2. Khajuraho | Bundelkhand |
| 3. Shirdi | Vidarbha |
| 4. Nasik | Malwa |
| 5. Tirupati | Rayalaseema |

- (a) 1, 2 and 4
- (b) 2, 3, 4 and 5
- (c) 2 and 5 only
- (d) 1, 3, 4 and 5

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(c) 2 and 5 only** · `Art & Culture` `Historical geography` — Bodhgaya is in **Magadh** (Bihar), not Baghelkhand; Shirdi is in **Ahmednagar district (Marathwada-adjacent western Maharashtra)**, not Vidarbha; Nasik is in **Khandesh/North Maharashtra**, not Malwa.

</details>

### 17. "Maagadha"
With reference to the cultural history of India, the memorising of chronicles, dynastic histories and epic tales was the profession of which of the following?

- (a) Shramana
- (b) Parivraaj
- (c) Agrahaarika
- (d) Maagadha

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(d) Maagadha** · `Art & Culture` `Oral tradition` — a hereditary bard/genealogist-narrator caste.

</details>
