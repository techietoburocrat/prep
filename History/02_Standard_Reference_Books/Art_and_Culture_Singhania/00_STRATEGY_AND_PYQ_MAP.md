> 🔴🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE TWO FACTS THAT SHOULD DRIVE YOUR DECISION:**
>
> **(1) THE 2022–2025 LULL IS OVER.** Art & Culture fell to **1, 1, 2, 2** questions in 2022–2025 — four lean years that made it look skippable. ⭐⭐⭐ **Then 2026 delivered NINE.** *Anyone who de-prioritised on the four-year trend walked into an 18-mark paper.*
>
> **(2) 2020 IS THE TRAP IN THE OTHER DIRECTION.** ⭐⭐ **Prelims 2020 had ZERO Art & Culture questions — and Mains 2020 had FOUR, worth 50 marks.** *The subject does not disappear; it moves between the two papers.* **You cannot drop it on one paper's evidence.**

### Why 8 marks matters more than 8 marks

⭐⭐⭐ **Prelims is a hard gate, not a score.** With General Studies cutoffs typically in the **85–100** band, **8 marks is roughly 9% of the cutoff** — and in a bad year for you elsewhere, it is the difference. ⭐ **It is also the most *closed* content in the paper: unlike Economy or Environment, the syllabus does not grow. A dance form learnt once stays learnt.**
