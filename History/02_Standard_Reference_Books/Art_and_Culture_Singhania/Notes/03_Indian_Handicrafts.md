# 📚 PART A — TEXTILES

## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The four resist-dye techniques — *learn these apart*

| Technique | ⭐ How it works | Where |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Bandhani / Bandhej** | ⭐ **"Tie and dye"** — a series of **knots** tied so the tied portions **do not take the colour** | **Rajasthan, Gujarat**; also AP, TN |
| ⭐⭐ **Laharia** | ⭐⭐ **A special tie-and-dye giving RIPPLE or WAVE-like patterns** | ⭐ **Jaipur and Jodhpur** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Ikat** | ⭐⭐ **Resist dyeing applied to the YARN, repeatedly, BEFORE the cloth is woven** *(the key discriminator)* | ⭐ **Telangana, Odisha, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh** |
| ⭐⭐ **Batik** | ⭐ **One end permeated with MOLTEN WAX, then dyed COLD** | ⭐ **Madhya Pradesh and West Bengal** |

⭐ **Kalamkari** — hand painting on fabric with **vegetable dyes** *(→ [Ch.2 §14](02_Indian_Paintings.md))*, **Andhra Pradesh**.
⭐ **Tanchoi** — ⭐⭐ **derived from CHINESE inspiration**, reaching **Surat** via trading communities; *"resembles a fine miniature painting."*
⭐ **Jamdani** — **West Bengal**; ⭐⭐ **finest muslin with OPAQUE patterns on a TRANSPARENT background.**

# 📚 PART B — MATERIALS AND TECHNIQUES

## 4️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ Glassware

⭐ **First reference in the *Mahabharata*.** ⭐⭐ **No glass beads in the EARLY Harappan; the first material evidence is from the PAINTED GREY WARE culture of the Ganges valley (c. 1000 BC).** In the **Satapatha Brahmana** glass is called ***kanch / kaca***.

⭐ **A glass industry at BRAHMAPURI and KOLHAPUR (Maharashtra), 2 BC–2 AD**, producing ⭐ **lenticular beads**; ⭐⭐ **the Sanskrit *Vyasayogacharita* refers to SPECTACLES**, suggesting a foray into optical lenses. Other sites: **Maski** *(Chalcolithic, Deccan)*, **Ahar**, **Hastinapur**, **Ahichchhatra**, **Eran**, **Ujjain**.

⭐ **Mughals**: glass in the **Sheesh Mahal**; **glass hukkas**, ***ittardans*** *(perfume boxes)*, engraved glass.

| Modern centre | Product |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Hyderabad** | ⭐ **Glass bangles — `Churikajodas`** |
| ⭐ **Firozabad** | **Chandeliers and decorative pieces** |
| **Saharanpur** | ⭐ **`Panchkora` — glass toys** |
| ⭐ **Patna, Bihar** | ⭐⭐ **`TIKULI` — decorative glass beads**, nearly lost to industrialisation; ⭐ **still worn by the SANTHAL tribes** |

| Pottery | Place | ⭐ Feature |
|---|---|---|

| **Pokhran** | **Rajasthan** | Household items |

⭐ **Ancient sequence for reference**: **Mehrgarh (6000 BC)** the earliest evidence · **PGW (1500–600 BC, Vedic)** · **Red-and-Black (1500–300 BC)** · **NBPW in two phases (700–400 BC, 400–100 BC)**, partly coinciding with the Mauryas · ⭐⭐ **ROULETTE POTTERY (200–100 BCE) from ARIKAMEDU near Puducherry** — *a Roman-trade marker worth knowing*.

⭐ **Terracotta**: ⭐⭐ **the BANKURA HORSE from Panchmura, Bankura district, West Bengal**, plus the **terracotta temples** there; and the **Pala-period Buddhist viharas**.

## 6️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Metalwork — the named techniques

| Technique | Where | ⭐ What it is |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Bidri** | ⭐ **Bidar, Karnataka** | ⭐⭐ **SILVER inlay against a DARK (blackened alloy) background** — a stark contrast |
| ⭐⭐ **Koftagiri** *(damascening)* | **Jaipur, Alwar** | ⭐ **Inlaying a LIGHT metal on a DARK one** |
| ⭐⭐ **Tarkashi** | **Rajasthan** | ⭐ **Fine COPPER or BRASS WIRE set into chiselled grooves** |
| ⭐⭐ **Marori** | **Rajasthan** | ⭐ **Etching the base metal and FILLING THE GAPS WITH RESIN** |
| ⭐⭐ **Dokra** | ⭐ **Odisha & West Bengal** | ⭐⭐ **Lost-wax BRASS casting for ornaments** *(→ the technique in [Ch.1 §1](01_Architecture_Sculpture_and_Pottery.md))* |
| ⭐ **Pembarthi** | ⭐ **Warangal, Telangana** | ⭐ **Sheet-metal BRASS art for chariots and temples** |
| **Repoussé / embossing** | — | Raised design **in relief** |
| ⭐ **Baarik Kaam / Nakashi** | ⭐ **Moradabad** | **Delicate work / engraving style** |
| **Badla** | **Marwar, Rajasthan** | ⭐ **Rounded ZINC pots** |
| **Pahaldar lamps** | **Jaipur, UP** | Copper and brass lamps |

⭐ **Bronze**: earliest literary evidence of **casting methods in the *Matsya Purana***; ⭐ **Nagarjuna's *Rasa Ratnakara*** on **metal purity and the distillation of zinc**. Centres: ⭐ **UP (Etawah, Sitapur, Varanasi, Moradabad)** — ritual objects **tamrapatra, kanchantal, panchpatra**; and **Tamil Nadu** for Pallava/Chola/Pandya/Nayaka-style statuary.

⭐ **Silver**: ⭐ **Filigree** work; **Odisha** anklets — ***painri*** and ***paijam***, and knitted ***gunchi***.

## 7️⃣ ⭐⭐ Ivory, leather, wood, stone, toys

| Craft | ⭐ Key facts |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Ivory** | Called ***danta*** in the Vedic period. ⭐⭐ **Harappan ivory objects (incl. DICE) were EXPORTED to Turkmenistan, Afghanistan and the Persian Gulf**; ⭐ **a 2 BCE Sanchi inscription names the GUILD OF IVORY WORKERS from VIDISA** who carved there; an **ivory comb from Taxila**. Centres: **Delhi, Jaipur, WB** — the famous ***Ambari Hathi***. ⭐ **Kerala: painting on ivory · Jodhpur: ivory bangles · Jaipur: ivory jali work** |
| ⭐ **Leather** | Tanning from **3000 BCE**; ⭐ **camel** the most popular skin. ⭐⭐ **Mojadis — Jaipur and Jodhpur** · **Kolhapuri chappals — Maharashtra** · **Punjabi juttis** *(applique)* · **Kanpur** the industrial centre · ⭐ **`MANOTI` art of BIKANER — decorating articles with CAMEL SKIN** |
| ⭐ **Wood** | ⭐ **Kashmir** — walnut and deodar since the 13th c.; ⭐⭐ **lattice work: `acche-dar`, `khatamband`, `azlipinjra`**. **Himachal** *(Brahmour, Chatrahi)* — **naghbel, kutheriphool, jali, dori**. ⭐ **Karnataka sandalwood · AP raktachandan (red sandalwood) · Kerala teak (Tharavad houses) · Nagaland `kumisyng` (huge LOG DRUMS) · Rajasthan `rohilda` · Ratnagiri hale/pangora (LACQUERED IMITATION FRUITS)** |
| ⭐ **Stone** | ⭐ **Rajasthan's `SANG-E-MARMAR`** — white **Makrana** marble; ⭐ **Jhansi's `SANG-E-RATHAK`** — dark brown stone; **Masrur** monolithic rock-cut temple, **Kangra** |
| ⭐⭐ **Toys** | ⭐ **Toy carts from Harappan sites** — Alamgirpur, Ambkheri, Kalibangan, Lothal; early historic at Atranjikhera, Nagarjunakonda, Ujjain. ⭐⭐ **Tirupati dolls — RED SANDALWOOD (AP)** · ⭐ **Kondapalli (AP) — soft `PONKI` wood, the *Ambari Hathi*** · ⭐ **Mysore and Channapatna — LACQUERED wood** · **Assam — dolls of PITH (Indian cork)** · **Rajasthan — cloth dolls** |

# 📚 PART C — FLOOR DESIGNS ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⭐⭐ **The shared grammar: drawn FREEHAND, beginning at the CENTRE with a DOT and expanding in concentric geometric patterns.** ⭐ **Materials are natural, non-staining and easily erased — they are NOT meant to be permanent.** White comes from **chalk, lime (chuna), powdered marble, or rice powder with lime**.

| Design | State | ⭐ Identifier |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Chowkpurna** | **Punjab & UP** | ⭐⭐ ***Chowk*** *(square)* from ***chowki*** — **the SEAT OF LAKSHMI** |
| ⭐ **Aipan** | **Himachal Pradesh** ⚠️ | Squares, circles, triangles |
| ⭐⭐ **Mandana** | **Rajasthan & MP** | ⭐ From ***mandan*** *(decoration)*; ⭐⭐ **ground cleaned with COW DUNG, finished in CRIMSON RED from *reti* (red earth)** |
| ⭐ **Santhia** | **Gujarat** | ⭐ **Decorates the ENTRANCE of houses** |
| ⭐⭐ **Rangoli** | **Maharashtra** | **Lotus, swastika** motifs |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Kolam** | **South India** | ⭐⭐ **Drawn by LINKING AN ARRAY OF DOTS**; thin lines of **rice powder or crushed stone on WET ground**, outlined with **red geru**. ⭐ **Called `HASE` in Karnataka, `MUGGULU` in AP, `GOLAM` in Kerala.** ⭐⭐ **`Mandapa Kolams` are large designs for MARRIAGE HALLS (wet rice paste); a `Graha Kolam` sanctifies the home shrine each morning** |
| ⭐ **Jhonti** | **Odisha** | Conch shells, fish, serpents, flowers |
| ⭐ **Aripana** | **WB & Assam** ⚠️ | ⭐ **Chalk powder filled with coloured powder or rice paste — *alta*/sindoor for red, turmeric for yellow**; ⭐⭐ **customary to place a FLOWER before each design** |

> ⚠️ **[VERIFY] — two placements to treat with caution.** *Singhania puts **Aipan** in **Himachal Pradesh** and **Aripana** in **West Bengal/Assam**. ⭐⭐ **Aipan is much more commonly associated with UTTARAKHAND (Kumaon)**, and ***Aripana*** **with BIHAR (Mithila)**. *The names are cognates and sources genuinely differ — **if a question offers Uttarakhand for Aipan, take it**.*

## 🔁 Recap — in six lines

1. **Four resist techniques**: **Bandhani** *(tie the cloth)*, **Laharia** *(waves)*, **Ikat** *(dye the YARN first)*, **Batik** *(molten wax, cold dye)*.
2. **Sarees to fix cold**: **Pochampalli** *(ikat, Air India)*, **Patola** *(Patan)*, **Baluchari** *(stories on the pallu)*, **Chanderi** *(see-through)*, **Nauvari** *(nine yards)*, **Kasavu** *(gold border)*, **Paithani** *(parrot)*, **Konrad** *(temple saree)*.
3. **Embroideries to fix cold**: **Chikankari** *(Lucknow, white)*, **Kantha** *(Bengal, old cloth)*, **Phulkari/Bagh** *(Punjab)*, **Kashida** *(Kashmir, no figures)*, **Kasuti** *(Karnataka, identical both sides)*, **Pipli** *(Odisha applique)*, **Chamba Rumal** *(HP, Pahari painting)*.
4. **Metalwork**: **Bidri** *(silver on dark, Bidar)*, **Koftagiri**, **Tarkashi**, **Marori**, **Dokra** *(lost-wax brass)*, **Pembarthi** *(Warangal)*.
5. **Floor designs**: **Chowkpurna** *(Punjab-UP)*, **Mandana** *(Rajasthan-MP)*, **Rangoli** *(Maharashtra)*, **Kolam** *(South, dot-linked; Hase/Muggulu/Golam)*, **Jhonti** *(Odisha)*, **Santhia** *(Gujarat)*.
6. **Crafts double as TRADE EVIDENCE** — Harappan ivory in the Gulf, Roulette pottery at Arikamedu, Tanchoi from China.
