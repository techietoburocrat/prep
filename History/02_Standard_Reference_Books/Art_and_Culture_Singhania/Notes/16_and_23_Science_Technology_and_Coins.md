# 📚 PART A — SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ Mathematics — *`Ganita`* and its five branches

| Branch | ⭐ Sanskrit name |
|---|---|
| **Arithmetic** | **Pattin Ganita / Anka Ganita** |
| ⭐⭐ **Algebra** | ⭐ **`BIJA GANITA`** — literally **"the OTHER mathematics"** *(*bija* = another/second)*, ⭐ **implying it was recognised as a PARALLEL system to the conventional one** |
| **Geometry** | **Rekha Ganita** |
| **Astronomy** | ⭐ **Khagola Shastra** |
| **Astrology** | **Jyotisha** |

| Text / figure | Date | ⭐ Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Baudhayana — *Sulvasutra*** | **6th c. BC** | ⭐⭐ **THE EARLIEST book on mathematics.** ⭐ **Mentions `Pi` and concepts very similar to the PYTHAGORAS THEOREM** |
| ⭐ **Apastamba** | 2nd c. BC | ⭐ **Practical geometry — acute, obtuse and right angles**, used for **constructing fire altars** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Aryabhata — *Aryabhatiya*** | **c. 499 AD** | ⭐ **Four sections**: denoting big decimals by alphabets; number theory/geometry/trigonometry/*bijaganita*; astronomy. ⭐⭐ **Stated the earth is ROUND and ROTATES ON ITS OWN AXIS**; gave the **area of a triangle**; ⭐ **a value of Pi more accurate than the Greeks'**. ⭐⭐⭐ **Explained eclipses by SHADOWS — the earth's shadow on the moon, the moon's on the earth — displacing the orthodox theory that a demon swallowed the planet**. ⭐ **He studied at `KHAGOL`, the astronomical laboratory at NALANDA** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Brahmagupta — *Brahmasphuta Siddhanta*** | **7th c. AD** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The FIRST to treat ZERO as a NUMBER**; ⭐⭐ **introduced NEGATIVE numbers, describing them as DEBTS and positives as FORTUNES** |
| ⭐⭐ **Mahaviracharya — *Ganita Sara Sangraha*** | **9th c. AD** | ⭐⭐ **The first textbook of arithmetic in present-day form**; ⭐⭐⭐ **described the method of finding the LOWEST COMMON MULTIPLE — "an invention NOT by John Napier but by Mahaviracharya"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Bhaskaracharya — *Siddhanta Shiromani*** | **12th c. AD** | ⭐ **Four sections: `Lilavati` (arithmetic), `Beejaganita` (algebra), `Goladhyaya` (spheres), `Grahaganita` (planets)**. ⭐⭐ **Introduced the `CHAKRAVALA` (cyclic) method for algebraic equations**. ⭐ **James Taylor translated *Lilavati* in the 19th century** |
| **Narayana Pandit** | Medieval | *Ganitakaumudi*, *Bijaganitavatamsa* |
| ⭐ **Nilakantha Somayaji** | — | ***Tantrasamgraha*** — **rules of trigonometric functions** |

> ⭐⭐ **The `SHUNYA` point, worth quoting**: *"Zero or *shunya* is derived from the concept of a VOID. The concept of void existed in Hindu philosophy, hence the derivation of a symbol for it"* — and ⭐ **it influenced South-East Asia through the Buddhist concept of NIRVANA, "attaining salvation by merging into the void of eternity."**

> ⭐⭐⭐ **A superb one-line fact for any answer: THE ARABS CALLED MATHEMATICS `HINDISAT` — "the Indian art."**

⭐ **Medieval patronage**: **Faizi translated *Lilavati* AND Bhaskara's *Beejaganita* into Persian** at Akbar's court, and ⭐ **Akbar ordered mathematics made a subject of study**. ⭐⭐ **Observatories: Firuz Shah Tughlaq at Delhi; Firuz Shah Bahmani at Daulatabad — whose court astronomer `MAHENDRA SURI` invented the `YANTARAJA`**; and ⭐⭐ **Sawai Jai Singh built FIVE observatories — Delhi, Jaipur, Varanasi, Ujjain, Mathura** *(→ Jantar Mantar, [UNESCO §4](04_and_10_UNESCO_Heritage_Lists.md))*.

## 2️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ Medicine, chemistry, shipbuilding

| Field | ⭐ Key content |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Medicine** | ⭐ **Vedic period: the `ASHWINI KUMARAS` were the divine physicians; `DHANVANTARI` the god of medicine.** ⭐⭐ **The `ATHARVA VEDA` is the first book mentioning diseases, cures and medicines** — attributing disease to **demons and spirits entering the body**, curable by **charms and spells**; it names cures for **diarrhoea, sores, cough, leprosy, fever and seizure**. ⭐⭐ **Rational medicine emerges ~600 BC.** ⭐ **Centres of medical learning: TAKSHASHILA and VARANASI.** ⭐⭐ **The *Sushruta Samhita* covers SURGERY — including RHINOPLASTY and OPHTHALMOLOGY** |
| ⭐⭐ **Chemistry / alchemy** | ⭐ **`NAGARJUNA` was the alchemist**. ⭐⭐ **Applied fields: METALLURGY, extraction of SUGAR, production of PAPER and distillation of PERFUMES — all four.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***RASARNAVA*** **is the text named in a 2012 Mains question on the history of Indian chemistry** |
| ⭐⭐ **Shipbuilding** | ⭐⭐ **The text is the *`YUKTI KALPA TARU`***. ⭐ **Warfare vessels: `Sarvamandira` and `Madhyamandira`.** ⭐⭐ **Ship vocabulary — a clean matching set: anchor = `nava bandhan kilaha` · sail = `vata vastra` · rudder = `jeni pata`/`karna` · keel = `nava tala` · ⭐⭐ compass = `MACHAYANTRA`, "the fish machine," shaped like a fish** |

## 3️⃣ ⭐⭐ Two sports with a claim

- ⭐⭐⭐ **`KALARIPAYATTU`** — the Kerala martial art, ⭐ **said to have been transmitted to CHINA in the 5th century BC by the sage `BODHIDHARMA`**, and ⭐⭐ **claimed as the origin of modern JUDO and KARATE.** ⚠️ *(Treat the transmission claim as traditional rather than established — but it is what the book, and the examiner, expect.)*
- ⭐⭐ **Chess = `CHATURANGA`** — **"four bodies"**; played with counters and ***aksha*** *(dice)*; ⭐ **also called `ASTAPADA`, "the game of eight steps"**; ⭐ **mentioned in the *Mahabharata*, played between Kauravas and Pandavas**.

# 📚 PART B — COINS ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⭐ ***Coin*** from the Latin ***cuneus***; the study is ⭐ **`NUMISMATICS`**. ⭐⭐ **First recorded use: China and Greece ~700 BC; India in the 6th century BC.** ⭐⭐ **The earliest reference to coins in India is in the VEDAS — `NISHKA` was the term for metal coins.**

## 4️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The dynastic sequence — **the table that answers the question**

| Issuer | Period | ⭐ Metal | ⭐⭐ The identifying feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Mahajanapadas** *(punch-marked)* | **6th–2nd c. BC** | **Silver** | ⭐ **Called `PURANAS`, `KARSHAPANAS` or `PANA`**; **irregular shape, standard weight**; ⭐⭐ **each unit a `RATTI` = 0.11 g**; ⭐ **die-struck on ONE side only**. ⭐⭐ **Regional marks: Saurashtra a HUMPED BULL · Dakshin Panchala a SWASTIKA · Magadha generally FIVE SYMBOLS** *(Magadhan coins became the most circulated in South Asia)*. ⭐ **Mentioned in the Manusmriti and the Jatakas**; ⭐ **Panini's *Ashtadhyayi* describes the stamping** |
| ⭐⭐ **Mauryas** | 322–185 BC | Silver, gold, copper, lead | ⭐⭐ **Chanakya's *Arthashastra* names `rupyarupa` (silver), `suvarnarupa` (gold), `tamrarupa` (copper), `sisarupa` (lead)**; ⭐ **the SUN and the SIX-ARMED WHEEL were the most consistent symbols**; **~50–54 grains of silver, 32 rattis** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Indo-Greeks** | 180 BC – 10 AD | Silver, copper, nickel, lead | ⭐⭐⭐ **INTRODUCED SHOWING THE BUST/HEAD OF THE RULER.** ⭐⭐ **BILINGUAL — GREEK on the obverse, PALI in KHAROSTHI script on the reverse.** ⭐ **Greek deities Zeus, Hercules, Apollo, Pallas Athene**, later Indian deities too. ⭐⭐ **Carried the issuing monarch's name AND THE YEAR OF ISSUE — which is why they matter to historians** |
| ⭐⭐ **Kushanas** | — | Gold, copper | ⭐ **Helmeted BUST of the king on one side, his FAVOURITE DEITY on the reverse**; ⭐⭐ **Kanishka's coins employed ONLY GREEK characters** |
| ⭐⭐ **Satavahanas** | 232 BC – 227 AD | ⭐⭐ **LEAD** *(silver rare)*; also ⭐ **`POTIN`, a silver-copper alloy** | ⭐ **"Devoid of any beauty or artistic merit"** but a **valuable source for dynastic history.** ⭐⭐ **Elephant, horse, lion or chaitya on one side; the `UJJAIN SYMBOL` — a CROSS WITH FOUR CIRCLES at the ends — on the other.** Dialect: **Prakrit** |
| ⭐⭐ **Western Satraps** *(Indo-Scythians)* | 35–405 AD | — | ⭐ **Of SAKA origin**, in Malwa/Gujarat/Kathiawar. ⭐⭐⭐ **THEY BEAR DATES IN THE SAKA ERA (from 78 AD) — which is why they are "of great historical importance."** ⭐ **King's head on one side; a Buddhist CHAITYA/STUPA on the other, borrowed from the Satavahanas** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Guptas** | 319–550 AD | ⭐ **Mainly GOLD**; silver and copper too | ⭐⭐ **Silver issued only AFTER Chandragupta II overthrew the Western Satraps.** ⭐⭐⭐ **INSCRIPTIONS IN SANSKRIT (Brahmi) FOR THE FIRST TIME IN THE HISTORY OF COINS.** ⭐ **Obverse: the king making oblations, PLAYING THE VEENA, performing *ashvamedha*, riding, or slaying a lion/tiger/rhinoceros.** ⭐ **Reverse: LAKSHMI on a throne or lotus — also Durga, Ganga, Garuda, Kartikeya.** ⭐⭐ **Gupta kings put their GIVEN name on the front and an assumed name ending in "-ADITYA" on the back** |
| ⭐⭐ **Vardhanas** | Late 6th–7th c. | Silver | ⭐⭐ **King's head on one side, a `PEACOCK` on the other.** ⭐ **Harsha's coins are dated in a new era beginning AD 606 — his coronation year** |
| ⭐ **Western Chalukyas** | 6th c. AD | — | ⭐ **The BOAR** *(Varaha)* as the common symbol |
| ⭐⭐ **Sher Shah Suri** | 16th c. | Silver | ⭐⭐⭐ **INTRODUCED THE `RUPEE`** — ***rupya***, **~11.34 g of silver**; one rupee = **four copper coins**; ⭐ **his copper `DAM` weighed 320–330 grains.** *India's currency is still called the rupee* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Mughals** | — | Gold, silver, copper | ⭐ **Gold `MOHUR` ~170–175 grains; ⭐⭐ Abul Fazl's *Ain-i-Akbari* says a mohur = NINE rupees.** Silver rupee and copper dam both **adopted from Sher Shah**. ⭐⭐⭐ **AKBAR issued ROUND AND SQUARE coins, and in 1579 the gold `ILAHI` coins to propagate `DIN-I-ILAHI` — inscribed "God is great, may his glory be glorified", worth 10 rupees.** ⭐ **`SAHANSAH` was the largest gold coin**; coins bore **Persian SOLAR month names**. ⭐⭐ **JAHANGIR put a COUPLET on his coins, added `NUR JAHAN`'s name to some, and issued the famous `ZODIAC` series** |
| ⭐ **Shivaji** | 17th c. | Gold, copper | ⭐ **Gold `huns` and copper `Shivarais`, titles in NAGARI script** |
| **Wodeyars / Haidar Ali / Tipu** | 1399–1947 | Gold | ⭐ **Kanthirava Narasa's coins bore the `NARASIMHA` avatar**; Haidar Ali continued **Shiva and Parvati** on the gold pagodas; ⭐ **Tipu used TWO ERAS on his coins** |

> ⭐⭐ **`COWRIE SHELLS`** — the other medium of exchange, *"used in large numbers by the ordinary masses for small-scale transactions"*, carrying **definite market value just as coins did**. ⭐ **A useful corrective: the coin record is a record of STATES, not of everyday exchange.**

> ⭐ **The post-Gupta note worth keeping**: after the **Hun invasion**, local kingdoms issued region-specific coins **"poor in both metallic content and artistic design"** — while ⭐⭐ **SOUTH INDIA moved towards a GOLD STANDARD inspired by ROMAN gold coins** arriving in the first three centuries CE *(→ Arikamedu, [Ch.3 §5](03_Indian_Handicrafts.md))*.

## 🔴 Prelims traps

| The plausible claim | The truth |
|---|---|
| *"The earliest treatise on mathematics is the Aryabhatiya."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The `SULVASUTRA` of Baudhayana (6th c. BC)** |
| *"Bijaganita means arithmetic."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALGEBRA** — *"the other mathematics"* |
| *"Aryabhata was the first to treat zero as a number."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BRAHMAGUPTA**, 7th c., in the *Brahmasphuta Siddhanta* |
| *"The LCM method was invented by John Napier."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **MAHAVIRACHARYA**, 9th c. |
| *"The Yukti Kalpa Taru is a mathematical text."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **SHIPBUILDING** |
| *"The ship's compass was called nava tala."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **`MACHAYANTRA`.** *Nava tala is the KEEL* |
| *"Nagarjuna was an astronomer."* | ⭐⭐⭐ **An ALCHEMIST/chemist** |
| *"Indo-Greek coins used Sanskrit and Greek."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **GREEK and PALI (in KHAROSTHI script)** |
| *"Kanishka mostly issued lead coins."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Lead is the SATAVAHANA metal.** Kushana coins are **gold and copper**, and Kanishka's used **only Greek characters** |
| *"The Ujjain symbol is a Kushana feature."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **SATAVAHANA** |
| *"Peacocks appear on Gupta coins."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **VARDHANA** *(Harsha)*. **Gupta reverses carry Lakshmi, Durga, Ganga, Garuda, Kartikeya** |
| *"Sanskrit first appears on coins under the Mauryas."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **Under the GUPTAS — the first time in the history of coins** |
| *"Ilahi coins were issued by Jahangir."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **AKBAR, 1579**, for Din-i-Ilahi. *(Jahangir's are the ZODIAC coins)* |
| *"The rupee was introduced by Akbar."* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ⭐ **SHER SHAH SURI** |
| *"Western Satrap coins are dated in the Vikrama era."* | ⭐⭐⭐ **The `SAKA` era, from 78 AD** |

## 🟢 Mains angles

| Angle | The line to take |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"India was far ahead in science and technology in ancient and medieval times" — critically examine** | ⭐⭐ **The word is "critically", so do both halves.** **FOR**: zero as a number, negative numbers as debts, the *Sulvasutra*'s proto-Pythagoras, Aryabhata's rotating earth and shadow-theory of eclipses, the LCM, the chakravala method, Sushruta's rhinoplasty, zinc distillation, ⭐ **and the Arabs calling mathematics *Hindisat***. **AGAINST**: transmission was **text-bound and elite**, tied to **ritual need** *(geometry for fire altars, astronomy for calendars and horoscopes)*; ⭐⭐ **there was no institutional continuity** — observatories depended on individual patrons *(Firuz Shah, Jai Singh)*; and much was **preserved abroad rather than developed at home**. ⭐ **Conclude on Aryabhata displacing the demon-swallowing-the-planet theory — the real achievement was METHOD, not just result** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Significance of the *Rasarnava*"** *(Mains 2012)* | Place it in the **alchemical tradition** with **Nagarjuna** and the ***Rasa Ratnakara*** *(zinc distillation, → [Ch.3 §6](03_Indian_Handicrafts.md))*; the applied fields — **metallurgy, sugar, paper, perfume distillation**; ⭐ **and the practical proof: the rust-resistant Mehrauli iron pillar** *(→ [Ch.1 §27](01_Architecture_Sculpture_and_Pottery.md))* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Coins as historical evidence** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The strongest numismatics answer, and it argues rather than lists.** What coins uniquely give: **dynastic sequence where texts fail** *(Satavahanas — "devoid of artistic merit" yet "valuable source-material")*; ⭐ **absolute chronology** *(Western Satraps dated in the Saka era; Harsha's era from 606)*; **religious affiliation** *(Kushana deities, Gupta Lakshmi, Chalukyan boar)*; **economic reach** *(Magadhan coins circulating across South Asia)*; **ideology** *(Akbar's Ilahi coin as religious propaganda)*. ⭐⭐ **Then the limitation: coins record STATES. The `COWRIE SHELL` economy of ordinary people leaves almost no trace** — ⭐ **cross-link [Cl.12 Th.2](../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/02_Theme2_Kings_Farmers_and_Towns.md) on what numismatic evidence can and cannot support** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Shipbuilding and navigation** | The ***Yukti Kalpa Taru***; the vessel types; the vocabulary *(especially the **fish-shaped compass**)*; ⭐ **then connect to the maritime story: Lothal's dockyard, Roman trade at Arikamedu, and the Chola and Kalinga voyages that carried temples to Angkor and Prambanan** *(→ [Ch.1 §25](01_Architecture_Sculpture_and_Pottery.md))* |

## 🔁 Recap — in six lines

1. **`Ganita` has five branches**; ⭐ **`Bijaganita` (algebra) means "the OTHER mathematics."**
2. ⭐ **Baudhayana's *Sulvasutra* (6th c. BC) is the earliest maths text** — Pi and proto-Pythagoras.
3. ⭐⭐ **Aryabhata (499): round rotating earth, eclipses by shadow, accurate Pi. Brahmagupta (7th c.): ZERO as a number, negatives as debts. Mahaviracharya (9th c.): the LCM. Bhaskaracharya (12th c.): the chakravala method.**
4. **Medicine: the Atharva Veda first, then rational medicine from ~600 BC at Takshashila and Varanasi; Sushruta on rhinoplasty and ophthalmology.** ⭐ **Chemistry: Nagarjuna; metallurgy, sugar, paper, perfume.**
5. ⭐ **Coins: punch-marked *karshapanas* → Indo-Greeks introduce the RULER'S PORTRAIT and bilingual legends → Satavahana LEAD with the UJJAIN symbol → Western Satraps DATED in the Saka era → Guptas in GOLD with SANSKRIT for the first time → Vardhana PEACOCK.**
6. ⭐⭐ **Sher Shah introduced the RUPEE; Akbar the square and ILAHI coins; Jahangir the ZODIAC series.**
