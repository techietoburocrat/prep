## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ The Vedic corpus — the shape to hold

| Layer | ⭐ What it is |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The four Vedas** | **Rig** *(chief deity **INDRA**; goddesses **Usha, Prithvi, Vak**)* · **Yajur** · **Sama** *(⭐ the musical Veda — → [Ch.5 §1](05_Indian_Music.md))* · ⭐⭐ **Atharva** — 🔴 **the Veda of MAGICAL CHARMS AND SPELLS, and of the treatment of ailments** *(a 2004 Prelims answer)* |
| **Brahmanas** | Ritual explanation |
| **Aranyakas** | The "forest books" |
| ⭐ **Upanishads** | ⭐ **Ritualistic information on the birth-and-death cycle and the complexity of the soul**; ⭐⭐ **the source of *`VASUDHAIVA KUTUMBAKAM`*** |

> ⭐⭐ **Two literary-form terms the book tests:** ⭐ **a `FABLE` features ANIMALS, inanimate objects, mythical creatures or plants given human qualities**; ⭐ **a `PARABLE` features a HUMAN character.** 🔴 **The book's own practice question states these the wrong way round and marks the answer "neither" — get the definitions right, not the question.**

## 2️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Jain literature — **the cleanest delta in the chapter**

⭐ Jain monks wrote in **Prakrit and Ardha-Magadhi**, but also **Tamil (in the Sangam age), Sanskrit, Shauraseni, Gujarati and Marathi**.

| | ⭐ Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **The two categories** | **Canonical — the `AGAMAS`** *(the tirthankaras' teachings)* and **non-canonical literary works** |
| **Compilers** | Originally by the ⭐ **`GANDHARAS`**, Mahavira's immediate disciples |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The Vallabhi council** | ⭐ **Re-compiled at a council of SVETAMBARA monks at VALLABHI (Gujarat), mid-5th century AD.** 🔴⭐⭐⭐ **THE DIGAMBARAS REJECT THIS — they hold the original teachings were lost long ago and do not accept the authority of the Vallabhi Agamas** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The arithmetic** | ⭐⭐ **46 texts = 12 Angas + 12 Upangas + 10 Prakirnakas + 4 Mulasutras + 6 Chedasutras + 2 Chulika Sutras**, in **Ardha-Magadhi Prakrit** |
| **What the Angas teach** | **Reverence for all life, strict vegetarianism, asceticism, compassion, non-violence**. ⭐ ***Acharanga Sutra* is the OLDEST agama**; *Sutrakritanga* gives the monks' code |

## 3️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sikh literature — a clean, dated sequence

| Text | ⭐ Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Adi Granth** | ⭐ **Compiled 1604 by BHAI GURDAS under the fifth guru, GURU ARJAN DEV**; in **Gurmukhi**; ⭐ **contains the teachings of the Gurus AND FIFTEEN BHAGATS of the Bhakti and Sufi traditions** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Guru Granth Sahib** | ⭐⭐ **The Adi Granth expanded in 1678 under the tenth guru, GURU GOBIND SINGH**; ⭐⭐⭐ **regarded as the ELEVENTH AND FINAL spiritual authority of the Sikhs**. ⭐⭐ **Written in `SANT BHASA`** — a composite drawing on **Punjabi, Apabhramsha, Hindi, Braj Bhasha, Sanskrit, Khadiboli and Persian**. ⭐⭐ **Contains 13 Bhakti `bhagats`** *(Ramananda, Namdev, Ravidas, Parmanand, Sain, Surdas…)* **and TWO MUSLIM bhagats — `KABIR` and `BABA FARID`** |
| ⭐ **Dasam Granth** | Hymns attributed to **Guru Gobind Singh** |

⭐ **`Gurbani`** = the compositions and hymns of the Gurus and of the Guru Granth Sahib.

## 4️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ Zoroastrian literature

⭐⭐ **The *AVESTA*** — compiled in final form under the **Sasanians, ~4th century CE**, in the **now-extinct Avestan language**, *"similar to Sanskrit."* ⭐⭐ **`YASNA` has 72 chapters**, of which ⭐⭐⭐ **five chapters — the `GATHAS`, containing 17 hymns — are the most revered and are attributed to ZOROASTER HIMSELF.** ⭐ **Yasna is also the name of the faith's most important ceremony.** Other parts: **Visperad, Yashts, Siroza, Nyayeshes** *(→ the five-part division in [Ch.12 §4](12_and_14_Religions_and_Philosophy.md))*.

> ⭐⭐ **A striking claim worth quoting**: *"According to scholars, Zoroastrianism was the FIRST religion to believe in **angels, a day of judgment, a demonic figure, and a battle between the forces of good and evil**."*

## 5️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ Medieval literature — Persian and the vernaculars

⭐ **Persian's roots are as old as Sanskrit's, but it CAME TO INDIA with the Turks and Mongols in the 12th century** and became the court language.

| Figure | Work |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Amir Khusrau Dehlavi** | His ***Diwan***; ⭐ ***Nuh Sipihr***; ⭐ ***Masnavi Duwal Rani Khizr Khan*** — **a tragic love poem** |
| ⭐ **Zia-ud-din Barani** | ***Tarikh-i-Firuz Shahi*** |

| ⭐ **Ibn Battuta** | The **Moroccan** traveller's account *(→ [Cl.12 Th.5](../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/05_Theme5_Through_the_Eyes_of_Travellers.md))* |

## 6️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Modern literature — **the four Hindi yugs**

|---|---|
| ⭐ **Bharatendu Yug** | **1868–1893** |

## 🟢 Mains angles

| Angle | The line to take |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Canon formation as a historical process** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The best available argument, and it uses THREE traditions at once**: the Buddhist canon fixed at **Rajagriha** by three named disciples; the Jain canon re-compiled at **Vallabhi in the 5th century — and REJECTED by the Digambaras**; the Sikh canon **compiled in 1604 and CLOSED in 1678, then declared the eleventh Guru.** ⭐⭐ **The point: a canon is an act of ORGANISATION as much as of revelation — and every closure creates a dissenting party** |

## 🔁 Recap — in six lines

1. **Atharva Veda = charms, spells and ailments.** The Upanishads give ***vasudhaiva kutumbakam***.
2. ⭐ **The Jain Agamas number 46** *(12 Angas + 12 Upangas + 10 Prakirnakas + 4 Mulasutras + 6 Chedasutras + 2 Chulikas)*, re-compiled at ⭐ **Vallabhi in the mid-5th century — and rejected by the Digambaras.**
3. ⭐⭐ **Adi Granth 1604 (Bhai Gurdas under Guru Arjan Dev) → Guru Granth Sahib 1678 (Guru Gobind Singh) = the ELEVENTH and final Guru**, in **Sant Bhasa**, including **Kabir and Baba Farid**.
4. **The Avesta's Yasna has 72 chapters; the five Gatha chapters (17 hymns) are Zoroaster's own.**
5. **Persian arrived with the Turks in the 12th century** — Amir Khusrau, Barani, Minhaj-us-Siraj, Ibn Battuta.
6. ⭐ **Modern Hindi runs in four yugs**: **Bharatendu (1868–93) → Dwivedi (1893–1918) → Chhayavad (1918–37) → Contemporary (1937–)**.
