# 📚 PART A — RELIGIONS: THE SECT TAXONOMY

## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hinduism's four traditions

| Tradition | ⭐ Supreme deity / basis |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Vaishnavism** | **Vishnu**. Roots in the **1st millennium BCE** as **Bhagavatism / Krishnaism** |
| ⭐ **Shaivism** | **Shiva**. ⭐⭐ **Traces its origin EARLIER than Vaishnavism — to the 2nd millennium BCE, as the Vedic deity RUDRA** |
| ⭐ **Shaktism** | ⭐ **The feminine — Devi as supreme**; known for its **Tantra** sub-traditions |
| ⭐⭐ **Smartism** | ⭐⭐ **Based on the PURANAS**; ⭐⭐⭐ **domestic worship of FIVE deities as EQUALS — Shiva, Shakti, Ganesha, Vishnu, Surya** *(the `panchayatana` puja)*. ⭐ **Accepts both `Saguna Brahman` (with attributes) and `Nirguna Brahman` (without)** |

> ⭐⭐ **A `sampradaya`** is a teaching tradition with **autonomous practices, monastic centres and a GURU LINEAGE**, transmitted by each successive generation.

| Sampradaya | ⭐ Identifier |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Varkari** *(Maharashtra)* | Devotees of Vishnu as ⭐ **VITHOBA**, centred on **Pandharpur**; ⭐ **strict avoidance of alcohol and tobacco**; the annual pilgrimage ⭐ **`VARI`**, carrying saints' ***padukas*** in ***palkhis***; events ⭐ **`Ringan`** *(a sacred horse runs through the rows and pilgrims smear the kicked-up dust on their heads)* and **`Dhava`**. Figures: ⭐ **Jnaneshvar, Namdev, Eknath, Tukaram** |
| ⭐ **Ramanandi** | Follows **Ramananda**; ⭐⭐ **the LARGEST monastic group within Hinduism in Asia**; monks called **Ramanandis, Vairagis or Bairagis**; worship **Rama** |

## 2️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The early Buddhist schism — **the 2017 Prelims question**

> ⭐⭐ **After the Buddha's death the original Sangha split, between 383 and 250 BC, into TWO early schools:**

| School | ⭐ Named sub-sects |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Sthavira nikaya** | **Mahisasaka, SARVASTIVADA, Sankrantika, SAUTRANTIKA, Dharmaguptaka, Vatsiputriya, Dharmottariya, Bhadrayaniya, Sannagarika, SAMMITIYA** |
| ⭐⭐ **Mahasamghika** | **Gokulika, Prajnaptivada, Bahusrutiya, Ekavyaharika, Caitika** |
| **Unassigned** | Hemavatika, Rajagiriya, Siddhatthaka, Pubbaseliya, Aparaseliya, Apararajagirika |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PRELIMS 2017 ASKED EXACTLY THIS.** *Statement: **"Sautrantika and Sammitiya were the sects of JAINISM."*** ⭐⭐⭐ **FALSE — they are BUDDHIST sub-sects of the Sthavira nikaya.** *(Jainism's two sects are **Digambara** and **Svetambara**.)* **A second statement in the same question — that the Sarvastivadins held that the constituents of phenomena were perpetually in existence — was TRUE.**

⭐ **Later schools: Theravada, Mahayana, Vajrayana** — *and then:*

> ⭐⭐⭐ **`NAVAYANA` — a genuinely modern gap.** *A new branch propounded by ⭐⭐ **Dr B. R. AMBEDKAR**, which **rejects Theravada, Mahayana and Vajrayana alike** and ⭐⭐ **discards monasticism, karma, rebirth, samsara, meditation, enlightenment AND the Four Noble Truths** — ⭐⭐⭐ **radically re-interpreting Buddhism as being about CLASS STRUGGLE AND SOCIAL EQUALITY.** *A direct GS1-Society and GS2 crossover.*

## 3️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Jainism's sub-sect tree

> ⭐ ***Jina/Jaina*** = **"the Conqueror."** ⭐⭐ **Jainism has NO single founder** — truth comes through a **Tirthankara** in each age. 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐ **Mahavira was NOT the founder — he was the LAST and 24th tirthankara**; **23 preceded him**.

```
   JAINISM
   |
   +-- DIGAMBARA -- +-- BISPANTHI
   |                +-- TERAPANTHI
   |
   +-- SVETAMBARA - +-- STHANAKAVASI
                    +-- MURTIPUJAKA (Deravasi)
                    +-- TERAPANTHI
```

| Sub-sect | ⭐ Distinguishing practice |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Digambara Bispanthi** | Worship **tirthankaras AND Yaksha/Yakshini** *(Bhairava, Kshetrapala)*; **aarti and offerings of flowers, fruits, prasad**; ⭐ **their dharma-gurus are the `BHATTARAKAS`**; concentrated in **Rajasthan and Gujarat** |
| ⭐ **Digambara Terapanthi** | ⭐⭐ **OPPOSES the Bhattarakas; worships ONLY tirthankaras**; uses ***ashta-dravya*** but ⭐ **replaces flowers and fruits with DRY substitutes** |
| ⭐ **Svetambara Sthanakavasi** | ⭐ **Pray to SAINTS rather than idols**; saints ⭐⭐ **wear a `MUHAPATTI`** over the mouth |
| ⭐ **Svetambara Murtipujaka** *(Deravasi)* | ⭐ **Keep and worship tirthankara IDOLS**; saints do **NOT** wear a muhapatti |
| ⭐ **Svetambara Terapanthi** | Like Sthanakavasi — **saints, not idols**, and **do wear the muhapatti** |

> 🧠 ⭐⭐⭐ **The clean discriminator: `MUHAPATTI` = the two SAINT-WORSHIPPING Svetambara sects (Sthanakavasi and Terapanthi). No muhapatti = MURTIPUJAKA, the idol-worshippers.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Two practices worth knowing — one is a live legal issue

| Practice | ⭐ Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Sallekhana** | ⭐ **Voluntary fasting to death by gradually reducing food and liquids.** ⭐⭐ **Jain scholars deny it is suicide — "it is not an act of passion, nor does it deploy poisons or weapons."** Open to **both ascetics and householders**. 🔴⭐⭐⭐ **THE CASE: in 2015 the RAJASTHAN HIGH COURT BANNED it as suicide; later that year the SUPREME COURT STAYED that decision and LIFTED the ban.** *A textbook Article 25 vs Article 21 conflict — use it in GS2* |
| ⭐ **Pratikraman** | **Repentance for daily sins** and a reminder not to repeat them. ⭐ **Five types: Devasi, Rayi, Pakhi, Chaumasi, Samvatsari** |

# 📚 PART B — SCHOOLS OF PHILOSOPHY ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## 5️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐ The frame — four goals, two families

| Goal | Meaning | ⭐ Its treatise |
|---|---|---|
| **Artha** | Economic means, wealth | ⭐ **Arthashastras** |
| **Dharma** | Regulation of the social order | ⭐ **Dharmashastras** |
| **Kama** | Physical pleasure, love | ⭐ **Kamasutra / Kamashastra** |
| ⭐ **Moksha** | **Salvation** | ⭐ **Darshana texts** |

| Family | ⭐ Defining test |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **ORTHODOX** *(`Shad-Darshana` — six)* | ⭐⭐ **Accept the Vedas as supreme revealed scripture; do NOT question their authenticity** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **HETERODOX** *(three)* | ⭐⭐ **Do NOT believe in the originality of the Vedas, and question the existence of God** |

## 6️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The six orthodox schools

| School | Founder / text | ⭐ The core idea |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Samkhya** | ⭐ **Kapila** — *Samkhya Sutra* | ⭐⭐ **The OLDEST school.** *Samkhya* = **"count."** ⭐⭐⭐ **DUALISM (`dvaitavada`) — soul and matter are SEPARATE.** Reality rests on ⭐ **`PRAKRITI`** *(nature — thought, movement, transformation; feminine)* and ⭐ **`PURUSHA`** *(spirit/consciousness — unchangeable; masculine)*. ⭐ **Salvation through KNOWLEDGE; lack of knowledge is the root of misery.** Three means of knowledge: ⭐ **`Pratyaksha` (perception), `Anumana` (inference), `Shabda` (hearing)** |
| ⭐⭐ **Yoga** | ⭐ **Patanjali** — *Yogasutra* *(~2nd c. BCE)* | ⭐ **Salvation by combining MEDITATION and physical technique**, releasing **Purusha from Prakriti**. ⭐⭐ **The eight limbs**: **Yama** *(self-control)* · **Niyama** *(rules of life)* · *asanas* · *pranayama* · **Pratyahara** *(choosing an object)* · **Dharana** *(fixing the mind on it)* · **Dhyana** *(concentrating on it)* · ⭐ **Samadhi** *(merging of mind and object — dissolution of the self)* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Nyaya** | ⭐ **Gautama** — *Nyaya Sutra* | ⭐⭐ **LOGIC.** Salvation only through **"real knowledge"**, verified by ⭐ **inference, hearing and analogy**. *The stock example: there is fire in the jungle, because there is smoke, and everything that emits smoke has fire.* ⭐ **God created, sustains AND destroys the universe** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Vaisheshika** | ⭐ **Kanada** | ⭐⭐ **PHYSICALITY — realistic and objective.** Everything is made of **five elements: fire, air, water, earth, ether** *(= `DRAVYA`)*. ⭐⭐⭐ **Developed the ATOMIC THEORY — "responsible for the beginning of PHYSICS in the Indian subcontinent."** ⭐ Still theistic: **God decides the merits and demerits of our actions**; the **laws of karma** guide the universe |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Mimamsa** | ⭐ **Jaimini** — *Sutras* *(3rd c. BCE)*; later **Sabara Swami** and **Kumarila Bhatta** | ⭐ *Mimamsa* = **the art of reasoning, interpretation and application**; analyses the **Samhita and Brahmana** portions of the Vedas. ⭐⭐ **The Vedas are eternal truth and the repository of all knowledge; salvation requires PERFORMING VEDIC RITUALS — and understanding their justification.** 🔴⭐⭐⭐ **The social consequence Singhania flags: because most people could not understand the rituals, they needed PRIESTS — so this philosophy "inherently LEGITIMISED the social distance between classes" and was used by Brahmans to maintain their clout** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Vedanta** | ⭐ **Badarayana** — *Brahmasutra* *(2nd c. BCE)* | ⭐ ***Veda* + *anta*** = **"the end of the Vedas"** — upholds the **Upanishads**. ⭐⭐ **Brahman is the only reality; everything else is `MAYA`. `Atman` = `Brahman`** — know the self and you know Brahman. ⭐ **Accepts KARMA and `PUNARJANMA` (rebirth)** |

### 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Shankaracharya vs Ramanuja — **a guaranteed comparison**

| | ⭐ **Shankaracharya** *(9th c.)* | ⭐ **Ramanuja** *(12th c.)* |
|---|---|---|
| **Brahman** | ⭐⭐ **WITHOUT attributes** *(nirguna)* | ⭐⭐ **WITH certain attributes** *(saguna)* |
| ⭐⭐ **Path to salvation** | ⭐⭐⭐ **`JNANA` — knowledge** | ⭐⭐⭐ **Loving faith and DEVOTION (bhakti)** |
| **Doctrine** | ⭐ **Advaita Vedanta** | *(Vishishtadvaita)* |

## 7️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ The three heterodox schools

| School | ⭐ The core idea |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Buddhist** | ⭐ **Vedic teaching "might not be useful… one should not trust it blindly."** The ⭐⭐ **Four Noble Truths** — *dukkha, samudaya, nirodha, magga* — and the ⭐⭐⭐ **Eightfold Path**: **Right Vision, Right Resolve, Right Speech, Right Conduct, Right Livelihood, Right Effort, Right Mindfulness, Right Concentration.** ⭐ **The First Council at RAJAGRIHA codified the three pitakas** — ⭐⭐ **`Vinaya` by UPALI** *(rules of order)*, **`Sutta` by ANANDA** *(sermons)*, **`Abhidhamma` by MAHAKASHYAPA** *(philosophy)* |
| ⭐⭐ **Jain** | First elaborated by ⭐ **Rishabhadeva (Adinath)**; also **Aristanemi** and **Ajitnath**. ⭐ **Also opposes the primacy of the Vedas.** Salvation by ⭐ **right perception + right knowledge + right conduct** *(and `brahmacharya`)*. ⭐⭐ **Seven fundamentals: `jiva, ajiva, asrava, bandha, samvara, nirjara, moksha`.** ⭐⭐ **Two kinds of existence: `ASTIKAYA`** *(has physical shape)* **and `ANASTIKAYA`** *(has none — e.g. **TIME**)*. ⭐ **Substance is eternal and unchangeable; its qualities keep changing** *(consciousness is the soul's substance; happy/sad is the changing attribute)* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Charvaka / Lokayata** | Founded by ⭐ **Brihaspati**; mentioned in the **Vedas** and the **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad**. ⭐⭐ ***Lokayata*** = **derived from the common people**, and **attachment to the material world (*loka*)**. ⭐⭐⭐ **The ONLY thoroughly MATERIALIST school**: denies any supernatural agent, denies **Brahman and God**, denies the need for salvation; ⭐⭐ **rejects ETHER as an element — the universe has only FOUR (fire, earth, water, air) — because ether cannot be perceived.** ⭐⭐ **Attacks the priestly class: "a Brahman manufactures false rituals so as to acquire `dakshina`."** ⭐⭐ **"Death is the end" → the doctrine of "EAT, DRINK AND MAKE MERRY."** *(Asked in Mains 2007)* |

## 🟢 Mains angles

| Angle | The line to take |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Explain the six orthodox schools"** | ⭐ **Do not list — PAIR them, which is how they actually developed**: **Samkhya-Yoga** *(metaphysics + its practice)*, **Nyaya-Vaisheshika** *(logic + physics)*, **Mimamsa-Vedanta** *(the ritual half of the Vedas + the philosophical half)*. ⭐⭐ **That three-pair structure is worth a mark on its own** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Orthodox vs heterodox"** | The test is **acceptance of Vedic authority**, not theism — ⭐⭐ **and the sharp point is that the line does NOT track belief in God**: **Samkhya is orthodox yet effectively atheistic**, while **Vaisheshika is atheistic in method yet theistic in conclusion**, and **Jainism is heterodox yet deeply spiritual.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Only CHARVAKA is materialist all the way down** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Write on Charvaka"** *(Mains 2007)* | Brihaspati; *Lokayata* as **"of the common people"**; **four elements, not five**; **no afterlife, no salvation, no God**; ⭐⭐ **its sharpest edge is SOCIAL — the attack on priests manufacturing rituals for *dakshina***. ⭐ **Close honestly: we know it largely THROUGH ITS OPPONENTS' refutations, which is itself a lesson in how heterodoxy survives in the record** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Philosophy and the social order** | ⭐⭐⭐ **The strongest available argument, and Singhania hands you both halves.** **Mimamsa** *"inherently legitimised the social distance between classes"* by making salvation depend on priest-mediated ritual; **Vedanta**'s equation of atman and brahman implied that *"true spirituality was implicit in the unchanging social and material situation in which a person is born."* ⭐⭐ **Set against them: Charvaka's attack on priestcraft, Buddhism and Jainism's rejection of Vedic authority, and — a millennium later — AMBEDKAR'S NAVAYANA re-reading Buddhism as CLASS STRUGGLE.** ⭐ **That arc, from Mimamsa to Navayana, is a complete answer** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Sallekhana — faith vs the right to life** | **Article 25** *(freedom of religion)* against **Article 21** *(right to life)*; the Jain argument that it is **"not an act of passion" and uses "neither poison nor weapon"**; the **Rajasthan HC ban (2015)** and the **Supreme Court's stay**. ⭐ **Compare euthanasia jurisprudence for a GS2/GS4 crossover** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **"Buddhist vs Jain philosophy"** | **Shared**: rejection of Vedic authority, karma, rebirth, non-violence, salvation by conduct. **Divergent**: ⭐ **Jainism's SEVEN fundamentals and its astikaya/anastikaya ontology** vs ⭐ **Buddhism's Four Noble Truths and Eightfold Path**; **Jain extreme asceticism** *(sallekhana)* vs the **Buddhist Middle Path**; **Jainism has 24 tirthankaras and no founder**, Buddhism one founder |

## 🔁 Recap — in eight lines

1. **Hinduism has four traditions** — **Vaishnavism, Shaivism** *(older, from Rudra)*, **Shaktism, Smartism** *(five deities as equals)*.
2. ⭐ **The Buddhist Sangha split into `Sthavira` and `Mahasamghika`** — and **Sautrantika and Sammitiya are Sthavira sub-sects, NOT Jain.**
3. ⭐⭐ **Ambedkar's `NAVAYANA` rejects all three classical vehicles and re-reads Buddhism as class struggle.**
4. **Jainism divides Digambara** *(Bispanthi vs Terapanthi, over the Bhattarakas)* **and Svetambara** *(Sthanakavasi, Murtipujaka, Terapanthi — the muhapatti marks the saint-worshippers)*.
5. ⭐ **Sallekhana was banned by the Rajasthan HC in 2015 and un-banned by the Supreme Court the same year.**
6. **Six orthodox schools, best learned in three pairs**: **Samkhya-Yoga**, **Nyaya-Vaisheshika**, **Mimamsa-Vedanta**.
7. ⭐ **Shankara: Brahman without attributes, salvation by KNOWLEDGE. Ramanuja: Brahman with attributes, salvation by DEVOTION.**
8. **Three heterodox schools** — Buddhist, Jain and ⭐ **Charvaka, the only true materialist, which rejects ether, the afterlife and the priesthood alike.**
