# 🎭 Nitin Singhania — *Indian Art and Culture*
## The **ART & CULTURE** standard reference — the last gap in this folder, now sourced

> **What this folder is.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The one book here that is NOT mostly a delta.** *Every other book in this folder overlaps heavily with your NCERT spine, so its notes cover only the increment. **Art & Culture is different**: your spine has ~60,000 lines of political and economic history and **almost no thematic culture at all**. There is no NCERT chapter on **dance forms**, **musical gharanas**, **painting schools**, **theatre**, **puppetry**, **UNESCO lists**, **languages**, **martial arts** or **GI tags**. ⭐ **For most of this book the delta IS the chapter.**

## 📖 The source

**`Textbook_PDF/13-General-Indian Art and Culture-Nitin Singhania.pdf`** — ***Indian Art and Culture*, 2nd edition**, **Nitin Singhania**, **McGraw Hill Education (India)**, **© 2016, 2018**. **923 pages · 2 sections · 24 chapters · 5 appendices.**

> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Provenance, stated honestly.** *Like [Spectrum](../Modern_India_Spectrum/00_INDEX.md), **this is an IN-PRINT COMMERCIAL BOOK** — the circulating copy came through UPSC-materials channels. It is used here **as a private reference to write ORIGINAL notes** — **paraphrased, not quoted at length** — and **the PDF is not redistributed**. *(Same rule as recorded in the [folder index](../00_INDEX.md).)*

> ⭐⭐ **Two things about this book you should know before trusting it.** *(1)* **It is written FOR UPSC** — each chapter ends with **actual Previous Years' Questions, Prelims and Mains, with answer keys**. That makes its PYQ register more valuable than its prose. *(2)* ⚠️ **It contains real, checkable errors** — the notes tag them ⚠️`[VERIFY]` in place rather than repeating them. *Three already found in Chapter 1 alone: a Mathura-school date given as **BC** instead of **CE**, the **Kalsi edict dated "450 B.C."** (impossible — Ashoka reigned 269–232 BCE), and an **answer key that calls Dasharatha Ashoka's "son"** while the main text correctly calls him his grandson.*
