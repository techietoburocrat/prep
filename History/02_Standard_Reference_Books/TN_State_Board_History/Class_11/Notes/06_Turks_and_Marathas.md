# 🏛️ PART A — UNIT 10: THE ADVENT OF ARABS AND TURKS

## 1️⃣ Historiography — how to frame the Sultanate ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THREE SCHOOLS, AND THE TEXTBOOK REFUSES TO PICK
   ================================================================

   (1) ** THE CONVENTIONAL / PERSONALITY-ORIENTED ** :
       "the regimes of the Sultanate have been evaluated in
       terms of ** ACHIEVEMENTS AND FAILURES OF INDIVIDUAL
       SULTANS. ** "

   (2) ** THE COMPOSITE-CULTURE SCHOOL ** : critiquing the
       above, they see the Sultanate as "having contributed to
       ** MATERIAL AND CULTURAL DEVELOPMENT, leading to the
       EVOLUTION OF A COMPOSITE CULTURE in India. ** "

   (3) ** THE CLASS-RELATIONS SCHOOL ** : "the medieval state
       served as ** THE AGENT OF THE RULING CLASS ** ", and so
       the Sultanate regimes were ** "DIMINUTIVE IN THEIR
       INSTITUTIONAL ADVANCEMENT when compared with the Great
       Mughals." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE VERDICT: "Thus there is NO CONSENSUS YET
      amongst scholars in determining the TRUE NATURE of the
      Sultanate." **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE SUNIL KUMAR QUOTATION IS THE SHARPEST SOURCE-CRITICISM LINE IN THE UNIT — MEMORISE IT.** ⭐ *"**PERSIAN CHRONICLES speak about the Delhi Sultanate in HYPERBOLIC TERMS. Their views dealing with the happenings during the period of a certain Sultan were UNCRITICALLY APPROPRIATED INTO MODERN SCHOLARSHIP.**"* — **Sunil Kumar, *Emergence of Delhi Sultanate***. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The charge is not that the chronicles exaggerate — everyone knows that. It is that MODERN historians absorbed the exaggeration without filtering it, so the court's own self-image became the textbook's account.** *(⭐ **This is exactly [Class 7's three limitations of the *tawarikh*](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/03_Delhi_12th_to_15th_Century.md), stated as a charge against historians rather than against sources — and it is the same lesson as [Theme 2's "who paid for the stone?"](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_I/Notes/02_Theme2_Kings_Farmers_and_Towns.md).**)*

**⚠️ And the textbook's correction of the label "the Muslim period" — worth reproducing exactly:**

> *"Though it is customary to describe this period as **the Muslim period**, the rulers of medieval India **came from DIFFERENT REGIONS AND ETHNICITIES: ARABS, TURKS, PERSIANS and CENTRAL ASIANS** were involved militarily and administratively. ⭐ **Iltutmish was an ILBARI TURK** and many of his military slaves were of **different Turkish and Mongol ancestries**, brought to Delhi by merchants from **Bukhara, Samarkhand and Baghdad**. There were some slaves of other ethnicities as well — ⭐ **notably HINDU KHAN, captured from Mihir in Central India** — **but Iltutmish gave them ALL TURKISH TITLES**."*

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Iltutmish gave them all Turkish titles" is a wonderful detail, because it shows the label being MANUFACTURED.** ⭐ **The ruling elite was ethnically mixed and included at least one man captured in Central India — and the regime dressed all of them in a single Turkish identity.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So "Turkish rule" is partly a BRANDING DECISION taken at the Delhi court, and calling the era "the Muslim period" repeats a simplification the sources themselves created.**

## 2️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The Arab conquest of Sind (712)

**🌊 First, the context — and it is commercial, not military:**

> ⭐ *"The geographical location of Arabia facilitated **TRADE CONTACT** between India and Arabia. As **SEA-FARING TRADERS the PRE-ISLAMIC ARABS had maritime contacts** with the western and eastern coasts of India. **Arabs too settled in MALABAR and the COROMANDEL COAST.**"*

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE MAPPILLAIS — a term worth knowing:** *"The Arabs who **married MALABAR WOMEN and settled down on the West Coast** were called **MAPPILLAIS (sons-in-law)**."*

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Open any answer on Islam in India with this, because it corrects the default assumption before it forms.** ⭐ **Islam arrived on the MALABAR COAST by SEA, through TRADE AND MARRIAGE, generations before any army crossed the north-western frontier — and the community it produced was named for the marriage, not for the conquest.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two entirely different modes of arrival, and the textbook is explicit about the contrast: the military expeditions "created **A RELATIONSHIP OF THE CONQUEROR AND THE CONQUERED**", whereas the coastal settlement created **sons-in-law**.** *(⭐ **Then link forward: [Unit 6's yavana merchants at Puhar](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) had lived in earmarked quarters at Tamil ports seven centuries earlier. **The Indian Ocean coast had been absorbing foreign trading communities for a very long time, and it had a settled social technology for doing it.**)*

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE CONQUEST OF SIND, 712
   ================================================================

   ** WHO ORDERED IT: ** HAJJAJ BIN YUSUF ** , the Arab
      ** GOVERNOR OF IRAQ ** -- "under the ** PRETEXT of acting
      against the PIRATES ** ." **

   ** THE FIRST TWO ATTEMPTS: two expeditions against
      ** DAHAR ** , the ruler of Sind -- ** one by LAND, one by
      SEA. BOTH WERE DEFEATED AND THE COMMANDERS KILLED. ** **

   ⭐ ** THE THIRD: Hajjaj sent, ** WITH THE CALIPH'S
      PERMISSION ** , a full-fledged army --
        ** 6,000 STRONG CAVALRY ** + a large ** CAMEL CORPS ** --
      under his ** SON-IN-LAW, the SEVENTEEN-YEAR-OLD
      MUHAMMAD BIN QASIM. ** **

   ** THE BATTLE: Qasim marched on the fortress of
      ** BRAHMANABAD ** , where Dahar was stationed with a huge
      army. **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** WHY DAHAR LOST -- AND IT IS A POLITICAL, NOT A
      MILITARY, EXPLANATION: **
      - ** Dahar's WAZIR (prime minister) BETRAYED HIM ** ,
        followed by ** the DESERTION of a section of his
        forces ** ;
      - the predecessors of Dahar, ** the BRAHMIN rulers of
        Sind, HAD USURPED POWER FROM THE EARLIER BUDDHIST
        RULING DYNASTY ** , and with Dahar's patronage
        ** Brahmins had OCCUPIED ALL HIGHER POSITIONS ** ;
      - ⭐ ** "This led to DISCONTENTMENT and therefore
        ** DAHAR LACKED POPULAR SUPPORT. ** " **

   ** AFTER: Qasim ** ravaged and plundered DEBAL (the port) for
      THREE DAYS ** ; ** "called on the people of Sind to
      SURRENDER, PROMISING FULL PROTECTION TO THEIR FAITH" ** ;
      and ⭐ ** sent "the CUSTOMARY ONE-FIFTH of the plunder to
      the CALIPH" and DIVIDED THE REST AMONG HIS SOLDIERS. ** **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE VERDICT IS THE MOST QUOTABLE PHRASE IN UNIT 10 AND IT IS A SET QUESTION: the Arab conquest of Sind has been described as *"A TRIUMPH WITHOUT RESULTS"* — *"because it TOUCHED BUT A FRINGE OF THE COUNTRY, which, after Qasim's expedition, had A RESPITE FROM INVASIONS FOR ABOUT THREE CENTURIES."*** ⭐ **712 to roughly 1000 CE: three hundred years in which nothing followed.** *(⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The analytical point to add: the conquest of Sind is famous out of all proportion to its consequences, precisely BECAUSE of what came later. It is remembered as a beginning only in retrospect — at the time it was a frontier province that changed hands.** ⭐ **Saying that is worth marks, because most answers treat 712 as the opening move of a continuous story. It wasn't.**)*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And note the two details that cut against a purely religious reading: Qasim promised *"FULL PROTECTION TO THEIR FAITH"* to those who surrendered, and Dahar's defeat is explained by a BRAHMIN–BUDDHIST power struggle inside Sind that had left the ruler without popular support.** *The textbook's causes are political and factional, not confessional.*

## 3️⃣ Mahmud of Ghazni ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**🏔️ The Ghaznavid line — the sequence NCERT never gives:**

| Ruler | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALAPTIGIN**, **963** | **"A TURKIC SLAVE who had served the SAMANIDS as their governor in KHURASAN"** — he **seized the city of GHAZNI in eastern Afghanistan** and established an independent kingdom. *He **died soon after*** |
| **SABUKTIGIN** | Enthroned by the nobles after three of Alaptigin's successors failed. ⭐ **"Initiated the process of SOUTHWARD EXPANSION into the Indian sub-continent"** — defeated the **Shahi ruler JAYAPAL** and made his eldest son **Mahmud** governor |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MAHMUD** | Sabuktigin died **997** naming his younger son **ISMAIL**; ⭐ **Mahmud, aged 27, DEFEATED ISMAIL in battle and took the throne.** The Caliph acknowledged him with **a robe of investiture** and the title ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***YAMINI-UD-DAULAH*, "RIGHT-HAND OF THE EMPIRE"** |
| **The long decline** | *"a story of **endless clashes over succession** between brothers, cousins and uncles"* — with exceptions: ⭐ **Sultan IBRAHIM, over 42 years**, and his son **MASUD, 17 years** |
| ⭐ **The end** | Threatened by the **GHURIS** from the north and the **SELJUQ TURKS** from the west; the later rulers held **only the LAHORE region**, and only for **three decades**. **1186: the Ghuri prince MUIZZ-UD-DIN MUHAMMAD invaded Punjab and seized Lahore.** ⭐ **The last ruler KHURAV SHAH was imprisoned and murdered in 1192** |

**⚔️ The raids — the facts first:**

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"Mahmud ruled for **THIRTY-TWO YEARS**. During this period, he conducted as many as **SEVENTEEN MILITARY CAMPAIGNS into India**. He **targeted HINDU TEMPLES that were DEPOSITORIES OF VAST TREASURES**."*

| Motive the textbook names | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Loot** | The primary motive |
| ⭐ **Military advantage** | *"there was also **a MILITARY ADVANTAGE in demolishing temples and smashing idols**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Ideological display** | *"The Ghaznavid soldiers viewed it also as **a DEMONSTRATION OF THE INVINCIBLE POWER OF THEIR GOD**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The army's wage bill** | *"the plundering raids of Mahmud were meant to **REPLENISH THE TREASURY TO MAINTAIN HIS HUGE ARMY**"* |

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE ARMY THAT HAD TO BE PAID -- THE ECONOMIC
              ENGINE BEHIND THE RAIDS
   ================================================================
   ** "The Turks relied on ** A PERMANENT, PROFESSIONAL ARMY ** .
      It was built around ** AN ELITE CORPS OF MOUNTED ARCHERS
      WHO WERE ALL SLAVES -- BOUGHT, TRAINED, EQUIPPED, AND
      PAID IN CASH ** from the war booty taken ** ALIKE FROM
      HINDU KINGDOMS IN INDIA AND MUSLIM KINGDOMS IN IRAN ** ." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THAT LAST CLAUSE IS THE DECISIVE EVIDENCE IN THE WHOLE DEBATE, AND IT IS ONE LINE LONG — DEPLOY IT.** ⭐ **Mahmud's booty came *"ALIKE FROM HINDU KINGDOMS IN INDIA AND MUSLIM KINGDOMS IN IRAN."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A ruler who plunders co-religionists with the same enthusiasm as unbelievers is not running a religious campaign; he is running a payroll.** *(⭐ **The textbook supplies the corroborating figure: **Mahmud's plunder of the IRANIAN city of RAY in 1029** brought **500,000 dinars in jewels, 260,000 dinars in coins, and over 30,000 dinars in gold and silver vessels** — a Muslim city, sacked for the same reason.** ⭐ **Somnath (1025) is said to have brought **twenty million dinars**.)*

**⚖️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE HISTORIOGRAPHY OF SOMNATH — the set "critically examine" question:**

| Element | The textbook's statement |
|---|---|
| **The event** | ⭐ **The raid on the temple city of SOMNATH, on the seashore in GUJARAT, in 1025** — after defeating the Shahi king **ANANDAPALA**, Mahmud went **beyond Punjab into the Indo-Gangetic plain**, raiding **MATHURA** before reaching **Kanauj** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **How it was remembered** | *"In **LATER HISTORIOGRAPHY, of BOTH THE BRITISH AND INDIAN NATIONALISTS**, Mahmud is notorious for his invasion of Somnath"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The scholarly reading** | *"**Many scholars argue that these plundering raids were MORE OF POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC CHARACTER THAN OF RELIGIOUS CHAUVINISM.** **Desecration of temples, vandalising the images of deities were all part of ASSERTING ONE'S AUTHORITY in medieval India.** Mahmud's raids fit this pattern — **though their memories went into THE CREATION OF COMMUNAL DIVIDE**"* |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE THAPAR POINT IS THE STRONGEST MOVE AVAILABLE HERE AND ALMOST NOBODY MAKES IT, SO MAKE IT.** ⭐ **The most detailed and lurid accounts of the Somnath raid come from the ARAB AND PERSIAN chronicles — that is, from the VICTORS' side. The Hindu and Jain sources of the region are comparatively SILENT.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **If the raid had been experienced at the time as the epoch-defining religious catastrophe that later historiography made of it, the local literary tradition would be expected to say so. It largely does not.** *(⭐ **The methodological lesson: the intensity of a memory is not proof of the intensity of the original event. Memories are made later, by people with later purposes — and the textbook says exactly that: *"communal character was attributed to them LATER."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Handle this carefully and neutrally in an answer: state the evidence, name the readings, note that the chronicles on both sides had reasons to write as they did.**)*

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AL-BERUNI — the box, and he is a Prelims regular:** *"**Mathematician, philosopher, astronomer and historian**, he **came to India ALONG WITH MAHMUD OF GHAZNI**. >

One man, three transmissions — and he arrived in the baggage train of a plundering army.** *(⭐ **Note the connection back to [Unit 2](05_Megalithic_and_Iron_Age.md): the Aryabhata thesis Al-Beruni carried west is the same one that unit credits to the Gupta age. ⭐ And forward to **§7**: the Sultanate's Persian scholarship kept translating Sanskrit — Al-Beruni starts a tradition, he does not stand alone in it.**)*

              ANSWER TO "COMPARE MAHMUD AND MUHAMMAD GHORI"
   ================================================================

   ** GHAZNAVID: "If Ghaznavid invasions were INTENDED FOR
      ** LOOT ** ..." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** GHURID: "...the Ghurids ** ENLARGED THEIR SCOPE
      TO ESTABLISH GARRISON TOWNS ** to ensure ** THE REGULAR
      FLOW OF PLUNDER AND TRIBUTE ** ." **

   ** "Muhammad Ghori ** INVESTED IN TERRITORIES HE SEIZED ** .
      Through the ** 1180s and 1190s ** Ghori established
      garrisons in the modern provinces of ** PUNJAB, SIND and
      HARYANA ** ." **

   ⭐ ** AND THE SELF-REINFORCING EFFECT: "These centres of
      military power soon ** ATTRACTED THE IN-MIGRATION OF

      mercenaries were ** RECRUITED TO ORGANIZE FISCAL AND
      MILITARY AFFAIRS OF THE SULTANATE ** ." **

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS THE BEST COMPARISON IN UNIT 10 AND IT EXPLAINS WHY ONE SET OF INVASIONS LEFT A STATE BEHIND AND THE OTHER DID NOT.** ⭐ **Mahmud raided seventeen times and took the wealth home to Ghazni. Ghori built GARRISONS and stayed.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A garrison needs revenue, revenue needs administration, administration needs men — and men came, because a permanent military centre is a job market.** *So the Delhi Sultanate was not founded by a bigger victory than Mahmud's; **it was founded by a different BUSINESS MODEL** — extraction as an annual flow rather than as a periodic raid.* *(⭐ **The textbook is precise about the personnel too: the Sultan's commanders were drawn from **his ELITE MILITARY CLASS — "SPECIALLY TRAINED IN WARFARE AND GOVERNANCE"** and explicitly **"DIFFERENT FROM AGRESTIC (field-labour) AND DOMESTIC SLAVES."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is [Class 7's *BANDAGAN*](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/03_Delhi_12th_to_15th_Century.md) — and the distinction between a military slave trained to govern and a labouring slave is the whole reason the institution worked.**)*

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *CHAHALGANI* — THE FORTY — a set question, and NCERT does not name it:**

> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BALBAN ABOLISHED THE CORPS OF FORTY**, ending the domination of the Turkish nobles; **ALAUDDIN KHALJI later used SPIES reporting directly to him** on their *"clandestine and perfidious activities."*
>

Both are true and the combination is a much stronger answer.**)*

                              |
                              v

      The transactions in the bazaars, the buying and selling

      SPIES had to send ** DAILY REPORTS ** on the prices of
      essential commodities." **

   ⚠️ ** THE PENALTY: "If any DEFICIENCY IN WEIGHT was found,
      ** AN EQUAL WEIGHT OF FLESH WAS CUT FROM THE SELLER'S BODY
      AND THROWN DOWN BEFORE HIS EYES ** !" **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CAUSAL CHAIN IS THE ANSWER TO *"DISCUSS THE ECONOMIC REFORMS OF ALA-UD-DIN KHALJI"*, AND IT RUNS BACKWARDS FROM THE ARMY.** ⭐ **He needed a huge standing army against the Mongols → a standing army must be paid in cash → cash wages must be kept low or the treasury breaks → low wages only work if PRICES ARE LOW → therefore the market control, the spy network and the daily price reports.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The market reforms were not economic policy for its own sake; they were MILITARY policy expressed as price control.** *(⭐ **Which also explains why they did not outlive him: the mechanism was tied to one Sultan's army and one Sultan's willingness to enforce it with atrocity. ⭐ Pair this with [Class 7's account of his THREE TAXES](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/03_Delhi_12th_to_15th_Century.md) and you have both halves of the fiscal-military machine.**)*

| Year | Target |
|---|---|
| **1307** | ⭐ **DEVAGIRI fort** — Malik Kafur sent to capture it |
| ⭐ **1309** | **PRATAPARUDRADEVA, the KAKATIYA ruler of WARANGAL** *(Telangana)*, defeated |
| ⭐ **1310** | **The HOYSALA ruler VIRA BALLALA III "surrendered all his treasures"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **then** | **The TAMIL COUNTRY** — *"though Kafur's progress was obstructed by **heavy rains and floods**, he continued southward, **plundering and ravaging the temple cities of CHIDAMBARAM and SRIRANGAM as well as the PANDYAN CAPITAL MADURAI**"* |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THAT ONE SENTENCE — TAMIL MUSLIMS FIGHTING FOR THE PANDYAS AGAINST A DELHI ARMY — IS WORTH MORE THAN THE REST OF THE CAMPAIGN, AND IT IS EXACTLY THE KIND OF EVIDENCE THAT WINS MARKS.** ⭐ **The long-settled Muslim trading communities of the Coromandel — descendants of the same maritime settlement that produced the **MAPPILLAIS** of §2 — took the side of their own regional ruler against a co-religionist invader.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Local political loyalty overrode confessional identity, and it did so on a battlefield.** *(⭐ **Deploy this against any framing of medieval Indian conflict as religiously determined. It sits alongside Mahmud sacking Ray, Balban slaughtering the Meos, and Ghori's army recruiting whoever came — four independent instances, all in this one unit.**)*
>
> ⭐ **And [Unit 9](03_South_Indian_Culture_and_Temples.md) and [Unit 11](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md) supply the other side of this story — Chidambaram and Srirangam are the Chola and Vaishnava temple centres whose construction those units describe. Kafur's raid is where that architectural story meets the Sultanate's.**

### 5.4 The Tughlaqs, Sayyids and Lodis — the delta ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Ruler | The delta |
|---|---|
| **GHIYAS-UD-DIN TUGHLAQ** *(1320–25)* | Formerly **GHAZI MALIK, "a veteran of several campaigns against the Mongols"**; ⭐ **murdered the Khalji ruler KHUSRAU "and thereby PREVENTED ANYONE FROM THE KHALJI DYNASTY CLAIMING THE THRONE."** Followed **a policy of RECONCILIATION with the nobles** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MUHAMMAD BIN TUGHLAQ** *(1325–1351)* | Formerly **JAUNA KHAN**. ⭐ *"**learned, cultured and talented**… but gained a reputation of being **merciless, cruel and unjust**"*; ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"MUHAMMAD WAS AN INNOVATOR. BUT HE, UNLIKE ALA-UD-DIN, LACKED THE WILL TO EXECUTE HIS PLANS SUCCESSFULLY."** Died in his **26th regnal year (1351)** while pursuing a rebel in **Gujarat** |
| **FIRUZ SHAH TUGHLAQ** *(1351–1388)* | Son of **RAJAB**, Ghiyas-ud-din's younger brother, ⭐ *who **had married a JAT PRINCESS**;* Firuz was **seven** when his father died, and was given **command of a 12,000-strong cavalry** |
| **The Sayyids** *(1414–51)* | ⭐ **KHIZR KHAN**, appointed by **TIMUR** as his deputy in the Punjab marches, **seized Delhi**. **Four sultans**; the early ones **PAID TRIBUTE TO TIMUR'S SON**. ⭐ *"By the end of their rule the empire was **largely confined to the CITY OF DELHI**"* |
| **The Lodis** *(1451–1526)* | **BAHLUL LODI** *(1451–89)* — **conquest of the SHARQI kingdom**; ⭐ **SIKANDER LODI *(1489–1517)* SHIFTED THE CAPITAL FROM DELHI TO AGRA IN 1504**; **IBRAHIM LODI defeated by BABUR at the FIRST BATTLE OF PANIPAT (1526)** |

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ MUHAMMAD BIN TUGHLAQ's THREE EXPERIMENTS — and why each failed:**

| Experiment | The plan | Why it failed |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TRANSFER OF CAPITAL** | Shift **Delhi → DEVAGIRI**, renamed **DAULATABAD**, *"after his realization that **it was DIFFICULT TO RULE SOUTH INDIA FROM DELHI**."* ⭐ **Centrally located, with "a STRONG FORT ATOP A ROCKY HILL."** He ordered **important officers and leading men, including many SUFI SAINTS, to shift** | ⭐ *"soon Muhammad realised **it was difficult to rule NORTH INDIA FROM DAULATABAD**. He again ordered transfer of capital BACK TO DELHI"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TOKEN CURRENCY** | **BRONZE coins**. ⭐ *"This currency system **HAD ALREADY BEEN EXPERIMENTED IN CHINA AND IRAN**. For India **it was MUCH AHEAD OF ITS TIME**, given that it was a time when **coins were based on SILVER CONTENT**"* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"FAKE COINS WERE MINTED which could NOT BE PREVENTED by the government."** The coins were **devalued so far that the government had to WITHDRAW them and REPLACE them with SILVER — "which told heavily on the resources of the empire"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AGRICULTURAL EXPANSION** | A separate department — ⭐ ***DIWAN-I-AMIR KOHI*** — with **loans advanced to farmers for cattle, seeds and the digging of wells** | ⭐ **It "coincided with a PROLONGED AND SEVERE FAMINE IN THE DOAB"**; *"the famine was **linked to the OPPRESSIVE AND ARBITRARY COLLECTION of land revenue**"*; **officers appointed to monitor crops "were NOT EFFICIENT"**; and **the peasants who rebelled were harshly dealt with** |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE TOKEN-CURRENCY FAILURE IS THE MOST INSTRUCTIVE, AND THE TEXTBOOK NAMES THE REAL CAUSE PRECISELY: IT WAS NOT A BAD IDEA, IT WAS AN UNENFORCEABLE ONE.** ⭐ **Token currency works when the STATE HOLDS A MONOPOLY ON MINTING and can punish forgery. Bronze is cheap and easy to strike — so *"fake coins were minted WHICH COULD NOT BE PREVENTED."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A silver coin is self-authenticating because the metal IS the value; a token coin's value rests entirely on state credibility, and the Tughlaq state did not have the enforcement reach to supply it.** *(⭐ **China and Iran had done it — the textbook says so — which is why "ahead of its time" is the wrong diagnosis. The right one is "ahead of his ADMINISTRATION."** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And that is the single sentence that ties all three experiments together, and it is the textbook's own: he was an innovator who "LACKED THE WILL TO EXECUTE."**)*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The comparison the textbook draws is the best closing line for a Mains answer:** *"**ALA-UD-DIN KHALJI HAD NOT ANNEXED DISTANT TERRITORIES, knowing full well that they COULD NOT BE EFFECTIVELY GOVERNED.** He preferred to **establish his SUZERAINTY** over them. **BUT MUHAMMAD ANNEXED ALL THE LANDS HE CONQUERED.**"* ⭐ **Hence the rebellions of **BENGAL, MADURAI, WARANGAL, AWADH, GUJARAT and SIND** at the end of his reign. **One ruler understood the limits of his administrative reach; the other did not.**

**🕊️ FIRUZ SHAH TUGHLAQ — the reversal, and it is a clean contrast:**

| Policy | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Towards nobles** | **Conciliatory.** ⭐ **RESTORED property confiscated under Muhammad** · ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **REINTRODUCED HEREDITARY APPOINTMENTS TO OFFICES — "a practice NOT FAVOURED by Ala-ud-din Khalji"** · **increased salaries** · **reduced several taxes** · **abolished many varieties of torture** |
| ⭐ **Slaves** | *"a genuine concern"* — a **separate government department**, ⭐ **caring for 180,000 SLAVES**, *"trained in HANDICRAFTS and employed in the ROYAL WORKSHOPS"* *(⭐ of whom **12,000 worked as artisans**; his minister **Khan Jahan Maqbul possessed over 2,000 women slaves**)* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **War** | ***"Firuz waged NO WARS OF ANNEXATION"*** — only two Mongol incursions, both repulsed; a Bengal campaign; and a **major campaign against SIND (1362)**, hampered by **enemies and a famine**, but ending in the ruler's surrender and tribute |
| ⚠️ **Religion** | **Favoured ORTHODOX ISLAM**; **proclaimed his state an Islamic state "largely to satisfy the theologians"**; ⭐ **heretics persecuted, un-Islamic practices banned**; ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **imposed *JIZYA*, "which EVEN THE BRAHMINS were compelled to pay"** — ⚠️ **"Yet Firuz DID NOT PROHIBIT the building of new Hindu temples and shrines"** |
| ⭐ **Culture** | **Translation of many Sanskrit works on religion, medicine and music**; *"a **LIBERAL PATRON of the learned, INCLUDING NON-ISLAMIC SCHOLARS**"*; **fond of music**; founded **educational institutions, mosques, palaces and forts** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Public works** | **A canal from the SUTLEJ to HANSI, and another from the JUMNA** |
| ⭐ **His vizier** | **KHAN-I-JAHAN, "a BRAHMIN CONVERT to Islam, originally known as KANNU, captured during the Sultanate campaigns in WARANGAL"** |

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***JIZYA* — the full timeline in one box, and it spans your whole medieval syllabus:** *"A tax levied and collected **PER HEAD** by Islamic states on **non-Muslim subjects**. In India, ⭐ **QUTB-UD-DIN AIBAK imposed jizya on non-Muslims FOR THE FIRST TIME**. Jizya was **ABOLISHED by AKBAR in the 16th century** but **RE-INTRODUCED by AURANGZEB in the 17th**."*

> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE STRUCTURAL COST OF FIRUZ'S GENEROSITY — the textbook states it as a cause of collapse, and this is the analytical payoff of the whole dynasty:** *"**THE PRINCIPLE OF HEREDITY, permitted for the nobles and APPLIED TO THE ARMY, WEAKENED THE DELHI SULTANATE.** The nobility that had **REGAINED POWER got involved in POLITICAL INTRIGUES** which undermined the stability of the Sultanate. **Within SIX YEARS of Firuz Tughlaq's death, FOUR RULERS succeeded him.**"* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Every kindness Firuz did the nobility was a transfer of power away from the throne — and hereditary office is the same disease the [Guptas](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) had, and the same one that **ended the *Chahalgani* experiment** (§5.1).**

**⚔️ TIMUR'S INVASION** — under the last Tughlaq, **NASIR-UD-DIN MUHAMMAD SHAH (1394–1412)**. ⭐ **Timur, "who could claim a BLOOD RELATIONSHIP with the 12th-century great Mongol CHENGIZ KHAN", RANSACKED DELHI "VIRTUALLY WITHOUT ANY OPPOSITION"** — the Sultan **fled**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"Timur also **TOOK INDIAN ARTISANS — MASONS, STONE CUTTERS, CARPENTERS — whom he ENGAGED FOR RAISING BUILDINGS IN HIS CAPITAL SAMARKHAND**."*

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The artisan deportation is the detail to keep, because it is a form of plunder people forget: SKILL taken as loot.** ⭐ **Timur's Samarkand was built partly by craftsmen carried off from Delhi** — which is why the architectural vocabulary of Central Asia and north India stays in conversation for centuries afterwards. *(⭐ **And it runs the other way too: §8 below records **artisans arriving FROM WEST ASIA** so that *"the arch and dome began to show up with precision"*. **Craftsmen moved, forcibly and voluntarily, and the buildings record the traffic.**)*

> 📦 ⭐ **A nice Sayyid footnote worth remembering, from Abraham Eraly's *The Age of Wrath*:** *"In the entire history of the Delhi Sultanate there was **ONLY ONE SULTAN WHO VOLUNTARILY ABDICATED HIS THRONE** and moved to a small town away from Delhi, where he lived for **THREE FULL DECADES in contentment and peace**. He was **ALAM SHAH of the SAYYID dynasty**."*

## 6️⃣ Administration, economy and society — the delta ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⚠️ **[Class 7 Ch.3 §5–7](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/03_Delhi_12th_to_15th_Century.md) has the *iqta* system and its controls, the three taxes and the frontier problem. Take only what follows.**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The theory of the state** | *"Formally considered **an ISLAMIC STATE**. Most Sultans preferred to call themselves **the LIEUTENANT OF THE CALIPH**. ⭐ **In reality, however, the Sultans were THE SUPREME POLITICAL HEADS**"* — **commander-in-chief** and **highest court of appeal** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two claims of authority** | **BALBAN "claimed that he ruled as THE REPRESENTATIVE OF GOD ON EARTH."** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALA-UD-DIN KHALJI "claimed ABSOLUTE POWER, saying HE DID NOT CARE FOR THEOLOGICAL PRESCRIPTIONS, but DID WHAT WAS ESSENTIAL FOR THE GOOD OF THE STATE and the benefit of the people"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Extent** | *"The Delhi Sultanate **deserves to be considered AN ALL-INDIA EMPIRE**. Virtually all of India **EXCEPT KASHMIR AND KERALA** at the far ends… had come under the direct rule of Delhi towards the close of **Muhammad bin Tughlaq's** rule"* |
| ⚠️ **Succession** | *"There were **NO WELL-DEFINED AND ACCEPTED RULES OF ROYAL SUCCESSION**, and therefore **CONTESTED SUCCESSION BECAME THE NORM**"* |
| ⭐ ***KHALISA*** | **Areas retained under the Sultan's DIRECT control** — from whose revenue he paid **his own troops, the *HASHM-I QALB***; *iqta*-holders *(**MUQTIS** or **WALIS**)* maintained troops from **their own collections** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The tax rate and its victims** | **"The tax rent — SET AT HALF THE VALUE OF THE PRODUCE — was rigorously sought to be imposed over a very large area."** ⭐ **The claims of hereditary intermediaries — now called *CHAUDHURIS* — and of the village headmen *(KHOTS)* "were DRASTICALLY CURTAILED"** |
| ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The consequence** | *"The **continuous pressure for larger tax-realization provoked A SEVERE AGRARIAN UPRISING, notably in the DOAB near Delhi (1332–34)**"* — and **it was this, plus the ensuing famine, that persuaded Muhammad Tughlaq to attempt his agricultural-credit scheme** |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE HALF-OF-PRODUCE RATE IS THE NUMBER TO CARRY, BECAUSE IT MAKES SENSE OF EVERYTHING ELSE IN §5.4.** ⭐ **Compare across your notes: the Rigvedic *bhaga* was ONE-SIXTH; [the Guptas' *bhaga*](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) was ONE-SIXTH; [the Pallavas took one-sixth to one-tenth](03_South_Indian_Culture_and_Temples.md); ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ the Sultanate demanded ONE-HALF.** *That is a step-change in state extraction, and the Doab uprising of 1332–34 is what it cost.* *(⭐ **And it explains the sequence the textbook narrates: high demand → uprising → famine → the *Diwan-i-Amir Kohi* credit scheme. **Muhammad Tughlaq's famous "failed experiment" was an attempted repair of a crisis his own fiscal system had produced.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Saying that turns a list of eccentric schemes into a coherent argument.**)*

**💰 The economy — three genuinely new points:**

| Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Cash rent drove urbanisation** | *"**the payment of LAND TAX TO THE LEVEL OF RENT IN CASH**. Because of this, **FOOD-GRAINS AND OTHER RURAL PRODUCTS WERE DRAWN TO THE TOWNS**, thereby leading to **A NEW PHASE OF URBAN GROWTH**"* |
| ⭐ **The great cities** | *"In the fourteenth century, **DELHI and DAULATABAD emerged as GREAT CITIES OF THE WORLD**."* Others: **MULTAN, KARA, AWADH, GAUR, CAMBAY (Khambayat), GULBARGA, LAHORE, LAKHNAUTI, ANHILWARA** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Gold returns** | **"GOLD COINS, RARELY ISSUED IN INDIA AFTER THE COLLAPSE OF THE GUPTA EMPIRE, BEGAN TO APPEAR ONCE AGAIN, indicating the REVIVAL OF THE INDIAN ECONOMY."** *And the Sultans minted **gold and silver alongside copper from early in the thirteenth century**, indicating **brisk commerce*** |
| ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **But no guilds** | **"There is NO EVIDENCE OF THE EXISTENCE OF TRADE GUILDS, which had played a crucial role in the economy IN THE CLASSICAL AGE."** |
| ⭐ **Who traded what** | **JAIN MARWARIS, HINDU MULTANIS, MUSLIM BOHRAS, KHURASANIS, AFGHANS and IRANIANS.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"GUJARATIS and TAMILS dominated the SEA trade; HINDU MULTANIS and MUSLIM KHURASANIS, AFGHANS and IRANIANS dominated the OVERLAND trade with Central Asia"** |
| **Irfan Habib's view** | ⭐ *"Despite the Mongol conquests of the western borderlands, **India's EXTERNAL trade, BOTH OVERLAND AND OCEANIC, GREW CONSIDERABLY during this period**"* |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PUT THE GOLD AND THE GUILDS TOGETHER AND YOU HAVE A COMPLETE ECONOMIC ARGUMENT THAT SPANS FOUR OF YOUR FILES.** ⭐ **[Unit 7 told you](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) that *"the POST-GUPTA period saw A DECLINE IN THE CIRCULATION OF GOLD COINS"* — R.S. Sharma's sixth feudal feature. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This unit tells you that gold RETURNS under the Sultanate, "rarely issued since the collapse of the Gupta Empire."*** *So the numismatic curve is: **Gupta gold → post-Gupta collapse → Sultanate revival.*** ⭐ **But the guilds did NOT come back** — and [Unit 7 §17.3's *shreni*](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md), that self-governing corporate order with its own law, courts and banking, has no Sultanate successor. *(⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So the money economy revived while the CORPORATE INSTITUTIONS of the old commercial order did not. Trade came back under a different organisational form — individual merchant communities (Marwaris, Multanis, Bohras) rather than chartered guilds. That contrast, stated in two sentences, is a genuinely strong economic-history point and it is available to you only because you have both units.**)*

**🏭 Industrial expertise — four technology transfers:**

| Technology | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PAPER** | *"evolved by **the CHINESE** and learnt by **the ARABS**, introduced in India **during the rule of the Delhi Sultans**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SPINNING WHEEL** | *"invented by the **Chinese**, came to India **through IRAN in the FOURTEENTH century**, and **enabled the spinner to INCREASE HER OUTPUT SOME SIX-FOLD**"* |
| ⭐ **TREADLES in the loom** | *"similarly helped **SPEED UP WEAVING**"* |
| **SERICULTURE** | ⭐ *"a centre was established in **BENGAL by the FIFTEENTH century**"* |
| ⭐ **Building** | *"attained a new scale by the **large use of BRICK AND MORTAR**, and by the adoption of **VAULTING techniques**"* |

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"INCREASE *HER* OUTPUT SOME SIX-FOLD" — note the pronoun, the textbook chose it deliberately, and note the magnitude.** ⭐ **A six-fold productivity gain in yarn-spinning is an industrial-scale change, and the spinners were women.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **It is the technological precondition for the vast cotton-textile export trade that [Unit 6 saw beginning with Rome](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) and that the [Europeans came for](../../../Modern_India_Spectrum/) three centuries later.** *(⭐ **And the transmission route is worth stating: CHINA → the Arabs / Iran → India. The Sultanate's Central Asian connection was not only a military frontier; it was a technology pipeline.**)*

**🎓 Education — the *maktab* and the *madrasa*:**

⭐ **At the base, the *MAKTAB*** — a schoolmaster teaching children to read and write. **At a higher level, individual pupils read important texts with particular scholars who gave *DARS* (instruction).** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The *MADRASA*, "a more institutionalised form of higher education", became established in Central Asia and Iran in the ELEVENTH century and spread from there.** *Usually a building with **cells for resident students, a library and a mosque**.* ⭐ **FIRUZ TUGHLAQ built a large madrasa at Delhi "whose splendid building still stands"** — and ⚠️ **from Barani's description teaching there was "mainly confined to *QURAN-COMMENTARY, the PROPHET's SAYINGS and MUSLIM LAW (FIQH)*."** ⭐ **SIKANDER LODI appointed teachers in maktabs and madrasas throughout his dominions**, provided for by **land or cash grants**.

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The historiography point the textbook makes is worth taking: alongside the "secular sciences that came with Arabic and Persian learning", the notable addition was ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *SYSTEMATIC HISTORIOGRAPHY*.** *The **CHACHNAMA**'s collection of **witnesses' narratives and documents** "represents **advancement in historical research**", even if it lacks the coherence of later work like **Minhaj Siraj's *Tabaqat-i-Nasiri*, written at Delhi c. 1260.*** *(⭐ **Which is a nice irony to notice: the tradition that gave us the hyperbolic Persian chronicles Sunil Kumar warns about in §1 is the same tradition that introduced documentary history-writing to India. Both things are true.**)*

## 7️⃣ Society, Sufism and syncretic culture ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **[Theme 6](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/06_Theme6_Bhakti_Sufi_Traditions.md) is enormously better than this section on Sufism and bhakti. Take only the specifics below.**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The two Sufi orders** | *"In the 13th and 14th centuries, two most influential orders emerged: **the SUHRAWARDI, centred at MULTAN**, and **the CHISTI at DELHI and other places**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The pre-pantheistic phase** | **"The most famous Chishti Saint, SHAIK NIZAMUDDIN, offered a classical exposition of Sufism of the PRE-PANTHEISTIC phase in the conversations (1307–1322)"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **When it turned pantheistic** | *"Sufism **began to turn pantheistic only when the ideas of IBN AL-ARABI (d. 1240) began to gain influence**"* — first through the Persian poetry of ⭐ **JALAL-UD-DIN RUMI (1207–1273)** and **ABDUR RAHMAN JAMI (1414–1492)**, then through **ASHRAF JAHANGIR SIMNANI** in India *(early 15th c.)* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The parallel the textbook draws** | **"Significantly, this wave of QUALIFIED PANTHEISM began to dominate Indian Islamic thought ABOUT THE SAME TIME that the PANTHEISM OF SANKARACHARYA's school was attaining increasing influence within Vedic thought."** |
| ⭐ **Monotheism's shift** | *"Though a monotheistic trend in Hinduism had long existed — as in the **BHAGAVAD GITA**, as noted by Al-Beruni — **its PROXIMITY TO ISLAM did help to MOVE MONOTHEISM FROM PERIPHERY TO THE CENTRE**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The southern monotheists** | **The VIRASHAIVA / LINGAYAT sect of KARNATAKA, founded by BASAVA (13th c.)** — believed in **one God (PARASHIVA)**, ⭐ **caste distinctions DENIED, women given a better status, and Brahmans could no longer MONOPOLISE PRIESTHOOD**. ⭐ **A parallel movement in TAMIL NADU: the *SIDDHARS*, who "sang in Tamil of ONE GOD, and criticised CASTE, BRAHMANS and the doctrine of TRANSMIGRATION OF SOULS"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The transmitters north** | **"Two little known figures who played a part in TRANSMITTING THE SOUTHERN BHAKTI AND MONOTHEISM TO NORTHERN INDIA were NAMDEV of MAHARASHTRA — 'a rigorous monotheist who opposed IMAGE WORSHIP and CASTE DISTINCTIONS' — and RAMANAND, a follower of RAMANUJA."** |

## 8️⃣ Why the Marathas rose — three causes ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================

   ================================================================
   ** The region "consisted of a narrow strip of land called
      ** KONKAN ** . Its ** PRECIPITOUS MOUNTAINS, INACCESSIBLE
      VALLEYS AND IMPREGNABLE HILL-FORTS ** were MOST FAVOURABLE
      FOR MILITARY DEFENCE." **

   ** The Marathas "prided themselves on their ** LOYALTY,
      COURAGE, DISCIPLINE, CUNNINGNESS AND ENDURANCE ** ." **

   ⭐ ** THE APPRENTICESHIP: "They had earlier served under the
      ** BAHMANI SULTANS ** and later, after its disintegration,
      under the Sultans of ** AHMEDNAGAR, BIJAPUR, GOLCONDA,
      BIDAR and BERAR ** ." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE MILITARY METHOD, STATED PRECISELY: **
     ** "Marathas AVOIDED DIRECT BATTLES with the Mughal armies

       CANNONS ** . ' ** GUERRILLA WARFARE ** ' was their
       strength. They possessed the ability to plan and execute
       ** SURPRISE LIGHTNING ATTACKS AT NIGHT ** . Further, they

```

The textbook adds the organisational condition that makes it possible: **DEVOLVED TACTICAL AUTHORITY.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A Mughal army with cannon and heavy cavalry had to be commanded from the top; a Maratha detachment could improvise on the spot.** *That is not a shortage of discipline — **it is a different and harder discipline**, and it is precisely what a slow, centralised opponent cannot answer.* *(⭐ **Then note the inheritance: the Marathas learned soldiering IN THE SERVICE OF THE DECCAN SULTANATES. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Shivaji's father served Bijapur; the sultanates trained the men who would dismantle the empire that destroyed the sultanates. [Unit 12](../00_INDEX.md) is where the Bahmani half of that story sits.**)*

```
   ================================================================
   (b) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE BHAKTI MOVEMENT
   ================================================================

      EKNATH ** were the leading lights. ⭐ ** The hymns of the
      Bhakti saints were sung IN MARATHI, and they CREATED A

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT WORD IN THIS SECTION IS *"ADVISORY"*, AND MOST ANSWERS MISS IT ENTIRELY.** ⭐ **The *Ashta Pradhan* was **NOT a cabinet in the modern sense and NOT a check on the king** — *"its functions were ADVISORY."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Shivaji built a specialised, salaried, NON-HEREDITARY departmental administration while keeping ALL sovereignty in his own hands.** *(⭐ **And that makes the eighteenth century deeply ironic: **the ONE office in this advisory council — the PESHWA — went on to swallow the monarchy entirely.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Shivaji's rule that "NO OFFICE WAS TO BE HEREDITARY" (§10.2) was reversed by Shahu in 1713, the Peshwaship became hereditary under Balaji Vishwanath, and by the 1740s the Chhatrapati was a figurehead at Satara while the Peshwa ruled from Poona. The state was undone by the abandonment of its own founding administrative rule.** **That sentence is the spine of any Mains answer on Maratha administration.**)*
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the second rule is worth noticing: the *Nyayadhish* (justice) and *Pandit Rao* (religion) were EXEMPT from military command — the two offices whose independence would be compromised by a field career were the two kept out of the army.**

### 10.2 Provincial administration ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | ⭐ **The kingdom divided into FOUR PROVINCES, each under a VICEROY**; provinces divided into ***PRANTHS*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The three rules** | **(1) "The practice of granting *JAGIRS* WAS ABANDONED and ALL OFFICERS WERE PAID IN CASH."** **(2)** *"Even when the revenues of a place were assigned to an official, **HIS ONLY LINK WAS WITH THE INCOME GENERATED FROM THE PROPERTY. HE HAD NO CONTROL OVER THE PEOPLE ASSOCIATED WITH IT.**"* **(3)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"NO OFFICE WAS TO BE HEREDITARY."** |
| ⭐ **The fort** | *"**THE FORT WAS THE NERVE-CENTRE of the activities of the *pranth*.**"* |
| **The village** | ⭐ *"The lowest unit of the government was the village, in which **THE TRADITIONAL SYSTEM OF ADMINISTRATION PREVAILED**"* |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **RULE (2) IS THE SUBTLEST AND THE BEST — IT IS A DELIBERATE SEPARATION OF REVENUE FROM AUTHORITY.** ⭐ **An assignee got the INCOME of a place but *"NO CONTROL OVER THE PEOPLE"* in it.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is precisely the safeguard the [*iqta* system](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/03_Delhi_12th_to_15th_Century.md) tried and failed to sustain, and precisely what the [Gupta *uparikas* titled *maharaja*](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md) had already breached.** *A land assignment becomes a fief the moment the assignee acquires JURISDICTION over the people on it — Shivaji cut that link explicitly.* *(⭐ **Put rules (1), (2) and (3) together and you have an anti-feudal administrative charter: cash salaries, no jurisdiction, no heredity. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ And then note that the Peshwas reversed all three — jagirs "revived" under Balaji Vishwanath, the Peshwaship made hereditary, and offices AUCTIONED under Baji Rao II (§14). The comparison of Shivaji's system with the Peshwas' is a set TN question, and this is the axis on which to answer it.**)*

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *SARDESHMUKHI* JUSTIFICATION IS THE PART YOUR CLASS 7 NOTES DO NOT EXPLAIN, AND IT IS THE CLEVEREST THING IN SHIVAJI'S FISCAL SYSTEM.** ⭐ **[Class 7](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/08_Eighteenth_Century_Political_Formations.md) gives you the RATE — 9–10% — but not the CLAIM behind it.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Shivaji did not levy *sardeshmukhi* as a conqueror. He levied it as the *SARDESHMUKH* — the hereditary chief headman above all the local *deshmukhs* — which recast a war exaction as the collection of a CUSTOMARY HEREDITARY DUE.** *An indefensible demand became a legally shaped one.* *(⭐ **This matters because it tells you what kind of state he was building: not a foreign occupier taxing subject districts, but a claimant to LEGITIMATE TRADITIONAL OFFICE across the whole Deccan.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Same logic as the Yadava–Sisodia genealogy in §9 and the *Chhatrapati* coronation of 1674 — three separate manoeuvres, all converting POWER into RIGHT. That is the thesis of any good answer on Shivaji's state-building.**)*
>
> ⚠️ **Note the small discrepancy to hold: this unit says *sardeshmukhi* was **10%**; Class 7 says **9–10%**; and the textbook's own MCQ tests "15%" as the WRONG answer. Use 10% (or "9–10%") and you are safe either way.**

## 1️⃣2️⃣ The Peshwas, 1713–1818 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> 📦 ***PESHWA* is a PERSIAN word meaning "FOREMOST" or "the FIRST MINISTER."** ⭐ *"The Peshwa **was one of the *Ashta Pradhan*** of Shivaji. **This office was NOT A HEREDITARY ONE.** As the power and prestige of the king declined, the Peshwas rose to prominence. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The genius of BALAJI VISHWANATH (1713–1720) made the office of the Peshwa SUPREME AND HEREDITARY.** They **virtually controlled the whole administration, USURPING THE POWERS OF THE KING**, and were also **RECOGNIZED AS THE RELIGIOUS HEAD OF THE STATE**."*

| Peshwa | Achievements |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BALAJI VISWANATH** *(1713–1720)* | **Helped Shahu consolidate control after the civil war.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **KANHOJI ANGRE — "the most powerful NAVAL CHIEF on the western coast" — had supported Tara Bai during the civil war; "the Peshwa CONVINCED HIM OF THE COMMON DANGER FROM THE EUROPEANS and SECURED HIS LOYALTY TO SHAHU."** ⚠️ **"The practice of granting *JAGIRS* WAS REVIVED. And the OFFICE OF PESHWA WAS MADE HEREDITARY."** ⭐ *Built **naval bases at KONKAN, KHANDERI and VIJAYADURG*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BAJI RAO I** *(1720–1740)* | *"**Enhanced the POWER AND PRESTIGE of the Maratha Empire**"* — **defeated the NIZAM OF HYDERABAD, the Rajput Governor of MALWA and the Governor of GUJARAT**; ⭐ **freed BUNDELKHAND from the Mughals, "and for this the Marathas got ONE-THIRD OF THE TERRITORIES from its ruler."** ⭐ **Defeated and killed the commander-in-chief TRIMBAK RAO at the BATTLE OF DABHAI near BARODA in 1731**, and **assumed the office of commander-in-chief himself**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **TREATY OF WARNA (1731) — Sambhaji of Kolhapur forced to accept Shahu's sovereignty.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **1738 — THANA, SALSETTE and BASSEIN captured from the PORTUGUESE, "and they were DRIVEN OUT OF THE KONKAN COAST."** ⚠️ *At the same time **"the ENGLISH made FRIENDLY OVERTURES to the Marathas and got THE RIGHT TO FREE TRADE in the Deccan region"*** |
| ⭐ **BALAJI BAJI RAO** *(1740–1761)* | **Known as NANA SAHIB** — *"a **good administrator and an EXPERT IN HANDLING FINANCIAL MATTERS**."* Presided over **the CARNATIC expedition, the BATTLE OF UDGIR, and PANIPAT** *(below)*. ⚠️ **"The Peshwa DIED BROKEN HEARTED IN JUNE 1761"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MADHAV RAO I** *(1761–1772)* | Became Peshwa **under the regency of RAGHOBA**; *"tried to **REGAIN THE MARATHA POWER lost at Panipat**."* **1763 — a fierce battle with the Nizam**; ⭐ **1765–67 expeditions against HAIDER ALI of Mysore, successful; Haider recovered his territories, but MADHAV RAO REGAINED THEM IN 1772 and Haider was FORCED TO SIGN A HUMILIATING TREATY.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Reasserted control over the north — defeating the ROHILLAS, subjugating the RAJPUT states and JAT chiefs, and in 1771 BRINGING SHAH ALAM II BACK TO DELHI from British protection at Allahabad; "the Emperor CEDED KORA AND ALLAHABAD to them."** ⚠️ *"the **SUDDEN DEATH of the Peshwa in 1772** brought an end to his glorious career"* |
| **The last Peshwas** | **NARAYAN RAO** *(1772)* — **murdered the next year**; **SAWAI MADHAV RAO (MADHAV RAO II)** — *"his POSTHUMOUS son, proclaimed Peshwa **ON THE 40TH DAY OF HIS BIRTH**"*; then ⭐ **BAJI RAO II, son of Raghunath Rao — THE LAST PESHWA** |

**⚔️ THE CARNATIC EXPEDITION — the Tamil thread again:**

> ⭐ **CHANDA SAHIB**, son-in-law of the **Nawab of Arcot**, captured **Tiruchirappalli** and threatened **Thanjavur**; ⭐ **its Maratha ruler APPEALED TO SHAHU FOR HELP IN 1739**. **The Peshwa sent RAGHOJI BHONSLE (Shahu's brother-in-law)**, who ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **defeated and killed the Nawab of Arcot, DOST ALI, in 1740**; **Tiruchirappalli was captured and Chanda Sahib imprisoned.** ⚠️ *But as the Peshwa turned to Bundelkhand and Bengal, **MOHAMMED ALI, Dost Ali's successor, retook Arcot and recaptured Tiruchirappalli in 1743**. The Peshwa then sent his cousin **SADASIVA RAO** — *"although the authority of the Marathas was re-established, **TIRUCHIRAPPALLI COULD NOT BE REGAINED**."**

**🏆 THE BATTLE OF UDGIR, 1760** — after a **war of succession following the death of Nizam Asaf Jah in 1748**, the Peshwa **backed the eldest son** and sent **SADASIVA RAO**, who **won at UDGIR in 1760**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***"This success marked THE CLIMAX OF MARATHA MILITARY MIGHT."*** **The Peshwa took over BIJAPUR, AURANGABAD, DAULATABAD, AHMEDNAGAR and BURHANPUR.**

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Hold the dates together and the tragedy writes itself: the CLIMAX of Maratha military might was 1760. PANIPAT was 14 January 1761. Less than a year separates the high-water mark from the catastrophe.**

Panipat was a catastrophe, not an extinction — the Maratha state was ended by the BRITISH in 1818, not by Abdali in 1761.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Saying that is what separates a careful answer from a rhetorical one.**)*

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Maratha administration under the Peshwas ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This whole section is new — and the set TN question is *"COMPARE AND CONTRAST Shivaji's land-revenue administration with the Peshwas'."* Read it against §10 throughout.**

| Level | Structure |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The centre** | **THE PESHWA SECRETARIAT AT POONA** — dealing with *"the **revenues and expenditure of all the districts**, the **accounts submitted by village and district officials**, the **pay and rights of all grades of public servants**, and the **budgets under CIVIL, MILITARY AND RELIGIOUS heads**."* ⭐ **A DAILY REGISTER recorded all revenues, all grants and payments received from foreign territories** |
| **Provinces** | ⭐ Larger provinces under **provincial governors called *SAR-SUBAHDARS***; divisions termed ***SUBAHS* and *PRANTHS*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **District** | ***MAMLATDAR* and *KAMAVISTAR*** — the Peshwa's representatives, *"responsible for **EVERY BRANCH OF DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The check** | ***DESHMUKHS* and *DESHPANDES*** — district officers **"in charge of ACCOUNTS and to OBSERVE THE ACTIVITIES OF MAMLATDARS AND KAMAVISTARS."** ⭐ **"IT WAS A SYSTEM OF CHECKS AND BALANCES"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The anti-corruption device** | ***RASAD*** — *"In order to **PREVENT MISAPPROPRIATION OF PUBLIC MONEY**, the Maratha government collected **A HEAVY SUM (*RASAD*) FROM THE MAMLATDARS AND OTHER OFFICIALS**… **collected ON THEIR FIRST APPOINTMENT to a district**"* |
| ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The corruption** | **"IN BAJI RAO II's TIME, THESE OFFICES WERE AUCTIONED OFF."** ⚠️ *"The clerks and menials were **paid for 10 OR 11 MONTHS IN A YEAR**"* |
| **Village** | ⭐ **The *PATEL* — "the CHIEF VILLAGE OFFICER, responsible for REMITTING REVENUE COLLECTIONS to the centre."** ⚠️ ***"HE WAS NOT PAID BY THE GOVERNMENT. HIS POST WAS HEREDITARY."*** Helped by the ***KULKARNI*, accountant and record-keeper**. ⭐ **Hereditary village servants performed communal functions; carpenters, blacksmiths and other artisans gave *BEGAR* — COMPULSORY LABOUR** |
| ⭐ **Towns** | **The *KOTWAL*** — **maintenance of peace and order, REGULATION OF PRICES, settling civil disputes, sending monthly accounts.** *"He was the **head of the city police and also functioned as the MAGISTRATE**"* |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE *RASAD* IS A FASCINATING AND SELF-DEFEATING DEVICE, AND EXPLAINING WHY IS WORTH REAL MARKS.** ⭐ **To prevent embezzlement, the state took **a heavy payment from an official ON APPOINTMENT** — effectively a security deposit against future misconduct.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **But an officer who has PAID heavily to obtain a district must RECOVER that outlay from the district — so a measure designed to stop extortion creates a powerful incentive for it.** *(⭐ **And under Baji Rao II the pretence collapsed entirely: *"these offices were AUCTIONED OFF."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A deposit against corruption became an open sale of office — and the clerks were being paid for ten months of a twelve-month year, which guarantees that the shortfall was made up from the taxpayer.** **That is a complete miniature of how a fiscal system rots, and it is a much better answer than "the later Peshwas were weak."**)*

**💰 SOURCES OF REVENUE UNDER THE PESHWAS ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

| Source | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The great change** | **"The Peshwas GAVE UP THE SYSTEM OF SHARING THE PRODUCE of the agricultural land followed under Shivaji's rule. THE PESHWAS FOLLOWED THE SYSTEM OF *TAX FARMING*. Land was settled against A STIPULATED AMOUNT to be paid annually to the government."** |
| **Assessment** | ⭐ *"based on **A CAREFUL SURVEY**. Land was divided into **THREE CLASSES: according to the KINDS OF CROPS, FACILITIES FOR IRRIGATION, and PRODUCTIVITY**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Peasant right** | *"The villagers were **THE ORIGINAL SETTLERS WHO ACQUIRED THE FOREST. THEY COULD NOT BE DEPRIVED OF THEIR LANDS.** ⚠️ **But ONLY THE PATEL COULD REPRESENT THEIR RIGHTS to the higher authorities**"* |
| **Forests** | ⭐ **Permits on payment of a fee for CUTTING TREES and USING PASTURES**; revenue from the sale of **GRASS, BAMBOO, FUEL WOOD, HONEY** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **CHAUTH and SARDESHMUKHI** | See the split below |
| **Mints** | ⭐ *"**GOLDSMITHS were allowed to MINT COINS on payment of ROYALTY** and getting a **LICENSE**… They had to maintain a certain standard. **When it was found the standard was NOT being met, ALL PRIVATE MINTS WERE CLOSED IN 1760 and A CENTRAL MINT WAS ESTABLISHED**"* |
| ⭐ **Justice as revenue** | **"A fee of 25% was charged ON MONEY BONDS. FINES were collected from persons suspected or found guilty of ADULTERY."** ⚠️ **"BRAHMINS WERE EXEMPTED from duty on things imported for their own use"** |

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  HOW THE CHAUTH WAS DIVIDED -- A FOUR-WAY SPLIT,
              AND IT IS A PRELIMS ITEM
   ================================================================
     ** 25 % ** ....... for ** THE RULER **
     ** 66 % ** ....... for ** MARATHA OFFICIALS AND MILITARY
                        HEADS, for the MAINTENANCE OF TROOPS **
     **  6 % ** ....... for ** THE PANT SACHIV ** ( Chief,
                        "a BRAHMAN by birth" )
     **  3 % ** ....... for ** THE TAX COLLECTORS **
   ================================================================
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **LOOK AT WHAT THAT SPLIT ACTUALLY SAYS: THE RULER GOT A QUARTER, AND THE ARMY GOT TWO-THIRDS.** ⭐ **Sixty-six per cent of the *chauth* went to *"MARATHA OFFICIALS AND MILITARY HEADS FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF TROOPS"* — not to the treasury.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This is a war-financing system in which the men who collect the tribute are the men who keep most of it, which means every commander has a direct personal interest in CONTINUED EXPANSION.** *(⭐ **And that explains the strategic behaviour [Class 7 diagnoses](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_07_Our_Pasts_II/Notes/08_Eighteenth_Century_Political_Formations.md): the Marathas kept raiding rather than consolidating, and made tributaries rather than allies, because **the revenue system paid them to.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A structural explanation beats a moral one — the problem was not Maratha greed, it was an incentive design in which peace paid nobody.**)*

**📋 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE ELEVEN MISCELLANEOUS TAXES — the textbook's own list:**

> **1.** Tax on land held by **Deshmukhs and Deshpandes** · **2.** Tax on land kept for the **village Mahars** · **3.** Tax on **land irrigated by wells** · **4.** ⭐ **HOUSE TAX from all EXCEPT BRAHMINS and village officials** · **5.** Annual fee for the **testing of weights and measures** · **6.** ⚠️ **TAX ON THE RE-MARRIAGE OF WIDOWS** · **7.** Tax on **sheep and buffaloes** · **8.** **Pasture fee** · **9.** ⭐ **Tax on MELON CULTIVATION IN RIVER BEDS** · **10.** **Succession duty** · **11.** Duty on the **sale of horses**.
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the emergency levy:** *"When the Maratha government was in financial difficulty, it levied on all land-holders ***KURJA-PATTI* or *TASTI-PATTI*, A TAX EQUAL TO ONE YEAR'S INCOME OF THE TAX-PAYER.**"*

> ⚠️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two entries in that list are worth pausing on, and both belong in a GS1 Society answer.** ⭐ **A TAX ON THE RE-MARRIAGE OF WIDOWS is a fiscal disincentive to a practice the reformers of the next century would fight to legalise** *(the Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act, 1856)*. **The state was not neutral on the question; it was profiting from the obstacle.** ⭐ **And the **HOUSE TAX from all EXCEPT BRAHMINS**, alongside the Brahmin exemption from import duty, shows caste privilege written directly into the revenue code** — *the same structure as [the Pallavas exempting Brahmins from tax AND capital punishment](03_South_Indian_Culture_and_Temples.md) and [the Gupta *agrahara*](04_Post_Mauryan_and_Guptas.md).* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A thousand years apart, the same beneficiaries.**

**⚖️ Police, justice, army and navy:**

| Branch | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Police** | **Watchmen — "GENERALLY THE MAHARS" — in every village.** *When crime rose, government sent **irregular infantry**, and ⚠️ **"the RESIDENTS OF THE DISTURBED AREA HAD TO PAY AN ADDITIONAL HOUSE TAX"** to pay for them.* ⭐ **Baji Rao II appointed additional police officers**; in towns, magisterial and police powers went to the ***KOTWAL***, whose duties included **monitoring prices, taking a CENSUS of inhabitants, trying civil cases, SUPPLYING LABOUR to the government, and levying fees**, with a ***NAGARKA* or POLICE SUPERINTENDENT** below |

THERE WERE NO RULES OF PROCEDURE."** ⭐ ***ARBITRATION was given HIGH PRIORITY***; failing that, the case went to **a PANCHAYAT appointed by the PATEL in villages and by THE LEADING MERCHANTS in towns**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"The panchayat was A POWERFUL INSTITUTION. RE-TRIAL ALSO TOOK PLACE."** Appeals went to the **Mamlatdar**. ⭐ In **criminal** cases the hierarchy ran **RAJA CHHATRAPATI → PESHWA → DEPUTY SUBAHDAR → MAMLATDAR → PATEL**. ⚠️ **"FLOGGING AND TORTURE WERE INFLICTED TO EXTORT CONFESSION"** |

Shivaji had recruited soldiers LOCALLY from the Maratha region. BUT THE PESHWAS DRAFTED SOLDIERS FROM ALL PARTS OF INDIA AND FROM ALL SOCIAL GROUPS"** — **ARABS, ABYSSINIANS, RAJPUTS, ROHILLAS and SIKHS.** ⚠️ *"The Peshwa's army comprised **MERCENARIES OF THE FEUDAL CHIEFTAINS**. As the fiefs of rival chiefs were in the same area, **there were LOTS OF INTERNAL DISPUTES. IT AFFECTED THE SOLIDARITY of the people of the Maratha state**"* |
| ⭐ **Cavalry** | *"naturally the main strength. **Every JAGIRDAR had to bring A STIPULATED NUMBER OF HORSEMEN for a GENERAL MUSTER, EVERY YEAR.** Horsemen were divided into **THREE CLASSES based on THE QUALITY OF THE HORSES they kept**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Infantry and artillery** | *"**The Marathas PREFERRED TO SERVE IN THE CAVALRY.** So men for infantry were recruited from other parts of the country. ⚠️ **The ARABS, ROHILLAS, SIKHS and SINDHIS in the Maratha infantry were PAID A HIGHER SALARY compared to the Maratha soldiers.** ⭐ **The Maratha artillery was manned MOSTLY BY THE PORTUGUESE AND INDIAN CHRISTIANS. Later on, the ENGLISH were also recruited**"* |
| **Navy** | ⭐ *"built for **guarding the Maratha ports, CHECKING PIRACY, and COLLECTING CUSTOMS DUTIES** from incoming and outgoing ships."* **BALAJI VISHWANATH built naval bases at KONKAN, KHANDERI and VIJAYADURG**, with **dockyard facilities** |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SHIVAJI-VERSUS-PESHWA COMPARISON IS THE SET QUESTION, AND THIS TABLE IS YOUR ANSWER. EVERY SINGLE ONE OF SHIVAJI'S PRINCIPLES WAS REVERSED.**
>
> | Shivaji's rule *(§10)* | The Peshwas' practice *(§14)* |
> |---|---|
> | ⭐ ***JAGIRS* ABANDONED**; all officers **paid in cash** | ⚠️ ***JAGIRS* REVIVED** under Balaji Vishwanath |
> | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **NO OFFICE HEREDITARY** | ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The PESHWASHIP ITSELF made HEREDITARY**; the *Patel*'s post hereditary; ⚠️ **offices AUCTIONED under Baji Rao II** |
> | ⭐ **Revenue = a SHARE OF PRODUCE** *(30%, later 40%)*, assessed on survey | ⚠️ ***TAX FARMING*** — a stipulated annual sum |
> | ⭐ **Assignees had income but NO CONTROL OVER PEOPLE** | ⚠️ **Fief-holding chieftains with their own mercenary contingents** |
> | ⭐ **Soldiers recruited LOCALLY, salaried, in a STANDING army** | ⚠️ **Drafted from ALL INDIA; foreign infantry PAID MORE than Marathas; the army "comprised MERCENARIES OF THE FEUDAL CHIEFTAINS"** |

## 1️⃣5️⃣ The three Anglo-Maratha Wars ⭐⭐⭐⭐

> ⚠️ ⭐ **Boundary note: this is where Unit 15 crosses into [Spectrum's](../../../Modern_India_Spectrum/) territory. Take the MARATHA side and the treaty names; leave Wellesley's system and the Company's wider policy to Spectrum.**

| War | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **FIRST (1775–1782)** | **MADHAV RAO NARAYAN was an infant Peshwa under the regency of NANA FADNAVIS.** ⭐ *"The **USURPING OF POWER BY RAGUNATH RAO**, uncle of the former Peshwa Madhava Rao I, **provided the scope for the Company administration TO FISH IN THE TROUBLED WATERS**."* **The Bombay administration supported Ragunath Rao IN RETURN FOR SALSETTE AND BASSEIN.** As **Mahadaji Scindia and the Bhonsle of Nagpur turned pro-British**, the Marathas had to concede **THANE and SALSETTE**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE TREATY OF SALBAI, 1782 — Ragunath Rao PENSIONED OFF; "peace prevailed between the Company and the Marathas FOR ABOUT TWO DECADES"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SECOND (1803–1806)** | **The death of NANA FADNAVIS "resulted in a SCRAMBLE for his huge possessions."** **Peshwa BAJI RAO II was DETHRONED** and *"had to accept the help of the British."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **WELLESLEY forced the SUBSIDIARY ALLIANCE on the Peshwa — THE TREATY OF BASSEIN, 1802**, under which *"the territory to be ceded should fetch **an income of Rs. 26 LAKHS**."* ⚠️ **"The leading Maratha States regarded the treaty AS HUMILIATING and hence decided to DEFY IT"** — hence the war. *"In spite of the brave resistance… the Maratha leaders were **completely routed**. The Subsidiary Alliance was accepted."* ⭐ **The British got the DOAB, AHMEDNAGAR, BROACH and all the hilly regions** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIRD (1817–1818)** | **Baji Rao II became anti-British after GANGADHAR SASTRI, prime minister of the GAIKWAR of Baroda, was killed by TRIMBAKJI, a favourite of the Peshwa.** ⭐ **At the instance of the Resident at Poona, MOUNTSTUART ELPHINSTONE, Trimbakji was imprisoned — and ESCAPED WITH THE PESHWA's ASSISTANCE.** The Peshwa was also charged with **creating the MARATHA CONFEDERACY and plotting with SCINDIA, BHONSLE and HOLKAR**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE TREATY OF POONA, 1817: the Peshwa RESIGNED THE HEADSHIP OF THE MARATHA CONFEDERACY, CEDED KONKAN, and RECOGNISED THE INDEPENDENCE OF THE GAIKWAR.** ⚠️ *Baji Rao then **BURNT DOWN THE POONA RESIDENCY** while the British were busy with the **PINDARIS**; **General SMITH captured Poona and then Satara, defeating his forces at ASHTA, KIRKEE and KORGAON**; ⭐ **Baji Rao SURRENDERED TO ELPHINSTONE IN 1818*** |

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE OUTCOME OF THE THIRD WAR -- FIVE POINTS, AND
              THIS IS THE END OF THE MARATHA STATE
   ================================================================
   ** (1) "The British ABOLISHED THE ** PESHWAI ** (the office of
       the Peshwa) and ** ANNEXED ALL THE PESHWA'S DOMINIONS ** .
       ⚠️ BUT ** THE JAGIRS OF THE FIEF HOLDERS WERE
       RESTORED. ** " **

   ** (2) ** BAJI RAO II remained a PRISONER with an ANNUAL
       PENSION until his death in 1851. ** **

   ** (3) ** PRATAP SINGH, a descendant of Shivaji, was made king
       of a SMALL KINGDOM CARVED AROUND SATARA. ** **

   ** (4) ** THE MARATHA CONFEDERACY organised by BAJI RAO I --
       comprising BHONSLE, HOLKAR and SCINDIA -- WAS

```

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **POINT (1) CONTAINS THE WHOLE LOGIC OF COMPANY EXPANSION IN ONE CLAUSE, AND IT IS EASY TO READ PAST: the Peshwai was abolished, but *"THE JAGIRS OF THE FIEF HOLDERS WERE RESTORED."*** ⭐ **The British destroyed the CENTRAL authority and PRESERVED the LOCAL landholders.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is the standard method — remove the sovereign who can coordinate resistance, keep the intermediaries who collect the revenue and have no interest in fighting.** *(⭐ **And point (5) is worth a moment too: the **RESIDENT** at Poona became **GOVERNOR of Bombay**. The diplomatic post that had spent years working inside Maratha politics converted directly into the administrative post that governed the result. ⭐ **The Residency system was a career path into annexation, and Elphinstone is the case in point.** **Spectrum will develop both of these; note them here and cross-link when you write it.**)*

## 1️⃣6️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Maratha rule in Tamil Nadu — and SERFOJI II

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **This section is a COMPLETE GAP in your notes — nothing in any NCERT file touches it. And SERFOJI II is one of the best single examples available to you for any question on INDIAN INITIATIVE IN MODERN EDUCATION, because he anticipates both the missionaries and the colonial state.**

### 16.1 How the Marathas came to Thanjavur ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The *NAYANKARA* system** | **KRISHNA DEVARAYA (r. 1509–1529)** developed it. *"the Tamil country was divided into **THREE LARGE NAYANKARAS: SENJI, THANJAVUR and MADURAI**. Under the new system the subordinate chieftains were designated ***PALAYAKKARARS* and their fiefdom *PALAYAMS*"* |
| ⭐ **Thanjavur's descent** | *"Thanjavur, which remained **a part of the CHOLA territories first and then of the PANDYA kingdom**, became **a VASSAL STATE OF THE MADURAI SULTANATE**, from which it passed into the hands of the **NAYAKS**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The eclipse** | *"The **RIVALRY BETWEEN THE NAYAKS OF MADURAI AND THANJAVUR** finally led to the eclipse of Nayak rule of Thanjavur **in 1673**. Troops from **BIJAPUR, led by the Maratha general VENKOJI**, defeated the Nayak of Madurai and captured Thanjavur"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **1676** | **"VENKOJI CROWNED HIMSELF KING, and MARATHA RULE BEGAN IN THANJAVUR."** ⭐ *When **Shivaji invaded the Carnatic in 1677 he removed Venkoji and placed his half-brother SANTAJI on the throne — but VENKOJI RECAPTURED Thanjavur**, and after his death his son **SHAHJI** became ruler* |
| **The line** | Shahji *(no heir)* → **SERFOJI I** *(1712–1728, sixteen years)* → **TUKKOJI** → **PRATAP SINGH** *(1739–1763)* → **THULJAJI** *(to 1787)* → ⭐ **SERFOJI II, crowned AGED 10**, with Thuljaji's brother **AMARSINGH** as Regent |
| ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The British move** | *"**Disputing this succession, the English THRUST AN AGREEMENT ON SERFOJI II, according to which the latter was FORCED TO CEDE THE ADMINISTRATION OF THE KINGDOM TO THE BRITISH.** Serfoji II was **the LAST RULER of the BHONSLE dynasty of the Maratha principality of Thanjavur**"* |

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **RAJA DESINGHU — the box, and it is a set TN question.** *The Maratha king **RAJARAM**, fleeing Raigarh, **took asylum in SENJI**; Mughal forces under **ZULFIKAR KHAN** and then **DAUD KHAN** took it. ⭐ **A BUNDELA RAJPUT chieftain, SWARUP SINGH, was employed as *KILADAR* (fort commandant) of Senji in 1700** and gained control of the whole of Senji. **After his death in 1714 his son TEJ SINGH (DESINGHU) assumed the governorship.** ⚠️ **Desinghu REFUSED TO PAY TRIBUTE to the Mughal emperor and invited the wrath of Nawab SADAT-UL-LAH KHAN. In the ensuing battle RAJA DESINGHU, ONLY TWENTY-TWO YEARS OLD, WAS KILLED. His young wife COMMITTED SATI.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"The gallantry displayed by the daring Rajput youth against the Nawab is IMMORTALIZED IN MANY POPULAR BALLADS IN TAMIL."***
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The last line is the interesting one: a BUNDELA RAJPUT boy, killed defending a fort in the Tamil country, becomes the hero of TAMIL FOLK BALLADS.** ⭐ *Popular memory does not sort its heroes by region or language — it sorts them by story.* *(⭐ **And it is a reminder of how thoroughly mixed the Deccan and Tamil political world had become by 1714: a Maratha king sheltering at a Rajput-commanded fort in Tamil country, besieged by Mughal generals, remembered in Tamil verse.**)*

### 16.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ SERFOJI II (r. 1798–1832) — the scholar king

| Field | What he did |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Education and languages** | *"Taught by the **GERMAN MISSIONARY FRIEDRICH SCHWARTZ**, he had **MASTERED SEVERAL EUROPEAN LANGUAGES**. His library contained **important books in EVERY BRANCH OF LEARNING**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Printing** | **Established a PRINTING PRESS for MARATHI and SANSKRIT** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The library** | **Substantive expansion of the SARASWATI MAHAL LIBRARY** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Medicine** | **Established the *DHANVANTARI MAHAL*, "a RESEARCH INSTITUTION that produced HERBAL MEDICINE FOR HUMANS AND ANIMALS."** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"MAINTAINING CASE-SHEETS OF PATIENTS WAS INTRODUCED."** *Physicians of **MODERN MEDICINE, AYURVEDA, UNANI and SIDDHA** undertook research on drugs and herbs and **produced EIGHTEEN VOLUMES of research material**. ⭐ He also **catalogued the important herbs in the form of EXQUISITE HAND PAINTINGS*** |
| ⭐ **Literature** | Authored ***KUMARASAMBHAVA CHAMPU*, *DEVENDRA KURAVANJI*, and *MUDRARAKSHASCHAYA*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Music** | **"INTRODUCED WESTERN MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS LIKE THE CLARINET AND VIOLIN INTO CARNATIC MUSIC"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Painting** | **"Credited with POPULARISING THE UNIQUE THANJAVUR STYLE OF PAINTING"** |
| ⭐ **Other interests** | *"painting, **gardening, COIN-COLLECTING, MARTIAL ARTS**", and he patronised **chariot-racing, hunting and bull-fighting**.* ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"He created THE FIRST ZOOLOGICAL GARDEN IN TAMILNADU in the Thanjavur palace premises"** |

**🎓 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE EDUCATION STORY — and this is the part that makes him a first-class Mains example:**

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE CHRONOLOGY THAT MAKES THE ARGUMENT
   ================================================================

   ** 1707 ** -- "from the start, the ** GERMAN MISSIONARIES ** had
      run several ** FREE VERNACULAR AND ENGLISH SCHOOLS ** in
      the southern provinces."

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** 1803 ** -- ** SERFOJI II establishes AT THANJAVUR
      "THE FIRST MODERN PUBLIC SCHOOL FOR NON-CHRISTIAN
      NATIVES." **

   ** 1812 ** -- the missionary scholar ** C.S. JOHN ** of
      ** TRANQUEBAR ** submits a project to the English colonial
      government ** "urging it to sponsor FREE SCHOOLS FOR INDIAN
      CHILDREN, for instruction in TAMIL AND ENGLISH" ** --
      ⭐ "at a time when ** ENGLISH EDUCATION WAS NOT AVAILABLE TO
      NON-CHRISTIAN INDIANS ** ."

   ** 1820s ** -- ** THOMAS MUNRO ** , Governor of Madras,
      ** PROPOSES a scheme for elementary public schools ** .

   ⚠️ ** 1841 ** -- "the Company government ** DID NOT ESTABLISH A
      MODERN SCHOOL FOR NATIVES IN MADRAS TILL 1841 ** ."

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE TEXTBOOK'S CONCLUSION: "SERFOJI II WAS IN
      ADVANCE OF BOTH THE MISSIONARY AND THE COLONIAL STATE." **
   ================================================================
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIRTY-EIGHT YEARS. SERFOJI OPENED HIS SCHOOL IN 1803; THE COMPANY OPENED ITS FIRST IN MADRAS IN 1841. THAT GAP IS THE ANSWER TO A WHOLE FAMILY OF QUESTIONS.** ⭐ **The standard narrative of modern education in India runs: missionaries → Macaulay's Minute (1835) → the Wood's Despatch (1854) → a colonial system.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Serfoji breaks that narrative at its start — an Indian ruler establishing free, modern, public schooling for non-Christian natives BEFORE the colonial state did, and before Macaulay wrote a word.** *(⭐ **And the textbook is careful to say what was NEW about it, because Indian rulers had always endowed learning: *"While Indian rulers OFTEN ENDOWED EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS OF HIGHER LEARNING, THEY DID NOT ESTABLISH ELEMENTARY SCHOOLS, NOR DID THEY ADMINISTER ANY SCHOOLS OR COLLEGES."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The innovation is not patronage — it is the STATE ADMINISTRATION OF ELEMENTARY SCHOOLING. Say that precisely and the example lands.**)*

**📊 THE EVIDENCE — and it is quantitative, which is what makes it usable:**

| Source | Figures |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The 1823 report for Governor Munro's CENSUS OF EDUCATION** | *"**21 OF THE 44 FREE SCHOOLS in the wider Thanjavur district were RUN BY SERFOJI'S GOVERNMENT, 19 BY THE MISSIONARIES, ONE BY A TEMPLE.** There were **three schools run by teachers themselves free of cost**. ⭐ **In the state-run free schools Serfoji MADE MODERN EDUCATION AVAILABLE TO ALL**"* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MUKTAMBAL CHATTIRAM, 1822** | *"at the free school in Muktambal Chattiram — **the king's favourite almshouse, established in 1803** — **15 TEACHERS TAUGHT A TOTAL OF 464 STUDENTS OF DIVERSE CASTES, IN TWO CLASSES, IN THE MORNING AND IN THE EVENING**"* |
| ⭐ **The range of schools** | *"**free ELEMENTARY and SECONDARY schools for ORPHANS AND THE POOR** in Thanjavur city and adjacent places… **schools for ALL LEVELS, CHARITY SCHOOLS, COLLEGES and *PADASHALAS* for SANSKRIT HIGHER LEARNING**", catering to **"the COURT ELITES, VEDIC SCHOLARS, ORPHANS AND THE POOR"*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The curricular innovation** | ***NAVAVIDYA*** — **"'MODERN' or 'NEW' LEARNING", introduced in the STATE-RUN SCHOOLS** |
| ⭐ **And across the line** | **"Serfoji ALSO SUPPORTED A FREE SCHOOL FOR NEEDY CHRISTIANS, run by missionaries in the village of KANNANDANGUDI"** |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE MUKTAMBAL FIGURES ARE THE ONES TO QUOTE — *"464 STUDENTS OF DIVERSE CASTES, IN TWO CLASSES, MORNING AND EVENING"* — BECAUSE EVERY WORD IS DOING WORK.** ⭐ ***"DIVERSE CASTES"*** in a court-sponsored school in 1822 is a genuinely significant social fact. ⭐ ***"MORNING AND EVENING"*** means **two shifts** — a school designed around children who WORKED, which is a deliberate accommodation of the poor. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And a HINDU king funding a MISSIONARY-RUN school for needy Christians at Kannandangudi, while a German missionary had taught him and another was his collaborator, describes a working relationship across the religious divide that neither the missionary nor the nationalist account of this period leads you to expect.**

**🏛️ ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE SARASWATI MAHAL LIBRARY — the box:**

> *"Built by the **NAYAK rulers** and **enriched by SERFOJI II**, it contains **a record of the DAY-TO-DAY PROCEEDINGS OF THE MARATHA COURT — as *MODI* DOCUMENTS** — and **FRENCH–MARATHA CORRESPONDENCE of the 18th century**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***MODI* was the SCRIPT used to write the MARATHI language.** It is a **treasure house of rare manuscripts and books in many languages**."*

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The *MODI* script detail is a Prelims-grade fact and a research point at once: a Marathi-language, Modi-script archive of court proceedings, sitting in a Tamil town, alongside 18th-century French correspondence.** *(⭐ **The Saraswati Mahal is one of the oldest continuously functioning libraries in Asia and is today a designated manuscript resource centre. **For a question on "sources for regional history", it is a better example than anything in your other files** — a working archive, in a script most Marathi speakers can no longer read, documenting a Maratha state in the Tamil country.**)*

**🎓 THE AFTERLIFE — why Serfoji's schooling mattered politically:**

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"Serfoji's strategic initiatives in modern education **ENABLED THE THANJAVUR COURT ELITE AND SUBJECTS TO ENTER AND BENEFIT FROM THE EMERGING COLONIAL SOCIAL AND ECONOMIC ORDER.** ⭐ **The court officials, MOSTLY BRAHMINS, trained in EUROPEAN KNOWLEDGE, TECHNOLOGIES AND ARTS, became LEADING AGENTS OF COLONIAL MODERNITY — equal to the English-educated *DUBASHES*, writers and interpreters, both Hindu and Christian, who MEDIATED BETWEEN THE EUROPEANS AND INDIAN COURTS.** Two of Serfoji's pandits — **one of them KOTTAIYUR SIVAKOLUNDU DESIGAR** — **joined the Company's COLLEGE OF FORT ST. GEORGE and became LEADERS IN TRANSLATION AND PRINT CULTURE**."*
>
> ⭐ **And the textbook's closing verdict:** *"The careers and projects of Serfoji and John **illuminate the important roles that ENTERPRISING INDIVIDUALS, AND SMALL PLACES — such as a DANISH-TAMIL FISHING VILLAGE and a MARATHA-TAMIL PRINCIPALITY — played in the history of change in colonial Tamilnadu.**"*

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THAT PARAGRAPH IS A COMPLETE, SOPHISTICATED AND SLIGHTLY UNCOMFORTABLE ARGUMENT, AND YOU SHOULD REPRODUCE ITS COMPLEXITY RATHER THAN FLATTENING IT.** ⭐ **Serfoji's modern schools produced men who staffed the COLONIAL order — *"leading agents of colonial modernity"*, teaching at Fort St. George, driving translation and print.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So Indian educational initiative did not resist colonial modernity; it EQUIPPED a class to participate in it — and, the textbook notes, that class was *"MOSTLY BRAHMINS."*** *(⭐ **Two things follow, and a good answer holds both: the achievement is real — free elementary schooling for 464 children of diverse castes, decades ahead of the state — AND its long-run beneficiaries were disproportionately the already-privileged, which is the seed of the very question that would dominate twentieth-century Tamil politics.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Note the phrase *"a DANISH-TAMIL FISHING VILLAGE"* — that is TRANQUEBAR, C.S. John's base, and the textbook's point is that the decisive innovations came from SMALL PLACES AND INDIVIDUALS, not from imperial policy. That is an argument about how historical change actually happens, and it is worth borrowing.**)*

> ⭐ **The death, and it is a good closing line for an answer:** *Serfoji II **died on 7 March 1832 after almost forty years of rule**. *"His death was mourned throughout the kingdom and his funeral procession was attended by **MORE THAN 90,000 PEOPLE**."* ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **At his funeral, Rev. Bishop HEBER observed: *"I have seen many crowned heads, but NO ONE WHOSE DEPORTMENT WAS MORE PRINCELY."***

## 🔁 Recap — the two units in twelve lines

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **There is NO CONSENSUS on the nature of the Sultanate** — the personality school, the composite-culture school and the class-relations school all disagree — and **SUNIL KUMAR's warning is that hyperbolic Persian chronicles were *"UNCRITICALLY APPROPRIATED INTO MODERN SCHOLARSHIP."***
2. ⭐ **Islam reached the MALABAR COAST by SEA, through trade and marriage — the *MAPPILLAIS*, "sons-in-law" — long before any army came.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The 712 conquest of SIND by the seventeen-year-old MUHAMMAD BIN QASIM, sent by HAJJAJ of Iraq against DAHAR, was *"A TRIUMPH WITHOUT RESULTS"* — three centuries of quiet followed.**
3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **MAHMUD OF GHAZNI raided SEVENTEEN times in thirty-two years — but his booty came *"ALIKE FROM HINDU KINGDOMS IN INDIA AND MUSLIM KINGDOMS IN IRAN."*** ⭐ **The raids paid a permanent professional army; ROMILA THAPAR notes the *"CURIOUS SILENCE"* of Hindu and Jain sources on Somnath (1025); and the textbook says communal character *"was ATTRIBUTED TO THEM LATER."***
4. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The GHURIDS differed from the GHAZNAVIDS in one decisive way: GARRISON TOWNS instead of raids** — permanent centres that drew mercenaries who then ran the administration. **PRITHVIRAJ won TARAIN I (1191) and lost TARAIN II (1192) because he read the Ghurids through the Ghaznavid template — *"believing this battle to be A FRONTIER FIGHT."***
5. ⭐ **The Rajputs lost not on weapons or courage but on DISCIPLINE, TACTICS, CAVALRY and MOUNTED ARCHERY** — their **war elephants *"SPECTACULAR BUT SLOW MOVING."***
6. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ILTUTMISH's greatest act was a REFUSAL — declining to help Jalaluddin Khwarizmi against Chinggis Khan, because *"HAD HE SUPPORTED JALALUDDIN, THE MONGOLS WOULD HAVE OVERRUN INDIA WITH EASE."*** ⭐ **He created the *CHAHALGANI*; BALBAN abolished it.**
7. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ALAUDDIN's market control was MILITARY policy in fiscal disguise** — the first Sultan to pay cash wages needed low prices, hence the spies and the daily reports. ⭐ **MUHAMMAD BIN TUGHLAQ's token currency failed not because it was "ahead of its time" — China and Iran had done it — but because *"FAKE COINS… COULD NOT BE PREVENTED."*** ⭐ **And FIRUZ weakened the Sultanate by kindness: hereditary office restored, and four rulers in the six years after his death.**
8. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Four items in Unit 10 cut against a confessional reading of the period: Mahmud sacking RAY, Balban slaughtering the MEOS, TAMIL MUSLIMS fighting for the PANDYAS against Malik Kafur, and the SANSKRIT PALAM BAOLI inscription of 1276 saying that under Balban *"GOD VISHNU SLEEPS IN PEACE IN THE OCEAN OF MILK."***
9. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Marathas rose from KONKAN's terrain, a BHAKTI movement whose saints came *"chiefly from THE LOWER ORDER"* (Ranade), and the collapse of Bijapur and Golconda** — and their method was guerrilla war with **tactical initiative devolved *"WITHOUT WAITING FOR ORDERS FROM A SUPERIOR OFFICER."***
10. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SHIVAJI's state rested on three rules: NO *JAGIRS* — cash salaries; assignees got INCOME BUT NO CONTROL OVER PEOPLE; and NO OFFICE HEREDITARY.** ⭐ **The *ASHTA PRADHAN* was ADVISORY; revenue was 30% of gross produce rising to 40%; *SARDESHMUKHI* was claimed as a HEREDITARY RIGHT rather than a conqueror's levy; and he was crowned *CHHATRAPATI* at Raigarh on 6 JUNE 1674.**
11. ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Peshwas REVERSED every one of those rules** — *jagirs* revived, the Peshwaship made hereditary, offices auctioned under Baji Rao II, share-of-produce replaced by TAX FARMING, and an army of *"MERCENARIES OF THE FEUDAL CHIEFTAINS"* in which foreign infantry was **paid more than Marathas**. ⭐ **Two-thirds of the *chauth* went to the military commanders who collected it — an incentive system in which peace paid nobody.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PANIPAT (14 January 1761) was lost to isolation, to the Peshwa's *"UNDUE INTERESTS IN DELHI"*, and to an army that waited until *"the SCARCITY OF FOOD BECAME ACUTE"* — but the Marathas recovered within a decade, and it was the British in 1818, not Abdali in 1761, who ended them.**
12. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And in the far south, VENKOJI's 1676 conquest produced a Maratha Thanjavur that lasted to 1832 — where SERFOJI II founded the FIRST MODERN PUBLIC SCHOOL FOR NON-CHRISTIAN NATIVES IN 1803, thirty-eight years before the Company opened one in Madras.** ⭐ **By 1823, **21 of the 44 free schools** in the district were his; at Muktambal Chattiram **15 teachers taught 464 students "OF DIVERSE CASTES" in morning and evening shifts.** ⚠️ **And his graduates, *"MOSTLY BRAHMINS"*, became *"LEADING AGENTS OF COLONIAL MODERNITY."***
