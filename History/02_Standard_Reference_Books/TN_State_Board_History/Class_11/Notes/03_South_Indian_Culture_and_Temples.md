> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE KURAM COPPER PLATE — read it as PROPAGANDA, not as a chronicle. It is a wonderful specimen of the genre.** *Parameshwaravarman's plate describes his grandfather **Narasimhavarman** as one *"who proved **a lion to the elephant-herd of hostile kings**"*, who *"**repeatedly defeated the CHOLAS, KERALAS, KALABHRAS, and PANDYAS**"*, ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ who *"**WROTE THE THREE SYLLABLES OF *VIJAYA* (VICTORY), AS ON A PLATE, ON PULIKESIN'S BACK**"* — i.e. **made him turn and flee** — in the battles of **Pariyala, Manimangala and Suramara**; *"and who **DESTROYED THE CITY OF VATAPI, just as the pitcher-born (AGASTYA) destroyed the demon VATAPI**."*
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Agastya pun is the whole point: the sage Agastya swallowed a demon named *Vatapi*, and the Pallava king destroyed a city named *Vatapi*.** ⭐ **A military campaign is being written into myth by a play on a name** — which is exactly what the *[Ain](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/08_Theme8_Peasants_Zamindars_and_the_State.md)*, the [Mughal chronicles](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/09_Supplement_Kings_and_Chronicles_The_Mughal_Courts.md) and the [Sangam praise-poems](02_Sangam_Age.md) all do. *(⭐ **And note the symmetry: the Chalukyas' own AIHOLE inscription is a *prashasti* by a court poet doing the same job in the opposite direction. Two dynasties, two commissioned poets, one war.**)*

## 2️⃣ The Chalukyas of Badami ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ⚠️ ** FIRST, THE DISAMBIGUATION: there are TWO Chalukya
      families -- ** CHALUKYAS OF BADAMI (VATAPI) ** and
      ** CHALUKYAS OF KALYANI ** . This unit is BADAMI only. **
      *(The Kalyani Chalukyas are the ones the [Cholas fought](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md).)*

   ================================================================
   ** THE LINE **
   ================================================================
   ** PULIKESIN I (c. 543-566) ** -- the founder; ⭐ "fortified a
      hill near BADAMI" and ** DECLARED INDEPENDENCE FROM THE
      KADAMBAS ** ; conducted yagnas and the ** ASVAMEDHA ** .
   ** KIRTIVARMAN (566-597) ** -- ⭐ ** FOUNDED THE CAPITAL,
      BADAMI ** .
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** PULIKESIN II (609-642) ** -- grandson of Pulikesin I;
      "after defeating ** MANGALESHA ** , proclaimed himself king,
      an event described in the ** AIHOLE INSCRIPTION ** ."

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** PULIKESIN II'S RECORD -- the famous one first **
   ================================================================
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "One of the most outstanding victories… was the
     DEFEAT OF ** HARSHAVARDHANA'S ARMY ON THE BANKS OF THE
     NARMADA ** ." **
   * ** "The kings of MALWA, KALINGA and EASTERN DECCAN accepted
     his suzerainty." **
   * ** Victories over the ** KADAMBAS OF BANVASI ** and the
     ** GANGAS OF TALAKAD (MYSORE) ** . **
   * ⚠️ ** "However, his attempt to attack KANCHIPURAM was
     THWARTED BY MAHENDRAVARMA PALLAVA. This led to a PROLONGED
     WAR between the Chalukyas and the Pallavas." **

   ================================================================
   ** AND THE REVENGE **
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "NARASIMHA VARMAN I (630-668), the Pallava king,
      ATTACKED AND OCCUPIED BADAMI. ** PULIKESIN II DIED IN THE
      BATTLE. ** Pallava control over Badami and the southern
      parts of the Chalukya empire continued for several
      years." **
   ** "In the MID-EIGHTH CENTURY, the Badami Chalukyas were
      overpowered and replaced by the ** RASHTRAKUTAS ** ." **
```

> 📦 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE AIHOLE INSCRIPTION — a complete Prelims item, so learn all six facts.** *It is on the **eastern wall of the MEGUDI JAINA TEMPLE** at **Aihole (Karnataka)**, which stands on top of a hill. It is a **19-LINE SANSKRIT** inscription, **dated Saka era 634–635 CE**, composed by the poet **RAVIKIRTI**. ⭐ **It is a *PRASHASTI* of the Chalukyas**, especially the reigning king **Pulikesin II**, referred to as ***SATHYASRAYA* — "the abode of truth."** It highlights the **history of the dynasty and the defeat of all his enemies, especially HARSHAVARDHANA.***
>
> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two details make it examinable twice over: it is a HINDU dynasty's royal eulogy carved on a JAINA temple, and its author RAVIKIRTI was himself a JAIN SCHOLAR** *(§2.3 below)*. *That single fact tells you more about Chalukya religious policy than a paragraph of assertion would.*

### 2.1 Administration ⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ** THE KING **
   ================================================================
   ⚠️⭐ ** "In dynastic succession ** PRIMOGENITURE WAS NOT
      STRICTLY FOLLOWED ** . Generally, the elder was to be
      appointed as ** YUVARAJA ** while the king was in office."
      The heir apparent was trained in ** literature, law,
      philosophy and martial arts ** . **
   ** Kings claimed to rule according to ** DHARMA-SASTRA and
      NITI-SASTRA ** ; Pulikesin I was "well-versed in
      MANU-SASTRA, PURANAS and ITIHASAS." **

   ** EARLY TITLES: MAHARAJAN · SATHYASRAYAN ·
      SRI-PRITIVI-VALLABAN **
   ⭐ ** After defeating Harshavardhana, Pulikesin II assumed
      ** PARAMESWARAN ** ; BHATTARAKAN and MAHARAJATHIRAJAN also
      became popular. **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE TWO ROYAL INSIGNIA -- a guaranteed match-the-
      pairs: **
      ** CHALUKYAS = THE WILD BOAR ** ("claimed that it
         represented the ** VARAHA AVATAR OF VISHNU ** ")
      ** PALLAVAS  = THE BULL ** (** SIVA'S MOUNT ** )

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** ROYAL WOMEN -- and the contrast is the point **
   ================================================================
   ** CHALUKYA: "the dynasty of JAYASIMHAN I line ** APPOINTED
      ROYAL LADIES AS PROVINCIAL GOVERNORS ** . They also ** ISSUED
      COINS ** in some instances. ⭐ ** VIJYA BHATTARIGA ** , a
      Chalukya princess, ** ISSUED INSCRIPTIONS ** ." **
   ** PALLAVA: queens "DID NOT take active part in the
      administration, but they ** BUILT SHRINES, INSTALLED IMAGES
      and ENDOWED TEMPLES ** ." The image of ** QUEEN RANGAPATAKA ** ,
      queen of Rajasimha, is in the inscription in the
      ** KAILASANATHA temple, Kanchipuram ** . **

   ================================================================
   ** MINISTERS AND DIVISIONS **
   ================================================================
   ⚠️ ** "Inscriptions do NOT specifically speak of a COUNCIL of
      ministers", but refer to the ** MAHA-SANDHI-VIGRAHIKA ** . **
   ** FOUR ministerial categories: **
     ** PRADHANA ** (head minister) ·
     ** MAHASANDHI-VIGRAHIKA ** (foreign affairs) ·
     ** AMATYA ** (revenue) · ** SAMAHARTA ** (exchequer)

   ⭐ ** DIVISIONS: ** VISHAYAM · RASTRAM · NADU · GRAMA ** **
   ** OFFICIALS: ** VISHAYAPATIS ** (exercised power at the king's
      behest) · ** SAMANTAS ** (feudal lords under state control) ·
      ** GRAMAPOHIS / GRAMKUDAS ** (village officials) ·
      ** MAHATRAS ** (prominent village men) **
   ** Province: governors were usually THE KING'S SONS, titled
      raja, marakka-rajan, rajaditya-raja-parameswaran; some held
      ** MAHA-SAMANTA ** and maintained troops. Vishaya was divided
      into ** PUKTI ** , headed by a ** POGAPATI ** . **

   ** VILLAGE: revenue officials ** NALA-KAVUNDAS ** ; the central
      figure ** KAMUNDA or POKIGAN ** (appointed by the king);
      accountant ** KARANA ** (also ** GRAMANI ** ); law and order
      with the ** MAHAJANAM ** ; a special officer ** MAHAPURUSH **
      for peace; town officials ** NAGARAPATIS or PURAPATIS ** . **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Chalukya royal women are a genuinely striking fact and worth a line in any GS1 answer on women in ancient India: *royal ladies appointed as PROVINCIAL GOVERNORS, issuing COINS and INSCRIPTIONS in their own name.*** ⭐ **And the textbook immediately supplies the control case — Pallava queens **did not** govern, but **built shrines and endowed temples.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Two neighbouring dynasties, the same century, opposite practice.** *(⭐ Set it beside [Unit 5's thirty named Sangam women poets](02_Sangam_Age.md) and **[Jahanara designing Chandni Chowk](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/09_Supplement_Kings_and_Chronicles_The_Mughal_Courts.md)** — three quite different modes of elite female agency.)*

### 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Chalukya architecture — Aihole, Badami, Pattadakal

```
      TEMPLES USING ** SOFT SANDSTONES ** AS MEDIUM." **

   ** THEIR TEMPLES FALL IN TWO GROUPS: **
     ** EXCAVATED CAVE TEMPLES ** and ** STRUCTURAL TEMPLES **
   ⭐ ** BADAMI is known for BOTH; ** PATTADAKAL and AIHOLE are

```

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ AIHOLE (AYYAVOLE)** — *built **634**.*

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it was** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The headquarters of the famous medieval AYYAVOLE MERCHANTS' GUILD** — *"an important COMMERCIAL centre"* |

| ⭐ **MEGUDI JAIN temple** | *"Illustrative of the EVOLUTION of temple architecture under the Chalukyas"* — **and it carries the Aihole inscription** |

**⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ PATTADAKAL** — *a village in **Bagalkot district, Karnataka***

```
   ⭐ ** "Pattadakal was a CENTRE FOR PERFORMING ROYAL RITUALS." **

   ** THE VIRUPAKSHA TEMPLE ** -- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ "built at the order of
      ** QUEEN LOHAMAHADEVI ** to commemorate ** THE CONQUEST OF
      KANCHIPURAM BY HER HUSBAND VIKRAMADITYA II ** ."

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE ARCHITECTS ARE NAMED -- the best fact in the

     ** KANNADA inscription EULOGIZING THE ARCHITECT ** who
     designed it; he was given the title ** TRIBHUVACHARYA
     ("MAKER OF THE THREE WORLDS") ** . **

     THE SCULPTORS who carved them." **
   * ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** The PAPANATHA temple's eastern wall has a short

      ** SHIKARA IN THE NORTHERN STYLE ** ; outer walls richly

   ⭐ ** Pattadakal has ** MORE THAN TEN temples ** , classified by
      style into ** INDO-ARYAN and DRAVIDIAN ** . **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE NAMED ARCHITECTS ARE THE SINGLE BEST THING IN THIS UNIT, AND YOU SHOULD USE THEM DELIBERATELY — because everywhere else in your syllabus, the builders are anonymous.**
> - ⭐ **[Theme 7 §7](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/07_Theme7_An_Imperial_Capital_Vijayanagara.md) asks, of Vijayanagara: *"Who drew up the plans? Where did the masons, stonecutters, sculptors come from? What kind of wages did they get?"* — and cannot answer.**

> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **At Pattadakal, the architect is EULOGISED BY NAME, given the title *TRIBHUVACHARYA*, and the sculptors SIGNED THEIR OWN RELIEFS. We even have a second architect's name — REVADI OVAJJA.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Write that comparison and it will stand out: *the Chalukya monuments are among the very few in Indian history where we know the name of the person who designed the building, and not merely the king who paid for it.*** *(⭐ Pattadakal is also a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** — worth knowing alongside Ellora.)*

### 2.3 Chalukya religion, language and learning ⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "The Chalukyas patronised BOTH SAIVISM AND VAISHNAVISM",
      built temples for Siva and Vishnu, and ** INVITED BRAHMIN
      GROUPS FROM THE GANGETIC REGIONS ** to perform pujas and
      conduct festivals. **
   ** KIRTIVARMAN I, MANGALESA (597-609) and PULIKESIN II
      performed ** YAGNAS ** ; they bore the titles
      ** PARAMA-VAISHNAVA and PARAMA-MAHESWARA ** . **
   ⭐ ** "Chalukyas gave PROMINENT PLACE TO ** KARTIKEYAN, THE WAR
      GOD ** ." Saiva monasteries became centres for popularising
      Saivism. **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** AND THE HETERODOX SIDE: "Chalukyas patronised
      HETERODOX SECTS ALSO and ** LAVISHLY DONATED LANDS TO THE
      JAIN CENTRES ** ." **
   * ⭐ ** RAVIKIRTI, the POET-LAUREATE OF PULIKESIN II, WAS A
     JAIN SCHOLAR. ** **
   * ** In Kirtivarman II's reign a ** JAIN VILLAGE OFFICIAL BUILT
     A JAIN TEMPLE at ANNIGERE ** . **
   * ** The prince Krishna appointed ** GUNAPATRA, A JAIN MONK, AS
     HIS MASTER ** . **
   * ** HIUEN TSANG: many Buddhist centres in Chalukya territory,
     with ** MORE THAN 5,000 followers of the HINAYANA AND
     MAHAYANA sects ** . **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** LANGUAGE -- one sentence that is worth a whole
      answer **
   ================================================================
   ** "A SEVENTH-CENTURY INSCRIPTION of a Chalukya king at BADAMI
      mentions ** KANNADA AS THE LOCAL *PRAKRIT* -- MEANING THE
      PEOPLE'S LANGUAGE -- AND SANSKRIT AS THE LANGUAGE OF
      CULTURE ** ." **
   ** A chieftain of Pulikesin II authored a grammar work,
      ** SAPTAVATARAM ** , in Sanskrit. **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That Badami inscription gives you a SEVENTH-CENTURY STATE'S OWN LANGUAGE POLICY, stated explicitly: *Kannada = the people's language; Sanskrit = the language of culture.*** ⭐ **A two-tier linguistic order, named by the state itself.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *It is the exact structure the Mughals would later run with **Persian as the language of empire and local languages for village accounts** ([the supplement §8.3](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/09_Supplement_Kings_and_Chronicles_The_Mughal_Courts.md)) — and the exact structure the bhakti saints would **attack**, by putting scripture into Tamil.*

## 3️⃣ The Pallavas ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ⚠️⭐ ** ORIGIN -- "There is NO SCHOLARLY CONSENSUS." ** Some
      early scholars held ** Pallava = a variant of PAHLAVA
      (the PARTHIANS) ** , who moved from western India to the
      eastern coast during the Saka-Satavahana wars of the 2nd
      century CE. ** "But many scholars TODAY regard them NATIVE
      TO SOUTH INDIA or 'with some mixture of north Indian
      blood'." **

   ⭐ ** Associated with ** TONDAIMANDALAM ** , "the land between
      the ** NORTH PENNAR and NORTH VELLAR rivers ** ." **

   ================================================================
   ** THE LINE, AND THE WAR **
   ================================================================
   ** SIMHAVISHNU ** -- "conquered the Chola country up to the
      KAVERI", consolidating rule started by his father
      SIMHAVARMAN; ⭐ ** VANQUISHED THE KALABHRAS ** , thereby
      coming into conflict with the Pandyas.
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** MAHENDRAVARMAN I (590-630) ** -- ** "whom APPAR
      CONVERTED FROM JAINISM TO SAIVISM" ** ; "a patron of arts,
      and a POET AND MUSICIAN in his own right."
      ⚠️ During his reign Pulikesin II's army annexed the northern
      Pallava kingdom and ** almost reached Kanchipuram ** .
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** NARASIMHAVARMAN I (630-668) ** -- won several
      victories over the Chalukyas ** with the aid of his ally
      MANAVARMAN, A SRI LANKAN PRINCE ** who later became ruler
      of the island. ⭐ ** THE CLIMAX: his invasion (General
      PARANJOTHI) and CAPTURE OF BADAMI. ** He claims to have
      defeated the ** Cholas, Cheras and Kalabhras ** . ⭐ ** TWO
      NAVAL EXPEDITIONS ** sent to help Manavarman succeeded --
      though that ruler subsequently lost his kingdom.
   ** PARAMESVARAVARMAN I (670-695) ** -- VIKRAMADITYA of the
      Chalukyas invaded; he fought back ** with the support of the
      GANGAS and PANDYAS ** , which then drew the Pallavas into
      conflict with the Pandyas.
   ** DANTIVARMAN ** -- the Rashtrakuta ** GOVIND III invaded
      KANCHI ** in the early 9th century.
   ** NANDIVARMAN III ** -- aided by the western Gangas and
      Cholas, ⭐ ** defeated the Pandyas at the battle of
      THIRUPURAMBIYAM ** .
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** APARAJITA ** , grandson of Nandivarman III, ** "lost
      his life in a battle fought against ADITYA I of the Chola
      kingdom" ** , who invaded Tondaimandalam. ** "This SEALED THE
      FATE of the Pallavas." ** Tondaimandalam passed to the
      Cholas.
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Note how the unit ENDS exactly where [Unit 11](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md) BEGINS.** ⭐ **Aditya I's Cholas take Tondaimandalam from Aparajita — and Unit 11 opens with **Vijayalaya (850–871) taking the Kaveri delta from the Muttaraiyar** and the Cholas emerging *"as subordinates to the PALLAVAS in the Kaveri region."*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Later Chola empire is built on the corpse of the Pallava state, by a family that had been the Pallavas' vassals.**

### 3.1 Pallava administration, village life and *ERIPATTI* ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ** THE CENTRE **
   ================================================================
   ** "Kingship was held to be OF DIVINE ORIGIN and was
      HEREDITARY." Titles like ** MAHARAJADHIRAJA ** were
      "BORROWED FROM NORTH INDIAN TRADITIONS." **
   ⭐ ** In the LATER Pallava period the ministerial council
      "played a PROMINENT PART in state policy"; some ministers
      "bore SEMI-ROYAL TITLES and may well have been APPOINTED
      FROM AMONG THE FEUDATORIES." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE DISTINCTION TO LEARN: **
      ** MANTRI ** = "generally understood to be A DIPLOMAT"
      ** AMATYA ** = "A COUNSELLOR"
      ** MANTRI MANDALA ** = the council of ministers

   ** OTHER CENTRAL OFFICERS: **
   * ** RAHASYADHIKRITA ** -- the king's PRIVATE SECRETARY
   * ⭐ ** MANIKKAPPANDARAM-KAPPAN ** -- officer in charge of the
     TREASURY *(Manikka = valuables; Pandaram = treasury;
     Kappan = keeper)*
   * ** KODUKKAPPILLAI ** -- the officer of GIFTS
   * ** KOSA-ADHYAKSA ** -- supervisor of the
     Manikkappandaram-kappan

   ** JUSTICE: courts called ** ADHIKARNA MANDAPA ** ; judges
      ** DHARMADHIKARINS ** . The ** KASAKUDI PLATES ** of
      Nandivarma Pallava name two fines --
      ** KARANADANDAM ** (superior/higher court) and
      ** ADHIKARANADANDAM ** (district level). **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** VILLAGE LIFE -- and the SABHA / URAR distinction **
   ================================================================
   ** "In the village, the basic assembly was the ** SABHA ** ,
      which was concerned with ALL matters relating to the
      village, including ** ENDOWMENTS, LAND, IRRIGATION,
      CULTIVATION, PUNISHMENT OF CRIME, THE KEEPING OF A CENSUS
      and other necessary records ** ." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "The sabha was a ** FORMAL institution ** but it
      worked closely with the ** URAR, AN INFORMAL GATHERING OF
      THE ENTIRE VILLAGE ** ." **
   ** Village courts dealt with petty criminal cases; higher
      courts in towns and districts were presided over by
      government officers, ** with the king as the supreme
      arbiter of justice ** . **
   ⭐ ** "Villages populated ENTIRELY OR LARGELY BY BRAHMANS
      PRESERVED RECORDS of the functioning of assemblies and
      councils." **
   ** The link between village assembly and official
      administration was ** THE HEADMAN ** . **
```

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That last line is a source-bias point you should carry: *the villages whose assembly records survive are the BRAHMIN villages.*** ⭐ **Which is precisely why [Uttaramerur](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md) — our showpiece of South Indian local government — is a ***brahmadeya***.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **We do not know less about ordinary villages because they were less organised; we know less because they did not keep, or did not preserve, inscriptions.**

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** ERIPATTI -- "a special category of land… KNOWN ONLY
      IN SOUTH INDIA" **
   ================================================================
   ** ERIPATTI or TANK LAND ** -- "donated by individuals, THE
      REVENUE FROM WHICH WAS SET APART FOR THE MAINTENANCE OF THE
      VILLAGE TANK."

   ** "Rainwater was stored in the tank so that land could be
      irrigated during the annual long, dry spell. The tank,
      ** LINED WITH BRICK OR STONE, WAS BUILT THROUGH THE
      COOPERATIVE EFFORT OF THE VILLAGE, AND ITS WATER WAS SHARED
      BY ALL CULTIVATORS ** ." **
   ⭐ ** "PRACTICALLY EVERY INSCRIPTION from the Pallava period
      pertaining to rural affairs REFERS TO THE UPKEEP OF THE
      TANK." ** Next in importance came ** WELLS ** .
   ** "Water was distributed by CANALS, which were fitted with
      ** SLUICES to regulate the water level and prevent
      overflowing at the source ** ." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "The distribution of water for irrigation was
      supervised by a ** SPECIAL TANK COMMITTEE APPOINTED BY THE
      VILLAGE ** . ** WATER TAKEN IN EXCESS of that allotted to a
      particular cultivator ** WAS TAXED ** ." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS A COMPLETE, FUNCTIONING WATER-COMMONS REGIME, AND IT IS EXCELLENT GS3 MATERIAL. NOTICE THE FOUR ELEMENTS:** ⭐ **(i) a DEDICATED ENDOWMENT — *eripatti*, land whose entire revenue funds tank maintenance; (ii) COOPERATIVE CONSTRUCTION and SHARED water; (iii) a SPECIAL TANK COMMITTEE elected by the village to allocate it; and (iv) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A PRICE ON OVER-EXTRACTION — "water taken IN EXCESS… was TAXED."**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A seventh-century village that metered and taxed excess water use is a genuinely arresting fact for an Essay on the commons or on groundwater governance.** *(⭐ And it sets up [Unit 11's Chola system exactly](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md) — the ***eriayam*** tax ring-fenced for tank repair, ***nirkkintravaaru*** water shares, the turn system and *enkalkulam*. **The Cholas inherited a working institution and elaborated it.**)*

```
   ================================================================
   ** REVENUE AND TAXATION **
   ================================================================
   ⭐ ** "Revenue came ALMOST EXCLUSIVELY FROM RURAL SOURCES,
      mercantile and urban institutions being largely
      unplanned." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** TWO CATEGORIES OF TAX: **
   ** 1. ** LAND REVENUE to the state -- "varied from ** 1/6th TO
        1/10th OF THE PRODUCE ** ", collected by the village and
        paid to the state collector. **
   ** 2. ** LOCAL taxes -- "also collected in the village ** BUT
        UTILIZED FOR SERVICES IN THE VILLAGE ITSELF ** … spent for
        ** REPAIRING IRRIGATION WORKS, ILLUMINATING THE
        TEMPLE ** ." **

   ⚠️ ** When the state land tax was inadequate, revenue was
      supplemented by ** ADDITIONAL TAXES ON: draught cattle,
      toddy-drawers, MARRIAGE-PARTIES, potters, goldsmiths,
      washermen, textile-manufacturers, weavers, brokers,
      letter-carriers and the makers of ghee ** . **
   ** "The LOOT AND BOOTY obtained in war added to the revenue." **
   ⭐ ** Pallavas "considered war to be very important" -- a series
      of sculptures of Nandivarama Pallava's reign, ** notably
      PALLAVA TROOPS ATTACKING A FORT ** , survive in the
      ** VAIKUNTA PERUMAL temple at Kanchipuram ** . **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The **local tax retained and spent in the village** is the institutionally interesting half — a seventh-century equivalent of an untied local fund, and the direct ancestor of the Chola ***eriayam***.** ⭐ *And the list of supplementary taxes is worth a glance for what it reveals: **a tax on MARRIAGE-PARTIES** tells you the state taxed events, not just land, once the land tax fell short.*

### 3.2 Army, trade and society ⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ================================================================
   ** THE ARMY **
   ================================================================
   ** A STANDING ARMY under the king's direct control: foot-
      soldiers, cavalry, and a SMALL force of elephants. **
   ⭐ ** "CHARIOTS were by now ALMOST OUT OF USE and in any case
      were INEFFECTIVE IN THE HILLY TERRAINS." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "CAVALRY, though effective, was EXPENSIVE, AS
      HORSES HAD TO BE IMPORTED." **
   ⭐ ** "The Pallavas DEVELOPED A NAVY and built ** DOCKYARDS AT
      MAMALLAPURAM AND NAGAPATTINAM ** . However, the Pallava navy
      was ** INCONSIDERABLE COMPARED TO the naval strength of the
      CHOLAS ** who succeeded them." **

   ================================================================
   ** TRADE AND THE GUILDS **
   ================================================================
   ** KANCHIPURAM was an important trading centre; ⭐ "merchants
      had to ** OBTAIN A LICENSE ** to market their goods." **
   ** Barter generally prevailed, "but LATER the Pallavas issued
      ** GOLD AND SILVER COINS ** ." **
   ** Merchants' organizations such as ** MANIGRAMAM ** . **
   ** EXPORTS: spices, cotton textiles, precious stones and
      medicinal plants -> ** JAVA, SUMATRA, CAMBODIA, SRI LANKA,
      CHINA and BURMA ** . MAMALLAPURAM was an important
      seaport. **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE GUILD DETAIL: traders founded guilds calling
      themselves ** SUDESI, NANADESI, AINURRUVAR ** ; "their MAIN
      GUILD FUNCTIONED AT ** AIHOLE ** ." ** FOREIGN MERCHANTS WERE
      KNOWN AS NANADESI. ** **
   ⭐ ** "It had a SEPARATE FLAG WITH THE FIGURE OF A BULL at the
      centre, and they enjoyed the RIGHT OF ISSUING
      ** VIRA-SASANAS ** . ** THE JURISDICTION OF THIS GUILD
      STRETCHED OVER ENTIRE SOUTH-EAST ASIA. ** " **
   ** Guild chief: ** PATTANSWAMY · PATTNAKILAR · DANDANAYAKA ** ;
      members ** AYYAVOLE-PARAMESWARIYAR ** . **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** MARITIME TRADE -- and a sentence about DECLINE that
      most students miss **
   ================================================================
   ** "UNLIKE in the Ganges plain, where large areas were
      available for cultivation, ** the regions controlled by the
      Pallavas and the Chalukyas commanded A LIMITED INCOME FROM
      LAND ** ." **
   ** Maritime trade with SE Asia, then holding three major
      kingdoms: ** KAMBUJA (Cambodia) · CHAMPA (Annam) ·
      SRIVIJAYA (southern Malaya peninsula and Sumatra) ** . **

   ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** ON THE WEST COAST: "the initiative in the trade
      with the West was ** GRADUALLY PASSING INTO THE HANDS OF THE
      FOREIGN TRADERS settled along the coast, MAINLY ARABS ** .
      ** INDIAN TRADERS WERE BECOMING SUPPLIERS OF GOODS RATHER
      THAN CARRIERS OF GOODS ** to foreign countries, and
      communication with the west became ** INDIRECT, VIA ARABS,
      AND LIMITED TO TRADE ALONE ** ." **
```

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THAT LAST PASSAGE IS A MAJOR STRUCTURAL POINT AND IT IS EASY TO READ PAST. IT DESCRIBES INDIA MOVING DOWN THE VALUE CHAIN.** ⭐ **Indian merchants stop being **CARRIERS** — shipowners who go and sell — and become **SUPPLIERS** who hand goods to Arab intermediaries at the coast.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Contact with the West becomes *"INDIRECT… AND LIMITED TO TRADE ALONE"* — so the cultural exchange narrows as the commercial role shrinks.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Set this in a line of three and you have a Mains-grade argument about a millennium of Indian Ocean trade:** ⭐ **[Sangam era](02_Sangam_Age.md): Tamil traders' own potsherds at BERENIKE in Egypt — they went there.** ⭐ **PALLAVA era: they supply; Arabs carry.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **[CHOLA era](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md): the state itself intervenes militarily against SRI VIJAYA to keep the eastern corridor open.** *Three different answers to the same problem of controlling the carrying trade.*

```
   ================================================================
   ** SOCIETY -- and the Aryanisation argument **
   ================================================================
   ** BRAHMINS: learned scholars in literature, astronomy and law,
      functioning as ROYAL COUNSELLORS -- "not only in the
      TEACHING profession, they were also involved in
      ** AGRICULTURE, TRADE AND WAR ** ." ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** They were
      EXEMPTED FROM PAYING TAXES ** AND ** FROM CAPITAL
      PUNISHMENT ** . **
   ** SAT-KSHATRIYAS ("quality kshatriyas") -- "NOT ALL the
      kshatriyas were of warring groups; some were involved in
      TRADING." They "enjoyed the RIGHT TO READ THE VEDAS, a
      privilege DENIED TO LOWER VARNAS." **
   ** The TRADING group "maintained warriors for protection and
      founded TRADE GUILDS." **
   ** At the bottom: agriculture, animal husbandry, handicrafts. **
   ⚠️⭐ ** "People engaged in ** SCAVENGING, FISHING, DRY-CLEANING
      AND LEATHER WORKS were positioned OUTSIDE THE VARNA
      SYSTEM ** ." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "Most scholars agree that ** ARYANISATION, or the
      northern influence on the south, PICKED UP PACE DURING THE
      PALLAVA PERIOD ** . This is evident from the ROYAL GRANTS
      issued by the kings. ** THE CASTE STRUCTURE HAD FIRMLY
      ESTABLISHED. SANSKRIT CAME TO BE HELD IN HIGH ESTEEM. ** " **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Put this beside [Unit 5's finding](02_Sangam_Age.md) that in the Sangam age *"the VARNASRAMA ideology was YET TO TAKE ROOT in the Tamil region"* — and you have a datable social transformation.** ⭐ **Between the Sangam age and the Pallavas, **varna went from absent to "firmly established"** — and the textbook names the mechanism: **ROYAL LAND GRANTS to Brahmins invited from the Gangetic north.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **That is the same instrument the [Satavahanas pioneered](02_Sangam_Age.md) and the [Cholas perfected](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md). **One institution, carrying a social order southward over eight centuries.**

### 3.3 Learning, Sanskrit and the rock-cut temple ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

```
   ** "In the EARLY part of this period education was CONTROLLED
      BY JAINS AND BUDDHISTS, but GRADUALLY THE BRAHMINS
      SUPERSEDED THEM." **
   ⭐ ** "The JAINS, who had brought with them their religious
      literature in Sanskrit and Prakrit, ** BEGAN TO USE
      TAMIL ** ." **
   ⚠️ ** "MAHENDRAVARMAN I LOST INTEREST IN JAINISM and took up
      the cause of Saivism, ** THUS DEPRIVING THE JAINS OF
      VALUABLE ROYAL PATRONAGE ** " -- and he was "INTOLERANT of
      Jainism and ** DESTROYED SOME JAIN MONASTERIES ** ." **
   ** Jain centres near Madurai and Kanchi, and
      ** SHRAVANABELAGOLA in Karnataka ** ; but "a vast majority of
      the Jaina monks tended to ISOLATE THEMSELVES IN SMALL
      CAVES, IN HILLS AND FORESTS." **

   ⭐ ** BUDDHISM "was FIGHTING A LOSING BATTLE. ** ROYAL PATRONAGE,
      WHICH THE BUDDHISTS LACKED, GAVE AN EDGE to the protagonists
      of Vedic religions. ** " ** -- though HIUEN TSANG reported at
      Kanchi ** 100 Buddhist monasteries and 10,000 priests of the
      MAHAYANA school ** .

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** KANCHI: "Apart from the UNIVERSITY AT KANCHI, WHICH
      ACQUIRED A FAME EQUAL TO THAT OF ** NALANDA ** , there were a
      number of other Sanskrit colleges." **
   ⭐ ** MATHAS (MUTTS) became popular in the EIGHTH century --
      "a combination of ** A REST HOUSE, A FEEDING-CENTRE, AND AN
      EDUCATION CENTRE ** , which indirectly brought PUBLICITY to
      the particular sect with which it was associated." **

   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** SANSKRIT LITERATURE -- three works, three authors **
   ================================================================
   ** MAHENDRAVARMAN I ** -> ** MATHAVILASA PRAHASANAM **
   ** BHARAVI ** -> ** KIRATARJUNIYA **
   ** DANDIN ** -> ** DASHAKUMARACHARITA ** , and the great
      treatise on rhetoric ** KAVYADARSA **
   ⭐ ** "DANDIN OF KANCHIPURAM… seems to have stayed in the
      Pallava court for some time." **
```

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** ROCK-CUT TEMPLES -- and the MANDAGAPPATTU
      INSCRIPTION is the fact to memorise **
   ================================================================
   ** "MAHENDRAVARMAN I is credited with the INTRODUCTION OF
      ROCK-CUT TEMPLES in the Pallava territory." **

   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** In his MANDAGAPPATTU INSCRIPTION he claims that his
      shrine ** to BRAHMA, ISVARA and VISHNU ** was made
      ** WITHOUT USING traditional materials such as BRICK,
      TIMBER, METAL and MORTAR ** . **

   ** FORM: "usually the ** MANDAPA TYPE ** with a PILLARED HALL or
      the mandapa IN FRONT and a SMALL SHRINE at the rear or
      sides." **
```

> 💡 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Mandagappattu boast is a beautiful piece of evidence, because the king is advertising an ABSENCE.** ⭐ **"No brick, no timber, no metal, no mortar" is how you describe a building **carved out of living rock** — and it tells you the technique was **new enough to be worth boasting about.*** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And it is the exact inverse of [Theme 7's Vijayanagara finding](../../../../01_NCERT/Class_12_Themes_in_Indian_History_II/Notes/07_Theme7_An_Imperial_Capital_Vijayanagara.md), where temples were **entirely masonry** and palaces had **perishable superstructures**. **A rock-cut shrine is the one building type that cannot decay** — which is precisely why so much of what survives from this period is religious.**)*

## 4️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ELLORA

```
   ** Aurangabad district, MAHARASHTRA. **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE ONE-LINE DISTINCTION TO NEVER GET WRONG:
      ** ELLORA is famous for SCULPTURES; AJANTA for
      PAINTINGS. ** **

   ** DATES: "range from ** c. 500 to c. 950 CE ** . But the
      activity of creating cave temples ** MAY HAVE STARTED TWO
      HUNDRED YEARS EARLIER ** ." **
   ⭐ ** "THE FIRST CAVE TEMPLE WAS CREATED FOR THE ** AJIVIKAS ** ."
      Some of the temples are INCOMPLETE. **

   ** ** 34 CAVES ** , carved in the ** CHARANADRI HILLS ** . **
   ** PATRONS "range from the dynasties of ** CHALUKYAS to
      RASHTRAKUTAS ** ." **
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** "THE HETERODOX SECTS ** FIRST ** SET THE TREND of
      creating this model of temples. LATER, ORTHODOX SECTS
      ADOPTED IT as a medium of disseminating religious
      ideologies." **
   ** Linked to ** AJIVIKAS, JAINISM, BUDDHISM and BRAHMANISM ** --
      ⭐ FOUR traditions in one site. **
   ** MURAL PAINTINGS survive in FIVE caves, "but ONLY IN THE
      KAILASH TEMPLE are they preserved"; some murals in Jain
      temples are well preserved. **
   ⭐ ** DESIGNATED A ** UNESCO WORLD HERITAGE SITE IN 1983 ** . **
```

| Group | ⭐ Detail |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **BUDDHIST *(Heterodox I)*** | **TWELVE caves.** Some modest, others **double- or triple-storeyed**. Designed as centres where **monks stayed and disciples were trained**; the main hall with **cubical rooms on either side** served as monasteries. ⭐ **Cave 6 has a figure of a man READING A MANUSCRIPT ON A FOLDING TABLE.** Panels show scenes from the Buddha's life |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The three MUDRAS** | **DHYANA MUDRA** *(meditating)* · **VYAKHYANA MUDRA** *(preaching)* · **BHUMI-SPARSHA MUDRA** *(touching the earth with the index finger of the right hand)* |
| ⭐ **Buddhist goddesses** | **TARA · KHADIRAVANI-TARA · CHUNDA · VAJRADHAT-VISHVARI · MAHA-MAYURI · SUJATA · PANDARA · BHRIKUTI.** *In cave 12, a stout female figure wears **a waistband and the headgear of a cobra**; Khadiravani-tara also holds a cobra* |
| **JAIN *(Heterodox II)*** | **A few caves, distinct from the others — but INCOMPLETE.** Figures of **YAKSHA-MATANGA, MAHAVIRA, PARSVANATHA and GOMATESVARA**, surrounded by attendants |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **VEDIC caves** | The earliest are **modest, simple and mostly square** — **except KAILASANATHA** |

> 🔴 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE KAILASANATHA CAVE (CAVE 16) — the showpiece, and every detail is examinable.**
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***"A massive MONOLITHIC structure, CARVED OUT OF A SINGLE SOLID ROCK."*** **It is said to represent KAILASH, the abode of Shiva.**
> - **TWO STOREYED**, with the Kailasa temple on the first.
> - ⭐ **The lower storey has carved LIFE-SIZE ELEPHANTS, which look as if they are HOLDING UP THE TEMPLE ON THEIR BACKS.**
> - **Exterior: richly carved windows, images of deities, and *MITHUNAS*** *(amorous male and female figures)*.
> - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The arrangement is deliberate: *"most of the deities to the LEFT of the entrance are SAIVITE, and the deities to the RIGHT are VAISHNAVITE."***
> - **Courtyard: two huge pillars with the flagstaff, and a NANDI MANDAPA.**
> - **Named panels: the WEDDING OF SIVA AND PARVATI · RAVANA'S ATTEMPTED LIFTING OF KAILASA · DURGA DESTROYING MAHISASURA.**
> - ⭐ **And the river goddesses: GANGA MOUNTED ON A CROCODILE, YAMUNA ON A TORTOISE.**
>
> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The textbook makes an engineering point worth repeating: *"WITHOUT KNOWLEDGE OF TRIGONOMETRY, STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING AND METALLURGY, the Indian architects COULD NOT HAVE CREATED such exquisite edifices."*** ⭐ **A monolith is carved **top-down from the living rock, with no possibility of correcting a mistake** — there is nothing to add back. *That is why it is an argument about applied mathematics, not just about devotion.*

### 7.1 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Adi Sankara and Ramanuja — the two philosophers

```
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ** THE HINGE SENTENCE: bhakti canons were composed
      "in TAMIL in simple syntactic and semantic style. ** BUT,
      WITH THE ARRIVAL OF ADI SANKARA, BHAKTI DISCOURSE BEGAN IN
      SANSKRIT IN A PHILOSOPHICAL MODE. ** " **
```

| | ⭐ **ADI SANKARA (788–820)** | ⭐ **SRI RAMANUJAR (1017–1138)** |
|---|---|---|
| **From** | ⭐ **KALADI, KERALA** | ⭐ **SRIPERUMPUDUR** |
| **Doctrine** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***ADVAITA* — NON-DUALISM**, with the doctrine of ***MAYA* (illusion)** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ ***VISISHTADVAITA* — "QUALIFIED MONISM"** |
| **Roots** | *"had its roots in **VEDANTA or UPANISHADIC philosophy**"* | Trained under **YATAVAPRAKASAR** in Kanchipuram **in the Sankara school** — *and disagreed with his guru* |
| **Institutional work** | ⭐ **Established *SMARTA* (traditionalist) MATHAS at SRINGERI, DVARAKA, BADRINATH and PURI**, headed by Brahmin pontiffs | **Invited to Srirangam by YAMUNACHARYA** *(who died as he arrived)*; **made head of the Srirangam monastery**; *"a **TEACHER-REFORMER and a great ORGANISER**"* |
| **On sects** | ⭐ *"looked upon **SAIVA and VAISHNAVA worship as TWO EQUALLY IMPORTANT ASPECTS** of the Vedic religion"* | **Challenged Sankara's Advaita**, and worked *"to **WIDEN THE SOCIAL BASE to include social groups other than Brahmans**"* |
| **Two thrusts / legacy** | ⭐ **MONASTIC ORGANIZATION** and **PRESERVATION OF SANSKRIT SCRIPTURES** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Influenced temple authorities to PERMIT SOCIAL GROUPS OUTSIDE THE *VARNASHRAMA* SYSTEM TO ENTER THE TEMPLE AT LEAST ONCE A YEAR** |
| **Aftermath** | Attempts *"to **ROOT OUT BUDDHISM**"* | ⚠️ A century after his death a **schism** produced two schools under **VEDANTA DESIKAR** and **MANAVALA MAMUNI**. ⭐ *"It is believed that due to the perceived threat… **Ramanujar HAD TO LEAVE HIS PLACE OF RESIDENCE**"* |

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Ramanuja temple-entry fact is the one to carry, and it should be stated precisely — *at least once a year*, by persuading temple authorities, for groups outside the *varnashrama* order.** ⭐ **That is a **real, limited, negotiated** reform, not an abolition of exclusion — and stating the limit is what makes the answer credible.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *(⭐ **And it connects to [Unit 11](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md): the man who tried this is the same Ramanuja whom the Saiva zeal of **Kulothunga II drove out of the Chola country to Melkote**. **This unit gives you the reformer; Unit 11 gives you his exile.**)*

| The *sabha* and the *urar* were the same body | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The *SABHA* was the FORMAL assembly; the *URAR* was "an INFORMAL gathering of the entire village"** |
| The Pallava navy surpassed the Cholas' | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"the Pallava navy was **INCONSIDERABLE COMPARED TO** the naval strength of the Cholas"* — though they built **dockyards at MAMALLAPURAM and NAGAPATTINAM** |
| Brahmins under the Pallavas were exempt only from taxes | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **From taxes AND from CAPITAL PUNISHMENT** |
| The merchant guild's main seat was Kanchipuram | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AIHOLE** — the guild had a **flag with a BULL**, issued ***VIRA-SASANAS***, and its **jurisdiction stretched over the whole of South-East Asia** |
| Ellora is famous for paintings | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ELLORA = SCULPTURES; AJANTA = PAINTINGS** |
| Ellora has 30 caves | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ELLORA = 34 caves** *(Charanadri hills)*; **AJANTA = 30 caves** |
| The first Ellora cave temple was Buddhist | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"The first cave temple was created for the **AJIVIKAS**"* — the site covers **Ajivikas, Jainism, Buddhism and Brahmanism** |
| Ellora's orthodox caves came first | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"**THE HETERODOX SECTS FIRST SET THE TREND**… later, orthodox sects adopted it"* |
| Ellora became a World Heritage Site in 1993 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **1983** |
| Kailasanatha is cave 12 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **CAVE 16** — a **MONOLITH carved from a single solid rock**, two-storeyed, with **life-size elephants** appearing to carry it |
| Ellora's Kailasanatha mixes deities randomly | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Deities to the LEFT of the entrance are SAIVITE; to the RIGHT, VAISHNAVITE** |
| Ganga is shown on a tortoise at Ellora | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **GANGA on a CROCODILE; YAMUNA on a TORTOISE** |
| Ajanta was begun by the Mahayana sect | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The HINAYANA sect started the excavation**; early paintings are in **caves 9 and 10**, of the **SATAVAHANA** period |
| Ajanta's plaster was lime and sand only | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **VEGETABLE FIBRES, PADDY HUSK, ROCK-GRIT and SAND**, then a **thin lime layer** — and **a stretch of CLOTH** was reinforced on the surface |
| The Shore Temple was built by Mahendravarman I | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **By RAJASIMHA (700–728)**; it has **THREE shrines**, chiefly to **Siva and Vishnu** |
| The Bhima Ratha is the most exquisite | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The DHARMARAJA RATHA** — *three-storied vimana, square base*. *(The **BHIMA** ratha is **RECTANGULAR**)* |
| Mahendravarman's Mandagappattu shrine was to Siva alone | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **To BRAHMA, ISVARA and VISHNU** — and made **without brick, timber, metal or mortar** |
| There are 12 Nayanmars and 63 Azhwars | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **REVERSED — AZHWARS 12, NAYANMARS 63** |
| The *Nalayira Divyaprabandham* was compiled by Nambi Andar Nambi | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **By NADAMUNI**, priest at the **Ranganatha temple, Srirangam**, end of the **9th century**, **4,000 poems**. *(**NAMBI ANDAR NAMBI** compiled the **SAIVA** hymns, end of the **10th century**)* |
| The *Periyapuranam* is the eleventh *tirumurai* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The TWELFTH** — Sekkilar's hagiography of the **63 Nayanmars** |
| Nammalvar wrote the *Tiruvasagam* | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Nammalvar wrote the *TIRUVAYMOLI*** *(one of four works)*; the ***TIRUVASAGAM* is MANIKKAVASAGAR's** |
| Bhakti hymns had two themes | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THREE — devotion to a personal god · protest against Brahmanical exclusiveness · and ⚠️ THE OUTRIGHT CONDEMNATION OF JAINS AND BUDDHISTS AS HERETICS** |
| Sankara established mathas at four southern sites | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SRINGERI, DVARAKA, BADRINATH and PURI** — spanning the subcontinent |
| Ramanuja opened temples to all, permanently | ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ He *"influenced **SOME** temple authorities to permit the social groups outside the *varnashrama* system to enter the temple **AT LEAST ONCE A YEAR**"* |

### 🟢 Mains angles

| Angle | How to build it |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Pallava and Chalukya contributions to Indian art and architecture"** *(the core GS1 question)* | ⭐ **CHALUKYA:** soft-sandstone technique · **cave AND structural** temples · **Aihole** *(70 temples; Lad Khan; Durga on a chaitya model; Megudi)* · **Badami** *(four caves; Mangalesa's Vishnu cave)* · **Pattadakal** *(Virupaksha, Papanatha, Indo-Aryan and Dravidian groups)* · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the NAMED ARCHITECTS** · Vakataka-style painting. ⭐ **PALLAVA:** **rock-cut** from Mahendravarman *(Mandagappattu)* → **monolithic rathas** → **structural Shore Temple**. **Close with the unit's own verdict: *"borrowed basic form, native brilliance."*** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Ellora and Ajanta"** | ⭐ **Distinguish first** *(Ellora 34 caves, sculptures; Ajanta 30 caves, paintings)*. **ELLORA:** four religions, **heterodox sects set the trend first**, first cave for the **AJIVIKAS**, patrons Chalukya→Rashtrakuta, **UNESCO 1983**, and **Kailasanatha as a monolith** with its Saivite-left/Vaishnavite-right arrangement. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AJANTA:** Hinayana beginning, two phases, **patrons from kings to MERCHANTS**, the **four-step plaster technique**, Jataka subjects, **compassion and peace** as dominant moods |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Bhakti as state ideology"** *(the best Mains angle here)* | ⭐ **The unit's own framing** — regional polities *"necessitated states based on a certain ideology"*, and *"religion alone could be the rallying point"*; patronised by **Pallavas and Pandyas**, *"spearheaded by the ELITE and the WEALTHY MERCHANT CLASS"*, with **the temple as nucleus**. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Then the impact** — temples, invited Brahmin settlements, endowed festivals, *"directly SPEEDED UP THE EMERGENCE OF STATE"*. **Close on NARAYANAN and VELUTHAT: bhakti as "the cementing force… to strengthen the rule of the newly established Hindu kingdoms BASED ON THE CASTE SYSTEM"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Tank irrigation and water governance in the Pallava country"** *(GS1 + GS3)* | ⭐ ***ERIPATTI*, "known ONLY in south India"** · **cooperative construction, shared water** · *"practically every inscription… refers to the upkeep of the tank"* · **canals with SLUICES** · **a SPECIAL TANK COMMITTEE appointed by the village** · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **and excess use TAXED.** **Then carry it into the [Chola system](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md)** — *eriayam*, *nirkkintravaaru*, the turn system, *enkalkulam* |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Aryanisation of the south"** | ⭐ **Start from [Unit 5's baseline](02_Sangam_Age.md)** — *"varnasrama was YET TO TAKE ROOT"* in Sangam Tamizhagam. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Then this unit: *"Aryanisation… PICKED UP PACE during the Pallava period"*, evidenced by ROYAL GRANTS; caste "firmly established"; Sanskrit "held in high esteem"; Brahmins exempt from tax AND capital punishment; Kanchi's university rivalling Nalanda.** ⭐ **Name the mechanism: land grants to Brahmins invited from the Gangetic north — the same instrument from the [Satavahanas](02_Sangam_Age.md) to the [Cholas](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md)** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Advaita and Visishtadvaita"** | ⭐ **Sankara** *(Kaladi; *maya*; Advaita rooted in Vedanta; four mathas; Saiva and Vaishnava as equally valid; monastic organisation + preserving Sanskrit scripture)* vs **Ramanuja** *(Sriperumpudur; trained in the Sankara school and broke with it; Srirangam; qualified monism; widening the social base; **annual temple entry for groups outside varnashrama**; the later schism)*. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The framing sentence: with Sankara, "bhakti discourse began in SANSKRIT in a PHILOSOPHICAL mode"** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"The decline of Buddhism and Jainism in the Tamil country"** | ⭐ **[Unit 5's baseline](02_Sangam_Age.md): Jainism was the STRONGER tradition** *(the cave shelters)*. **Then the reversal: bhakti's third theme — outright condemnation as heretics · Mahendravarman I's conversion by Appar and his destruction of Jain monasteries · Jains losing royal patronage and retreating to caves and forests · *"Buddhism was fighting a losing battle. ROYAL PATRONAGE, WHICH THE BUDDHISTS LACKED, gave an edge"*.** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Thesis: the decisive variable was PATRONAGE, not doctrine** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **"Indian traders became suppliers rather than carriers"** *(a sharp economic angle)* | ⭐ **The three-stage arc:** **[Sangam](02_Sangam_Age.md)** *(Tamil-Brahmi potsherds at Berenike — Indians going there)* → **PALLAVA** *(west-coast initiative passing to Arabs; contact "indirect… and limited to trade alone")* → **[CHOLA](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md)** *(state force against Sri Vijaya to hold the eastern corridor)*. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **And the structural cause the unit names: these regions "commanded A LIMITED INCOME FROM LAND", so trade mattered more than it did in the Ganges plain** |

## 🔁 Recap — the unit in ten lines

1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The sixth to ninth centuries in the south are one long war between the CHALUKYAS OF BADAMI and the PALLAVAS OF KANCHI** — Pulikesin II beat **Harshavardhana on the Narmada** but was **killed when Narasimhavarman I took Badami**.
2. ⭐ **Both sides commissioned poets to write it up** — **Ravikirti's AIHOLE inscription** for the Chalukyas, the **Kuram plates' Agastya pun** for the Pallavas.
3. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **The Chalukyas introduced SOFT SANDSTONE temple building**, and at **PATTADAKAL we know the ARCHITECTS' NAMES** — one titled ***TRIBHUVACHARYA***, another **REVADI OVAJJA**, with **sculptors signing their own reliefs.** *Almost nowhere else in Indian history.*
4. ⭐ **Chalukya royal women governed provinces, issued coins and inscriptions**; Pallava queens did not govern but **endowed temples**.
5. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A seventh-century Badami inscription states the state's language policy outright: *Kannada the people's language, Sanskrit the language of culture.***
6. ⭐ **Pallava villages ran a genuine water commons** — ***ERIPATTI* tank land**, a **village tank committee**, and **excess water use TAXED** — the institution the [Cholas would inherit](01_Cholas_and_Pandyas.md).
7. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ELLORA: 34 caves, four religions, the HETERODOX sects first, and KAILASANATHA carved from a SINGLE ROCK.** **AJANTA: 30 caves, painting on a plaster of vegetable fibre, paddy husk, rock-grit and sand.** *Ellora sculpture, Ajanta painting.*
8. ⭐ **MAMALLAPURAM runs the whole evolution** — Mahendravarman's **rock-cut** shrines *"without brick, timber, metal or mortar"* → the **monolithic Panchapandava Rathas** → Rajasimha's **structural Shore Temple** — and the **DESCENT OF THE GANGA.**
9. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Bhakti was patronised as STATE IDEOLOGY** by Pallavas and Pandyas, *"spearheaded by the elite and the wealthy merchant class"* — and its three themes were devotion, **protest against Brahmanical exclusiveness**, and ⚠️ **the condemnation of Jains and Buddhists as heretics.**
10. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Then SANKARA moved the discourse into Sanskrit philosophy, and RAMANUJA reopened it socially** — winning temple entry for groups outside *varnashrama* **at least once a year.** ⭐ **And the verdict of Narayanan and Veluthat: bhakti was *"the cementing force"* binding kings, priests and people — to strengthen *"newly established Hindu kingdoms BASED ON THE CASTE SYSTEM."***
