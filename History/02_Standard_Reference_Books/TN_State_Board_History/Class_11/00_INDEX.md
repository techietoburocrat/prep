## 📖 The source

**`Textbook_PDF/TN_Class11_History_English_2024-25.pdf`** — *History, Higher Secondary First Year*, **Government of Tamil Nadu**, English Medium. **First edition 2018; revised 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2025; reprinted 2021, 2024.** Content creation by the **State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT)**; printed by the **Tamil Nadu Textbook and Educational Services Corporation**. **320 pages, 19 units.**

✅ **Legitimately free.** *The title page states it verbatim:* **"A publication under FREE TEXTBOOK PROGRAMME of Government of Tamil Nadu"** *and* **"NOT FOR SALE."** *This is a state government's own free-distribution textbook — the cleanest provenance of any source in this folder.*

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **Why this rather than the old NCERTs (R.S. Sharma / Satish Chandra).** *Those two are also in `02_Standard_Reference_Books/` and stay there as backup depth — but they are **1970s scans with real OCR damage**, requiring a cross-check on every lone date. **This book is a current, cleanly-typeset 2025 reprint**, and it covers **Ancient AND Medieval in one continuous narrative**, which is how the cross-period questions are actually asked *(temple architecture from the Pallavas through Vijayanagara, for instance)*.

## 1️⃣ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE HEADLINE FINDING — read this before the table

**Map the 19 units against your existing notes and a very clear pattern falls out. It is not random which units are gaps.**

```
   ================================================================
   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  THE GAPS IN YOUR NOTES ARE ALMOST ENTIRELY
              ** SOUTH INDIA **
   ================================================================

   NCERT's Ancient/Medieval coverage is built around the
   ** GANGETIC NORTH ** -- Harappa, the mahajanapadas, Magadha,
   the Mauryas, the Guptas, the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughals.
   ** You have all of that, in depth. **

   What NCERT gives ** three pages or nothing ** :

     UNIT 5   Evolution of Society in SOUTH INDIA
              -> the ** SANGAM AGE ** . Almost absent from NCERT.
     UNIT 9   Cultural Development in SOUTH INDIA
              -> Pallava/Chola temple architecture, Tamil
                 literature, the ** Art & Culture core **
     UNIT 11  Later ** CHOLAS AND PANDYAS **
              -> Chola ** local self-government ** , the
                 Uttaramerur inscriptions, maritime expansion

   ⭐ ** THIS IS EXACTLY WHY TN BOARD BOOKS ARE ON EVERY UPSC
      LIST. ** Not because they are better books -- because they
      cover the half of India that NCERT compresses.
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **So the plan for this book is NOT to write 19 files.** *It is to write **the South Indian units in full**, take a **medium delta** on the transitional units, and **skip outright** the four or five units where your Class 12 notes are already better than this textbook. ⭐ **Roughly a third of the book needs real work; two-thirds is already yours.**

## 3️⃣ ⚠️⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE BOUNDARY RULE — so nothing gets written twice

```
   ================================================================
   ** TN CLASS XI OWNS UNITS 1-15 ONLY. **
   ================================================================

   Units 16-19 (Europeans -> British rule -> resistance ->
   modernity) are ** MODERN INDIA ** , and Spectrum covers all of
   it in far more exam-shaped detail.

   ⭐ ** THE CUT: ** this folder stops at ** THE MARATHAS (Unit 15). **
      Everything from ** the Coming of the Europeans onward is
      Spectrum's ** .

   ⚠️ ** Do not write Units 16-19 here. ** If a South Indian
      detail from Units 16-18 is needed later (Anglo-Mysore wars,
      the Vellore Mutiny 1806, the Poligar rebellions), it goes
      into ** Spectrum's ** files as a ⭐ "TN adds" box, ** not into
      a duplicate chapter here. **
```

> 🎯 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **One genuine exception worth naming now: the SOUTH INDIAN resistance movements.** *Spectrum, like most all-India books, is **thin on the Poligar rebellions, the Vellore Mutiny (1806) and the Anglo-Mysore wars from the Mysore side.** **TN Units 17–18 are good on these**, and they are examinable. ⭐ **So they get extracted as boxes INTO the Spectrum files — the content is not lost, only its location is decided.**
